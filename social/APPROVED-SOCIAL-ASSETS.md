## Approval record requirements

Every approved entry must include:

- exact immutable asset path or design/export identifier;
- channel;
- post or defined campaign use;
- required crop/overlay/version;
- Ali's approval date; and
- any expiry or reuse limit.

Approval for one use does not carry into another post, channel or campaign.
Before an asset can be entered here, it must pass the style, colour, canon,
purpose, composition, craft and platform-fit eligibility checks in
`social/SOCIAL-VISUAL-APPROVAL-GATE.md`.
