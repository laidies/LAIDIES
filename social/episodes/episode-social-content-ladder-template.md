## Reach-candidate test

- [ ] Immediate Rewind Era or workplace recognition.
- [ ] Specific identity truth rather than generic relatability.
- [ ] Surprising but accurate connection.
- [ ] Useful, sendable or saveable even before the site click.
- [ ] Easy participation or response.
- [ ] Works with sound off; captions and alt text exist.
- [ ] At least two of these are genuinely strong.
