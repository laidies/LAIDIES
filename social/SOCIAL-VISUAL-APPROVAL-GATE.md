# Social visual approval gate

**Status:** standing publication rule
**Owner:** Ali
**Scope:** every LAiDIES social post, Story, Reel, carousel, paid placement and
launch announcement

## Blocking rule

No social visual may be published unless Ali has explicitly approved the exact
asset for that exact post or a clearly defined campaign use.

The repository is a working studio, not an approved media library. It contains
experiments, rejected generations, review candidates, superseded work and
production assets side by side. Therefore visual selection is **default deny**:
an agent must not browse the general asset tree and choose what appears usable.
Only assets entered in `social/APPROVED-SOCIAL-ASSETS.md` may enter a social
composer.

The following do **not** count as visual approval:

- the asset exists in the repository;
- the asset ships on the website;
- an agent, draft or operating packet recommends it;
- the asset was approved for a different page, post, channel or campaign;
- the image is technically valid, on-brand by checklist or available in the
  authenticated composer; or
- publication mechanics have otherwise been authorized.

## Required evidence before publication

The publication record must identify:

1. the exact asset path or immutable design/export identifier;
2. the exact post/campaign and channel for which it is approved;
3. Ali's explicit approval; and
4. the final visual as it will appear after crop, text overlay and platform
   formatting.

If any item is missing, the visual and post are `HOLD`.

An empty campaign registry means no visual is approved. It is not permission
to choose the best available substitute.

## Candidate eligibility gate

Owner approval is the final decision, not the first quality check. Before an
asset may even be proposed for approval, it must pass all of these against the
current owner-approved brand and visual sources:

- **Style:** the locked LAiDIES illustration/photographic register for that
  campaign; no generic AI look, mixed rendering system or off-style substitute.
- **Colour:** the current approved palette and contrast relationships; no
  approximate, faded, muddy, oversaturated or legacy palette.
- **Canon:** correct characters, faces, wardrobe, locations, signage, spelling
  and era details.
- **Purpose:** the image communicates the actual post idea and has an obvious
  reason to stop the intended audience.
- **Composition:** clear hierarchy, intentional focal point, appropriate energy
  and enough safe space for the channel treatment.
- **Craft:** final-resolution source, clean edges, no generation defects,
  malformed anatomy, accidental text, visual drift or visible repair seams.
- **Platform fit:** the final crop, overlay, aspect ratio and thumbnail have
  been reviewed as the audience will receive them.

Failing any item makes the asset `REJECTED`, not “good enough for now.” Passing
all items only makes it `REVIEW CANDIDATE`; it still cannot publish until Ali
approves the exact use and it is entered in the allow-list.

## Major-launch addition

For an opening, reopening or other major campaign, visual approval is only one
part of the final gate. Publication also requires Ali's explicit approval of:

- the website state being announced;
- the exact final channel copy; and
- the exact final image or video.

Approval of one item never implies approval of the other two.
