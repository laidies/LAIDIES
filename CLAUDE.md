## Building-page visual firewall

Do not show Ali a concept image, generated image, mockup, screenshot,
prototype or coded building-page direction until its exact bytes are in the
owner review queue and `node scripts/check-design-review-admission.mjs` passes.
This applies before visual options are presented, not only after
implementation. A failed or unadmitted visual remains internal repair and must
not be attached, opened or described as ready for Ali.

Before visual production, bind a page architecture that places all core
features, the four canonical visitor states and cross-building objects. Reject
single-image overload, tiny primary objects, fixed-scene growth, misplaced
products, label-only features and decorative filler. Require independent
research, information architecture, product/UX, Brand, red-team and cross-model
evidence against the real artifact.

Maker evidence is not independent judgment. A local PASS is not deployment or
public verification. Use the repository's exact status words and leave the
Control Room dispatcher paused.
