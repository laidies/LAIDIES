The distinction (because it gets asked)

MAiKEOVER on MAiN holds the personal card. Sign-up moment for new members. Weekly dashboard for everyone else. This is where you see your progress.

The Sorority House on Wisteria Lane is the social use of the card. That's where you USE it with other members — chat rooms, Wins, Burn Book, Dear LAiDIES, Girl Talk. All gated.

Same card, two stops. The parlor holds your view of it. The house lets you in to be with everyone else.

Why a beauty parlor?

Because the Y2K beauty parlor is the canon place where you went to become something. New look, new role, new chapter. The Resident Card is the same idea — small ritual, real shift. You walk out the door looking like a member of the LAiDIES because you are.
