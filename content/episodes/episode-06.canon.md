## The two metaphors (spine)
1. **"Your usual."** A new café, you spell out the whole order every time. Your regular café, you walk in and it's already being made — because over time you built the relationship. AI memory is you turning the machine from the new café into your regular.
2. **The Skeleton Key.** Most keys open one door. Set your custom instructions + memory once and you've cut a skeleton key — it opens every new chat already knowing you.

## The teaching — TWO moves (both verified 2026-07-08, see ledger)
**Move 1 — Tell it who you are, once (custom instructions).** The account-wide "how should you respond to me" box. Your job, your audience, your voice, your standing rules — written once, applied to every chat. (ChatGPT "custom instructions"; Claude "Instructions for Claude"; Gemini via Gems; Copilot custom instructions.)
**Move 2 — Let it remember (memory).** Two kinds: **saved memories** (you say "remember that I…", an editable list) and **referenced history** (it picks up preferences from past chats on its own). On by default in Claude (Mar 2026) and available in ChatGPT (saved + reference history; "Dreaming" synthesis, June 2026). You control/see/delete it in Settings → Personalization → Memory.
**Bonus — a saved room for a recurring job (Projects / Gems / Custom GPTs).** When one job repeats, give it its own workspace with its own instructions + files, so you don't re-brief it each time.
**The guard rail (sets up Ep 6):** memory is powerful *because* it keeps things — so be deliberate about what you let it keep. (Full "what not to paste" = Ep 6.)
