How this dictionary works

This is a front door, not a competing source of truth. AI Fundamentals 101 owns foundational definitions; Working with AI 101 owns additional practice terms. Every entry sends you to the maintained explanation, examples and limits.

Use the contents to jump by letter. If a term also has a practical chapter, you will see a second route.
