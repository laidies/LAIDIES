# AI Fundamentals 101 — Section-Level Visual Needs Inventory

**Status:** planning inventory only. It derives visual needs from the vetted 20-chapter manuscript; it does not admit, approve, or produce an asset.

## Binding exclusions

Every prior HTML/CSS or Python-drawn teaching figure is rejected. The prior nine-visual `PASS` claims are invalid and convey no approval. Older quarantined Chapter 1 assets and the former Chapter 2 agent-arrow logic are rejected and are not candidates for reuse.

Prior Figma, Napkin AI, FigureLabs and bespoke ImageGen → Figma workflows are
also closed routes; see the handover's prior-tool-trials section and BTB-460.
Nano Banana was the strongest supporting-art generator tested, but its earlier
assets and the visual layer built around them remain rejected evidence.

Any future proposed visual must be a purpose-built asset with controlled, character-checked technical labels. Select the form that most directly teaches the section: an illustration, annotated scene, cutaway, sequence, comparison graphic, map, or diagram only where the relationship itself requires one. It must teach the named relationship, sequence, comparison, boundary, or failure path; no proposed asset is decorative.

**Key:** `NONE` means that prose, an existing table, or the reader exercise already does the teaching job. It is an intentional no-asset decision.
