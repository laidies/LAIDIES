# Bring Your People: social reward loops

**Status:** canonical product direction; the postcard → Resident Card →
BEST FRIENDS foundation exists; Butterfly Clip and background rewards are not
yet wired  
**Primary home:** Post Office, with rewards delivered to the Town Wallet and
Closet  
**Principle:** reward a real act of connection, then celebrate the relationship
more when it becomes real

## The role of social rewards

SUNNYVAiLE should grow because one person genuinely thought of another person:

> This is exactly your kind of place. Come with me.

That is different from paying residents to distribute links. The action should
feel like sending a postcard, burning a mix CD, passing a book across a desk or
saving someone a seat—not running an affiliate campaign.

It must also never resemble an MLM: no downlines, ranks, recruiting
commissions, income framing, rewards from a friend’s future invitations,
volume-based social status or guilt that somebody “cost” the sender a reward.
See `docs/product/sustainable-growth-and-revenue-principles.md`.

The healthiest loop is:

```text
think of one person
        ↓
send her something personal and useful
        ↓
receive a small immediate thank-you
        ↓
she chooses to visit and make her Resident Card
        ↓
both residents receive the reciprocal reward
        ↓
they have an optional reason to learn or explore together
```

## Canon postcard reward sequence

This is the original Post Office concept and should be treated as canon.

| Moment | Proof | Sender receives | Friend receives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Postcard mailed** | Signed-in resident creates a unique invitation and completes a send/copy action within the allowed cap | Post Office loyalty stamp, small Butterfly Clip thank-you and new Resident Card background choice(s) | The personal postcard and an invitation—not an obligation |
| **Friend arrives** | Unique invitation is opened | A visible `Delivered to SUNNYVAiLE` status; no additional currency | A warm arrival route that preserves the sender, postcard and note |
| **Friend moves into town** | Friend signs in and successfully claims a Resident Card through the invitation | Additional Butterfly Clips and one engraved BEST FRIENDS half | The same additional Butterfly Clips, her matching BEST FRIENDS half and her normal new-resident welcome |
| **They learn together** | Both opt into and complete a named shared activity | Matching Study Buddy/connection collectible or background | The same matching reward |

The exact clip amounts belong to the economy-balancing pass. The relative shape
is locked: **small immediate thank-you; larger reciprocal reward for a real new
resident.**

The sender does not lose her immediate reward if the friend never joins. The
friend is never told she must join to “earn money” for someone else.

## What the BEST FRIENDS necklace means

The necklace is the emotional reward, not a substitute for the Butterfly Clip
reward or the background unlock.

- One completed necklace exists for each distinct friend pair.
- Each half is engraved/linked to the other resident.
- The heart completes only when the invited friend makes her card.
- The Closet shows the person, not a referral count.
- There is no public leaderboard for “most friends recruited.”

The line remains:

> You can’t finish it alone. That’s the point.

## Reward roles stay distinct

| Reward | Job |
|---|---|
| **Post Office loyalty stamp** | Recognizes a meaningful action at Penny’s counter |
| **Butterfly Clips** | Universal spendable thank-you for the eligible send and confirmed join |
| **Resident Card background** | Immediate personalization and visible surprise |
| **BEST FRIENDS necklace** | Permanent relationship collectible |
| **Shared-activity reward** | Encourages useful participation together after joining |

One event can grant several of these through their own ledgers. They must not
be collapsed into one vague “points” event.

## Background-unlock direction

Resident Card backgrounds are a particularly good immediate postcard reward:
they are visible, inexpensive to fulfil, identity-building and do not gate
learning.

Possible reward families:

- **Air Mail** — ruled paper, postmark and red/blue envelope edging;
- **Wish You Were Here** — sunset postcard gradient and tiny town skyline;
- **You’ve Got Mail** — translucent desktop-window or inbox treatment;
- **Together in SUNNYVAiLE** — a matching background unlocked after the friend
  makes her card;
- **Study Buddies** — a shared locker, notebook or coffee-table background
  after a joint learning action; and
- rotating episode postcard backgrounds drawn from approved postcard art.

Names and art require the normal brand/art review. The unlock system must
record ownership; the MAiKEOVER should display owned and locked choices
honestly.

## More connection loops worth building

These are candidates, not simultaneous launch requirements.

| Town moment | Real action | Reward direction | Why it belongs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Penny’s Care Package** | Gift an owned duplicate card, printable or Hall Pass to a resident | Post Office stamp for the giver; delivered object for the recipient; kindness merit at genuine milestones | Creates neighbours, not a resale market |
| **Study Buddies** | Two residents opt into the same weekly Try-On or class and both complete it | Matching puffy sticker, locker photo or card background | Makes practical learning social without making it competitive |
| **KSVL Dedication** | Send a track/episode song with a personal dedication; recipient opens it | Matching two-part mix-CD or jewel-case collectible | Music carries the lesson and the relationship |
| **Pass This Note** | Send one memory hook, quote or useful lesson card from an episode | Post Office/episode stamp; recipient can save the hook | Turns the most memorable teaching lines into useful sharing |
| **LIBRAiRY Pass-Along** | Recommend a specific book, glossary entry or cheat sheet; recipient opens/saves it | Matching bookmark or librarian recognition | Rewards a useful recommendation, not a generic site link |
| **Save Her a Seat** | Invite a friend to a scheduled class, Book Fair or listening party and both attend | Ticket stub, table/locker collectible or small reciprocal clip bonus | Gives return visits a human reason |
| **Dream Phone: Compare Notes** | Both opt into the same safe scenario and compare the generated guidance | Matching phone charm/skin; no private prompt is exposed by default | Builds discernment through conversation |
| **Visitor’s Centre Buddy** | An established resident guides a new resident through the chosen first route; newcomer marks it helpful | Welcome merit badge or town-title recognition | Rewards useful help, not message volume |
| **Book Fair Gift Receipt** | Spend clips on a giftable duplicate/digital item and mail it | Gift receipt keepsake and Post Office stamp; no clip rebate | Makes clips emotionally useful without creating arbitrage |
| **Lunch Table** | A small, opt-in group completes one released weekly route | Group photo-booth strip/background | Supports belonging without a public popularity contest |

## What should and should not earn clips

Good clip events:

- the first eligible postcard send in a defined period;
- a distinct new resident completing a valid invitation and Resident Card;
- both people completing an optional, genuinely useful shared learning action;
- an invited resident returning for her first complete weekly route, if the
  activation bonus is made explicit.

Better as stamps or collectibles—not clips:

- opening an invite;
- sending repeated notes to the same person;
- sending encouragement or gifts;
- receiving likes/reactions;
- adding many people to a contact list; and
- raw community post/message volume.

This prevents kindness from becoming transactional and protects the clip
economy from easy farming.

## Attribution and fairness rules

- A postcard invitation uses an opaque, unguessable invite token; it does not
  rely on a typed public handle as proof.
- One successful new-resident acquisition bonus is attributed to the
  invitation accepted during card claim.
- A resident cannot refer herself.
- One sender/recipient pair cannot produce duplicate join bonuses.
- Reopening, copying or resending the same invitation does not duplicate an
  earn.
- The send reward is capped by period and deduplicated; it is not paid once per
  share destination or click.
- A friend can join without accepting an invitation, and can remove/ignore the
  relationship.
- The recipient’s email address or contacts are never uploaded merely to send
  a postcard.
- Failed, reversed or suspicious transactions do not silently alter either
  resident’s balance.
- A resident can see `mailed`, `delivered`, `joined` and `rewarded` without
  exposing private site activity.

## Current implementation truth — 2026-07-24

Present in the repository and wired as the current foundation; the complete
public cross-device path still needs deployment/smoke-test evidence:

- `postcard.html` provides thirteen postcard fronts, live note composition,
  native share, SMS, email and copy-link routes.
- An incoming postcard preserves `from`, postcard art and note, then carries
  the inviter into MAiKEOVER.
- `maikeover.html` calls `redeem_bestie_invite` after an authenticated Resident
  Card claim.
- `supabase/migrations/20260722234500_bestie_necklace.sql` idempotently grants
  both necklace halves and records both residents as besties.
- `laidies-card.html` renders a BEST FRIENDS collection linked to each friend’s
  public Resident Card.
- Six Resident Card background treatments already exist in MAiKEOVER and the
  Closet.

Not yet complete:

- Postcard sends do not currently earn Butterfly Clips.
- Confirmed joins do not currently award clips to either resident.
- Resident Card backgrounds are choices, not owned/locked rewards.
- No invitation record currently tracks mailed/delivered/joined/rewarded state.
- The current URL puts the sender handle and personal note in query
  parameters; a production invitation should use an opaque token and store
  private invite content appropriately.
- `laidies_invited_by` can remember one handle locally, but it is not a durable
  multi-invitation/referral record.
- Post Office loyalty stamps are not yet wired to the qualifying action.

Public copy already promises the BEST FRIENDS-on-card-claim path. Before a
growth campaign, that exact cross-device backend journey must be verified on
the deployed site. Public copy must not yet promise Butterfly Clips,
background unlocks, reliable delivery state or the additional loops above.

## Durable event contract

Invitation:

```text
invite_id
opaque_token_hash
sender_resident_id
postcard_id
note_reference
created_at
send_rewarded_at
opened_at
accepted_by_resident_id
accepted_at
join_rewarded_at
status
```

Connection reward event:

```text
connection_event_id
program_id
stage: sent | opened | joined | activated
resident_ids[]
invite_id
dedupe_key
reward_grants[]
created_at
metadata
```

The invitation record owns attribution and state. The loyalty ledger owns the
Post Office stamp. The Butterfly Clip ledger owns currency grants. The reward
catalog/ownership ledger owns backgrounds and collectibles.

## Recommended pilot

Launch one complete loop before adding the others:

1. Signed-in resident mails a unique postcard.
2. Penny awards the capped send clips, Post Office stamp and first background
   unlock with a clear receipt.
3. The Town Wallet shows the postcard as `On its way`.
4. The friend opens it and receives the current warm arrival route.
5. She claims her authenticated Resident Card.
6. Both clip balances update once; both BEST FRIENDS halves appear; the sender
   sees `She made it`.
7. Both are offered—but not forced into—one Study Buddy activity.
8. The full journey is tested across devices, duplicate clicks, abandoned
   invitations, self-referrals and private cards.

Only after that feels trustworthy should KSVL dedications, pass-alongs, care
packages or small-group rewards become public promises.

## Definition of done

A resident can:

1. understand the two reward moments before sending;
2. mail one personal postcard without exposing contacts;
3. receive the promised immediate clips, stamp and background exactly once;
4. see the invitation’s truthful state;
5. have a distinct friend accept and make a Resident Card;
6. see both residents receive the reciprocal clips and necklace halves;
7. find and use every unlocked reward in the Closet/MAiKEOVER; and
8. invite another real person because the experience felt thoughtful—not
   transactional.
