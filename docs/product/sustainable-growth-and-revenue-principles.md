# Sustainable growth and revenue principles

**Status:** canonical private product/business direction  
**Public implementation:** no paid offer is currently authorized or live  
**Private cost baseline:** `operations/finance/laidies-cost-and-sustainability-baseline.md`

## The goal

LAiDIES should reach a large audience because:

- the learning is genuinely useful;
- the experience is unusually enjoyable and memorable;
- women want other women to have it;
- the mission matters; and
- people confidently recommend work that makes them look thoughtful for
  sharing it.

LAiDIES should eventually earn revenue because maintaining work of this scope
costs real money and substantial founder time. Financial sustainability protects
the mission. It is not a betrayal of it.

## Confident growth is allowed

“Never cringe” does not mean never ask.

LAiDIES may clearly invite someone to:

- join the town;
- subscribe;
- return next Wednesday;
- send a specific lesson, song, tool or Field Note to one person;
- bring a colleague or friend to a class/listening event;
- buy something genuinely useful; or
- support the work once a credible mechanism exists.

The test is whether the invitation makes sense because of the value delivered,
not because the copy manufactures obligation, status anxiety or false urgency.

## The absolute anti-MLM boundary

LAiDIES will not use:

- downlines, tiers or rewards based on people recruited by someone else;
- “build your team” or income-opportunity framing;
- commissions for recruiting residents;
- public leaderboards for invites or social pressure to climb a rank;
- friendship language that disguises a sales quota;
- rewards for indiscriminate sharing, contact uploads or message volume;
- guilt that a friend’s failure to join cost the sender money;
- cash-equivalent Butterfly Clip schemes or promises of earnings;
- forced posting, templated testimonial pressure or “DM ten women” mechanics;
- fake scarcity, perpetual countdowns or manipulative urgency; or
- community access that depends on selling the community to others.

Butterfly Clips recognize meaningful participation inside SUNNYVAiLE. They are
not cash, an investment, a commission or a recruitment incentive.

## The sharing standard

A share should pass five tests:

1. **Useful alone:** the recipient receives a real lesson, laugh, tool, song or
   encouragement even if she never joins.
2. **One-person energy:** the copy helps the sender think of a particular
   person; it does not turn her into a distribution channel.
3. **No debt:** the recipient is not told she owes the sender a signup,
   reaction or reward.
4. **No volume game:** repeated sends do not create escalating status,
   currency or commissions.
5. **Honest next step:** the site clearly explains what joining provides and
   fulfils that promise.

The strongest CTA can still be direct:

> Know someone who would use this? Send it to her.

Direct is not pushy when the shared object earns the ask.

## The value-based revenue boundary

Future revenue must come from something people value—not access to other
people’s recruitment.

Potentially aligned paths:

- paid practical classes or workshops;
- premium templates, prompt packs, study packs or toolkits;
- an optional paid membership/resource layer after repeat behaviour exists;
- team, ERG or workplace learning packages;
- clearly disclosed affiliate links in the Reference Closet;
- carefully selected sponsorships with editorial independence;
- ticketed events or learning/listening experiences;
- physical/digital Book Fair goods and LAiDIES merchandise; and
- licensing selected learning materials or formats.

Every paid offer must preserve a meaningful free learning experience and
answer:

- Who is paying?
- What useful result do they receive?
- Why is the paid version materially better or more convenient?
- What does it cost LAiDIES to deliver and support?
- What demand signal justifies building it?
- Does the offer strengthen or cheapen trust?
- Can Ali sustain it without creating another exhausting job?

## Two tracks with different timing

### Track 1 — cost stewardship starts now

LAiDIES should immediately know:

- every recurring subscription/vendor;
- currency and billing cadence;
- actual monthly equivalent;
- what production job it performs;
- whether it is essential, seasonal, duplicative or unused;
- export/ownership/lock-in risk;
- downgrade/cancellation date; and
- what would break if it disappeared.

No subscription is cancelled merely because it looks expensive. First map its
dependencies and current production value.

### Track 2 — audience monetization waits for evidence

Do not add a paid tier simply because the project costs money. First establish:

- a stable, trustworthy public experience;
- repeat visits and completion behaviour;
- which lessons/tools people return to;
- direct requests or strong signals for deeper help;
- a small paid result LAiDIES can deliver excellently; and
- an operational model that includes Ali’s time.

Costs are evidence that sustainability matters. They are not evidence that a
particular audience offer will work.

## Sustainability ladder

1. **Know the burn:** complete the vendor/subscription and variable-cost
   inventory.
2. **Stop invisible waste:** consolidate or downgrade only where production
   evidence supports it.
3. **Measure audience value:** track joins, returns, completions, saves, shares,
   requests and qualitative feedback.
4. **Run one small paid test:** one buyer, one result, one delivery path, no
   membership complexity.
5. **Recover recurring cash cost:** learn whether revenue can reliably cover
   the direct monthly tool/service baseline.
6. **Value founder labour:** measure creation/operations time and design
   revenue that compensates it rather than merely reimbursing software.
7. **Reinvest deliberately:** expand only the experiences that improve
   learning, community or sustainable revenue.

## Revenue readiness gate

A monetization test is ready for Ali’s decision only when it has:

- a named buyer and job;
- a concrete deliverable/result;
- evidence of demand;
- price hypothesis and delivery cost;
- founder-time estimate;
- privacy/rights/accessibility implications;
- operational/support burden;
- free-versus-paid boundary;
- reputation review;
- success/stop signal; and
- no dependency on recruitment or relationship pressure.

Nothing publishes or charges without explicit Ali approval and an end-to-end
payment, fulfilment, refund/failure and support test.
