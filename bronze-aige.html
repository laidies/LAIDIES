The cocktail party explanations

This segment was born right here, during businesswomen's special happy hour — three sips in, somebody put down her drink and said, "okay, but what IS it? Actually?" The table went quiet. Everybody wanted to know; nobody wanted to be the one to ask. So now every episode hands you the answer, and the bar keeps them framed behind the counter. When the question comes up in real life, you already have your order.

Episode 01 · "okay, but what IS it? Actually?"

"It's the most talented new hire you'll ever manage. It's read everything — books, articles, forums, billions of documents — and lived nothing: no job, no consequences, no read on your office politics. Day-one talent. You don't hand it the keys — you onboard it, you manage it, and you review its work."

Episode 02 · "so… what's a prompt, really?"

"A prompt isn't code. It's a delegation. You're not programming a machine — you're briefing an assistant. And you already know how to brief. This one is just faster, never sleeps, and will absolutely fold the cheese wrong if you don't tell her how."

Episode 03 · "wait — why does it just make things up?"

"It's not lying. Lying takes intent. It's guessing — when it doesn't know, it doesn't stop, it reaches for the most plausible-sounding thing and says it with its whole chest. It's your most confident friend: she'll answer any question, whether or not she actually knows. The fix was never to catch her in a lie. It's to ask for the receipt before you repeat her in a meeting."

Episode 04 · "so… is this whole AI thing brand new, or what?"

"It's almost two hundred years old and about three years old at the same time. The science has been building for centuries; your access to it is brand new. And every real leap in it — the idea, the signal, the language, the finding, the sight — has a woman's name on it. Whether or not the textbook bothered to write it down."
