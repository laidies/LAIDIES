★ Girl power meets machine power

LAiDIES teaches AI in the language you already speak.

The 90s had Sunnydale, Bayside, and Capeside. Silicon Valley has Sunnyvale. You have SUNNYVAiLE.

The 90s wired us. Boy bands, girl groups, sleepover games, hair full of butterfly clips, blow-up furniture, calling in to request a song on the radio so you could record it on a cassette, and the glory days of 24-episode seasons.

Now AI is wiring the next thing. This is where you come to learn it.

SUNNYVAiLE is a functional town designed to help you learn AI and have fun doing it. It's a learning system grounded in fun examples and analogies to help the concepts stick. Episodes teach the concept. Study Packs reinforce. Songs on KSVL turn it into an earworm you can't forget. Pop quizzes test recall. Games practice application. The community pressure-checks. Every part of town echoes the others — that's how the learning sticks.

What every building teaches

The NewsStand is where you read the latest AI headlines and connect what you're learning to the real world. The Chick Flicks is the video store where every weekly episode lives. The Blend & Snap is the coffee shop where the Study Packs live. SUNNYVAiLE High is where you take the Pop Quizzes and dive deeper into core concepts. The LIBRAiRY is where you check the references. KSVL is where you listen to the tracks. The LUMINAiRY is where the PATRON SAiNTS of SUNNYVAiLE teach through their moves — and where the MAiVENS leading the field of AI are honored. And we even have our very own FAiRY Godmother who helps you with prompts and practical advice. Take a lap around the town map below to see every place you can visit.

Analogies that stick. In a fictional town from an era we know and love. That's how we're teaching it.

★ Meet the PATRON SAiNTS · A stop on your visit

The town's PATRON SAiNTS.

"We pledge Allegiance to the united SAiNTS of SUNNYVAiLE."

Every town has its patron saints — the figures locals point at when a stranger asks who this place is really about. Ours are eleven women (and one Rose) from the movies and TV that shaped a generation. Cher's wardrobe recommender in 1995. Elle's pink-stationery receipts. Miranda's chess-master gaze. Regina's lunch-ender. They live at the LUMINAiRY on Lantern Hill — visit any time. When a moment shows up you don't know how to handle, borrow their move.

C

Cher Horowitz

Making it yours

Cher's closet computer didn't just hold clothes — it knew her closet, her occasions, her rules. Set the tool up to know you, and it dresses you right every morning.
E

Elle Woods

Receipts

Elle walked into court with the perm rule, the timeline, and the witness already lined up. She didn't bring receipts. She had them.
M

Miranda Priestly

Standards

Miranda knew the cerulean's whole history before it touched a closet. Knowing what you're looking at is the difference between a yes and a yes-with-changes.
B

Buffy Summers

Slaying the tools

Buffy runs toward the vampire. Everybody else is still in the parking lot trying to get away.
R

Regina George

The Cautionary Tale

The anti-saint. Regina told Cady the skirt was cute, then someone else it was ugly — same skirt, total confidence, all of it in the Burn Book. Confidently wrong, careless with secrets. Don't be like Regina.
D

David Rose

Specificity

"Fold in the cheese" is an instruction. "Make this better" is also an instruction. They fail the same way.
D

Deb

Loop Me Out

Deb trademarked Deb-flection™ in 1997 and has survived every reorg since 2004 by looping herself OUT of every request. Also the PATRON SAiNT of Deb.
DP

Dolly Parton

Common Sense

You better get to building your own bridge, because ain't nobody building it for you, honey.
S

Samantha Jones

Orientation

Samantha reads a room the second she's in it — who matters, what it's for, which version of her it needs. Size up the situation before you reach for the tool.
O

Oprah Winfrey

Staying Current

Oprah read the whole stack so you didn't have to, then handed you the one worth your weekend. Current isn't reading everything — it's knowing what's worth your time.
J

Jennifer Lopez

Range

J.Lo sings, acts, dances, and runs the label — same woman, every medium. The tool does more than one thing too; don't use it for only one.
SM

Sister Mary Clarence

Teaching

Sister Mary Clarence took a stiff choir and had the whole room singing — nobody left in the back. Real teaching lifts everyone up with you.
