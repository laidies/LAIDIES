A Slayer Needs a Watcher

The SLAiYER Handbook, Chapter 1 — which AI tool to open for which job, what the words mean, and how to use any of them without getting staked.

This week I watched a CFO type "just make it better" into ChatGPT and act betrayed by what came back. Make what better. For whom. By when. Compared to what. The tools are good. The instructions are the gig — and the instructions are the part nobody is teaching us. So we wrote it down. Episode 4 is the SLAiYER Handbook: which AI to open for which job, what the words actually mean, and how to use any of them without getting staked.
