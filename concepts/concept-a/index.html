A Slayer Needs a Watcher

Your field guide to actually using AI at work — which tool, what to say, and how not to get vamped.

This week I watched a CFO type "just make it better" into ChatGPT and act betrayed by what came back. The tools are good. The instructions are the gig — and the instructions are the part nobody is teaching us. So we wrote it down. Episode 4 is the SLAiYER Handbook: which AI to open for which job, what the words actually mean, and how to use any of them without getting vamped.

From the Editor

LAiDIES started because too many smart women were getting left behind on AI — not because they couldn't keep up, but because nobody was explaining it in a way that respected their time. So we built the magazine we wanted. The Grimoire is the reference shelf. The Clubhouse is the group chat. Every Wednesday, there's a new episode. And the patron saints are who we keep coming back to when work gets weird.

— Ali · writing from Toronto
