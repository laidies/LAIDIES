How it all works

Why LAiDIES exists

Ada Lovelace · Keeper of the First Algorithm

Women have been shaping this field from the very beginning—AI is almost two hundred years old, and it was built, leap after leap, by women.

What it becomes next is being decided right now. Getting involved—learning it, using it, having opinions about it—is how we shape the future the way we shaped the past. That is why LAiDIES exists. And since we’re here anyway, we may as well have fun.

In the famous words of Founding Mother Karen Spärck Jones:

“Computing is too important to be left to men.”— Karen Spärck Jones · Keeper of the Index

So join LAiDIES and make sure women continue to light the way.

  1. 1

    Follow the plot

    Start with the trailer, then read or hear the episodes in order as our main character learns AI and discovers SUNNYVAiLE alongside you.

    Season One teaches the AI basics in 24 episodes, because we remember the glory days when a season had time for a proper plot, a questionable haircut and at least one inexplicable musical episode. Future seasons will continue the story—and take your AI fluency further.

  2. 2

    Unlock the idea

    Each episode pairs an unfamiliar AI concept with a film, television moment, character, lyric or gloriously specific Y2K reference your brain already understands.

    Because sometimes “context window” makes more sense when explained through The Devil Wears Prada—and butterfly clips, roll-on glitter and a suspiciously translucent landline have waited long enough to contribute to your professional development.

  3. 3

    Make it click

    Take the idea for a spin with a practical Try-On, then reinforce it with a printable guide, Pop Quiz and collectible teaching cards.

    You’ll finish with something you can actually use—not just another tab you opened with excellent intentions and quietly abandoned three Tuesdays ago.

  4. 4

    Make it stick

    Every episode has an original song that turns its central lesson into something you can hear, hum and remember.

    You’ll find it on KSVL 99.9, SUNNYVAiLE’s community radio station—motto: don’t just learn from books, learn from hooks. KSVL also spins the rest of the town’s hits: the anthems, odes to the Patron Saints, and the jams that help SUNNYVAiLE stick. Because if one chorus can occupy your brain since 1997, it might as well teach you something.

    Tune into KSVL 99.9
  5. 5

    Join the town

    You can explore SUNNYVAiLE without signing up. But becoming a resident gives you a community of women to learn alongside—and a place to keep everything you learn and earn.

    At Delta LAi Nu—SUNNYVAiLE’s sorority house—residents can ask questions, share wins and trade teaching cards in the chat rooms. You’ll also unlock Girl Talk, a SUNNYVAiLE spin on the classic ’90s truth-or-dare game.

    Membership is free: no pledging, no rush week and no matching pink velour Juicy tracksuit required—although the outfit is highly encouraged… especially on Wednesdays.

Save, collect and continue

Everything you learn—and earn—has somewhere to live.

Your Resident Card is your SUNNYVAiLE town ID. Make it once at MAiKEOVER on MAiN—it takes about two minutes—and the town can remember you: which episodes you’ve finished, where you left off and what you’ve collected.

Your Resident Card also opens the doors to Delta LAi Nu, SUNNYVAiLE’s sorority house, and gives you a Closet inside. Its residents-only chat rooms are where you can ask questions, share wins and trade teaching cards. Delta LAi Nu is also home to Girl Talk, our SUNNYVAiLE spin on the classic ’90s truth-or-dare game.

It’s considerably more fun than joining yet another professional networking group.

Your Closet is where your SUNNYVAiLE life lives: a room of your own at Delta LAi Nu, with a page you can make yours and show off. Inside, you’ll find your progress, saved LIBRAiRY finds and the haul you collect as you explore:

  • Charms — Hidden around town. Spot one and it snaps onto your bracelet.
  • Puffies — Puffy stickers that mark your favourite Handbook pages. The Puffy Board keeps them together.
  • Stickers — Quizzes, games and activities pay out. Your sticker book lives on your Resident Card.
  • Trading cards — Collect a pack with every episode. Duplicates are the point: trade them with other residents.
  • Mail — Notes and gifts to your girls through the Post Office. Coming soon.

Cher was right: “It does not say RSVP on the Statue of Liberty.” But we’d love yours. Become a SUNNYVAiLE resident—we’ll save you a Closet.
