Why LAiDIES exists

Why women must shape AI.

New technology does not automatically leave old inequalities behind. AI can reproduce the biases in the data, assumptions and decisions used to build it. If women’s needs and experiences are left out, we risk repeating the same mistakes—continuing to embed bias in the systems that shape work, opportunity and everyday life. We must not let this happen again and we must participate in how those systems are built, used, and held accountable.

So why should women engage and participate with AI when we are already so busy?

Use it better. Take your career further.From “ARGH, WTF?” to “Ah. That’s why.”What you ask, what context you provide, what the system can see and how you evaluate its answer can all change the result. Understand why an AI tool has gone off track—and what you can do about it. These are practical career skills: strengthening your work, exercising judgment and preparing for new responsibilities.
See through the noise. Make sound decisions.From “OMG, the end is nigh!” to “Ugh, as if.”You cannot cut through that noise by memorising a list of approved opinions. You need enough of the fundamentals to ask: What evidence supports the claim? What does the demonstration leave out? What would have to be true for this prediction to happen? That is how you evaluate a tool, a workplace proposal or a headline on its merits.
Have a say. Help change the defaults.From “Whatever” to “RSVP: Yes. I have notes!”Once you understand how AI works, you can participate meaningfully in decisions about what happens next. These debates are already happening—in workplaces, governments, schools, city halls and homes. Refusing to engage does not stop decisions from being made. It only means they will be made without you.

Women have always been pivotal to AI

Meet the women behind AI and get inspired to help shape the future.

Women have shaped computing from its earliest foundations and helped build the ideas, machines and methods that made modern AI possible.

What it becomes next is being decided right now. Getting involved—learning it, using it, having opinions about it—is how we shape the future the way we shaped the past. That is why LAiDIES exists. And since we’re here anyway, we may as well have fun.

So join LAiDIES and make sure women continue to light the way.

The LAiDIES method

Your brain kept the references. We put them to work.

The plot explains it. The analogy unlocks it. Practice makes it click. The soundtrack makes it stick.

Your brain has been saving Clueless, Carrie Bradshaw, “Wannabe,” dial-up tones, butterfly clips and roll-on glitter for years. At LAiDIES, that is not useless trivia. It is a head start.

Every lesson unfolds in SUNNYVAiLE—a fictional town tuned to the Rewind Era (1990–2010), where sequential stories, accurate pop-culture analogies, practical activities and original songs work together to make AI easier to understand, use and remember.

Your AI question desk

Ask Miss Jeeves anything about AI.

Tell her what is happening, what everyone is talking about, or what you want to do. Miss Jeeves checks current LAiDIES books, episodes, NewsStand reporting, Study Packs, tools and activities. If LAiDIES covers it, she gives you a direct answer and the best place to continue. If it does not, she tells you honestly and can pass the question to the editorial team only with your consent.

For example:Why does the AI ignore part of my prompt?Why is everyone talking about Nvidia?Which AI tool or model should I use for meeting notes?

Ask in plain language. Do not include private, confidential or account information.

Resident Card · Closet · Community

Move to SUNNYVAiLE.

Ready to make it official? Create your Resident Card at MAiKEOVER on MAiN. It is your SUNNYVAiLE town ID and gives you your own Closet—the place where your Card, supported saves and keepsakes live.

Then visit Delta LAi Nu, SUNNYVAiLE’s sorority house and community home. Explore the rooms, join the conversation and try Girl Talk—our private honour-system spin on classic ’90s truth or dare.

It’s considerably more fun than joining yet another professional networking group.

Inside your Closet, you can keep track of what you have found around town:

  • Charms: Hidden around town. Spot one and it snaps onto your bracelet.
  • Puffies: Puffy stickers that mark your favourite Handbook pages. The Puffy Board keeps them together.
  • Stickers: Selected quizzes and activities can add keepsakes to your Card. They are saved in this browser.
  • Trading cards: Preview the teaching prompts planned for each episode. Packs, ownership, duplicates and trading are not available yet.
  • Mail: Notes and gifts to your girls through the Post Office. Coming soon.

Cher was right: “It does not say RSVP on the Statue of Liberty.” But we’d love yours. Move to SUNNYVAiLE and make yourself at home.
