The Origin Story

Built by one woman, pushed forward by the right group chat.

Six months ago, I could not get an Amazon Women-in-Tax newsletter to send properly. I work in tax, not tech — not a developer. The ideas were there, but they kept stalling behind someone else's time, budget, or availability.

Then I started using AI. Since then I have built a personal command centre, designed agents for research and operations, and joined an Amazon Women-in-Tax hackathon where our team built Echo — an AI product VPs now want in production. And I built everything you are looking at right now.

Receipt

Five days from "I have never built a website" to a living, glitter-covered AI world.

With AI, the idea stopped waiting for a traditional tech on-ramp. In under three weeks, the vision became a newsletter, website, visual system, quiz archive, games, agents, Episode packs, and a community space people can actually use.

P.S. I do not have this all perfectly figured out. If you spot a problem, have a better idea, or know a shortcut — tell me. We are all in this together, preferably with better snacks.

Use it like an app

Add LAiDIES to your Home Screen.

Quick access from your home screen — one tap, no app store. For now, scores and rewards stay on the browser and device you use.

iPhone / iPad
  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share. On newer iOS versions, tap the Share button in the toolbar or open the More menu first.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If you see Open as Web App, leave it on.
  4. Name it LAiDIES, then tap Add.
Android
  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap More, the three dots in the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen. Some Android versions say Install app or Create shortcut.
  4. Confirm Add or Install, then move the icon wherever you want it.
