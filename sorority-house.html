★ House rules · Delta LAi Nu, est. 2026

How we treat each other in here

  1. The messy draft is allowed. Half-finished thoughts count. Perfectionism is not a sisterly virtue.
  2. Cite the receipt or say you're guessing. Confidence is not a source.
  3. No burning a real person. The Burn Book is for Y2K references and takes, not sisters.
  4. Bring your card. Every post is signed by your Closet. Anonymous doesn't happen here.
  5. The dare is optional; the honesty isn't. Skip a dare, keep the truth.
