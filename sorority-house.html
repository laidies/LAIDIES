June’s house rules

We are not mean in this house.

  1. The messy draft is allowed. Half-finished thoughts count. Perfectionism is not a sisterly virtue.
  2. Cite the receipt or say you're guessing. Confidence is not a source.
  3. No burning a real person. The Burn Book is for Y2K references and takes, not sisters.
  4. Know the boundary. Hyvor controls its own sign-in, publication and moderation. A local Resident Card does not sign a community post.
  5. Keep public rooms safe. Do not post confidential work, private messages, personal identifiers, high-stakes personal details or another person's information.
  6. The dare is optional; the honesty isn't. Skip a dare, keep the truth.

Your Resident Card is local profile space — not a community login.
