## This is a reintroduction, not a routine episode post

The public audience has not yet been introduced to the full SUNNYVAiLE concept
or the rebuilt website. The most recent public post refers to the former
Episode 4 / Grimoire world, which has since been removed and repurposed.

The next major campaign therefore has to do three things in order:

1. introduce what LAiDIES is and why it exists;
2. orient people to SUNNYVAiLE and the Rewind Era without making the town the
   lesson itself; and
3. deliver a genuinely excellent episode and a working path through the site.

An episode announcement alone cannot carry that reset.

## The release covenant

When LAiDIES publicly promises that a visitor can read, watch, listen, learn,
play, search, collect, join or return, that action works on the public site.

“Ready” does not mean every imagined feature exists. It means:

- the promise is clear;
- the advertised journeys are complete;
- unfinished features are hidden or labelled honestly;
- errors have humane recovery paths;
- the site works for a new visitor on mobile and desktop;
- accessibility is part of the shipped experience;
- analytics can show where frustration occurs; and
- the architecture is stable enough for people to learn it.

## Gate A — current story and naming

- [ ] Homepage says clearly that LAiDIES teaches practical AI fluency.
- [ ] The Rewind Era is defined once as 1990–2010, from dial-up to downloads.
- [ ] SUNNYVAiLE is introduced as the learning world, not as homework required
      before a visitor can understand the offer.
- [ ] The reveal makes the experience promise clear: come for rigorous,
      practical AI learning; stay for the Rewind Era fun; keep building skill,
      knowledge, confidence and community while exploring.
- [ ] “Be kind, rewind” has its current LAiDIES meaning.
- [ ] Why LAiDIES exists is clear: practical learning, women’s participation,
      community and “computing is too important to be left to men.”
- [ ] Current terms, locations and feature names agree across navigation,
      homepage, episodes, search, email and social profiles.
- [ ] Grimoire-era public routes either redirect to the correct current
      destination or present an intentional archive; none imply the retired
      information architecture is current.
- [ ] No current page, metadata card or share preview resurrects the former
      Episode 4 premise accidentally.

## Gate B — first-visit comprehension

Test with someone or a clean browser state that has never seen the project.
Within the first visit, she can answer:

- [ ] What is LAiDIES?
- [ ] Who is it for without making allies feel excluded?
- [ ] What will I learn or be able to do?
- [ ] What should I click first?
- [ ] What is this week’s episode?
- [ ] Can I read, listen or watch it?
- [ ] What is one useful thing I can try immediately?
- [ ] Why would I come back on Wednesday?

The visual world may reward exploration. It may not hide the product promise.

## Gate B2 — immersion without overwhelm

The experience follows
`docs/brand/rewind-era-immersive-experience-principles.md`.

- [ ] The site feels like an inhabited 1990–2010 world, not a modern course
      with nostalgic decorations placed around it.
- [ ] Each building behaves like its location and its interactive objects look
      and act usable.
- [ ] A first visitor sees one clear explanation, the current episode and one
      inviting thing to try before the wider inventory.
- [ ] A returning visitor can see what is new, resume what matters and find one
      useful next move.
- [ ] The full town, archives, games, books, songs and Easter eggs remain
      available through an optional Explore path.
- [ ] Each page has one primary action and a clear back/next route.
- [ ] The map, signs, search and labels orient the visitor; she is not required
      to memorize the town.
- [ ] Surprise-and-delight moments are optional, accessible and do not
      interrupt a task or conceal essential information.
- [ ] Every promoted feature declares and fulfils an honest teach, practise,
      judge, retrieve, activate, encourage, belong or delight job.
- [ ] Mme CLAi-O/Girl Talk-style encouragement is treated as a legitimate
      outcome rather than disguised as technical instruction.
- [ ] Music is enjoyable enough to replay and its memorable hooks accurately
      reinforce the lesson.
- [ ] No page exposes unfinished inventory merely to make SUNNYVAiLE look
      larger.

## Gate B3 — practise what LAiDIES teaches

Every public lesson, tutorial, comparison, prompt example, factual claim and
Behind the Build piece follows the production integrity gate in
`docs/product/behind-the-build-learning-system.md`.

- [ ] Factual claims point to named evidence; changing tool/model/product
      claims include an as-of date and use primary or official sources where
      possible.
- [ ] Observation, inference, opinion and verified diagnosis are not collapsed
      into one confident statement.
- [ ] Prompts presented as good examples meet LAiDIES’ own standards for
      context, task, constraints and success criteria.
- [ ] An intentionally weak prompt is labelled, paired with the reason it
      fails and followed by a stronger version.
- [ ] Results were tested against the stated job; plausible-looking output is
      not treated as proof.
- [ ] Limitations, uncertainty and meaningful counterexamples are disclosed.
- [ ] Privacy, consent, copyright, quotation and attribution checks pass.
- [ ] Public descriptions match the implemented and deployed experience.
- [ ] A reader receives a practical action she can reuse.
- [ ] Any Behind the Build lesson is VERIFIED before publication and records
      the internal prompt, rule, checklist or test changed because of it.
- [ ] Corrections have an owner and update the public piece, source record and
      affected derivatives.

## Gate F — the public reveal sequence

Prepare the sequence as one campaign:

1. **Signal the change** — a short teaser that something new is opening.
2. **Reintroduce LAiDIES** — who it is for, why it exists and the learning
   promise.
3. **Reveal SUNNYVAiLE** — the new world and website, with one clear front door.
4. **Define the Rewind Era** — twenty years of pop culture, from dial-up to
   downloads (1990–2010).
5. **Orient the visit** — a fast tour showing where to start, not every feature.
6. **Prove the learning** — release/showcase the excellent new-format episode
   and its practical result.
7. **Invite the return** — Wednesday rhythm, follow/subscribe and one community
   action.

Before the reveal:

- [ ] Social bios, profile links, link-in-bio and LinkedIn descriptions match
      the new promise.
- [ ] Pinned posts create a current front door even though the older Grimoire
      post remains in the history.
- [ ] Reveal Reel/carousel/Stories/LinkedIn assets have correct safe areas,
      captions, alt text and public URLs.
- [ ] The website’s welcome path is the destination used by the reveal—not a
      stale homepage anchor.
- [ ] Comments, DMs, errors and common “where do I start?” questions have an
      owner and response plan.

## Gate G — stability after reveal

People should not have to relearn the site every week.

- Lock the core promise, primary navigation, town locations and public naming
  for at least the first six weekly releases after reveal, except for genuine
  bugs, safety, accessibility or legal corrections.
- Add weekly value through episodes and content inside the system.
- Batch substantial feature/architecture changes into an intentional release,
  with QA, migration/redirects and a short “what changed” note.
- Maintain one visible internal changelog and one public explanation only when
  a change affects how visitors use the site.
- Put attractive new ideas into the opportunity scan/backlog until their user
  job, dependencies and release window are clear.

This is not a freeze on improvement. It is protection for the audience’s
mental model.
