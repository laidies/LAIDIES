## Reusable Episode Masthead Rule

For each Episode template pass, choose one strong existing image from that Episode and treat it the way the homepage masthead is treated:

- Image is the masthead visual layer.
- LAiDIES logo, Episode number, title, premise, and date are live HTML.
- Do not recreate a new image if a strong Episode image already exists.
- Do not bake important text into a new image.
- Do not reuse another Episode's masthead or a generic homepage masthead.

This rule should guide Episode 2 later, using Episode 2's own imagery.
