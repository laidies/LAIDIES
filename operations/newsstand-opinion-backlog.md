## Queued (Ali 2026-07-23)
- **GENERAL AI (AGI)** — Ali wants a Tribune article on **General AI** (she corrected herself mid-sentence: *general*, explicitly NOT *generative*). Proposed angle: cut through the single most hype-drenched term in AI. Today's AI is **narrow / specialist** — dazzling at the one thing, clueless the moment you step off its lane; **"general" AI** = one system that can do basically anything a person can, including tasks it was never built for. The honest catch (the Tribune's whole reason to run it): **nobody agrees what AGI even means or how we'd know we'd reached it** — the definition keeps moving and the word does triple duty as a research goal, a marketing line, and a fundraising pitch. So "we're almost there" is a claim about a finish line **no one has painted**. **THE SPINE (sharpened after Ali's catch, 2026-07-23):** the article's core is the *line* between **AGI** (one *generally* capable mind — breadth is internal) and **agentic AI / an agent running sub-agents** (a dispatcher that stays narrow but *routes* to specialists — breadth by delegation; feels general, isn't). Today's "it did my whole project" tools are the second kind, which is exactly why people mistake them for AGI — that confusion IS the piece. NB: agent-orchestration is already LAiDIES canon as [[your-court-saint-agents]] (the saints = specialist agents), so AGI must be taught as DISTINCT from it, not blurred into it. **Analogy — PENDING Ali's pick:** she flagged the original "one clerk who does every job" as actually describing agent-orchestration, not AGI. Candidate A = keep the Mall but use *both* readings to draw the line (a concierge who walks you to the right specialist boutique = agent + sub-agents; one person who can personally do every trade *and* learn a new one tomorrow = AGI). Candidate B = 90s single-task kitchen gadgets (bread machine / quesadilla maker — each does one thing) = today's narrow specialists, vs. a person who can just *cook anything* = AGI (scope-flag: frame the cook as one general skill, NOT "using the gadgets," or it slides back into orchestration). Reader takeaway: you don't have to bet on whether AGI is coming; the tool on your desk *today* is narrow, so the skill is knowing what it's actually good at, not waiting for the everything-machine. **NEEDS before draft:** fact-check pass — real, current lab definitions of AGI + any dated public claims/timelines, each attributed as *their* characterization, not a LAiDIES verdict (same discipline as the Velvet Rope entry). Could stand alone or seed a thread on "the hype words" (AGI / superintelligence / etc.). — Ali 2026-07-23
