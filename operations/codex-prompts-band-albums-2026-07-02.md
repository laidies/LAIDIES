## Shared visual DNA (apply to all 10)

- **Register:** Y2K-era CD-jewel-case cover art (1998–2004), painterly illustration or stylized graphic — NOT photorealistic and NOT sticker/cartoon. Same visual universe as the LAiDIES patron-saint stained-glass portraits: painterly, considered, richly saturated but not gaudy.
- **No text on the artwork itself.** Band name + album title get overlaid on the site via a Sharpie-label CSS treatment (matching the existing Mix CDs). Codex renders **art only**.
- **No real people.** All figures are fictional composites — silhouettes, back-of-head, hands, or symbolic imagery. Never resemble the specific pop-culture reference the sound family points to.
- **Composition:** Center-safe, aspect-ratio 1:1, subject clearly readable at 300×300 thumbnail.
- **Palette:** LAiDIES core (plum #4b2148, rose #9b3f5f, gold #c9a227, cream #fffdfb) as the through-line, but each genre can lean into its own accent (see per-band notes).
