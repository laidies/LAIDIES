# Weekly printable construction — 2026-07-24

## Decision

The printable route is a reference-room worktable, not a muted editorial
landing page and not a two-column grid of document cards. The real weekly pages
are the interface.

## Construction grammar

- Each issue title opens on one near-black-blue reference-room arrival.
- Download and Extra Credit are the two immediate jobs, shown together without
  rounded pills.
- All four real preview pages sit on one native horizontal worktable as
  physical sheets with small sheet-number labels.
- The bottom checkout repeats the issue-specific PDF and Extra Credit actions
  and provides the single town exit.
- The former fixed return control is now part of the checkout and cannot cover
  page content.

## Preserved behaviour

- Episode 01, 02, 03, and 04 configurations remain.
- All sixteen existing preview images remain.
- All four existing PDF files and filenames remain.
- `from=this-week`, `issue`, `bag`, `group`, and draft return parameters remain.
- The Extra Credit route remains.
- Unknown issue numbers still hide the worktable, disable both download
  controls, and show the unpublished state.

## QA result

All four published issue variants and one unpublished issue state pass at
1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 with no page-level horizontal overflow. Titles,
previews, matching top/bottom PDF URLs, Extra Credit return parameters, and the
unavailable guard were verified. All four PDF files and every referenced
preview file exist.

## Art boundary

No generated scene is required. The sixteen printable pages are the real
visual objects. Their individual editorial styles may evolve issue by issue
without changing the reference-room page construction.
