Copy this file to `content/episodes/episode-NN.fidelity.md` before writing or
rewriting an episode substance sheet.

This gate prevents a candidate from becoming a rigorous answer to a different
brief. It is completed by Codex before Ali sees Gate 1. Ali still makes one
decision—approve or reject the substance—but she receives the comparison
evidence instead of having to discover drift by interrogation.

## E. Four independent vetoes

Each lens can fail the candidate. A technical pass cannot compensate for a
fidelity or episode-fit failure.

### 1. Concept fidelity

- [ ] The candidate still teaches the episode Ali approved.
- [ ] No supporting tool, checklist or example has replaced the premise.
- [ ] Every deferment/removal has Ali approval.

### 2. Technical truth

- [ ] The machinery is accurate after the analogy is stripped away.
- [ ] Where the analogy bends is taught without destroying its teaching job.
- [ ] Volatile claims have dated primary evidence or are excluded.

### 3. Teaching value and transfer

- [ ] A smart non-technical reader learns more than terminology.
- [ ] The worked example makes the mechanism observable.
- [ ] An unfamiliar example proves transfer beyond memorized brands.

### 4. LAiDIES episode fit

- [ ] The heroine has a real opening problem and changed understanding.
- [ ] The lesson maps to the locked recurring beats.
- [ ] The patron saint/reference and outfit have teaching/story jobs.
- [ ] The cocktail line, Try-On and next-week hook arise from the same lesson.
- [ ] The result is an episode journey, not a worksheet with jokes attached.
