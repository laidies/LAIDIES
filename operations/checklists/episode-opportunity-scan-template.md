# Episode opportunity scan — template

Copy this file to `operations/weeks/episode-NN-opportunity-scan.md` when an
episode's substance is approved.

This is a decision record, not a brainstorming dump. Every row must finish as
`required`, `selected`, `deferred`, or `not_applicable`. `Deferred` needs a
destination and trigger. `Not_applicable` needs a reason. A blank row is a
missing decision.

## Status key

- `required` — part of every episode package.
- `selected` — conditional opportunity chosen for this episode.
- `deferred` — good opportunity, intentionally moved; record where and why.
- `not_applicable` — does not strengthen this episode; record why.
- `blocked` is not a final status. Record the blocker, owner and next action,
  then keep the real status as `required` or `selected`.

### Learning quality check

- [ ] The episode teaches the concept directly before the analogy carries it.
- [ ] The worked example is real, useful and reproducible.
- [ ] The Try-On produces a result in about ten minutes.
- [ ] The quiz checks understanding and transfer, not reference trivia.
- [ ] The Study Sheet can refresh the lesson without rereading the episode.
- [ ] The Cheat Sheet is useful after the joke has worn off.
- [ ] Any class teaches a visible screen/action; it does not repeat the episode.
- [ ] Existing books were checked before proposing a new book.
- [ ] Selected class or LIBRAiRY impacts have a completed
      `learning-content-intake-template.md` derivative record naming the
      canonical concept owner and each surface's distinct job.
- [ ] The opportunity scan created owner handoffs; it did not automatically
      turn every episode concept into a class or book.

### Continuity check

- [ ] The same heroine outfit appears in every episode frame.
- [ ] The outfit's movie/show reference already exists in the Mall, or the Mall
      update is part of this release.
- [ ] The PATRON SAiNT has a teaching job, not just a cameo.
- [ ] A feature promoted as an episode companion practises the exact lesson or
      moves the person toward practising it.
- [ ] Every promoted feature declares an honest primary job: teach, practise,
      judge, retrieve, activate, encourage, belong or delight.
- [ ] An evergreen pep-talk, community or delight feature is allowed to do its
      real job without being misrepresented as technical instruction.
- [ ] The visit has one obvious next action; optional discoveries are revealed
      progressively and every rabbit hole has a back path.
- [ ] Surprise/Easter-egg content is optional, accessible and never required to
      understand the lesson or navigate the town.
- [ ] A postcard uses the postcard art register; episode comic art is not
      silently repurposed as painterly town art.
- [ ] Every charm has a name, slug, building, coordinates, image and collection
      proof.
- [ ] Butterfly clips are the only general spendable town currency; charms,
      stickers, badges and cards remain collectibles.
- [ ] Every clip-earning action has an amount, reason, completion signal,
      one-time/repeat/cap rule and dedupe key.
- [ ] No redemption is released until the real reward exists, balance changes
      once and the item appears where the copy promised.
- [ ] Core learning and accessibility are not clip-gated.
- [ ] FAiRY Plays are explained as a service allowance rather than a competing
      general currency.
- [ ] Building stamps represent meaningful place-specific actions; passive
      visits/page refreshes cannot farm them.
- [ ] Loyalty stamps stay on the building card and are not presented as
      spendable currency.
- [ ] Any completed-card reward is granted once through the shared
      reward/clip system and delivered before the milestone celebrates it.
- [ ] Loyalty depth remains optional: the visitor is not shown seventeen
      simultaneous programs or required to complete them for the episode.
- [ ] Any connection loop starts with a real reason to think of one person; it
      does not pay for indiscriminate sharing, contact uploads or message
      volume.
- [ ] Send and join rewards have separate proofs, caps, dedupe keys and named
      recipients; opening a link alone does not create currency.
- [ ] A reciprocal reward reaches both residents once, while gifts and
      encouragement cannot be farmed into a clip-rebate loop.
- [ ] Every promised background, necklace half or other connection reward
      appears in the named Closet/MAiKEOVER destination before release.
- [ ] Meaningful production failures/surprises/fixes are logged in
      `operations/painpoints-log.md` with evidence separated from diagnosis.
- [ ] A verified build learning changed a real prompt, rule, check or
      acceptance criterion before becoming founder content.
- [ ] Raw/unverified incidents remain internal; public pieces pass fact,
      privacy, IP, voice and practical-value review.
- [ ] Public Behind-the-Build pieces teach a transferable action and do not
      expose private prompts, people or unpublished project material.

### Audio-language check

- [ ] The readable script and captions use `LAiDIES`, `SUNNYVAiLE`, `MAiVENS`
      and other public brand spellings.
- [ ] The ElevenLabs script spells for the ear (`ladies`, `Sunnyvale`,
      `Mavens`, `K-S-V-L`, etc.) without changing the meaning.
- [ ] Pronunciation substitutions are mechanical and reviewable; they are not
      hand-edited back into the public copy.
- [ ] Homographs record meaning/context and intended pronunciation, not only a
      replacement spelling.
- [ ] Every listening-session edit is classified as pronunciation, delivery,
      spoken cadence, semantic wording, fact correction, cut/new beat or
      locked-phrase change.
- [ ] Meaningful recording changes have been reconciled back into canon and
      all affected article/learning/town/search/social surfaces.
- [ ] Intentional spoken-only phrasing is recorded rather than treated as
      accidental drift.
- [ ] Captions/transcript match the final spoken words but restore public brand
      spelling and omit delivery tags.
- [ ] The as-recorded transcript was produced from the exact approved audio;
      the original draft or TTS input was not assumed to be what the tool said.
- [ ] Timing, cues, animation and the cut were rebuilt from the final approved
      audio, not an earlier render.
- [ ] Episode song, fixed Wednesday Anthem, series title theme and DJ intro are
      labelled as four different assets.
- [ ] The episode song is genuinely enjoyable/replayable and carries one to
      three accurate memory hooks from the canonical lesson.
- [ ] Canonical, performance and as-recorded lyrics remain separate; phonetic
      spellings and tool cues never appear in public copy.
- [ ] Homographs and other sensitive words record meaning, intended sound,
      tool/model/version and the approved performance result.
- [ ] Every omitted, repeated, substituted or improvised sung word is
      reconciled rather than assuming the generator followed its lyric input.
- [ ] Public lyrics and timed captions match the approved audio after standard
      spelling is restored.
- [ ] Each lyric hook preserves enough nuance to remain true and can be
      retrieved in a real AI/work situation.
- [ ] The song does not introduce an unverified teaching claim merely to make
      the rhyme work.
- [ ] The song is in the KSVL registry and was actually played from the live
      page; a file on disk is not release proof.

### Social check

- [ ] The batch considers all seven jobs: stop, wonder, learn, save/send, join,
      visit/use and return.
- [ ] The reference-to-AI mystery has a real answer in the episode and helps
      explain the concept.
- [ ] At least one object gives useful value without requiring a click.
- [ ] The reach candidate has at least two credible travel reasons:
      recognition, identity, surprise, utility, participation or performance.
- [ ] Each social object has one primary job and signal; the batch does not
      manufacture seven weak posts to fill seven jobs.
- [ ] Social derives from the lesson; it is not generic promotion for the link.
- [ ] The visual can be understood with sound off.
- [ ] Captions and alt text exist.
- [ ] The target URL and UTM resolve to the exact episode/resource.
- [ ] The exact advertised action was completed from the public social link on
      mobile, desktop and a clean/logged-out state where relevant.
- [ ] The destination has a usable success state, honest failure/empty state
      and a clear next action.
- [ ] Analytics connect the post/UTM to the promised on-site action rather than
      stopping at platform likes.
- [ ] Quote/lyric cards link back to the exact song, scene or episode and are
      approved for sharing/attribution.
- [ ] Posted/scheduled proof is recorded; a draft is not marked released.
