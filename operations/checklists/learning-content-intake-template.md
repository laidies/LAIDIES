## 5. Format-specific admission question

### If this is a class

- What must be seen in narration, animation or a real current interface?
- What does the learner do rather than merely watch?
- What controlled comparison or failure makes the mechanism visible?
- What transfer task proves the skill survives a new situation?
- Which volatile product details live in a dated/reshootable appendix?
- Why would a book or paragraph be insufficient?

### If this is a LIBRAiRY item

- What question will a reader need to look up months later?
- Is the answer durable and revisable?
- Why does it not belong in an existing book, chapter or Concepts entry?
- How does it avoid becoming a transcript of the class or episode?

explicitly declined.
