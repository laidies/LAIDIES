## Narration readiness gate

Narration remains **HOLD** until all are true:

- [ ] The class has a compelling learner need and complete promise.
- [ ] Every lesson answers one named learner question.
- [ ] Every story creates and carries the technical question rather than
      decorating an unrelated lecture.
- [ ] The learner predicts, notices or explains something before the reveal.
- [ ] Every outcome is observable rather than “understand” alone.
- [ ] Every lesson explains mechanism/why, not only procedure.
- [ ] A true beginner can name the essential components and follow the causal
      chain without unexplained prerequisite knowledge.
- [ ] Adjacent concepts and analogy limits are explicit.
- [ ] Worked examples and controlled comparisons are fully specified.
- [ ] Every lesson makes the learner act and gives diagnostic feedback.
- [ ] Transfer is tested on something not copied from the demonstration.
- [ ] The ending returns to consequence and agency rather than stopping at a
      definition or correct answer.
- [ ] Current claims have primary-source evidence and freshness handling.
- [ ] Lesson boundaries create useful stop/resume points.
- [ ] An independent instructional reviewer has returned PASS.
