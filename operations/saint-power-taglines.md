# Saint taglines — Girl Power → Machine Power

Each patron saint carries two lines:
- **Girl Power** = the genuine, uplifting life message *she* embodies — the real thing her character/person teaches us about being a woman. (NOT about AI.)
- **Machine Power** = how that same wisdom applies to using AI.

It's the town tagline — *girl power meets machine power* — made personal, one saint at a time.
Voice: warm, in-group, true to who she really is. AI is "it," never "her."

| Saint · domain | Girl Power (the real message she teaches) | Machine Power (same wisdom → AI) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cher Horowitz** · Making It Yours | Take charge of your world — and lift everyone you bring into it. | Set it up to know you; make the tool yours, not one-size-fits-all. |
| **Dolly Parton** · Common Sense | Know exactly who you are, and do it on purpose. | Trust your gut — a confident answer still has to pass your common sense. |
| **Elle Woods** · Receipts | Let them underestimate you — then do the work and prove them wrong. | Don't take it on faith — ask for its sources and check them. |
| **Miranda Priestly** · Standards | Never apologize for holding the bar high. | Don't approve the fast, fine, first draft — push it to your standard. |
| **Buffy Summers** · SLAiYING | You're braver than the thing you're scared of. Do it anyway. | Point it at the task that intimidates you — the one you're not sure you can pull off. |
| **David Rose** · Specificity | Know exactly what you want — and never apologize for asking for it. | Tell it precisely what you want. Vague in, vague out. |
| **Deb** · NOPE | "No" is a complete sentence. Protect your time and your peace. | Let it draft — never let it decide. Keep the calls that are yours. |
| **Regina George** · The Cautionary Tale | Real power lifts people up — it doesn't keep a Burn Book. | The AI *is* the Burn Book: confident, quotable, sometimes flat wrong, and it keeps whatever you feed it. Don't trust it blindly; don't hand it secrets. |
| **Samantha Jones** · Orientation | Walk into any room like you belong — because you do. | Size up the tools before you start; pick the right one for the job. |
| **Oprah Winfrey** · Staying Current | Stay curious — and lift others as you rise. | You can't read everything — follow the few who point you to what matters. |
| **Jennifer Lopez** · Range | You were never meant to be just one thing. | It's not just a text box — feed it images, voice, screenshots, files. Use all of it. |
| **Sister Mary Clarence** · Teaching | Meet people where they are, and bring out the best in them. | It meets you where you are — no expert badge required; start from what you've got and it takes you further. |

## How it wires
- Girl Power + Machine Power pair goes on the saint card, labelled.
- Pairs with the longer "who + lesson" depth in `saint-card-back-depth.md` (taglines = the hook; depth = the expanded back). Layout confirmed with Ali before wiring.
