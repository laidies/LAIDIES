## Required counterarguments and holds

- Closed systems may centralize safety operations, revocation, abuse controls and incident response; opacity can also make independent scrutiny weaker.
- More-open access can improve inspection, local control and defensive use; it can also broaden capability access and make governance harder.
- The incident cannot decide that debate by itself.
- Before publication, recover a primary source for every exact access label used, an independent security-policy source, and an independent reviewer’s counterargument ruling.

**Freshness trigger:** technical report/final impact, a change in model access or governance policy, or a new independent analysis that changes the strongest counterargument.
