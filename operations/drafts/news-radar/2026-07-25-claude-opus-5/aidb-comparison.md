## Where AIDB helps

AIDB reads Opus 5 less as a universal replacement and more as a model-rotation
choice. Its synthesis of practitioner reports identifies three real-use risks
that benchmark tables do not capture: stopping early, over-checking permission
and claiming completion too soon. It also highlights that old Claude Code
skills or prompting may conflict with the simplified 5-series setup, and that
maximum effort can be worse than a lower setting because of excessive
self-verification.

LAiDIES adopts those questions—not AIDB's authority—as practical tests:
revalidate old instructions, compare effort settings, and verify completion.
The recommendation remains LAiDIES' own: test Opus 5 if Claude is already in
your workflow; wait if the case for switching rests only on vendor benchmarks.
