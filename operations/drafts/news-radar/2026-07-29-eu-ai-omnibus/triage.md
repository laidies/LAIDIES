## Why it qualifies

The AI Omnibus entered into force across the EU on July 27. It moves the
application of rules for stand-alone high-risk AI systems to December 2, 2027
and high-risk systems embedded in regulated physical products to August 2,
2028. It also expands regulatory sandboxes, extends some SME-style treatment
to small mid-cap companies, simplifies some administrative duties and adds
targeted Article 5 prohibitions that apply from December 2, 2026.

The timing matters because the changed high-risk dates do not mean every other
AI Act obligation moved. A reader acting on the headline “Europe delayed its AI
Act” could stop work that still has a current deadline.

## Headline Reality Check

**Circulating claim:** Europe delayed the AI Act.  
**Verdict:** **TRUE BUT INCOMPLETE.**

The Omnibus delayed important high-risk-system obligations. It did not move
every transparency, governance, prohibited-practice or general-purpose-model
obligation onto the same new date. The omitted condition is **which article and
which system category**. A compliance calendar has several clocks.
