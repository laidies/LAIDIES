## What the evidence shows

The operative AI Act calendar changed. The largest shift is for two categories
of high-risk systems, while other obligations retain different dates and
conditions. Smaller and mid-cap companies gain additional proportionality and
testing support, and the amendment adds or clarifies protections and
governance.

## What it does not show

- It does not show that the whole AI Act was postponed.
- It does not determine whether a particular product is high-risk.
- It does not provide legal advice for one company, country or use case.
- It does not establish that all organizations have more time for every duty.
- The Commission’s summary is not a substitute for the controlling text and
  national enforcement guidance.
