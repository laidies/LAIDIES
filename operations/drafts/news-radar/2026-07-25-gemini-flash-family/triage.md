## Why now

Google released Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash-Lite on July 21, but the morning
radar missed them. Both are GA and available now. The release changes more than
a leaderboard: 3.6 Flash cuts the predecessor's output-token price, becomes the
default for Google's Antigravity agent and introduces migration requirements;
Flash-Lite offers a different low-cost lane for high-volume work.
