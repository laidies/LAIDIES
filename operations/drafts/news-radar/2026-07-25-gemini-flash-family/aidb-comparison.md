# Gemini Flash family — AIDB comparison

**Status:** PRIVATE  
**Complete edition checked:** July 22, 2026, “Wait... Just How Good is GPT-6?”

## Agreement

Both reads identify the real 3.6 Flash change as token efficiency and a lower
output-token price, not a new frontier-capability crown. Both note mixed
reception and the missing/delayed 3.5 Pro context.

## Useful AIDB contribution

AIDB places the release in Google's wider model cadence and records skepticism
that some benchmark results trail the predecessor. That is useful pressure
against repeating Google's launch adjectives.

## LAiDIES difference

AIDB's “models nobody wanted” framing overweights disappointment about the
absent Pro model. LAiDIES treats a cheaper GA workhorse as a real choice change
for people paying for repeated agentic work. The important qualification comes
from independent task evidence: lower price and strong video/data results do
not erase object-detection and structured-output regressions.

LAiDIES also separates Flash Cyber from the two public releases. It is a
restricted future pilot, not an option ordinary readers can choose today.

No AIDB wording or argument should be publicly attributed unless the final
piece actually relies on it.
