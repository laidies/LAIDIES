# THE BREAKING

## ChatGPT can now read some health records. Check the four doors before you connect one.

OpenAI has begun rolling out Health in ChatGPT to logged-in U.S. adults on web
and iOS across Free, Go, Plus and Pro. The optional feature can connect Apple
Health and supported medical records so ChatGPT can use that information in
relevant conversations.

That is a meaningful product change, but not because ChatGPT has become a
doctor. It has gained access to a better folder of context. More context may
make an answer more relevant; it does not make the answer medically correct.

Before connecting anything, check four different doors:

1. **Connection:** what source can ChatGPT read?
2. **Permission:** does it ask each time, or have you selected always allow?
3. **Memory:** what can ChatGPT remember from the conversation?
4. **History:** what remains in the chat after you disconnect the source?

OpenAI says Health data and conversations that use it are not used to train its
foundation models or target ads. It says disconnecting starts deletion of
synced data within 30 days. But information already placed in conversation
history stays there until you delete those chats. OpenAI also warns that the
product can make mistakes and does not replace qualified care.

The privacy label needs care too. A consumer health app is not automatically
governed by HIPAA in the same way as a covered doctor, hospital or insurer.
Other consumer-health and breach rules may apply, but “health data” is not
itself a promise of one legal protection.

### What changes for you

The useful lane is preparation: organize a timeline, translate unfamiliar
language, compare notes with the original record, and draft questions for an
appointment. For diagnosis, urgent symptoms, medication changes or treatment,
use the original record and a qualified professional.

### What we are watching

Independent clinical testing, wider availability, privacy audits, changes to
memory and deletion controls, and any regulator guidance specific to this
rollout.

**Status:** PRIVATE — HOLD for independent health/privacy review.
