# Impacts and how to think

## The useful mental model

Think of this less like hiring a doctor and more like bringing a sealed folder
to a coffee-shop counter. The barista may make a more tailored order because
you supplied the folder, but four different questions still matter: who may
open it, whether they may open it once or every visit, what gets copied into
their notes, and what remains after you take the folder back.

In ChatGPT, those correspond to the connected source, permission setting,
memory and conversation history. “Disconnect” is not the same instruction as
“delete every conversation that used this information.”

## Who should do what

- **Test:** people with a specific low-stakes preparation task, after checking
  the four controls and using the original record as the authority.
- **Wait:** anyone unsure about retention, legal protection, family/shared
  accounts, or the consequences of an incorrect summary.
- **Do not delegate:** diagnosis, urgent-symptom triage, medication changes or
  treatment decisions.
- **Ignore for now:** readers without a concrete benefit worth the additional
  privacy exposure.

The real change is better context access. The change is not medical authority.
