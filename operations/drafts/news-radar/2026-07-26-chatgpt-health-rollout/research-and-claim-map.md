## Claim map

| Claim | Evidence | Publication language |
|---|---|---|
| The feature is rolling out to logged-in U.S. adults on web and iOS across Free, Go, Plus and Pro. | OpenAI launch/help. | Attribute availability to OpenAI and say “rolling out,” not universally available. |
| Users may connect Apple Health and supported medical records. | OpenAI launch/help. | Say optional connection, not automatic access. |
| ChatGPT asks before using connected data by default; users can choose always allow. | OpenAI launch/help. | Treat the permission default and the setting as separate facts. |
| OpenAI says Health conversations and connected data are not used for foundation-model training or targeted advertising. | OpenAI launch/privacy policy. | Attribute to OpenAI; do not call it independently audited. |
| Disconnecting starts deletion of synced data within 30 days, while health information in conversation history remains until those chats are deleted. | OpenAI launch/help. | Keep both halves together. |
| Health conversations may create memories, though OpenAI says it does not create memories directly from connected records. | OpenAI launch/help. | Explain memory, connected source and chat history as different storage/control layers. |
| Consumer health-app data does not automatically receive the HIPAA protection attached to covered providers and business associates. | HHS; FTC; OpenAI help. | Do not make a legal determination about every datum or every company relationship. |
| The product can still make mistakes and is not a substitute for medical care. | OpenAI; AP expert context. | Preserve the limitation near any suggested use. |

## What the evidence shows

- ChatGPT can use unusually sensitive, user-authorized health context in answers.
- Permission, memory, connected-source retention and chat history are separate
  controls.
- More context may make an answer more relevant.

## What it does not show

- that the feature is clinically validated;
- that more personal context makes an answer medically correct;
- that HIPAA governs every consumer interaction with the feature;
- that every plan, device or country has access now;
- that ChatGPT should make diagnosis, medication or treatment decisions.
