# Meta AI can read the calendar and keep the assignment. Check the permission slip.

Meta has started rolling out a version of Meta AI that can connect to email and
calendar apps, prepare recurring briefings, monitor changes, research topics
and create slides. It is powered by Muse Spark 1.1 and is initially available
in select markets in the Meta AI app and on meta.ai.

The important change is not that a chatbot can make a presentation. It is that
the assistant can keep an instruction, consult changing personal context and
return later with work. Instead of asking a question at the counter, you are
leaving a standing coffee order—and giving the shop access to your schedule so
it knows when to prepare it.

That can be genuinely useful. A daily briefing could spot a double booking or
save repeated setup. It also changes the trust decision: the assistant needs
more context, for longer, to become more relevant.

The Rewind Era version is giving a personal assistant your Filofax and a copy
of the inbox key. The system can make a better plan because it can see the
appointments and messages you would otherwise have to explain. The analogy has
a limit: a human assistant and a connected AI service do not have the same
memory, data flows or accountability, so “it can see this” is not a complete
answer to “where does this information go?”

Before connecting anything, readers should check:

- exactly which mailbox and calendar permissions are requested;
- whether access can be narrowed;
- which actions run automatically and which require confirmation;
- where recurring tasks can be reviewed and stopped; and
- what happens to connected information after access is revoked.

Meta's announcement does not yet give a complete answer to those questions,
nor does it specify every launch market, limit or separate price. That is why
this is a feature to test deliberately, not a reason to connect a primary
inbox on day one.

What to watch next: the actual permission screen, retention/deletion
documentation, hands-on reliability and the promised expansion to WhatsApp.
