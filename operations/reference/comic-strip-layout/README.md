# Comic strip layout references

These images are **layout and dialogue-treatment references only**. They do not define character identity, facial features, costumes, setting, or palette.

## `comic-strip-layout-01-varied-panels-dialogue-sfx.png`

Use for:

- varied panel heights and widths rather than equal boxes
- cinematic establishing, medium, and close detail crops within one strip
- speech balloons placed close to the speaker with clear reading order
- occasional overlapping balloons without covering faces or essential action
- compact narration/caption boxes
- a full-width impact or sound-effect panel used as a rhythmic beat
- bold gutters and clear separation between moments

Do **not** copy its characters, superhero costumes, muted colours, halftone treatment, or franchise-specific visual language.

For LAiDIES / SUNNYVAiLE, combine the layout principles with the locked house treatment:

- the master people style in `operations/episode-visual-system-lock.md`
- elegant variable-width black ink
- faceted, hard-edged, multi-level light and shadow planes
- dimensional colour with restrained printed texture
- detailed dimensional faces and environments
- vibrant 1990s teal, pink, purple, yellow, cyan, and related approved accents
- crisp designed lettering and balloons from the comic text/emphasis reference library
- no horizontal banding or repeated streaks in hair
- no generic flat-vector faces and no soft painterly rendering

## `comic-strip-layout-02-pop-cover-panel-template.png`

Use for:

- an irregular comic-cover grid with one or two dominant panels
- angled gutters and varied panel geometry
- integrated display words, bursts, lips, hearts, and small supporting marks
- using a limited colour family across several panels to hold the page together
- giving the LAiDIES title a deliberate panel or dominant cover position

Do **not** copy its sample words, colour recipe, mouth illustration, or stock
composition literally. It is a cover-layout and hierarchy reference. LAiDIES
characters, environments, lettering, and identity rules remain authoritative.
