# Comic Ident Background Reference Library

**Status:** REFERENCE ONLY  
**Date added:** 2026-07-25  
**Source:** Images supplied by Ali during development of the animated LAiDIES
brand/episode ident.

## Purpose

Use this set to ground the background language for short LAiDIES idents,
episode welcome beats, chapter cards and related high-energy graphic moments.
The intended synthesis is:

- clearly visible pop-comic construction rather than faint corporate texture;
- radial action lines, halftone fields, cropped bursts and graphic ink;
- purple/indigo, blue, hot pink, cyan and small yellow accents;
- exact AI-specific words rendered as designed foreground/background elements;
  and
- a deliberately quiet central window that protects the legibility of the
  `LAiDIES` wordmark and its changing lowercase `i`.

## Usage guardrails

- These are style/composition references, not cleared production assets.
- Several source images contain stock-library watermarks or unknown licensing.
  Never publish, ship, trace or directly reuse them.
- Rebuild the useful principles in original LAiDIES-owned artwork.
- Do not copy the sample words. Replace them with exact episode or AI
  terminology.
- Keep the densest rays, dots, bursts and words at the perimeter. The central
  wordmark must remain the dominant, instantly readable element.
- Avoid treating every reference as one mandatory layer. Select the smallest
  combination needed for the beat.
