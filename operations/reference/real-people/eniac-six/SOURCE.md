# ENIAC Six reference sources

These references are identity evidence, not permission to invent a composite group portrait.

- The ENIAC Programmers Project, memorials and team image: https://eniacprogrammers.org/eniac-programmers-project/memorials/
- 1946 ENIAC team image: https://eniacprogrammers.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/ENIAC-team.png
- ENIAC Programmers Project, author/history page: https://eniacprogrammers.org/about-the-author/
- 50th-anniversary image including Betty Holberton: https://eniacprogrammers.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/eniac-jean-bartik-marlyn-meltzer-kay-antonelli-betty-holberton.png

The 1946 team image shows Frances Bilas Spence, Marlyn Wescoff Meltzer, Ruth Lichterman Teitelbaum, Jean Jennings Bartik and Kathleen McNulty Mauchly Antonelli. Betty Snyder Holberton is bound through the separate 50th-anniversary image.
