# Facts & Citations Ledger — LAiDIES

**THE RULE:** No statistic, named study, expert quote, or factual claim ships in any LAiDIES content
(episode, article, quiz, tool) until it is (a) verified against a primary/reputable source via web
search, and (b) logged here with the source URL, the date verified, and — critically — **WHAT IT
MEASURES** (scope). Never state a stat or quote from memory. If it can't be verified, it doesn't ship
("not verified beats plausible"). This ledger exists because Ep1 mis-stated the BCG "14 points"
(an *adoption* number) as *outperformance*.

### ✅ Verified

- **"Senior women in tech lead male peers in GenAI *adoption* by ~14 percentage points."**
  · Scope: **ADOPTION** (share who use it), senior women in *technical* functions only; junior women
  *lag* men by 21 pts. NOT performance / "better at AI." Drivers = awareness + confidence + risk
  tolerance. · Source: BCG, *Women Leaders Are Paving the Way in GenAI* (May 2024), 6,500+ tech
  employees, 5 countries — https://www.bcg.com/publications/2024/women-leaders-in-tech-are-paving-the-way-in-genai
  · Verified 2026-07-07 · Used in: **Ep1** (fixed), referenced in Ep2 research.

  were the good briefers."** · Scope: 758 BCG consultants, GPT-4, tasks *inside* the "jagged frontier";
  quality/speed gains, biggest for lower performers. · Source: Dell'Acqua, McFowland, **Mollick**, et
  al., *Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier* (2023) — https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4573321

- **"'Prompt' is an old word the tech world borrowed — the computer nerds lifted it from the theater kids."**
  · Scope: the verb *prompt* dates to the **mid-1300s** ("incite to action," from Latin *promere*, "bring
  forth"); the **theatrical prompter** (feeding an actor a forgotten line) is **1590s–1600s**. Computing's
  "command prompt" + AI's "prompt" adopted the existing word — NOT a tech coinage. **Caveat (the Ep2 joke
  compresses this):** computing didn't borrow *specifically from theater* — both descend from the older
  general verb "to prompt" (to cue). Theater = the vivid pre-tech example. Joke spirit is accurate; "lifted
  from the theater kids" is approved comedic framing. · Source: https://www.etymonline.com/word/prompt ;
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prompter_(theatre) · Verified 2026-07-07 · Used in: **Ep2**.
