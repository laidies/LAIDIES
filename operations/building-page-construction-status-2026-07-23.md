## Boundaries

- SUNNYVAiLE High's class curriculum remains owned by
  `content/site/high-classes.json`. The page construction reads that source and
  does not fork or rewrite class content.
- Do not touch the Dream Phone game while its redesign is active.
- The Gift Shop remains outside the locked building canon until its permanent
  address, keeper, and final name are decided. Its construction does not claim
  any of those decisions.
- Final replacement art does not block page construction, but rejected art must
  never be used as a bridge.
- Every page must be checked full-page at desktop and approximately 390px
  mobile before it is called complete.
- The Book Fair's eight intended product images do not exist yet. The page
  displays honest merchandise labels and makes no broken image requests;
  future proofs can replace the labels without changing the rack mechanic.
- The Pop Quiz keeps the current classroom source as a structural bridge. Its
  one-question paper construction must survive a later locked-style room
  replacement without changing quiz data, scoring, or storage.
- The class page keeps the current classroom source and its precise television
  hotspot geometry. A later locked-style replacement must preserve the room
  crop, AV-cart target, chalkboard writing zone, and authoritative class
  register.
