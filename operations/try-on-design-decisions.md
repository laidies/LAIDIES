# Try-On fitting-room construction — 2026-07-24

## Decision

The Try-On is an activity inside MAiKEOVER, not a worksheet made of stacked
cards. The existing four-issue engine stays intact, but its content is rehoused
as a fitting-room arrival, an open numbered exercise rail, an issue-specific
prompt workbench, and one full-width fit-check counter.

## Construction grammar

- The current dimensional-comic MAiKEOVER vanity candidate establishes place.
- A single pinned five-minute note is allowed as a physical object; it is not
  used as a template for the rest of the page.
- The assignment is one open section with a four- or five-step cyan rail, not
  four step cards.
- Episode 03's Prompt Like Elle content is one dark workbench. Its mini example,
  builder, receipt guide, and full prompt are open sections divided by rules,
  not nested white cards.
- Saving happens on one near-black-blue fit-check counter with a real textarea,
  butterfly-clip rating, local save, and community handoff.

## Preserved behaviour

- `?issue=1` through `?issue=4` still load their existing episode-specific
  title, instructions, steps, prompt, receipt fields, and community route.
- Prompt Like Elle sample insertion and copy controls remain.
- The butterfly-clip rating still updates live.
- Save still writes the original `laidiesWednesdayTryOnNotes` record and
  marks the original Wednesday ritual completion key.
- Existing Study Pack, community, global header, analytics, KSVL, directory,
  and You Are Here wiring remains.

## QA result

All four issue states pass at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 with no page-level
horizontal overflow and no broken images. Issue 03's additional prompt and
receipt fields are visible and usable on both sizes. Sample insertion, rating,
save, reload persistence, and cleared form-state behaviour were exercised.

## Art boundary

The current vanity room is an installed structural candidate already used by
MAiKEOVER. It is more dimensional and inked than the old page, but it remains
lighter and peachier than the approved Episode 04 Heroine style lock. It is not
an approved generation reference.
