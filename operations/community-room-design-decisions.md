# Sorority House community-room construction — 2026-07-24

## Decision

The Sorority House subpages are rooms for a specific kind of conversation, not
a shared hero followed by differently labelled cards.

The current house interior is reused as a structural bridge so the pages can be
judged and used while final locked-style room art is still open. It is not an
approved visual reference.

## Construction grammar

- A full-width house arrival establishes place before content.
- Comic display type names the room; the title scale yields when the prompt is
  a sentence rather than a room name.
- Hyvor remains the real conversation engine, housed on one ruled conversation
  table rather than framed as a generic white card.
- The Comment Card is one writing desk with a dark editorial margin.
- The Chat Room Digest is one receipts desk: an open four-step process followed
  by a ruled live digest register.
- The Mix CD Exchange moves its two submission formats onto one liner-note
  spread below the arrival. It does not lengthen the hero or repeat prompt
  cards.
- Back links read as underlined room exits, not repeated pill buttons.

## Preserved behaviour

- Seven original Hyvor page IDs remain unchanged.
- The Comment Card retains its required episode and quick-read fields, reset
  action, status copy, and existing email-draft handoff.
- The Digest retains its live loader, room status, five live rows, five
  fallback/reference rows, and original source links.
- The Mix CD Exchange retains the existing Spotify/DJ SunnyV prompt formats.
- Existing global navigation, analytics, KSVL player, directory, Resident Card,
  and You Are Here scripts remain present.

## Responsive result

All nine pages pass at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 with no page-level horizontal
overflow and no broken images. Mobile keeps the same place and hierarchy,
stacks the Comment Card desk, turns the Digest process into a single readable
sequence, and restacks the liner-note spread.

## Art boundary

The current painterly Sorority House interior is a structural bridge only. It
does not replace the pending bright, dimensional graphic-novel house artwork
and must not become a future generation reference. The rejected pink-tracksuit
June image is absent.
