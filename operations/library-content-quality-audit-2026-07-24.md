## Cross-book defects already verified

1. **The taxonomy is blurred.** Vocab, Concepts, Briefing, Accounts and the
   Verification Rulebook repeat several of the same terms at different depths
   without a clear ownership rule.
2. **Extraction was mistaken for editorial development.** Recovered source
   material was rendered before its duplicates, stale links and conflicting
   jobs were resolved.
3. **Placeholders can look live.** `How to Check AI's Work` has a clickable
   cover and an embedded body even though no complete book is wired.
4. **Technical QA overclaimed readiness.** Link, DOM and responsive checks did
   not test whether a reader understood or benefited from the content.
5. **Current claims lack one shared currency gate.** Product abilities, account
   protections, prices, model behaviour and statistics require different
   recheck triggers.

## Corrective order

1. Finish and score **Vocab 101** as the representative quick-reference book.
2. Reverse-brief **Concepts 101** so its job is visibly different.
3. Reconcile and score **Briefing 101** against Episode 2.
4. Remove or clearly block the **How to Check** placeholder-book experience.
5. Reverify high-risk **Accounts 101** before any copy polish.
6. Reverify **Straight Answers** claim by claim.
7. Rebuild **Who’s Who / Tools** only after Episode 5 canon is approved.

The pattern may scale only after the first representative book passes in the
actual reader and Ali agrees that the result meets the LAiDIES bar.
