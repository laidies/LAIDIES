## Placement and integrity

- Both illustrations appear on the private homepage cards, the NewsStand
  publication covers, the front-story listings and the full article pages.
- The webpage supplies all real headlines and publication names. Generated
  pixels are never used as factual evidence.
- The explicit “LAiDIES illustration” caption prevents the scene from being
  mistaken for documentary photography or a source image.
- Production files and public story data remain unchanged.

### The Breaking ticker

- The Breaking is deliberately not a fourth static paper in the hero. A current
  issue appears as a live magenta-and-navy counter ticker carrying the real HTML
  headline and opening the same Breaking desk as the compact selector.
- This separates an interrupt from the slower Daily, Weekly and Tribune papers
  while giving all four publications a visible physical role before scrolling.
