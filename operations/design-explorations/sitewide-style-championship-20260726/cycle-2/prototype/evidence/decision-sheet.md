## Brand & Experience Director recommendation

**Recommend A. Ali has not approved it.**

The strongest evidence is not that A is the loudest image. It is that the blind
judge found the governing rule fastest, scored it highest for coherence
(`19/20`) and maintainability (`16/20`), and the same rule remains legible in
the room, host, operated objects, calm reader and episode handoff. It resolves

The material trade-off Ali is choosing is:

> accept the largest initial site-art replacement and a strict calmness/
> architectural-variety discipline in exchange for the clearest long-term
> cross-format identity and the least ambiguous recurring governance.

medium bridge must be deliberately restated. C has the strongest functional
idea, but its masks, hit-area boundaries, integration lighting and responsive
alignment create the highest ongoing review burden; one weak placement would
look like mixed-source art.
