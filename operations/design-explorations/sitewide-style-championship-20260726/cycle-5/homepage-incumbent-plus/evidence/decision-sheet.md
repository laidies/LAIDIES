## Why it beats the incumbent

The incumbent remains the strongest source of LAiDIES atmosphere, but its
pastel panel field, repeated entry modules, repeated destination images and
card-wall status treatment dilute that world. Cycle 5 retains the exact
luminous town while making the rest of the page feel like the same mature
nighttime place: stronger hierarchy, fewer repeated decisions, calmer
long-form reading, clearer section transitions, and one repeatable component
grammar across desktop and mobile.

Trade-off: the dark editorial system is denser and more cinematic than the
incumbent's lighter pastel rhythm. The contained activity and route cards
therefore retain light reading surfaces for legibility and contrast.
