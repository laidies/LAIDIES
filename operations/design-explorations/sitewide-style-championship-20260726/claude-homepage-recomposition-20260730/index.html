The LAiDIES method

How LAiDIES works—and why it matters

A) How it works · Learn it. Live it. Make it yours.

1 · Follow the plot

SUNNYVAiLE chapters

A continuing story introduces one useful AI idea in context—24 episodes across Season One.

2 · Unlock the idea

Rewind-Era analogy

A 1990s pop-culture analogy you already understand unlocks the idea and makes it stick.

3 · Make it click

Try-On Lab

Try it hands-on in a safe, playful Try-On—then a printable, Pop Quiz and teaching cards.

4 · Make it stick

KSVL soundtrack

Original KSVL songs, mnemonics and hooks help the lesson stick. Listen →

5 · Save it

Progress & collections

Track your progress, earn keepsakes and save favourites in your Closet.

6 · Keep growing

Keep going

Keep building skills as new episodes arrive. New ideas, new you.

B) Why it matters · Our past. Our present. Our voice in what’s next.

Women helped build computing from the beginning

Programmers wrote the first code. Engineers built the first systems. Visionaries imagined the future—and their work changed the world.

Women are shaping AI now

Designing safer systems, leading startups, using AI to solve real problems and advocating for fairness, privacy and people.

Women need knowledge, confidence and a voice

To understand how AI works, create what the world needs, speak up for what’s right—and help decide what comes next.

“Computing is too important to be left to men.”— Karen Spärck Jones · Keeper of the Index
