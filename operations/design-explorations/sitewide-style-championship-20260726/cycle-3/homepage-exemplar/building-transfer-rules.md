## Rules every option shares

### Architecture and rooms

Each building needs a credible, colourful, place-specific environment. The
room and its physical objects implement the primary actions. Header bars,
floating cards and page modules cannot become the dominant experience.

### Typography and UI

Use mature high-weight sans for evergreen navigation and masthead hierarchy;
use a controlled editorial serif only for publication or reading emphasis.
All labels, statuses, focus and results are deterministic HTML. Colour never
carries status alone.

### Colour

Anchor in deep plum/navy with calibrated teal, pink, coral, violet and gold.
Saturation is selective. Calm reading/print surfaces may use light paper
locally; the site is not rebuilt as a white or cream page.

### Illustration and characters

Preserve canon identity and mature anatomy. Cinematic composition and selective
ink/halftone are allowed. Glamour-cartoon poses, sticker bursts, novelty facial
stylization and dirty/grunge filters are not.

### Motion

Motion serves threshold, orientation or state. The candidate ident
`continuous-i-alternate-rewind-ai-v6.mp4` (`713c576b…`) is Ali-selected for
assessment only. It requires an exact-use and reduced-motion ruling before use.

### Study Pack family

Every episode family separates:

- café menu/receipt — screen transaction/status;
- Study Sheet — writable print learning;
- Try-On — guided interactive practice;
- Cheat Sheet — compact printable reference;
- concept/character cards — collectible recall objects; and
- quiz handoff — assessment route.

They share episode provenance, palette calibration and type discipline, but
must not become identical cards. The Cycle 3 Episode 04 specimen is bounded
decision evidence, not an asset-estate production order.

### Accessibility and failure

Desktop, 390px and 320px remain authored compositions. Focus targets attach to
visible environment objects. Failure states remain inside the building world;
reduced motion never removes meaning. Long reading/print surfaces may become
calm documents without redefining the building as a document shell.
