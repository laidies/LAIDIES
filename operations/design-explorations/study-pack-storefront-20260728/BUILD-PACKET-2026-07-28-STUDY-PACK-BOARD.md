# Study Pack Board — build packet

Date: 2026-07-28
Surface: Blend & Snap Study Pack storefront prototype

## Trigger

Ali wants the episode picker to feel like a board you would order from without
turning Study Packs into a confusing coffee-order workflow.

## Product decision

Use the visual grammar of a colourful Blend & Snap order board while keeping
every episode as a direct selection.

- The newest released episode is the prominent `LATEST` / `THIS WEEK` choice.
- Earlier released episodes remain visible together as smaller choices in
  ascending episode order.
- Adding a new episode to the data makes it Latest automatically and moves the
  former Latest into the earlier-pack grid.
- No prices, coffee selections, receipt step, hidden menu or mystery labels.
- No decorative stickers are pre-placed. Future sticker placement must use the
  signed-in learner’s actual Closet inventory and save with that account.
- Every episode tile uses the exact canonical episode title image.
- Every episode tile uses a vertical card anatomy: full-width title artwork on
  top, followed by its label, title and direct Open action underneath.

## Acceptance

- A new visitor can identify the current pack immediately.
- A new visitor can tell that all released episode tiles are selectable.
- The latest tile is larger than the others without dominating the page.
- The board remains clear with more than four episodes.
- The presentation feels like Blend & Snap and uses the LAiDIES pop palette.
- The existing pack-detail and asset routes continue to work.
