How LAiDIES and SUNNYVAiLE work

The LAiDIES Method

Your brain kept the references. We put them to work.

The plot explains it. The analogy unlocks it. Practice makes it click. The soundtrack makes it stick.

Your brain has been saving Clueless, Carrie Bradshaw, “Wannabe,” dial-up tones, butterfly clips and roll-on glitter for years. At LAiDIES, that is not useless trivia. It is a head start.

Every lesson unfolds in SUNNYVAiLE—a fictional town tuned to the Rewind Era (1990–2010), where sequential stories, accurate pop-culture analogies, practical activities and original songs work together to make AI easier to understand, use and remember.

    Resident Card and Closet

    Your town ID. Your saved finds. Your place to continue.

    Your Resident Card is your customizable SUNNYVAiLE town ID. Make it at MAiKEOVER on MAiN in about two minutes. The Card and supported Closet items stay in this browser; a private sign-in-link request does not yet make them portable.

    Learn everything a Resident Card gets you
    • A customizable town ID with the supported Card details you choose.
    • Building visit stamps added automatically as you move around town.
    • Charms found in images and kept with your supported browser-only collection.
    • Puffies and stickers from supported Handbook, quiz and activity moments.
    • Your selected favourites such as an episode, KSVL song and BRONZE AiGE choice.
    • A Closet dashboard for the Card, supported weekly items, luminaries and keepsakes this browser can prove.

    Anything not yet supported stays clearly labelled as future—not quietly promised as part of the Card.
