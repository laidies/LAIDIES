The LAiDIES method

Your brain kept the references. We put them to work.

The plot explains it. The analogy unlocks it. Practice makes it click. The soundtrack makes it stick.

Your brain has been saving Clueless, Carrie Bradshaw, “Wannabe,” dial-up tones, butterfly clips and roll-on glitter for years. At LAiDIES, that is not useless trivia. It is a head start.

Every lesson unfolds in SUNNYVAiLE—a fictional town tuned to the Rewind Era (1990–2010), where sequential stories, accurate pop-culture analogies, practical activities and original songs work together to make AI easier to understand, use and remember.

01 · Story

Follow the plot

Start with the Visitor’s Centre, then read or hear the episodes in order as our main character learns AI and discovers SUNNYVAiLE alongside you.

Season One teaches the AI basics in 24 episodes, because we remember the glory days when a season had time for a proper plot, a questionable haircut and at least one inexplicable musical episode. Future seasons will continue the story—and take your AI fluency further.

02 · Analogy

Unlock the idea

Each episode pairs an unfamiliar AI concept with a film, television moment, character, lyric or gloriously specific Y2K reference your brain already understands.

Because sometimes “context window” makes more sense when explained through The Devil Wears Prada—and butterfly clips, roll-on glitter and a suspiciously translucent landline have waited long enough to contribute to your professional development.

03 · Practice

Make it click

Open the practice pieces that are actually ready for that episode. Blend & Snap keeps each Try-On, one-page guide and teaching-card pack unavailable until its exact component passes review; the Pop Quiz remains a separate stop.

When a piece is available, it should leave you with something you can actually use—not just another tab you opened with excellent intentions and quietly abandoned three Tuesdays ago.
