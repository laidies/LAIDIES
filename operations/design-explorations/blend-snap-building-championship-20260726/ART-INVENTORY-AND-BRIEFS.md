## Universal art contract

Every environment or object must look as though it belongs inside the locked
LAiDIES episode world: adult dimensional comic / graphic-novel rendering,
confident ink, faceted light and shadow, spatial depth, and restrained printed
texture. It must be bright daytime SUNNYVAiLE with specific late-1990s/Y2K café
details. It must not be painterly, flat vector, juvenile cartoon, stained-glass,
anime, photorealistic, glossy corporate hospitality advertising, or a generic
SaaS illustration.

Lead colour family: raspberry, plum and coral. Supporting environment colours:
periwinkle and cobalt. Mint/teal are small utility accents, not the building's
default identity. Dark ink is near-black blue, never absolute black. Preserve
quiet cream-paper areas for live HTML copy and controls.

No generated image may contain changing episode numbers, component status,
availability, dates, routes, rewards, account state, “cards included,” or
“every Wednesday.” Dynamic truth stays in HTML. Do not generate a known
resident unless her face and character identity can be judged against an
approved reference. The present candidate may use JoJo only as a provisional
continuity study; it cannot approve her identity.
