# Blend & Snap building championship directions

**Status:** PROVISIONAL CHAMPION DESIGN WORK — independent blind scoring pending

## Recovered product truth

The café coordinates one released episode's pack; it does not teach the whole
lesson, claim completion, issue rewards or imply cross-device memory. The
locked verb is **ORDER**. JoJo may remember a drink and that this browser opened
a pack menu. Episode explains; Study Sheet reviews; Try-On practises; Cheat
Sheet references; Cards reinforce memory/collection when admitted; High's Quiz
checks understanding and owns any reward.

## Direction A — incumbent: Counter + ledger + corkboard

Keep the large JoJo counter scene, hero copy below, drink row, current-pack
ledger, receipt, past-pack disclosure and large corkboard.

**Strength:** already implements and passes the state/data contract.  
**Failure:** the room is mostly a banner. The first operable action is below a
941px image; mobile clips both menu context and headline; the pack becomes a
styled ledger/card rather than an order at a counter. Existing images contain
or visually imply static operational text. It looks closer to polished
generated café advertising than the locked episode world.

## Direction B — challenger: JoJo's pickup rail

The counter itself becomes the transaction:

1. a small live menu at the top names the verified current special;
2. choosing a usual updates a physical cup tag, explicitly “on this device”;
3. ORDER advances a ticket from JoJo's receipt rail in place;
4. the ticket lists available/held/planned components and one next action;
5. prior episode receipts sit on a back rail;
6. the community noticeboard is a secondary place to linger after pickup.

This retains the café metaphor but gives it a unique motor action. Mobile edits
to a close counter/pickup composition rather than cropping the whole room.
Dynamic copy is HTML over quiet paper/counter zones, never generated text.

**Risk:** overlay alignment could become decorative hotspot hunting. Mitigate
with a conventional text equivalent immediately adjacent, entire 48px minimum
buttons, and DOM order matching visual order.

## Direction C — challenger: The regulars' table

JoJo places the current pack as a café placemat at a shared booth. The placemat
reveals the component sequence; cup coasters select the usual; past packs live
in a tabletop receipt holder; a side-wall board links around town.

**Strength:** stronger “third place” and learning-sequence atmosphere.  
**Failure:** the user may read “table” as study/productivity, and the locked
ORDER verb becomes ceremony rather than the mechanic. The placemat risks
becoming a generic dashboard with café decoration.

## Champion recommendation

Advance Direction B as the isolated owner-review candidate. It transfers the
LIBRAiRY principle (operate the building's object) without cloning a shelf
room: the café-native mechanic is order ticket → pickup rail → receipt. Direction
C should remain a later experiment only if comprehension evidence shows that
visitors want a linger/review mode after ordering.

No direction changes the existing manifest or downstream authority.
