How SUNNYVAiLE works

Your brain kept the references. We put them to work.

Your brain has been saving Clueless, Carrie Bradshaw, “Wannabe,” dial-up tones, butterfly clips and roll-on glitter for years. At LAiDIES, that is not useless trivia. It is a head start.

Every lesson unfolds in SUNNYVAiLE—a fictional town tuned to the Rewind Era (1990–2010), where sequential stories, accurate pop-culture analogies, practical activities and original songs work together to make AI easier to understand, use and remember.

  1. Follow the plotStart with the trailer, then read or hear the episodes in order as our main character learns AI and discovers SUNNYVAiLE alongside you. Season One teaches the AI basics in 24 episodes, because we remember the glory days when a season had time for a proper plot, a questionable haircut and at least one inexplicable musical episode. Future seasons continue the story—and take your AI fluency further.
  2. Unlock the ideaEach episode pairs an unfamiliar AI concept with a film, television moment, character, lyric or gloriously specific Y2K reference your brain already understands. Because sometimes “context window” makes more sense when explained through The Devil Wears Prada—and butterfly clips, roll-on glitter and a suspiciously translucent landline have waited long enough to contribute to your professional development.
  3. Make it clickOpen the practice pieces that are actually ready for that episode. Blend & Snap keeps each Try-On, one-page guide and teaching-card pack unavailable until its exact component passes review; the Pop Quiz remains a separate stop. When a piece is available, it should leave you with something you can actually use—not just another tab you opened with excellent intentions and quietly abandoned three Tuesdays ago.
  4. Make it stickKSVL turns central lessons into memorable LAiDIES original songs. Twenty-nine tracks are ready to hear. Pick a song, start listening and let the hook do the revision.
  5. Join the townYou can explore SUNNYVAiLE without signing up. Becoming a resident gives you a Card. Any saved-state features appear only when the page can prove them; browser-only finds stay in that browser. Delta LAi Nu remains open to browse without a Card. Becoming a resident is free: no pledging, no rush week and no matching pink velour Juicy tracksuit required—although the outfit is highly encouraged… especially on Wednesdays.
