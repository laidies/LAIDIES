## THE SHOT LIST (episode order — from `episode-01.canon.md`; ~11 of the 16 beats, the visual ones)
1. **Steve gets the standing ovation. [PRO]** 4:52 Tuesday — a man ("Steve") getting a standing ovation for a clean AI analysis everyone knows took an hour; the Heroine watching, her own footnoted version still in her drafts. Warm office, cool envy.
2. **The terrible on-ramp. [PRO/HOMAGE]** Everything she found written by **men in fleece vests** or shallow "AI is transformative!" filler — a Devil-Wears-Prada impossible-ask energy (adding "become AI-literate" to a full calendar). Fluorescent, deflating.
3. **The invisible load → the canyon. [PRO]** The Heroine carrying the invisible load; a widening **canyon** opening week over week between her and the people who "just stopped doing it the hard way." Cool, vast.
4. **Dolly's bridge. [HOMAGE]** Dolly Parton energy — *"you'd better get to building your own bridge, honey, because ain't nobody building it for you."* Warm gold, hopeful-defiant.
5. **Fei-Fei Li. [HOMAGE]** Fei-Fei Li (*the world calls her the "Godmother of AI"* — a media nickname she distances from, NOT a claimed title) — a future built by half the population for all of it. Dignified, warm.
6. **The flip. [PRO]** Senior women who pushed past the awkward phase, now leading their male peers — *a career's worth of judgment* is the thing AI can't replicate. Confident, warm key light.
7. **The first tiny win — Blend & Snap. [Y2K]** Sunday at the Blend & Snap: the email dreaded for four days; she tells AI the truth (who it's for, what she needs, what she can't say out loud); a draft in nine seconds, 80% right. *Oh — I can do this.* Warm café glow, relief. *(The turn to Y2K.)*
8. **The most talented new hire. [Y2K]** AI as the most talented new hire you'll ever manage — superhuman range and speed, zero lived judgment; she onboards and reviews it. Warm desk.
9. **Cher's closet computer. [HOMAGE]** Clueless — Cher's outfit-matching closet computer: endless combinations, no idea the meeting is with a hostile client unless told. The **context** limit. Candy-pop closet light.
10. **The Burn Book. [HOMAGE]** Mean Girls **Burn Book** — Regina George energy: some entries accurate, some fabricated, all in the same unbothered handwriting. The **hallucination** limit. Moody pink.
11. **Don't pull a Cher — the Statue of Liberty. [HOMAGE]** Clueless: confidently, wrongly insisting it says "R.S.V.P." on the Statue of Liberty — confident-and-wrong. Bright, comic.
