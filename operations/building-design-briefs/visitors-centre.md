## THE MECHANIC (the one idea)
The MAP IS THE INTERFACE — a big fold-out town map on the Welcome Wagon lobby wall that you operate directly. The operable object is the SUNNYVAiLE town map (`final_map/sunnyvaile-town-map-final-v5.webp`), composited INTO a framed map-board on the lobby wall the same way the LIBRAiRY bookcase composites into its room. Click any building on the map and its Welcome Card opens in place — building name, address, the plain one-liner, its 2–3 'what you do here' mechanics, and 'Go there →'. This exact mechanic already ships on the homepage (`.map-spot` hotspots + `.map-pop` reveal) and inside `sunnyvaile-directory.js` (map markers cross-highlight a plain-language directory list). The Visitors Centre's job is to make that map the WHOLE PAGE instead of a buried widget. The named directory list sits right beside/under the map so nothing is hidden — you can read every building by name and mechanic without hunting a single pin (satisfies state-on-arrival + the 'no random hotspot numbers' rule).

## Signature detail (the 'cool S' of this building)
The map is drawn as a fold-out paper tourist map — the kind pulled from a wire rack in a motel/welcome-centre lobby — with visible crease lines, and a biro scrawl in the greeter's hand: a hand-circled 'you are here!' with a little arrow and a doodled star at No.1 MAiN, plus a faint coffee-cup ring in one corner where she set her mug down. It's the lived-in human trace equivalent of the LIBRAiRY's 'cool S doodled in the margins,' and it fits THIS building exactly (a Welcome Wagon literally handed new neighbours a marked-up map). NOTE: this is a diegetic pen mark on paper — NOT the retired plum-pill + gold-star 'YOU ARE HERE' chrome badge (`sv-you-are-here.js`), which is condemned sitewide.
