*Delta LAi Nu · a member's walk-in closet. 2026-07-21.*

**Generate ONLY this. Do not start the close-ups.** Every close-up is framed from this room, so the

---
## What this image is FOR

It is the **hub** of a member's collection. She clicks a piece of furniture and it opens into a
close-up where her things are shown large. So this image has one job:

> **Every piece of furniture must be instantly readable as a distinct place that holds something.**

You are not trying to make individual charms legible here — that is what the close-ups are for. You
ARE making the sash, the jar, the shelf and the calendar unmistakable at a glance.

⛔ **Do not overfill it.** A cluttered maximalist closet makes everything a smudge and defeats the
entire design. Fewer objects, bigger, with breathing space around each.

## The room

A **walk-in closet** at the Delta LAi Nu sorority house, viewed **from the doorway looking in** —
a straight-on one-point perspective so both side walls and the back wall are visible. Warm key light
from the upper left, soft daylight spilling in from a small high window.

### The seven zones — each holds one collection

**1 · RIGHT-HAND RAIL — the MERIT SASH.** The hero of the room.
A deep plum satin sash with gold edging, hanging **full-length and completely alone** on the right
rail, well lit, nothing crowding it. Embroidered merit badges down its length. It should be the first
thing the eye lands on.

**2 · LEFT-HAND RAIL — the MALL HAUL.**
Five or six 1990s pieces on pink velvet hangers, spaced with air between them, not jammed: a
butterfly-print slip dress, a denim jacket, a faux-fur shrug, a shell top. A small handbag hooked on
the rail end.

**3 · BACK WALL, UPPER SHELF — SAVED BOOKS.**
A row of six or seven hardback books upright between chrome bookends. Jewel-tone cloth bindings, gold
foil rules. **Spines completely blank — no titles, no lettering.**

**4 · BACK WALL, LOWER SHELF — THE BINDER + STICKER BOOK.**
A pink ring binder standing upright, slightly open, clear card pockets visible. Beside it a 1990s
sticker album lying flat and open, puffy stickers on the page.

**5 · DRESSER TOP (below the shelves) — THE JAR + THE BRACELET.**
A **clear glass jar of translucent butterfly hair clips** — pink, purple, teal, glitter — large enough
to read instantly and catching the light. Beside it a small **velvet jewellery pad with a gold charm
bracelet** laid on it, charms visible.

**6 · DRESSER DRAWER, pulled half open — THE WALLET + THE DIARY.**
A pink patent-leather bifold wallet lying open, a **blank** card in its clear window. Beside it a
quilted lilac 1990s diary with a small gold padlock. *(The padlock is correct here — this one is
genuinely private.)*

**7 · THE INSIDE OF THE DOOR / near side wall — THE CORKBOARD + THE SEASON CALENDAR.**
This is the wall a girl actually decorates, and it carries the dashboard:
- A **cork pin-board in a pink wooden frame** with a few puffy stickers and small polaroid-style
  photos pinned to it. **Photo panels blank cream** — the site prints into them.
- Beneath or beside it, a **1990s wall calendar showing ONE SEASON — roughly 16 squares in a neat
  grid**, a few squares marked with small stickers, most still blank. Header area blank.
  This is the member's weekly progress, so the grid must be **regular, evenly spaced and clearly
  countable.**

### Also in the room
A full-length mirror on the near wall, a small plush stool or pouffe, a soft rug, warm parquet floor.
Keep these quiet — they are set dressing, not zones.

---
## HARD RULES

- ⛔ **NO TEXT ANYWHERE.** No book titles, no calendar month name, no labels, no brand marks, no
  numbers in the calendar squares. Every surface the website prints into must be blank.
- ⛔ **No generic dressing-room filler** — no walls of handbags, no CD racks, no cosmetics arrays,
  no shoe walls. Every object on a shelf is one of the seven above.
- ⛔ No people, no faces, no mannequins.
- Nothing after 1999. No modern devices.
- The seven zones must be **spatially separated** — a viewer should be able to point at each one
  without ambiguity. If two collections read as one pile, the image has failed.
