## SHARED STYLE (all three wings, non-negotiable)
- **Pointed GOTHIC ARCH stained-glass window** filling the background — leaded cames, jewel facets.
- **Ornate GOLD filigree border/frame** around the whole card.
- **Gold sunburst HALO** behind her head.
- **Lit ivory CANDLES** standing at the base, left and right.
- **Flowers banked along the base** — per wing (see colours below): saints = roses · mavens = darker-blue forget-me-nots · trailblazers = California poppies.
- Subject **true waist-up, centered**: the bottom edge lands at the natural
  waist/belt line; do not show hips, thighs, knees or legs. The head and
  shoulders carry the card. Do not force a symbolic object; include one
  only when it is canonical, instantly legible at card size and adds more than
  the profile copy can say.
- **Hand-ILLUSTRATED / painterly**, reverent, richly detailed — visible
  brush/ink texture, simplified illustrated skin planes and hand-drawn hair,
  never a photograph in a stained-glass template. The exact people-rendering
  authority is
  `assets/episodes/ep-04/pixel/ep04-heroine-face-lock-approved-ali.png`; the
  current saint-card painterly/material authority is
  `assets/saints/y2k-stained-glass-v2/dolly-parton-y2k-stained-glass.png`.
  Recognizable likeness of HER.
- **Wardrobe is individual, not a wing uniform.** Research and preserve a
  characteristic outfit or clothing language from the bound likeness sources.
  Do not put every MAiVEN or Trailblazer into the same navy corporate suit.
- **Portrait 2:3, 1024×1536 PNG.** Same crop/scale as the anchor so all cards line up.
