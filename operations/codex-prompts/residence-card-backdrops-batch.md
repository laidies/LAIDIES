# Residence Card backdrops v1 — 8 scenes (separate product from postcards)

## What these are
Horizontal scenes members pick as the background of their Residence Card at the
Closet. The card UI (~600×800, centered) sits ON TOP of the art, so these are
**mood-first vistas with a calm center** — not postcards. No hero captions, no
big banner text. In-scene signage renders naturally (Codex bakes it — small,
incidental, part of the scene, letter-exact casing), but nothing that competes
with the card overlay.

**One prompt per scene, run one at a time.** Painterly-realistic, same warm
register as the y2k-v3 building renders. 2:1 landscape, 1920×960, PNG.
Deliver to `Website-homepage/assets/residence-card/backdrops-v1/`.

## Composition rule (every scene)
- Center third: visually calm (open street, sky, water, glow) — the card sits here.
- Left + right thirds: the scene's character and detail.
- Bottom 15%: simple ground plane (pavement, tile, lawn, reflection).

## The 8 scenes

### 1 · `backdrop-hillside-arrival.png` — Golden-hour arrival
The SUNNYVAiLE hillside sign (white Hollywood-style letters spelling SUNNYVAiLE,
letter-exact) high on a chaparral hill in the upper-left, a palm-lined boulevard
sweeping through the lower frame, long warm shadows, peach-gold sky filling the
calm center.

### 2 · `backdrop-main-street-dusk.png` — Main Street neon dusk
Storefront rows converging to a glowing vanishing point at center (the calm
zone), marquee and neon signs small in the flanks — a Blend & Snap awning left,
CHICK FLICKS neon right — first stars in a violet-peach sky.

### 3 · `backdrop-mall-court.png` — Mall centre court
Glossy Y2K mall atrium under the glass dome: chrome fountain mid-ground low,
indoor palms and storefront glows in the flanks, bright open skylight filling
the center. Polished tile floor for the bottom band.

### 4 · `backdrop-cafe-patio.png` — Café afternoon
Blend & Snap patio from a seated angle: striped umbrella canopy upper-left,
iced latte and a paperback on the table lower-right, sun-washed stucco and open
warm air across the center.

### 5 · `backdrop-bronze-aige-night.png` — Marquee night out
BRONZE AiGE's bulb-lit marquee glow spilling from the left edge, wet sidewalk
reflections below, deep plum night sky holding the calm center, palm

### 6 · `backdrop-ksvl-signal.png` — Radio sunset
The red-and-white KSVL tower left of frame, its blinking light against a huge

### 7 · `backdrop-sorority-porch.png` — Porch golden hour
Delta LAi Nu's porch string lights framing the top, wicker sofa + pink pillows
lower-left, lawn flamingos lower-right, honey light across the open lawn and

### 8 · `backdrop-luminairy-glow.png` — Stained-glass evening
The LUMINAiRY's Y2K-relic stained-glass windows (flip phone, CD, butterfly)
glowing from inside at the frame edges, warm light pooling on the courtyard,
dusky lavender-rose sky across the calm center.

## Sanity check per scene
1. Drop a 600×800 rectangle in the center — does the scene still read? (Must.)
2. Any big text or caption competing with where the card sits? (Must be none —
   small in-scene signage at the edges only.)
3. Painterly souvenir warmth, zero photo-realism drift, zero fairytale kit?
4. Would a member proudly keep this behind her card? Eight scenes = eight
   different moods (arrival / dusk / mall / café / night out / radio / porch /
   reverent) — no two feel alike.
