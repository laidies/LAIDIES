## The approach (one decision, then it's just volume)

A 34-minute audio feature needs **~50–70 stills** (≈ one every 20–30s) so the picture is always moving —
and we can make them cheaply because **we already own the whole town.** Two rules make it producible:

- **Location-anchored.** Every town beat plays out *inside an existing SUNNYVAiLE location* — the Blend & Snap
  corner table with the laptop + article up, the library at night, the Bronze AiGE at happy hour, DJ SunnyV at the
  KSVL decks. We're not inventing a look per episode; we drop the week's action + props into the storefronts and
  interiors we already have. The only "outside" set is a plain Y2K **office** (where the problem happens) — 1–2
  reusable office renders cover the whole season.
- **Character-light.** No recurring human heroine (that's the consistency trap + fights the object-only rule).
  The heroine is the **camera**, never on screen; Steve = his slide + a pickleball paddle, not a face.

Plus **existing cards** — patron-saint / pop-culture portraits (Miranda, Cher, Dolly…) + typographic stat/quote
cards (no new art). So per episode we only generate ~20–25 NEW scenes = action dropped into owned locations.

**Style lock:** match `assets/sunnyvaile-buildings/y2k-v3/` — warm, illustrated (not photoreal), Y2K-honest,
16:9, clean composition. **Banned:** pink scattered-glam flat-lays, empty-center clutter, floating post-applied text.
Save NEW renders to `assets/episodes/issue-01/screening/S##-slug.png`.
