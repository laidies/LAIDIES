## Rules (tell Codex every time)
1. **Plum `#4b2148` is the anchor**, **gold `#c9a227`** is the metallic, **rose `#9b3f5f`** is the accent. Build everything around plum.
2. **The BRAND accent pink is muted/dusty — not neon.** For flat brand elements — UI, type, "Ai" letters, frames, filigree — the pink is always the desaturated `#9b3f5f` / `#c25b7d` family, **never** a fluorescent magenta as the brand colour (`#ff2d9b`, `#e040a0`, `#ff69b4` are wrong *as the accent*).
   - **BUT neon is welcome in the world itself — think the town signs.** SUNNYVAiLE is a Y2K town lit by neon: the "Welcome to SUNNYVAiLE" marquee, KSVL, The Chick Flicks Video, BRONZE AiGE all glow. Diegetic neon signage, marquee tubing, and neon glow are on-brand and encouraged in town/scene art. The mental model: **a glowing neon town sign = great; a neon-magenta button or flat fill = wrong.** Keep the *palette* muted, let the *signage* glow.
3. **No pure black, no pure white.** Use warm ink `#211820` and cream `#fffdfb` instead.
4. **"Ai" letters** in brand words (LAiDIES, SUNNYVAiLE, MAiKEOVER, etc.) render in the **rose accent**, distinct from the surrounding letter colour.
5. Palette reads **Y2K / early-2000s girlhood** — jewel-toned, warm, a little glossy; never corporate-flat, never neon-rave.
