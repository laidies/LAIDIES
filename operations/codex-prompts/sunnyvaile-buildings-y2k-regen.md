# Codex Prompt · SUNNYVAiLE Buildings Y2K Regen (17 images)

Regenerate all 17 SUNNYV<span style="color:#9b3f5f">Ai</span>LE building hero images. ## The era test — read this first

Every image must pass one question: **"Could this storefront appear in Clueless, Legally Blonde, or She's All That without anyone noticing?"** If a building would look at home in a Disney fairytale village or a Victorian Christmas card, redraw it.

**Anchor references (invoke, don't copy):**
- *Clueless* (1995), *Legally Blonde* (2001), *She's All That* (1999) — production design, storefronts, streets
- Real 1999 California strip malls, main streets, and shopping plazas
- Blockbuster Video, Claire's Accessories, Sam Goody, Contempo Casuals storefront design
- Late-90s municipal buildings (schools, post offices, town halls) — civic, sunny, a little beige in a comforting way

## BAN LIST — none of these may appear on ANY building

- ❌ Ornate carved-wood or gilded plaque signs with scroll ends
- ❌ Storybook serif / flourished / calligraphic lettering on signage
- ❌ Gold filigree scrollwork, decorative frames, baroque trim
- ❌ Climbing roses, rose garlands, flower-dripping facades
- ❌ Pearl strings, gem-heart crests, jeweled ornaments on buildings
- ❌ Carriage lanterns, wrought-iron curlicues, Victorian streetlamps
- ❌ Cobblestones, thatch, timber framing, leaded windows
- ❌ Anything "enchanted," "quaint," "olde," or Christmas-village

## USE INSTEAD — the Y2K storefront kit

- ✅ **Signage:** flat channel letters, backlit acrylic sign boxes, neon tubes, printed vinyl awnings
- ✅ **Sign type:** chunky rounded sans-serif, blocky condensed caps, or italic neon script
- ✅ **Materials:** teal + pink plastic, chrome and brushed-metal trim, big mall-glass frontage, stucco, brick, concrete sidewalk
- ✅ **Street furniture:** 90s streetlights, parking meters, newspaper boxes, bike racks
- ✅ **Landscaping:** palm trees, low succulent planters, tidy municipal hedges
- ✅ **Light:** warm California sun or clean neon at dusk — cheerful, never moody
- ✅ Y2K palette: glossy teal, bubblegum pink, cream, chrome, warm sunset gold

**The LAiDIES heart mark:** may appear ONCE per building at most, small — as a neon heart in a sign, a decal on a door. Never as a giant jeweled crest.
