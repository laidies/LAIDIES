- **BLUE/TEAL stained-glass background** — jewel teal + soft blue, harmonious. NOT rainbow, NOT saturated primaries.
- **Gold sunburst HALO** behind her head.
- **Lit CANDLES** framing the base.
- **FLOWERS at the base — NOT roses** (roses belong to Regina). Use **forget-me-nots** (small blue blooms — thematically perfect: these are the women history forgot to name). Consistent across the wing.
- **Hand-ILLUSTRATED / painterly** — reverent holy-card / Mucha register. Recognizable likeness, but unmistakably a drawing, never a photo.
- **1–3 meaningful motifs** per maven (below). No random filler (the current Fei-Fei window has a floating dog, bird, car, house — banned).
- Same crop/scale as the saint set.
