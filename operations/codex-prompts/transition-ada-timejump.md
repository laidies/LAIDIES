# Codex prompt — TIME-JUMP transition frame: present → ADA (London, 1843)

**Why:** the comic "we're going back in time" beat that bridges the heroine's present into Ada Lovelace's
era. This is the reusable time-jump device (see `operations/episode-comic-grammar.md`): scene-to-scene jump
+ place/year caption + aged tone + comic style. Same pattern will repeat for each Founding Mother.

## THE FRAME — an establishing transition panel (Ada's world, not a portrait)
A wide comic **establishing shot** of **Victorian London, 1843** — Ada Lovelace's world: a candle/gaslit
study or workshop with **Babbage's Analytical Engine** (brass gears, cogs, cranks, punched cards), tall
windows onto foggy London rooftops, ink pens, mathematical notes/diagrams on the desk. Ada need NOT be the
focus (she gets her own scene next) — this is the *portal into her era*. If a figure appears, a Victorian
woman at the desk, small in frame.

## COMIC-GRAMMAR SIGNALS (make it read as a time-jump)
- **Caption box, top-left:** `LONDON, 1843` — a clean comic caption box, legible, rendered in-image.
- **Aged / past tone:** cooler, moodier, slightly desaturated / candle-warm period palette — reads as THE
  PAST (present-day scenes are brighter). Gaslight/candle key light.
- Optional heavier / slightly rounded panel border framing the shot (the "going back" beat).

## STYLE — the locked comic register (match these)
Bold black ink outlines, **hard angular grey shadow PLANES**, clean flat color, painterly-inked graphic-
novel finish. ⛔ NO halftone/ben-day dots, NOT pixel, NOT smooth/plasticy, NOT pop-art poster.
Match the register of the converted Grace scene + the character renders:
`assets/episodes/ep-04/pixel/ep04-scene-05-grace-b-mid-comic-v1-no-halftone-1920.png`,
`assets/episodes/ep-04/pixel/ep04-character-test-mayor-deb-comic-v3-no-halftone-1920.png`.
(Push the comic harder than a plain de-pixelation — bold outlines + hard planes, genuinely graphic-novel.)

## FORMAT
16:9, high-res (≥1920). One image. Comic time-jump establishing frame with the `LONDON, 1843` caption box.
