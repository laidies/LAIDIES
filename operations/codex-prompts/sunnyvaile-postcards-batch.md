# SUNNYVAiLE Postcards v3 — 24 finished postcards, ALL TEXT RENDERED IN

## What changed from the failed batches
1. **Codex renders every word.** Hero caption, sign wordmarks, stamps — all baked
   into the generation. The buildings batch proved Codex does text well in-render;
   every "blank panel + composite later" attempt looked terrible. Never ask for
   blank sign faces or a no-text image again.
2. **Painterly tourist-postcard illustration, NOT photography.** The old prompt's
   "painterly-photorealistic / Kodak photograph" line is what made everything come
   back as generic photos. The register is: *illustrated souvenir postcard*, same
   painterly warmth as the y2k-v2/v3 building renders.
3. **One prompt per postcard, run one at a time** — batch prompts converge into
   the same image with prop swaps.

## Shared spec (prepend to each prompt)
> A finished 1999 souvenir postcard, painterly-realistic illustration (same warm
> painted register as a hand-tinted vacation postcard — NOT a photograph, NOT
> flat vector, NOT anime). Fictional Y2K California town: palms, stucco, neon,
> chrome, vinyl awnings, teal + peach + cream + sunset gold. NO fairytale,
> cottagecore, cathedral, baroque, 1950s-diner, or meme-poster energy.
> 2:1 landscape, 1920×960. A thin cream postcard border frames the scene.
> The hero caption is RENDERED IN the image as classic Y2K travel-brochure
> lettering (chunky rounded letters, cream/gold, sitting in the bottom band or
> arched across the sky like a "Greetings from" postcard). Casing of every
> brand word is letter-exact — the lowercase i inside caps is intentional.

**Style + facade reference (open these first):** the finished building heroes at
`Website-homepage/assets/sunnyvaile-buildings/y2k-v3-rendered-signs/*.png`.
Every building postcard must show THAT building — same architecture, colors,
and letter-exact sign — reinterpreted as a souvenir postcard scene. No invented
facades, no blank sign bands.
