## HARD REQUIREMENTS — a frame failing any of these is rejected

**1 · Dimensions: exactly 1920 × 1080.** Not 1672 × 941, not "about" 1920. Wrong dimensions
are auto-rejected by `operations/ops/accept-new-art.py` before a human ever sees the frame.

**2 · Style: `comic-v1-locked`. This is the only permitted register.**
- Bold black ink outlines
- **HARD, ANGULAR shadow PLANES** — shadow is a shape with a defined edge, not a gradient
- Flat, vibrant, saturated colour fills
- ⛔ **NO halftone dots.** ⛔ **NOT painterly, NOT watercolour, NOT airbrushed.**
- ⛔ No soft blending, no blotchy or mottled texture. A frame that reads "over-generated"
  and blurry is rejected — this has happened and been sent back.

**3 · Do NOT choose your own reference.** Each frame names one exact reference file. Use that
file and no other. Do not browse the asset folders for something you think looks better.

**4 · "AI" is spelled with BOTH letters capital — never "Ai".** The lowercase-i treatment
belongs to brand words only (LAiDIES, SUNNYVAiLE, MAiVENS, LUMINAiRY). If the acronym appears
in any rendered text, it is **AI**.

**5 · Never mix generations.** Do not scavenge or adapt frames from the pixel, `comic-barsetter`
or `comic-v2-graphic-novel` passes. Those are superseded. If a beat needs something that only
exists in an older pass, draw it fresh in `comic-v1-locked`.

**6 · Text rendered in-generation.** Where a frame needs a sign, label or caption, render the
text as part of the image. Do not leave blank plates for text to be added later.

**7 · One scene per frame.** Every named woman gets her own shot. Never collapse several
people into one group static.

**8 · Populated background figures** are Y2K-dressed diverse women unless the frame is a
historical scene, in which case dress to that scene's period.
