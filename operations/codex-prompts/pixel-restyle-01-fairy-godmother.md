## Mode
**TOWN — NIGHT / DUSK mode** (her scene is a cozy cottage porch at golden dusk with warm string lights).
Vibrant, saturated, warmly lit and GLOWING like the homepage — plum-dusk warmth, rich palette color, warm
window / string-light glow, and **a little neon / bulb sparkle-glow suits a fairy godmother**. Not flat
daylight, not the dark episode mood. Take ONLY the pixel *technique* from the heroine sheet; take the
light/color from the "Dial-Up to Sunnyvaile" postcard ref (`pc-dial-up.png`).

## Who she is (preserve all of this — from the current scene)
A warm, beloved **grandmother fairy godmother** — kind, twinkly, mid-smile. An older woman with **dark
curly grey (salt-and-pepper) hair** in a soft updo and **warm olive skin**. A small silver **star tiara**;
gold wire-rim glasses; **star** drop earrings + a gold **star pendant**. A **dusty-pink beaded / sparkly
shawl** over a **cream lace dress**. In one hand she holds an **iridescent crystal star-tipped wand**; in
the other she reads a **gold star-map letter**. Around her, her letter-writer's world: a **purple
star-embossed mailbox stuffed with envelopes**, a **rose-patterned teacup** and a **glowing votive candle
jar** on a wicker side table, potted lavender + pink flowers, and her cozy **cottage porch** strung with
**warm string lights** (a "Willow Lane" sign, hanging flower baskets), all in **golden-hour glow**. She's
the town's advice-and-letters fairy (the Post Office / "Ask LAiDY"). Keep her age, warmth, diversity, and
body — a real, radiant older woman. Kind face, individual likeness. (No prominent fairy wings — don't add them.)

## Render as (bright TOWN-mode pixel)
- High-fidelity **pixel illustration**: visible pixels + ordered dithering, semi-realistic proportions,
  detailed expressive face, form from light/shadow (no thick cartoon outlines).
- **Bright + warm**: golden-hour cottage glow. Color from the palette — pinks (`#e982ab`), coral, cream
  (`#fffdfb`), periwinkle (`#b3abe7`), plum (`#4b2148`) for depth, **gold** (`#c9a227`) for the wand
  sparkle and metal accents. Cheerful, luminous, cozy — not dark, not muddy.
- Keep the roses/letters/tea world and the wings + wand. Y2K-cozy, ~1999.
- No AI-cliché imagery. No text in the image.
