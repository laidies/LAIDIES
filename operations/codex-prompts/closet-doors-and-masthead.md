Both live at Delta LAi Nu, the sorority house. The **doors** are the entrance on the house page; the
**masthead** is the banner at the top of the Closet page, with the member's actual collection below it.

# IMAGE 1 — THE CLOSET DOORS  ·  for the Delta LAi Nu page

A pair of **closed closet doors**, shot straight on, filling the frame — the way you'd stand in front
of them before opening.

- Painted panelled doors in the house's palette — soft pink or cream with teal detailing.
- **Gold heart-shaped handles** (the house's hardware motif), catching the light.
- A hint of warm light glowing from the seam between them and underneath — something good is in there.
- Subtle 1990s girlhood detail on the doors: a small mirrored panel, a butterfly clip hooked over a
  handle, a friendship bracelet looped on one knob. **Restrained** — two or three touches, not a
  collage.
- Enough of the surrounding wall or corridor to place it inside the sorority house.

**The feeling: closed, inviting, obviously openable.** It should make you want to click it.
⛔ Do not show the inside. Do not show them ajar. **Closed.**

# IMAGE 2 — THE CLOSET MASTHEAD  ·  banner at the top of the Closet page

A **wide banner-shaped view inside the walk-in closet** — the header of the page, so it must read well
at a very wide, short crop.

- A 1990s walk-in closet: hanging rails, open shelving, a teal chest of drawers with gold heart pulls,
  a lit vanity with bulb-framed mirror at one end, plush rug, warm parquet.
- Soft daylight from a small high window, warm key light from the upper left.
- **Styled but UNDER-FILLED.** A few clothes on pink velvet hangers, a couple of shelf objects — enough
  that it reads as a real lived-in closet, but **generous open space** so it works as a background
  behind a page title.
- The composition should be calm and horizontal, with **visual weight to the sides and space through
  the middle**, so a title can sit over it if needed.

⚠ **Deliberately do NOT show:** a sash covered in badges, a jar full of clips, a bracelet hung with
charms, or shelves of books. Those are the member's real collection and the site renders them below
this banner. Showing them here would tell every new member she already owns things she has not earned
— which is exactly why the previous version of this idea was rejected.
