## The brief

Illustrate 6 Y2K-illuminated portraits of women *shipping* frontier AI, in the same reverent register as the LAiDIES **Y2K stained-glass patron saint** cards and the **Mavens** batch (which you already have as visual reference for palette, painterly register, and rose base).

**Distinction from Saints and Mavens — this is critical:**

- Saints have a **round golden halo** behind the head (divine).
- Mavens have a **golden laurel wreath** behind the head (academic honor).
- Builders have a **radiant arc of thin architectural blueprint lines** behind the upper head — a semi-circle of pale-gold Y2K-tech engineering strokes, softly luminous. Reads as *"she designed and built the thing"* — not divine, not academic, but crafted.
- Saints have a **stained-glass cathedral window** behind them. Mavens have **soft warm chapel light + a subtle work artifact.** Builders have **soft warm chapel light + the product they shipped rendered as a subtle background artifact** (see per-Builder list below).
- All three groups keep **roses at the base of the portrait** — the anchor that unifies the SANCTUAiRY shelf across three rooms.

**Palette**: warm plum, cream, gold, plus a whisper of Y2K electric blue in the blueprint arc only. Same warmth as the Saint + Maven cards.
**Aspect ratio**: 2:3 vertical portrait.
**Format**: PNG.
**Register**: hand-illustrated, painterly, dignified. NOT photorealistic. NOT cartoon.
**Composition**: head and shoulders, subject facing forward or three-quarter angle, gaze steady, expression composed and confident — the look of a founder who just shipped.
