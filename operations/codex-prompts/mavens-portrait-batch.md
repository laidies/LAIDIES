## The brief

Illustrate 12 Y2K-illuminated institutional portraits of women who built the foundation of AI, in the same reverent register as the LAiDIES **Y2K stained-glass patron saint** cards (which you already have as visual reference for palette, register, and painterly density).

**Distinction from the saints — this is critical:**

- Saints have **round golden halos** behind their head (divine iconography). Mavens **do NOT have halos**.
- Instead, Mavens have a **golden laurel wreath** behind the head — the academic/Nobel-Prize honor signal, symbolizing "real person, real earned honor."
- Saints have a **stained-glass cathedral window** behind them. Mavens have **soft warm chapel light** with a **subtle work artifact** in the background (see per-Maven list below).
- Both Saints and Mavens keep **roses at the base of the portrait** — this is the visual anchor that makes the two groups feel like one SANCTUAiRY shelf, two rooms.

**Palette**: warm plum, cream, gold. Same warmth as the saint cards.
**Aspect ratio**: 2:3 vertical portrait.
**Format**: PNG.
**Register**: hand-illustrated, painterly, dignified. NOT photorealistic. NOT cartoon.
**Composition**: head and shoulders, subject facing forward or three-quarter angle, gaze steady, expression composed and confident.

## Style rules for consistency across all 12

- Same golden laurel wreath position (behind the upper head, framing softly).
- Same warm chapel light in the background — not stained glass, not modern lighting.
