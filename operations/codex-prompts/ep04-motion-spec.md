# PART 1 · 🌀 THE TIME-JUMP SWIRL

**The device:** she is standing in the LUMINAiRY looking up at Ada in stained glass. **The window is
the portal.** We go back through the glass. No caption-card cut.

## 1A · Canva — build ONE reusable swirl asset
Not tied to any frame; CapCut tints and reuses it at every era jump.

- **A 2-second clockwise vortex**, transparent background, 1920×1080.
- Light streaks and fine particles spiralling **inward toward centre frame**, accelerating as they go.
- Warm gold and stained-glass blue, soft-edged, no hard geometry, no lens flare.
- Ends on a **white bloom filling the centre** so CapCut can cut through it.
- Export **with alpha** (MOV/ProRes 4444 or WebM) so it can sit over any shot.

**Out:** `assets/video/fx/timejump-swirl-v1.mov`

## 1B · CapCut — the FULL transition, first jump only (into LONDON, 1843)

# WHY THIS ORDER
Loops 1 and 2 plus the swirl fix the actual complaint: the LUMINAiRY sequence stops being three static
pictures and becomes a lit room you walk into and fall through. Loops 3–5 break up the remaining
slideshow. If time is short, **do 1 and 2 and the swirl** — that is the fix.
