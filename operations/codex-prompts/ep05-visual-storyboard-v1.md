## Locked direction

- The analogy is **major-1990s-supermodel energy for different AI models**.
- Women only in the supermodel lineup: no men, no male silhouettes, no fictional “Nova / Prism / Orbit”
  characters, no robots, no superhero team.
- Backgrounds are unmistakably technological—servers, model-path diagrams, data grids, neural nodes—but
  the women stay the visual stars.
- Graphic-novel register: bold variable ink, clean flat saturated color, hard angular sculptural shadows;
  no glossy glamour-cartoon, no painterly drift, no halftone.
- Multiple images per scene are expected. **Only 1–2 shots per scene receive full animation**; every other
  shot is a still or gets slight source-native motion.
- No version numbers baked into art. Product lineups change; house/store/model concepts do not.

## Transition edit

- Default: 0.4–0.7s editorial crossfade between stills.
- Use match cuts for screen→screen, face→campaign poster, boutique→department-store entrance, and
  task card→counter.
- Use straight cuts on the title, “model ≠ tool,” and the department-store reveal.
- One dip-to-black is allowed before the next-week teaser. No novelty wipes or face morphs.
- Camera moves settle before a crossfade; the next move begins only after the transition lands.
