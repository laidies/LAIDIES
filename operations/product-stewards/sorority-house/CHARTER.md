## Product promise

Delta LAi Nu is a calm, specific place for women to bring a useful work/learning situation, see what each room is for, and choose a conversation with clear norms. It is not a generic comment wall, adult-membership fantasy, follower contest or reward farm.

## Scope and sub-champion boundaries

- **Building steward:** porch/arrival, room directory, resident-versus-visitor clarity, room handoff, brand/community quality and return path.
- **Community sub-champion:** Hyvor room embeds, post/reply/moderation/error flows, community guidelines, service dependency, privacy and member identity.
- **Girl Talk sub-champion:** private Truth/Dare reflection/card draw, local honour-system completion and its honest connection to—not proof of—a community room.
- **Identity/Rewards & Connection:** any durable member status, badge or reward; no room visit/click/post assumption is allowed to create entitlement.

## Non-negotiable gates

Community requires explicit conduct/moderation/reporting/escalation rules, data minimization, provider boundary, accessible keyboard/mobile states and a human-owned incident path. No raw community content, handles, personal work details or membership status enters analytics. Do not reward posts, shares, visits or count-based popularity; protect quiet participation and exit without penalty.
