# Miss Jeeves identity successor pilot

**Status:** INTERNAL PILOT — not integrated, admitted, published or deployed
**Owner direction:** Ali, 2026-09-01

## Decision

Miss Jeeves currently looks out of place in SUNNYVAiLE. Replace the current
face and overall character styling with a Miss Geist-inspired direction: the
warm, eccentric, slightly frazzled late-1990s teacher energy associated with
Twink Caplan's character in *Clueless*. The new Miss Jeeves may change face,
hair, ethnicity and wardrobe. This is a visual-character reference, not a
request to reproduce a film still or make a literal portrait of Twink Caplan.

## Representative proof

Create one 2:3 vertical, text-free adult graphic-novel portrait of Miss Jeeves
at the SUNNYVAiLE LIBRAiRY reference desk. This pilot decides the identity
direction before any Homepage, Library masthead, episode-credit or card
successors are produced.

## Required visual facts

- A woman in her late fifties or early sixties with a Miss Geist-inspired face:
  expressive brows, short dark softly curly hair, warm intelligent eyes and an
  open, slightly amused smile.
- Small oval reading glasses on a gold chain.
- Warm eccentric late-1990s teacher/librarian wardrobe: patterned blouse,
  softly structured cardigan or jacket, small gold open-book brooch and simple
  earrings. She should feel individual and lived-in, not costumed.
- Adult graphic-novel rendering with confident ink linework, controlled
  screenprint/halftone texture, faceted light and clean, human skin.
- A practical Y2K reference-desk setting: card catalogue, books, desk lamp and
  beige CRT computer. Her posture and expression should say that she is ready
  to help find the source.
- Electric SUNNYVAiLE colour accents, with warmth and daylight; avoid the old
  stately plum-and-gold vintage-library mood.

## Prohibited contradictions

- No glamour-cartoon, near-photographic oil painting, cottage-core, fantasy
  librarian, schoolmarm caricature or generic corporate illustration.
- No attempt to copy a specific *Clueless* frame, costume or background.
- No generated words, labels, nameplates, screen text or book-spine text.
- No youthful makeover, severe expression, bouffant silver coiffure, burgundy
  cable-knit uniform or ornate Victorian library styling from the predecessor.
- No malformed glasses, chain, hands, fingers, furniture, CRT, books or card
  catalogue; no wet or mottled skin.
- No random extra people, vehicles, magical props or decorative clutter without
  a reference-desk job.

## Continuity boundary

The character remains **Miss Jeeves**, the question-led LIBRAiRY reference-desk
guide. This pilot does not change her name, product role, answer behaviour or
any current public asset. The existing Homepage image, Library masthead,
episode frames and card candidate remain untouched until Ali approves this
identity direction.
