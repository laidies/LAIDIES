**Status:** SPECIFIED / LOCAL PRODUCTION ONLY
**Public or deploy authority:** none

## 1. Trigger and intended result

- **Intended learner:** a smart, busy nontechnical woman who understands the main ideas in *AI Fundamentals 101* (or has equivalent familiarity) and has used chat AI enough to be impressed, irritated and unsure why results vary.
- **Problem she brings:** “How do I turn what I understand about AI into reliable work I can actually use?”
- **Why LAiDIES is teaching this:** conceptual literacy is only useful if the reader can translate it into a bounded task, useful evidence, a decision and a reusable working habit.
- **Observable result:** she can run the full responsible working loop on one real low-risk task; leave with one personal setup, three saved briefs or workflows and a repeatable decision for when to accept, check, restart or decline AI.
- **Concrete next use:** prepare a work brief, analyse a supplied document, improve a draft or pilot a read-only recurring summary without surrendering judgment or exposing material she is not allowed to share.

## 2. Canonical concept ownership

- **Canonical concept:** end-to-end practical AI working loop.
- **Concept-map row:** `End-to-end practical AI working loop`.
- **Canonical LIBRAiRY owner:** *Working with AI 101* owns integration and troubleshooting across the loop.
- **Adjacent owners that stay distinct:**
  - *AI Fundamentals 101*: connected causal map of the AI system;
  - *Briefing 101*: complete five-part briefing procedure;
  - *Setup 101*: placement of standing instructions, optional memory, projects and one-job context;
  - *Accounts 101*: whether material may enter a particular account at all;
  - verification reference: the full source-checking method;
  - dated tool cards: current product names, controls, limits, plans, regions and data rules;
  - SUNNYVAiLE High: guided demonstration, feedback, diagnosis and transfer.
- **Misconceptions to prevent:** prompting is the whole skill; visible chat history equals the exact context used; citations prove truth; code execution proves correctness; a subscription determines privacy; an agent guarantees completion; a confident answer knows it is correct.
- **Freshness trigger:** any named vendor feature, model, plan, privacy/data rule, interface path, context limit, benchmark result or legal/workplace policy.

## 3. Existing-content reconciliation

- **Episode/canon:** Episode 1 owns the first safe encounter; Episode 2 owns the narrative case that prompting is delegation; Episode 3 owns the stakes of confidence without evidence.
- **LIBRAiRY:** extend the catalogue with one integrated operating reference; link to existing narrow owners instead of reproducing their full procedures.
- **Class/register:** High classes demonstrate current interfaces and controlled one-variable practice; the book remains a scannable reference.
- **Tools/games:** FAiRY may help shape one request; Dream Phone may rehearse verification; neither owns the complete loop.
- **NewsStand:** applies the loop to dated evidence and current product changes; it never supplies evergreen vendor claims to the book without primary-source reconciliation.

**Decision:** CREATE the practical companion, while correcting duplication and routing deep or volatile detail to the existing owner.

## 5. Format-specific admission answer

The durable lookup job is: “I am at this stage of an AI task; what do I do next, what must I inspect and what changes if it goes wrong?” The book is revisable because the operating loop is stable while dated tool cards isolate volatile implementation facts. It does not become a class transcript: practical actions are short, low-risk and artifact-producing, without pretending to supply guided observation or live feedback.
