# Who’s Who chip-chain pilot — source card

Internal research checked **31 August 2026**. Not a manuscript, complete
industry map, investment recommendation or observed trace of our model runs.
The teaching question to test: when a headline says a company “makes AI,”
does the reader understand which part it actually makes or supplies?

## Claims verified for the representative section

| Role to distinguish | Supported company example | Exact source and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Product design versus owning the factory | NVIDIA describes a fabless strategy, concentrating resources on design while suppliers perform manufacturing phases | [NVIDIA FY2026 10-K, Manufacturing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm), reporting period ended 25 Jan 2026. Historical reported supply strategy, not a claim this is the latest quarterly filing |
| Semiconductor wafer manufacture | NVIDIA names TSMC and Samsung as foundries producing its wafers | Same 10-K Manufacturing section. Does not say every NVIDIA product uses both, or that either supplier is exclusive |
| Memory supply | NVIDIA names SK hynix, Micron and Samsung as memory suppliers | Same 10-K. Does not establish the exact memory supplier for a particular model session or chip generation |
| Equipment for chip manufacture | ASML makes lithography equipment; its systems use light to form circuit patterns | [ASML company description](https://www.asml.com/en/company/about-asml). Equipment supplier is not the same role as foundry or chatbot provider |
| Packaging processor and memory components together | TSMC's CoWoS service integrates system-on-chip components and high-bandwidth memory stacks | [TSMC 2025 Annual Report](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/annual-report/2025/2025%20Annual%20Report.E.pdf), PDF page index 52, printed pages 102–103. This is component integration, not a cardboard shipping box; no inference that every AI chip uses this service |
| Cloud capacity versus model development | Anthropic names AWS its primary training/cloud provider for mission-critical workloads and describes use of Amazon's Trainium chips | [Anthropic–Amazon compute announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-amazon-compute), April 2026 announcement, checked today. “Primary” does not mean exclusive; announced future capacity is not all already running |

For NVIDIA's supply relationships, preserve the reporting period. Reopening an
annual report today does not turn it into a current-quarter inventory. Before
publication, check subsequent filings for changes to any relationship taught
as current. This card deliberately does not assert rankings, market share,
scarcity, exclusivity, or which data centre served a particular prompt.

## Proposed teaching proof — not yet run with a reader

implies every company belongs to one exclusive production line. For a familiar
AI application, ask what its visible brand fails to tell us about hardware,
memory, equipment, cloud hosting and the teams behind it. The role map is an
illustration of dependencies, not a trace of that application's exact backend.
