## Venue Decision

North Hall is recommended because it is the only option whose quoted facilities currently meet the approved accessibility and capacity requirements: confirmed step-free access, confirmed accessible washroom, and capacity 20 for 18 attendees. [S1][S2]

West Room's nominal cost is lower, but it is not an eligible choice while step-free access and an accessible washroom remain unconfirmed. [S1][S3]

For reference only, the West Room quoted cost would be $220.50 after 5% tax on the $210 room-plus-cleaning amount, before catering, printing, and reserve. This does **not** make West the recommendation because eligibility is unresolved. [S3]

## Budget Checks

North Hall calculation:

- Venue: $240.00
- Catering: 20 × $9 = $180.00
- Delivery: $25.00
- Printing: 18 × $2 = $36.00
- Subtotal: $240 + $180 + $25 + $36 = **$481.00**
- Reserve: 10% × $481 = **$48.10**
- Total: $481 + $48.10 = **$529.10**
- Remaining: $600 − $529.10 = **$70.90**

The reserve is applied once, after tax, to the combined venue, catering, delivery, and printing costs, matching S1. [S1][S2][S4][S5]
