## No-Account / Privacy Fallback

All hands-on activities have a paired, paper-based fallback that requires
no device, login, or AI account. Practice packs (one per attendee, per
printing quote) will use **fictional practice data only** — no attendee
personal data or real third-party data will be collected or used.
Attendees needing a paid AI subscription for full participation is not
required by design; free/no-account routes are built into every activity.
