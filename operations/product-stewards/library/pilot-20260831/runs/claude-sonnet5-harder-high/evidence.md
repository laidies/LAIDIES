## Contradictions Identified and Resolved

1. **North Hall capacity: 24 (S6 note) vs. 24 (Aug 12 leaflet, referenced in
   S2) vs. 20 (S2 current quote).**
   Resolution: S2's current quote is the authoritative source and explicitly
   states it replaces the Aug 12 leaflet figure of 24. S6 (informal note) is
   lower authority per S1. **Used: 20.** Still sufficient for 18 attendees.

2. **West Room "180 all-in" (S6) vs. CAD 180 + compulsory CAD 30 cleaning,
   both +5% tax (S3).**
   Resolution: S3 is the current written quote and controls. West Room's
   true cost is CAD 220.50, not CAD 180. This did not change the venue
   decision, since West Room was already excluded on accessibility grounds.

3. **West Room accessibility: "looks accessible in photos" (S6) vs. "NOT
   YET CONFIRMED" (S3).**
   Resolution: S3 controls. Brief (S1) requires *confirmed* accessibility.
   West Room fails this requirement and is therefore **ineligible**,
   regardless of its lower or higher price. Not used in the recommendation.

4. **Whether to include the reserve at all: S6 suggests skipping it "because
   the budget has spare room"; S1 mandates the reserve as a fixed formula.**
   Resolution: S1 (approved brief) controls. Reserve is calculated and
   included in the total (CAD 48.10).
