## Checks actually performed

1. **Venue eligibility:** Checked the stated attendance (18) against North Hall's written capacity (20); 18 fits.
2. **Accessibility:** Checked the brief's requirement against the written confirmation of step-free access and an accessible washroom.
3. **Equipment:** Checked that the required projector is included in the North Hall quote.
4. **Budget arithmetic:** Calculated 18 × $9.00 catering = $162.00; added $240.00 venue, $25.00 delivery, and 18 × $2.00 printing = $36.00, producing a $463.00 subtotal.
5. **Reserve:** Calculated 10% of the $463.00 subtotal once = $46.30.
6. **Total and remaining budget:** Calculated $463.00 + $46.30 = $509.30; $600.00 − $509.30 = $90.70 remaining.
7. **Tax treatment:** Used the written quotes as tax included and did not add extra tax.
8. **Agenda:** Checked that the agenda totals 90 minutes and contains a 10-minute break.
9. **Invitation length:** Checked the DRAFT invitation is 99 words, within the 120-word maximum.
10. **Subscription/fallback requirement:** Checked that the proposal states no paid AI subscription is required and includes a no-account paired-paper fallback using fictional practice data.
11. **Unresolved facts:** Preserved the source-stated unknowns: event date/venue availability and dietary needs.
12. **Booking status:** No booking, contact, publication, or purchase is claimed or performed.
