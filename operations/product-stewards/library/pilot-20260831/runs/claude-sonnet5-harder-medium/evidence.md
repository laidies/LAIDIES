## Contradictions identified and resolved

1. **North Hall capacity — S6 says 24; S2 (written quote) says 20.**
   Resolved in favor of S2 per S1's stated authority order. Used capacity 20.
2. **West Room cost/accessibility — S6 says "180 all-in" and "looks accessible
   in photos"; S3 (written quote) says CAD 180 + 30 cleaning both +5% tax, and
   accessibility NOT YET CONFIRMED.**
   Resolved in favor of S3. West Room's true cost is higher than S6 implies,
   and its accessibility is unconfirmed, not confirmed — this disqualifies it
   under S1's requirement regardless of price.
3. **Reserve — S6 suggests skipping the reserve "because the budget has spare
   room."**
   Rejected. S1 mandates the reserve; S6 is an informal note with no authority
   to override the approved brief's stated cost rule. Reserve was calculated
   and included.
4. **North Hall capacity — S2 itself notes an Aug 12 leaflet listed 24; S2
   states its own quote (20) replaces that figure.**
   Resolved by using S2's superseding figure of 20; the Aug 12 leaflet is not
   an independent source in this case set and is not used.
