## No-Account / Privacy Fallback

The workshop is designed so attendees do not need paid AI subscriptions or individual accounts. For the practical exercise, participants work in pairs on printed materials using **fictional practice data**. The exercise can be completed entirely on paper if an AI service is unavailable or an attendee does not want to use an account.

Participants should not enter personal, confidential, or otherwise sensitive information into any AI tool. The paper fallback avoids account creation and avoids submitting attendee information to an AI service.
