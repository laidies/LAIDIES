   West Room is now the only venue meeting all confirmed requirements, so it
   is recommended without needing a cost comparison.

   (current) confirms it.** Resolved in favor of S7, the later approved
   revision, per the case's stated update: "West has now confirmed step-free
   access and an accessible washroom; price/capacity are unchanged." Price
   and capacity figures from S3 remain in force.
3. **North Hall capacity — S6 (informal) says 24; S2 (written quote) says
   20.** This conflict is not moot: it is decisive for eligibility. Per S1's
   authority order, S2's written capacity of 20 controls over S6's informal

   attendance of 22, which is what makes North Hall ineligible under the
   brief's capacity requirement. Had S6's uncontrolled figure of 24 been used
   instead, North Hall would have appeared eligible, so resolving this
   contradiction correctly (in favor of S2) is what drives the venue
   decision — it is not a side issue.
4. **Reserve — S6 suggests skipping the reserve.** Unchanged: rejected: S1
   mandates the reserve and S6 has no authority to override the brief.
   Reserve recalculated on the new subtotal.
