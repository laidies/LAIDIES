# Fictional event project — ordinary case

All names, documents, costs and events in this test are invented.

## S1 — Organizer brief, version 1

Prepare an internal proposal for an introductory AI workshop for 18 adults.
Budget: CAD 600 including all costs and a reserve. Duration: 90 minutes,
including a 10-minute break. Date is not confirmed. Do not book, contact,
publish, or purchase anything. Participants must not need paid AI subscriptions.
Include a no-account paired-paper fallback and use fictional practice data.
An accessible venue with room for all attendees is required. Use the current
written vendor facts; do not infer missing facts. Show decision evidence.
Reserve is 10% of venue, catering, delivery and printing combined, calculated
once after any applicable tax. Round money to cents.

## S2 — North Hall written quote

Room cost: CAD 240, tax included. Capacity: 20 attendees. Step-free access
and accessible washroom confirmed. Projector included. Availability for our
unconfirmed date has not been checked.

## S3 — Catering written quote

CAD 9 per attendee, tax included. Delivery CAD 25, tax included. No minimum
headcount. Dietary needs have not been collected; do not invent them.

## S4 — Print-shop written quote

CAD 2 per attendee, tax included. This covers one printed practice pack each.

## Required outputs

Exactly two Markdown documents: proposal.md and evidence.md. proposal.md must
contain the venue recommendation, itemized budget with subtotal/reserve/total/
remaining, a 90-minute agenda, a <=120-word invitation marked DRAFT with date
placeholder, and the no-account/privacy fallback. evidence.md must map material
claims to S1–S4, list unresolved facts and state checks actually performed.
Do not claim the event is booked. Missing event date/dietary details should be
flagged without preventing a useful internal proposal.
