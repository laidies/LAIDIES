## Overlap rulings

1. **The complete workshop source pack stays in both vendor guides.** Each book
   must work independently for a reader who owns only one product. The
   tool-selection sample does not repeat it; it compares the preserved outcomes.
2. **Working with AI 101 remains the universal method owner.** The new guides
   may briefly use source hierarchy, inspection and targeted revision, but they
   must link rather than re-teach the entire brief/context/verify/revise loop.
3. **Model and effort recipes stay vendor-specific.** The selection guide may
   route to a named recipe but cannot flatten Light, Low, Medium, High, xhigh,
   Max or Ultra across vendors.
4. **Product setup stays out of the selection guide.** It can state which
   capability decides a choice; the exact menu, setting, Project or context-file
   placement belongs in ChatGPT or Claude.
5. **Company relationships stay out of vendor manuals.** A short ownership or
   infrastructure fact may explain a product boundary, but Who's Who owns the
   dated power and supply map.
6. **Companion High courses demonstrate; books remain references.** The course
   can show the failed revision live, ask the learner to diagnose it and test an
   unseen change. It must not narrate these chapters page by page.

## What the four samples establish

- The books can be practical without becoming prompt collections. Each prompt
  sits inside a complete job with inputs, output, inspection, correction and a
  stopping condition.
- Free is a real route, not a consolation paragraph. The observed ChatGPT Free
  account produced initial files and completed a text repair after a tool limit.
- Paid is a capability choice, not a correctness guarantee. Paid Claude Code
  created a controlled file environment and still introduced source-policy
  wording that was not supplied.
- More effort needs a demonstrated reason. One Sonnet 5 High run used more
  output tokens than Medium without establishing a decision-changing gain.
- A practical guide can show a residual defect without teaching failure. The
  Claude repair demonstrates why exact checks and honest stopping reports matter.
- The chip chapter can explain infrastructure without asking the reader to buy
  hardware or memorise a corporate family tree.
