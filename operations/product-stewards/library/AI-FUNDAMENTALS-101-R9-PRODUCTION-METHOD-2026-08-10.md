## Production sequence

1. Preserve the Introduction's three reasons, jokes, cadence and authorial
   flow without inserting concept teaching into it.
2. Open Chapter 1 with the real public-language problem before using any
   acronym, classification framework or technical definition.
3. Do not force unlike labels into a historical succession. Teach one stable
   reader question at a time: how working knowledge was obtained; what result
   is produced; what information is connected; what happens after an answer;
   whether action reaches the physical world; and how broad the abilities are.
4. Keep true nesting together: AI contains machine learning, which contains
   deep learning. Treat predictive/generative, multimodal, agentic, embodied
   and breadth labels as answers to distinct questions that may overlap.
5. Make every transition state the reader question that changes. A heading
   may not expose internal curriculum language such as `job family`,
   `classification axis` or `this part of the system`.
6. Use an actual AI system as every core example. A human analogy is optional
   and must never be allowed to impersonate the AI mechanism.
7. Distinguish ordinary automation from rule-based AI. The representative
   rule-based example requires a specialist knowledge base and reasoning across
   linked rules; a single threshold or trigger does not qualify.
8. Write every full term before its acronym. The first appearances are
   `artificial general intelligence (AGI)` and `artificial superintelligence
   (ASI)`, never unexplained shorthand.
9. Define each technical word in ordinary language where it first becomes
   necessary. Do not defer a term that the current explanation depends on.
10. Use history as a bounded qualification before synthesis. It must explicitly
    show overlap and prominence, never imply that agents, robots or machine
    learning appeared in a clean sequence after rule-based systems.
11. End with Recognise / Explain / Draw / Use checks, exact return routes for
    misconceptions, a compact reconstruction and a causal handoff to Chapter 2.
12. Sustain Ali's direct best-friend relationship through transitions, examples
    and the landing. Isolated jokes cannot compensate for generic exposition.

## Full-artifact review rule

The producer and independent reviewer read the complete continuous chapter from
the reader's first sentence to its handoff. They must explain why every section
follows the one before it. Sampled local clarity, accurate individual
definitions or a correct recap cannot compensate for a confusing route.

The known-bad R7 artifact must be rejected unaided for acronym order, trivial
automation misclassification, vague referents, framework-first organisation,
the video-clerk pseudo-example and broken continuous progression. R8 must also
be rejected for its false historical ladder, cross-family transition failures,
generic technical centre and missing correction/handoff before R9 may enter
Ali's review queue.
