## The production principle

This book is not produced by summarising reputable sources and adding LAiDIES
references afterward. Source evidence establishes accurate claims and
mechanisms. The governed curriculum, learner outcomes, LAiDIES voice and
teaching exemplars determine how a nontechnical reader encounters them.

Every section is written to create a specific change in understanding. It must
extend the cumulative AI-system map and leave the reader able to answer its
named questions, explain the mechanism in her own words and use the model on a
different everyday or work example.

### 2. Technical and factual evidence

Use exact current items from primary research, authoritative technical
references and official provider documentation. Stanford, MIT, Cambridge,
Harvard, Berkeley and comparable institutional sources are mechanism and

do not become the LAiDIES curriculum.

### 3. Nontechnical progression and pedagogy

Helsinki and OpenLearn are progression comparators. Carnegie Mellon may inform
worked-example, feedback and transfer design. Availability or institutional
prestige is not evidence of learning effectiveness or audience fit.

### 4. Practical and changing applications

Wharton GAIL and exact underlying papers may support bounded prompting claims.
Wharton Human-AI Research may supply dated workplace and human-impact evidence.
Ethan Mollick's exact dated original work may supply attributed practitioner
evidence. Social posts and AIDB summaries are discovery leads only and must
return to their exact original evidence.

Every material claim binds an exact source, scope and freshness trigger before
admission. Changing product facts are freshly verified for the candidate.

## Drafting sequence for every section

Before prose, recover the section's:

1. prior learner understanding;
2. teaching goal;
3. concepts and relationships added;
4. answerable outcome questions;
5. everyday or work relevance;
6. Draw it, Explain it or Use it evidence; and
7. connection back to the complete AI ecosystem.

Then draft in this order:

1. write the plain conversational explanation with no decorative branding;
2. begin from a human situation and a precise live question;
3. give the shortest accurate plain answer;
4. spend the largest share on the mechanism, link by link;
5. make the invisible process concrete with one useful case;
6. answer the natural follow-ups: how, why, what connects and what changes;
7. name limitations, consequences and material uncertainty;
8. give the reader one useful action, judgment or prediction;
9. reconnect the new relationship to the cumulative system map; and
10. land with a precise observation, better next question or earned joke.

If the plain conversational version is less clear than an ordinary chat
explanation, repair it before adding voice, analogy, humour or visual treatment.

## Communication style

The narrator is the smartest, most enthusiastic, passionate and funny best
friend from the Rewind Era, explaining a complex topic to someone with no
technical background. She assumes intelligence, anticipates follow-up
questions, conveys real delight in the subject and stays useful before clever.
She is warm, direct, specific, lightly irreverent and allergic to corporate
nonsense.

She is not a generic AI explainer, institutional lecturer, chatbot, girlboss
coach or writer adding fake sass to beige prose. No random celebrity names,
forced best-friend language, cute classification labels, generic empowerment,
em-dash title habit or parallel metaphor vocabulary.

## Analogy, humour and Rewind Era rule

No analogy or joke is required. Every proposed analogy must pass:

1. **Mapping:** its important parts correspond accurately to the mechanism.
2. **Clarity:** the explanation becomes easier to understand with it.
3. **Reasoning:** the reader can use it to make another correct prediction.
4. **Strip test:** the plain teaching remains a useful structure when the
   analogy is removed; otherwise the analogy is costume and is cut or reduced.

The Spice Girls prompting cue and Burn Book hallucination mapping are positive
examples only for their exact jobs. A reference may add delight for someone
who remembers it, but the teaching must remain complete for someone who does
not. Reader prose does not explain where an analogy stops unless a specific
material misconception requires the boundary.

Humour comes from precise workplace truth, corporate absurdity, practical
frustration, LAiDIES canon or an analogy whose joke is also the diagnosis. It
must sharpen understanding, distinction, action or recall. There is no joke or
reference quota.

## Examples and depth

Standard includes the complete core explanation, connected mechanism,
concrete example, practical application, material limitation or consequence
and useful next step. `Tell me more` and `Full nerd alert!` answer genuine
follow-up questions and extend the same model. They may not repair Standard,
create a second truth path or become vocabulary accumulation.

Examples come from recognizable interactions with AI: asking for an
explanation, receiving a recommendation, using an AI feature at work, supplying
a file, checking current information, connecting a tool, distinguishing a
proposed action from an executed one, deciding what is safe to share or
diagnosing a failed result. An example passes only when it exposes the
mechanism and supports transfer.

## Visual jobs

Prose stabilizes before any diagram or illustration is produced. A visual must
name the relationship it makes easier to understand and agree exactly with the
accompanying prose. CSS/HTML may lay out deterministic text but may not draw an
image, illustration or diagram. If prose explains the relationship more
clearly than the proposed visual, the visual does not belong.

## Producer stop conditions

The producer stops and repairs before independent review when any section:

- cannot support its promised outcome questions;
- introduces jargon before meaning or changes terms mid-mechanism;
- gives an example that does not expose the mechanism;
- uses a decorative, misleading or load-bearing-but-incomplete analogy;
- has a dominant generic, institutional, mocking or joyless register;
- contains humour or Rewind Era material that can be deleted without losing
  teaching, recognition or useful recall;
- leaves the reader with recognition or vocabulary but no explanation or use;
- fails to reconnect to the cumulative system map;
- contains an unbound, stale or overgeneralized material claim; or
- repeats any registered known defect.

## Candidate evidence before Ali review

The Introduction-through-Chapter-3 candidate is produced as one continuous
journey but checked section by section. Before it reaches Ali it requires:

- exact-prose producer self-review;
- technical claim-to-source mapping;
- LAiDIES voice and teaching-pattern review of the dominant prose, not samples;
- section outcome-question coverage;
- blank-page cumulative-system reconstruction;
- explain-back in different words;
- use on an unfamiliar practical case;
- analogy and example learning-gain evidence where either is used; and
- a truthful list of any missing independent learner or rendered evidence.

Mechanical checks can reject known defects and objective omissions. They
cannot prove that prose is funny, delightful or beautifully explained. Those
qualities require exact-artifact reading and learner evidence; a self-authored
`PASS` is never sufficient.
