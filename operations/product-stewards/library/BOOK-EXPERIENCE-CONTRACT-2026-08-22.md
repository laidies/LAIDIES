# LIBRAiRY book experience contract

**Status:** ALI-CONFIRMED EXPERIENCE / IMPLEMENTATION AND RELEASE HOLD

**Date:** 2026-08-22

**Applies to:** every Library book; AI Fundamentals 101 is the first complete
representative implementation

**Provenance:** Ali's seven-part guided Library walkthrough, retained in
`operations/ALI-OPERATING-CONTEXT-AND-PAGE-WALKTHROUGH.md`

## Reader outcome

Library books make a reader more capable with AI. They replace disconnected,
over-technical definitions and surface instructions with a current, accurate,
connected mental model that lets a non-technical reader:

- explain how the central mechanism works and where it fits;
- distinguish what happens before, behind, during and after her interaction;
- understand what she controls and how it changes the result;
- diagnose common problems from cause rather than guesswork;
- use tools more effectively;
- test clickbait, reporting and workplace claims;
- participate in informed decisions about benefits, risks and trade-offs; and
- experience repeated earned “Oh—that is why” moments.

AI Fundamentals owns the connected foundational system map. It is not a
glossary. Later books deepen a distinct workflow, work type or product and link
back to that foundation rather than duplicating or fragmenting it.

## Content and currentness

- Every material claim binds current trusted evidence, owner and recheck
  trigger. Newly popular terminology is evaluated for meaning, maturity and
  placement before inclusion.
- Product/model/version-specific guidance states its scope. When practice
  changes, explain what changed, why and where the successor applies.
- Societal debates are mapped from mechanisms and evidence without forced
  doomsaying, uncritical enthusiasm or an invented LAiDIES position. Route
  readers to separately sourced big-picture coverage when useful.
- Every book shows its last substantive review and one governed change history.
  Returning readers can see what changed since their last visit and jump to the
  exact added, corrected or changed section. The cross-town “what's new” view
  may consume the same record.
- Add recurring concept-bound “You may be seeing this because…” components
  that connect a common problem to the mechanism, an applicable check/response
  and an uncertainty boundary. Generic troubleshooting tips fail.

## Reading and navigation

- Preserve the coherence of a book without simulated-page-turn friction.
- Use continuous reading with an always-recoverable descriptive contents view,
  exact chapter/concept anchors, **continue where I left off** and **start
  anywhere**.
- Every chapter begins with what it covers and what the reader should understand.
- Use a clear heading hierarchy, digestible prose, recurring summaries and
  conversational language.
- An admitted whole book and every stable section have public share links.
  Shared URLs contain no private progress, note, highlight, sticker or Closet
  information.

## Visual teaching and recurring components

Visual teaching is meaning-bearing content. Choose the form that best exposes
the assigned mechanism or relationship: process/flow, diagram, map, comparison,
worked example, representational image or another faithful textbook aid. An
image added merely to interrupt prose fails.

The overall reader should feel like a polished 1990s textbook reimagined inside
the LAiDIES world—not a generic web article and not an overwhelming scrapbook.
Use the shared pop-art visual vocabulary selectively: bursts, speech/word
bubbles, stickers, arrows, colour fields, patterns and small attention devices
may establish hierarchy, signal a recurring component or reinforce a teaching
moment. They must not compete with the mechanism, create a wall of colour or
make the reader hunt for the primary explanation. Instructional text, labels
and controls remain deterministic live content; reference-image wording is
inspiration for form, not copy to reproduce or bake into artwork.

The book grammar should visibly belong to the same LAiDIES system used across
the site. Shared motifs retain recognizable roles while each destination adapts
their density and layout to its job. Cohesion does not require every page to
look identical or every paragraph to receive a pop-art treatment.

Use one accessible, colour-coded but not colour-dependent grammar for recurring
jobs such as:

- why this matters;
- worked example;
- anticipated reader question;
- concept connection;
- troubleshooting;
- limitation or debate;
- summary; and
- check your understanding.

End each chapter with key concepts/plain definitions and answer-reveal practice.
Practice must test the chapter's actual mechanism, not recognition of wording.

## LAiDIES and Rewind Era layer

Humour, analogy and Rewind references are optional teaching devices, never a
branding quota. Admit one only when it maps faithfully to the mechanism,
improves understanding or recall, fits naturally and preserves the explanation.
The producer must be allowed to return `NONE`.

Ali's positive exemplar is the *Scream* “the call is coming from inside the
house” connection for synthetic data: the mapping fit, made the passage funny
and created a durable cue. Forced quotes, decorative name-drops, tenuous
comparisons and checklist references fail. The reusable producer method starts
with the mechanism/teaching job, draws from governed references, states the
mapping and limit and rejects distortion before Ali review.

## Progress, saves, notes and identity

- Reading/chapter progress is tracked. Signed-in residents receive durable
  resume continuity. Anonymous reading remains available; local state may not
  impersonate account or cross-device persistence.
- Signed-in residents can privately highlight and annotate. Notes/highlights
  require explicit storage, export, deletion, migration and account-revocation
  behavior and never enter public search, analytics or shared links.
- A reader can save a whole book or exact section with one of her active ten
  Puffy stickers. My Closet reopens the exact scope without retracing the
  Library route.
- Residents may assign their own organizational meaning to stickers, but every
  save also carries a readable title, scope, destination and context.
- My Closet is the intended resident view of supported progress, collections,
  rewards and deliberate saves across SUNNYVAiLE. Current device-local behavior
  does not prove that intended account system.

## Completion and rewards

Progress is not inferred from scrolling. A chapter completion event requires a
meaningful, accessible evidence action appropriate to the material—for example
a mechanism-level knowledge check, explain-back or application—not a page view,
timer or hidden attention claim.

- A chapter can earn a smaller reward.
- Whole-book completion can earn a distinct, more substantial reward.
- Candidate rewards include Butterfly Clips plus stickers, badges or another
  collectible displayed in the Closet. Exact objects and payouts require the
  shared economy balancing and lifecycle contract.
- Completion, economic transactions and collectibles use signed-in,
  deduplicated, version-bound records. Failed or repeated submissions cannot
  mint duplicate value.
- A completion collectible recognizes meaningful engagement; it is not a
  professional credential or claim of expertise.
- Public recognition exists, but residents explicitly choose which eligible
  progress/collectibles appear on a public Card/profile. Private learning
  behavior is not exposed by default.
- A material book update identifies the completed edition and invites the
  reader to review changed sections without erasing previously earned history
  or creating a punitive streak.

The knowledge evidence must remain proportional and book-like. It may borrow a
good check mechanic without turning the Library into SUNNYVAiLE High or a chain
of mandatory quizzes.

## Audio and portable formats

Audiobook/listening versions are intended so readers can learn while commuting
or otherwise away from the screen. Text/audio edition identity, navigation,
updates, accessibility and progress synchronization require a production
contract before release.

Printing, downloading and offline reading remain **UNDECIDED**. A future
proposal must balance access and accessibility with update propagation,
canonical attribution, rights and the risk that stale copies become detached
from corrections. No implementation may promise perfect theft prevention.

## Reader-request and demand loop

Shelf discovery offers an explicit “I don't see my topic” request, while Miss
Jeeves handles both lookup and no/weak-coverage recovery. Passive aggregate
signals contain controlled topics and outcomes only—not raw questions. Any
free-text topic request is a deliberate, disclosed submission through the
separate governed intake.

Demand analysis must distinguish content people cannot find from content that
does not exist, and recommend the best format by learning job. A repeated
question may call for better routing, a Homepage or NewsStand feature, a
Library book/section, an Episode, class or practice—not automatic book
production. Popularity is evidence, not sole editorial priority or permission
to publish.

## Acceptance and non-goals

The representative AI Fundamentals implementation must prove, on desktop,
mobile, keyboard and native assistive technology:

1. direct concept lookup and coherent chapter reading;
2. visible mechanism understanding rather than definition recall;
3. purposeful visual teaching at real reader size;
4. chapter practice with answer reveal and meaningful completion evidence;
5. resume, exact save/reopen, share and private annotation boundaries;
6. change-summary and exact changed-section return;
7. chapter and whole-book reward lifecycle without duplicate earning;
8. resident-controlled public recognition; and
9. current exact source, render, deployment and public-origin proof.

Non-goals are simulated page turning, decorative textbook visuals, an
overwhelming colour/callout mishmash, glossary-only teaching, generic tips,
forced pop-culture density, passive-scroll rewards, mandatory public reading
history, false account sync, credential claims and unversioned downloadable
copies.
