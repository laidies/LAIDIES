**Status:** SPECIFIED — PRIMARY-SOURCE RESEARCH BUILDING
**Owner:** LIBRAiRY product steward with Learning System & Concepts

## 1. Trigger and intended result

- **Working title:** Move 37: The Breakthroughs That Changed AI
- **Trigger:** Ali idea
- **Trigger source and date:** Direct conversation, 2026-08-24
- **Intended learner:** A capable adult who uses or encounters AI but has never
  been given a connected history of the moments that changed what the field
  believed was possible.
- **Problem or question she brings:** Which AI breakthroughs actually mattered,
  why were AlphaGo and AlphaFold extraordinary, and what did researchers learn
  from moments such as Move 37?
- **Why LAiDIES is teaching this:** Headlines flatten decades of accumulated
  work into sudden magic. A sourced causal history lets the reader recognize a
  real breakthrough, resist hype and understand how methods, data, compute,
  evaluation and human institutions combine.
- **Why it is important now:** New products are described as breakthroughs
  weekly. The reader needs a durable test for the claim, not a longer news feed.
- **Where this appears in her life:** AI news, workplace strategy, public
  funding and regulation, scientific claims, investment claims and decisions
  about what technology deserves trust or attention.
- **Observable result:** She can explain at least three landmark moments in
  cause-and-effect terms, distinguish a research breakthrough from adoption or
  marketing, state what each moment did not prove and apply the book's test to
  a new claim.
- **Concrete next use:** When a company announces a “historic breakthrough,”
  she can ask what previously failed, what changed technically, how the result
  was independently checked and what capability or practice changed afterward.

## 2. Canonical concept ownership

- **Canonical concepts:** AI research history; learning systems; evaluation;

  science; breakthrough evidence.
- **Existing concept-map rows:** `AI history and named contributors`; `Pivotal
  AI breakthroughs and what they changed`; `Model, product, app, API and tool
  layers`.
- **Canonical LIBRAiRY owner:** AI Fundamentals 101 remains the system/mechanism
  authority. This book owns the chronological breakthrough-and-consequence map.
- **Adjacent concepts kept distinct:** invention versus product launch;
  benchmark result versus general capability; novelty versus correctness;
  discovery versus deployment; model capability versus surrounding system.

  breakthrough alone; Move 37 proves machine consciousness; AlphaFold “solved
  biology” or replaced experiments; a benchmark win proves broad intelligence;
  progress has been smooth and inevitable.

  passages and Straight Answers' hard-answer-key distinction.
- **Next useful destination:** AI Fundamentals for mechanism depth; LUMINAiRY

- **Current evidence and freshness:** Original papers, institutional archives,
  Nobel material and official lab records are being mapped. Stable historical
  claims recheck on contradictory archival evidence; recent scientific and
  capability claims recheck on peer-reviewed replication or authoritative
