## Production sequence

1. Preserve the Introduction's three reasons, jokes, cadence and authorial
   flow without inserting concept teaching into it.
2. Open Chapter 1 with the real public-language problem before using any
   acronym, classification framework or technical definition.
3. Teach through one chronological and causal spine: artificial intelligence;
   rule-based expert systems; machine learning; predictive AI; deep learning;
   generative AI; multimodal AI; agentic AI; embodied AI; specialised and
   general-purpose AI; artificial general intelligence; artificial
   superintelligence; then synthesis.
4. Make every transition explain why the next idea became necessary. A heading
   may not expose internal curriculum language such as `job family`,
   `classification axis` or `this part of the system`.
5. Use an actual AI system as every core example. A human analogy is optional
   and must never be allowed to impersonate the AI mechanism.
6. Distinguish ordinary automation from rule-based AI. The representative
   rule-based example requires a specialist knowledge base and reasoning across
   linked rules; a single threshold or trigger does not qualify.
7. Write every full term before its acronym. The first appearances are
   `artificial general intelligence (AGI)` and `artificial superintelligence
   (ASI)`, never unexplained shorthand.
8. Define each technical word in ordinary language where it first becomes
   necessary. Do not defer a term that the current explanation depends on.
9. After the chronological lesson, show true nesting separately from labels
   that describe different properties. Do not force all labels into one tree.
10. End with Recognise / Explain / Draw / Use checks that require reasoned
    answers and expose misconceptions rather than reward recall alone.

## Full-artifact review rule

The producer and independent reviewer read the complete continuous chapter from
the reader's first sentence to its handoff. They must explain why every section
follows the one before it. Sampled local clarity, accurate individual
definitions or a correct recap cannot compensate for a confusing route.

The known-bad R7 artifact must be rejected unaided for acronym order, trivial
automation misclassification, vague referents, framework-first organisation,
the video-clerk pseudo-example and broken continuous progression before R8 may
enter Ali's review queue.

## Visual boundary

R8 uses deterministic typography, tables and textbook boxes only. It contains
no CSS-drawn illustration or diagram. A future visual must be commissioned only
after the exact relationship claims pass semantic direction review, then pass
the separate visual-media contract and pixel review.
