# Miss Jeeves / Ms Geist identity correction

Status: PENCIL-FREE TEAL IDENTITY APPROVED — MASTHEAD INTEGRATION IN REVIEW

## Goal

Create one Miss Jeeves identity pilot that reads immediately as an original
SUNNYVAiLE character genuinely inspired by Twink Caplan's Ms Geist in the 1995
film *Clueless*, without copying the actor as a photoreal portrait.

## Acceptance

- Woman in her late forties, not elderly and not youthful/glamorous.
- Small, untidy dark-brown bob with loose wisps and modest volume—not dense salon curls.
- Narrow heart/oval face, blue-grey eyes, large round thin-wire glasses, fine brows,
  modest lips and an open, slightly frazzled, earnest expression.
- Unmistakably 1995 teacher wardrobe: oversized pointed white collar under a
  cherry-red textured sweater vest/cardigan. Optional pencil tucked in hair.
- Adult LAiDIES graphic-novel treatment; natural anatomy; no visible text.

## Rejected carry-over

Do not retain the candidate's voluminous glossy curls, heavy arched brows,
fashion makeup, sultry half-smile, ornate glasses chain, paisley blouse, dark
teal embroidered cardigan or gold book brooch. Together they read as a generic
stylised librarian and obscure the Ms Geist identity.
