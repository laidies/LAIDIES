## What the recent coverage adds

The recurring practical ideas are useful, but they are not missing mechanisms
in the live books:

- fit the AI to the task instead of using it everywhere;
- keep the human responsible for intent, judgment and acceptance;
- give the system sufficient context and explicit authority boundaries;
- evaluate the accepted result and total review cost, not just model price or
  apparent speed;
- improve the workflow around the model rather than chasing every model launch;
- use deliberate examples and demonstrations when teaching a model or a team;
- distinguish an agent, its harness and its workflow from the underlying model.

The editions supply some memorable labels — including the writing “effort
sandwich” and the “deputization audit” — but labels alone do not justify
duplicating an already-taught mechanism or reopening an admitted artifact.

## Book-by-book disposition

### AI Fundamentals 101 — no edit

The book already explains tokens, context, agents, harnesses, graph engineering,
data centres, limits, evaluation and the wider AI system. The recent editions
add current examples and opinions, not a missing causal link. Product-feature
roundups and dated statistics belong in a current service surface or NewsStand,
not in this evergreen foundation.

### Working with AI 101 — no edit

The book already teaches task-fit, human/manual/collaborative/delegated modes,
context, iteration, evaluation, delegation, authority and verification. The
“effort sandwich” and “deputization audit” are optional future editorial boxes,
not corrections. Adding them now would repeat existing teaching and invalidate
the exact reviewed artifact without creating a new reader capability.

### Straight Answers About AI — no edit

Recent jobs, economy, adoption and data-centre episodes do not establish a new
durable answer by themselves. The live book already separates evidence,
contested interpretation, unknowns and recheck triggers. Any changed conclusion
must begin with the named original research, not AIDB's synthesis.

### The AI Dictionary — no edit

No owner-book definition changed. New product names, feature lists and coined
workflow labels do not enter the Dictionary merely because they appeared in a
recent episode. Dictionary entries remain derived from terms actually taught in
the owner books and link back to those exact explanations.
