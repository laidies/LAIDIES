# Known-bad calibration: technical words pretending to explain technical words

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the broad field of building machine-based
systems that infer outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations or
decisions from inputs.

A **model** is the component that performs an inference.

This is deliberately rejected. It uses `infer` before meaning, defines
`model` through the still-unexplained word `inference`, supplies no event a
beginner can picture and gives no second example or wider-system connection.
