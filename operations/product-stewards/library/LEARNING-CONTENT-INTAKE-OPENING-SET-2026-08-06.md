## Reader and product job

The opening set serves a reader with a real AI task who needs a durable, scannable answer she can return to. It is not a course, lesson sequence or product walkthrough.

| Book | Governing reader question | Observable outcome | Scope boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concepts 101 | What happens between asking an AI for something and deciding whether I can use the result? | Locate the failed system layer and choose a matching repair. | Connected system model, not a provider catalogue or complete glossary. |
| Briefing 101 | What must I tell the system so the result has a usable job and shape? | Write and check a five-part brief. | Briefing procedure, not a guarantee of truth or a prompt-trick collection. |
| Setup 101 | Where should stable preferences, changing facts and one-job material live? | Sort ordinary context into standing instructions, optional memory, one project or nowhere. | Durable sorting model, not current menu instructions or upload authority. |
| Accounts 101 | May I put this material in this exact account? | Apply the five-step account/authority/current-rule/unknown-stop rule before pasting. | Decision aid, not legal advice or a substitute for employer policy. |

## Ecosystem relationship

- Concepts owns the causal model connecting product, model, context, tools, output and checking.
- Briefing owns the job/audience/format/tone/constraints procedure.
- Setup owns placement of working context.
- Accounts owns whether material may enter the selected account at all.
- Tool cards own volatile menus, plans and current product behaviour.
- Classes own guided practice and feedback; books remain reference surfaces.

## Evidence and risks

Primary or authoritative sources are bound in each source and claims record. Volatile provider behaviour is explicitly scoped and must be rechecked before public release. The highest-risk failure is admitting readable-looking prose without a connected mental model, worked case, limits, currentness contract and independent unfamiliar-reader result.

## Correction and maintenance

Each book exposes `hello@laidies.ai` with a book-specific correction subject and a privacy boundary. Any material correction invalidates the exact artifact hash and requires readmission.
