# AI Fundamentals 101 V4 human-impact source note

**Checked:** 2026-08-08
**Owner:** learning-system-concepts-director
**Use:** the Introduction's bounded explanation that AI risks can affect people
and deserve scrutiny proportionate to their consequence.

## Primary authority

National Institute of Standards and Technology, *AI Risk Management Framework
FAQs*: <https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/ai-risk-management-framework-faqs>

Checked source meaning: the AI RMF helps developers, users and evaluators
manage AI risks affecting individuals, organisations, society or the
environment. Its trustworthiness characteristics include fairness with harmful
bias managed. These characteristics apply across design, development,
deployment, use, testing and evaluation.

## Ruled claim

The Introduction may explain that an AI output used in a consequential context
can affect people, and that a higher-consequence use deserves stronger evidence
and less haste. It may name harmful bias or unreliable performance as risks to
investigate. It may not claim that a particular product, dataset, company or
sector has produced disparate impact without separate exact evidence.

## Freshness

Recheck before publication and if NIST replaces AI RMF 1.0 or materially changes
its public trustworthiness guidance.
