# Claude curriculum audit — AI Fundamentals 101 complete outline

**Reviewer:** Claude Sonnet, high effort, exact outline supplied with the
Learning source roster and institutional, Wharton and AIDB source maps
**Mode:** read-only independent curriculum challenge
**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Verdict:** `REVISE`; chapter order unchanged

## Blocking findings

1. The route omitted AGI and the distinction between present systems and a
   proposed general intelligence. A reader therefore lacked a way to place one
   of the most common public AI claims.
2. The route omitted the training/knowledge-cutoff bridge among training,
   current context and retrieval.

## Material findings

1. Chapter 2's active vocabulary load was too high for the first deep
   mechanism chapter. Separate concepts the reader must understand from terms
   she only needs to recognise.
2. MCP in Chapter 4 and schema in Chapter 5 were over-promoted as required
   beginner vocabulary. Teach the job first; retain either term only as a
   recognition-level example.
3. Chapter 2 named shortcuts in coverage but did not route the term before
   Chapter 6 reused it.
4. Chapter 3 omitted the system/developer-instruction versus user-prompt
   distinction.
5. Chapters 7–8 lacked the open, open-weight, source-available and closed
   access/control distinction.
6. Chapter 4's four-verb title risked reading like a feature catalogue rather
   than naming the unifying system relationship.
7. The total vocabulary load risked reproducing a sequential glossary even
   though the chapter jobs themselves were distinct.

## Optional findings

1. Make the Chapter 4–5 handoff distinguish a per-tool permission from a
   workflow-level human checkpoint.
2. Revisit Chapter 8's broad title only if the final landing remains unclear in
   prose.

## What the audit did not reject

- The Introduction → Chapters 1–8 → Concept Index order.
- The distinction between Chapter 4's single-turn capabilities and Chapter
  5's multi-step orchestration.
- The distinction between Chapter 6's result/failure job and Chapter 7's
  data/control job.
- The decision not to import calculus, code or technical search algorithms
  from advanced institutional courses.

## Required disposition

Resolve the two missing concepts and the vocabulary/placement findings before
the outline becomes the drafting authority. Do not add a ninth chapter or
reorder the current route.
