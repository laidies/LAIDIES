Ask AI for the structure of your update first. It does not need your client names, budget or project secrets to suggest useful headings.

Use a tool your employer permits, and try:

“Create a blank weekly project update for my boss. Include progress, decisions, risks, next steps and help needed. Keep it suitable for an update under 200 words. Use placeholders and questions for missing information; do not invent project facts.”

Then fill in the real details yourself inside your company’s approved document system. You have used AI to organize the update without sending it the information the update contains.

If you also want help polishing notes, first check that the specific information is allowed in that tool. Replacing a name with “Client A” is not a guarantee of anonymity: a distinctive project, date or combination of details may still identify someone. When you cannot tell whether the notes are safe to share, keep them out and work from a blank example.

Before sending the update, check every date, number, commitment and status against your records. A fluent draft does not know which deadline your team actually agreed to.
