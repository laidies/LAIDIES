## Job

Help a visitor who lacks the right vocabulary get a useful bounded
orientation, then route her to the exact admitted source or a truthful
alternative. Miss Jeeves is a reference-desk tool, not a generic chatbot and
not the source of record.

## Current system

- Four curated intent answers: work/jobs, choosing a tool, briefing/prompting
  and hallucinations.
- Lexical fallback over `content/site/site-index.json`.
- Controlled zero-result recovery.
- No backend, model call, raw-query logging or correction ledger.

Cycle 5 makes the catalogue status authoritative for curated book
destinations. Held links render as held labels, not buttons; an alternate real
route can remain.
