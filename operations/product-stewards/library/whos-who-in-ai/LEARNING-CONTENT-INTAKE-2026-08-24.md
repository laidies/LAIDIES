## 1. Trigger and intended result

- **Title:** Who’s Who in AI
- **Subtitle:** The companies, people and power behind the technology
- **Trigger:** Ali idea plus direct title correction
- **Trigger source and date:** Direct conversation, 2026-08-24
- **Intended learner:** A capable adult who recognizes names such as OpenAI,
  ChatGPT, NVIDIA or Google but cannot yet see which are companies, products,
  models, suppliers, partners or competitors.
- **Problem or question she brings:** Who are the main players in AI, what does
  each control, and how are they connected?
- **Why LAiDIES is teaching this:** Most AI coverage assumes an invisible org
  chart. Without the relationships, a reader can mistake a product for a
  company, a funding deal for ownership or a model launch for an independent
  scientific verdict.
- **Why it is important now:** Alliances, funding, compute supply, model access,
  regulation and leadership change quickly and materially affect which tools
  people can use and whose incentives shape them.
- **Where this appears in her life:** Tool choice, workplace procurement,
  privacy and policy decisions, AI news, investing, career planning and public
  debate.
- **Observable result:** She can place an unfamiliar AI name into the correct
  layer, explain one consequential dependency, distinguish ownership from a
  partnership and identify the current primary source needed before relying on
  a changing claim.
- **Concrete next use:** When an article says one company “owns,” “powers” or
  “partners with” another, she can trace the actual relationship and ask what
  changed for users, infrastructure or control.
