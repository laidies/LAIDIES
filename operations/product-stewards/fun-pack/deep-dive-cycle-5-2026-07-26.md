## Executive verdict

The incumbent page did not contain four episode packs. It contained four
episode labels that all opened the same four generic children. It called
FAiRY Godmother and Girl Talk “Available now,” linked Dream Phone despite its
launch hold, and relied on referrer/history or Bag routing rather than a
verified child-to-Fun-Pack return contract.

The correct P0 is a truthful, fail-closed shelf. Four published episode
contexts remain selectable, but zero children are admitted until their own
owners and the episode/return contracts pass. This is less visually busy and
materially more useful than pretending unresolved products are weekly extras.

## Journey and experience analysis

The new reader now sees the current episode, optional-product explanation and
exact empty state. A late reader can select any published archive; the pressed
state, title, exits and URL agree. A Bag arrival retains a Bag return that
updates when the episode changes. A missing/malformed/forged registry opens
nothing and provides Retry and episode escape.

The child-return contract is specified but currently admits no child:
`from=fun-pack`, exact `issue`, and encoded
`/games/fun-pack.html?issue=N`. A child must verify that route before its card
can render. History/referrer alone is not a durable return contract.

## Learning, delight and brand job

THE EXTRA CREDIT is not required revision. An admitted extra must either
rehearse a specific behaviour with feedback/consequence or provide honest
delight/ritual value. It must add a different modality or action from the
episode, Study Pack, Library, High or NewsStand. “A reading, a note, a call or
Girl Talk” is not enough without episode-specific purpose.

The strongest LAiDIES contribution here is curation and restraint: an empty
pink shelf that tells the truth is more on-brand than four attractive cards
that silently overrule product owners.

## Competition

| Candidate | Strength | Failure |
|---|---|---|
| Incumbent generic grid | colourful and immediately busy | fake per-episode variation; child-status overreach |
| Large episode toolkit | could gather every bonus object | duplicates the Bag and creates maintenance/filler pressure |
| Curated fail-closed shelf | exact context, child-owned admission, clean exits | temporarily empty while children remain held |

The red-team attack on the selected direction is that “zero extras” may
disappoint. That is real, but admitting unresolved activities would be a trust
failure. The next value move is to qualify one genuinely episode-specific
extra—not weaken admission.

## Accessibility, mobile and failure

The episode selector uses native buttons and `aria-pressed`, following the
[W3C Button Pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/button/).
Episode context is a live region; registry failure is an alert, consistent
with [WCAG status-message guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F103).
The exact candidate has no horizontal overflow at 320 CSS pixels, the target
described by [WCAG Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html).
Native Safari/VoiceOver/zoom evidence remains an independent hold.

## Persistence, rewards, safety and privacy

The only durable selection is the URL. There is no account, saved shelf,
streak, unlock, reward or cross-device claim. Child completion and state remain
child-owned. Future analytics must use aggregate episode/child IDs and bounded
failure codes only; never child inputs or private context.

## Upkeep, external capabilities and revenue

- Reconcile registry on every episode publication or child state change.
- Weekly: stale verdict/episode/return check.
- Monthly: archive link, mobile, keyboard and exact-artifact regression.
- Incident: set child owner disposition and admission to HOLD together.
- Consider strict JSON Schema validation with
  [Ajv strict mode](https://ajv.js.org/strict-mode.html) if several producers
  begin editing the registry. The present zero-dependency validator is adequate
  for this bounded candidate; installation is not authorized.
- Continue using Playwright network interception for hostile registry cases;
  [official Playwright documentation](https://playwright.dev/docs/network)
  supports tracking, modifying and handling fetch traffic.
- After real child outcomes exist, Plausible custom events can measure the
  small funnel. Its [official event documentation](https://plausible.io/docs/custom-event-goals)
  notes that link destinations may be captured automatically, so no sensitive
  child URL or content may be sent.
- Revenue remains deferred. Never paywall core learning, sell admission, let a
  sponsor influence curation, or use streak pressure. A future paid original
  keepsake/printable is only discussable after the free optional product has
  complete value, rights, accessibility, fulfilment and owner approval.
