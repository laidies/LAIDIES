# Trailer Study Pack — learn how to use the town

## Learner problem

The Trailer introduces SUNNYVAiLE as a town, but a new visitor still needs a
small take-home experience that turns the world into usable navigation:
where to go, what each place helps her do and how to complete one first visit
without learning the entire site at once.

## Pack promise

**WELCOME TO SUNNYVAiLE — YOUR FIRST-DAY PACK**

Learn the town by using it once.

## Three components

### 1. Town Orientation Cheat Sheet

A one-page visual map of the essential first-visit destinations:

- Screening Room — watch the episode;
- Blend & Snap — open the Study Pack;
- SUNNYVAiLE High — take the Pop Quiz;
- LIBRAiRY — find sources and further learning;
- NewsStand — understand what changed;
- Closet / MAiKEOVER — keep and personalize owned items;
- Delta LAi Nu — enter community rooms when available;
- KSVL — hear the episode’s music and audio.

Each place gets one sentence answering **“Go here when…”**. The sheet includes
the checked and updated dates because routes and availability can change.

### 2. First Day in SUNNYVAiLE Try-On

The learner chooses one goal:

- learn one thing;
- try one thing; or
- keep one thing.

The Try-On builds a three-stop route from real current destinations, opens each
stop, records what happened and ends with a small First Day receipt. It does
not claim town mastery or create an account reward by implication.

### 3. Town Place Card Pack

One card per essential destination:

- front: exact building/place artwork and name;
- back: what it is, when to use it and the direct route.

These are orientation cards, not episode concept cards. They use the same
collectible anatomy only because the memory job is similar.
