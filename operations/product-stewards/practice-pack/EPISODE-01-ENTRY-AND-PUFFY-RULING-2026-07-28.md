   decoration, then completes the account save and confirms the saved item.

   user’s task, pasted ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini responses, ratings, comparison
   notes, chosen answer/final edit, current progress and Puffy placements.

    Try-On content is private to the owner by default, is not used as analytics
    content, and can be edited or deleted by the owner.
