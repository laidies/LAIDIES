# Study Pack sub-champion charter

**Status:** EPISODE 01 COMPLETE-CONTENT REVIEW CANDIDATE BUILT LOCALLY;
BLEND & SNAP INTEGRATION AND PUFFY PERSONALIZATION NOT BUILT  
**Product ID:** `practice-pack`  
**Parent:** Blend & Snap

## Product promise

The Study Pack is the episode’s take-home set at Blend & Snap. A visitor orders
it at the café, opens one episode-specific pack cover, understands what each
piece is for and chooses the useful next move without mistaking an open, save
or collection mark for learning completion.

Episode 01 contains exactly:

1. one one-page Cheat Sheet;
2. one Three Tabs, One Task Try-On; and
3. one episode-sized Trading Card Pack containing only concepts the episode
   genuinely teaches; card count follows the lesson and is never a quota.

The Pop Quiz is a separate handoff to SUNNYVAiLE High. There is no separate
Episode 01 Study Sheet.

## Owned scope

- the episode-specific pack cover/contents route;
- admitted component identity, job, status and handbacks;
- shared Puffy Sticker Drawer behavior across the three pack items;
- the versioned decorated-artifact handoff to My Closet; and
- responsive, printable and accessible pack-level continuity.

The component owners retain their own content, interaction and release gates.
Blend & Snap owns café ordering. MAiKEOVER/Closet owns the Closet consumer.
Platform/Identity owns any verified account or cross-device persistence.

## Boundaries

- A pack cover coordinates; it does not duplicate the component content.
- Stickers personalize an artifact; they do not prove completion, mastery,
  reward, ownership or account sync.
- The Quiz remains outside the pack.
- A held or missing item is labelled honestly and is not a fake link.
- `Save to My Closet` explicitly preserves the Try-On’s task, pasted answers,
  ratings, notes, chosen/final result, progress and stickers so the owner can
  resume the useful work. It is private by default and never published or
  reused as analytics content by implication.
- Final visual production remains subject to the Study Pack admission gate and
  current Brand authority.

## Completion contract

Episode 01 is complete only when:

- Blend & Snap ORDER opens the correct Episode 01 pack cover;
- the three component jobs are understood within five seconds;
- each admitted component opens and returns coherently;
- Puffy stickers can be placed without obscuring required content;
- the decorated result can be saved, reopened and deleted from My Closet;
- My Closet save is bound to the verified account and restores across devices;
- signed-out decoration survives the sign-in handoff without becoming a false
  Closet save;
- storage denial, corrupt state and unavailable items fail clearly;
- keyboard, touch, reduced motion, 320/390/1440 and Letter/A4 paths pass; and
- owner, independent and public-origin gates pass separately.
