# Weekly Study Pack standard

**Status:** CANDIDATE STANDARD — EPISODE 01 OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED  
**Date:** 2026-07-28  
**Owner:** Study Pack, reporting to Blend & Snap

## Decision sequence

1. Build and review one complete Episode 01 Study Pack.
2. Correct it until Ali approves the experience and usefulness.
3. Freeze the reusable chassis, delivery checklist and admission gates.
4. Build the Trailer town-orientation pack.
5. Apply the admitted standard to Episodes 02–04.
6. Add the pack to the recurring weekly episode delivery package.

No later episode inherits approval from a shell, specification or similar
colour treatment. Its actual lesson, evidence, activity and cards must pass.

## Required weekly package

Every episode ships with exactly three default Study Pack jobs:

1. **One-page Cheat Sheet**
   - the lesson, key concepts, scoped data, memory phrases and first move;
   - responsive web treatment;
   - downloadable one-page US Letter and A4 PDFs;
   - current source links, checked date, updated date and plain-language reason.
2. **One Try-On**
   - one useful action the learner completes;
   - real controls and a useful saved/downloaded result;
   - truthful tool/model/version language when relevant.
3. **One Trading or Concept Card Pack**
   - the terms, people or concepts the learner should remember;
   - real front/back interaction;
   - printable/downloadable treatment when card geometry is admitted.

The Pop Quiz remains a separate SUNNYVAiLE High handoff. A fourth pack item is
allowed only when it has a distinct lesson-backed learner job.

## Shared experience rules

- The Study Pack is discovered and opened at Blend & Snap.
- Its pack page shows every included item and explains each item’s job.
- Every item opens for real and preserves a clear path back to the pack.
- The visual chassis is shared; episode artwork, palette, teaching and examples
  change with the episode.
- Puffy stickers are never pre-placed. The learner chooses from her real Closet
  inventory; account-backed save remains a separate completion gate.
- Nothing is labelled ready, current, printable, downloadable, saved or added
  to the Closet without the corresponding working result.

## Weekly delivery manifest

The episode delivery packet must include:

- canonical episode title and title-card asset;
- Cheat Sheet HTML, Letter PDF, A4 PDF and rendered print proofs;
- Try-On route, fixture/version receipts and completion-result proof;
- card manifest, front/back assets, interaction route and print proof;
- pack-cover entry with component job/status/route;
- source map with publication dates, access date and freshness triggers;
- desktop, 390-pixel and print QA;
- owner decision, independent review and exact release receipt.
