## 1. Stable promise and successful result

- SUNNYVAiLE High is LAiDIES' applied-learning building: a learner sees a real
  task demonstrated, practises judgment, inspects feedback, retries, and leaves
  by a useful next route. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Chick Flicks creates the need and memorable story; the LIBRAiRY owns durable
  reference; Classes owns guided performance and transfer; the High building
  owns their legible spatial relationship and the learner's arrival, result,
  resume and handback. `LOCKED LEDGER`
- A visit, preview, play click, quiz attempt, score, grade, superlative,
  sticker or Clip is not evidence of mastery. `LOCKED LEDGER`
- The successful building result is not “all rooms were visited.” It is that
  the learner can identify the right learning job, complete or honestly
  encounter its present state, understand the result and choose the next
  useful action without being misled about approval, persistence, reward or
  fulfilment. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- The High owns the complete building experience but does not replace the
  Classes owner, quiz content/assessment review, Book Fair catalogue and
  fulfilment owner, Library owner or shared Platform authority. `LOCKED LEDGER`

## 3. Place metaphor, feeling and ritual

- The High should feel like an inhabited late-1990s schoolhouse where the
  environment is the interface, not a generic learning dashboard wearing
  school decorations. `ALI CONFIRMED`
- The governing spatial model is the approved LIBRAiRY principle: separate,
  obviously operable objects in a room open the real experience; visitors do
  not hunt invisible hotspots on an inert photograph. `ALI CONFIRMED`
- The building verb is “sit a class and take the quiz.” Results, references,
  rewards and next-season material support that verb rather than compete with
  it. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- A corridor/room grammar is the leading recovered direction because a school
  naturally connects the AV classroom, Room 101, Registrar, Yearbook office
  and gym; it must still compete through the building visual-admission process
  before becoming the selected design. `INFERENCE`
- The Wednesday Pop Quiz is the recurring ritual. Its tone is encouraging and
  diagnostic: explanation and retry matter more than score, and no low result
  creates shame or public rank. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Nostalgia is useful only when it improves orientation, memory or delight.
  School records, grades and superlatives remain playful framing, never
  credentials or judgments of capability. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

## 7. Required journeys and transitions

1. First visit → understand the High's job → select a current preview, quiz or
   exact 101 handoff → reach that destination or an honest recovery state.
   `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
2. First quiz → answer all items → submit once → inspect explanation/result →
   follow review route → retry or continue. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
3. Leave → return without Card → resume valid device-local attempt/result
   without replaying cold onboarding or implying identity. `LOCKED LEDGER`
4. Visitor → create a device-local Card → return on the same device → see only
   authorized personalization and supported Closet continuity. `LOCKED LEDGER`
5. Device-local Card → verified account claim, when Platform supports it →
   explicit merge/replace/conflict result → second-device read-after-write.
   `LOCKED LEDGER`
6. Admitted completion → Platform grant → Wallet/Closet projection → Book Fair
   reserve → real fulfilment → committed spend → Closet entitlement/history.
   Any timeout or delivery failure releases/refunds value. `LOCKED LEDGER`
7. Update, correction, revoke/delete or refund → High, Book Fair and Closet
   recompute or remove the affected state and explain what changed. `LOCKED LEDGER`
8. Empty, malformed, future, expired, duplicate, storage-denied, offline,
   insufficient-balance, account-conflict and provider-failure states must
   recover without a success-shaped result. `LOCKED LEDGER`
