## Product promise

SUNNYVAiLE High is where a reader watches practical AI work being done, practises it, checks her understanding, and sees an honest record of that work. It complements Chick Flicks (the story/episode) and the LIBRAiRY (reference) without duplicating either. The High must make real learning more visible—not mistake clicks, visits, or local scores for durable mastery.

## Non-negotiables

1. Every class/quiz teaches or tests a named, accurate learning objective and provides explanation—not merely a score.
2. Content follows the LAiDIES editorial/currency standard; changing AI claims use current primary-source evidence and disclose uncertainty.
3. “Report Card,” superlative, sticker, clip and Book Fair language states whether it is device-local, account-synced, pending, disabled or fulfilled.
4. A visit, view, click or raw score is not automatically a mastery, reward or loyalty event.
5. Classes, quizzes, Book Fair and 101 shelf are distinct learning jobs with clear routes and no dead/unpublished inventory.
6. Core learning is never paywalled; revenue follows proven value, fulfilment, equity and accessibility—not the other way around.
