## 1. Trigger and intended result

- **Working title:** Get the Best Out of Your AI Tool — the LAiDIES
  Masterclass
- **Naming authority:** The masterclass is made of **lessons**, not chapters
  (Ali correction, 2026-07-27).
- **Trigger:** Ali idea / explicit Classes priority
- **Trigger source and date:** Ali, 2026-07-27: “why don't we try the
  masterclass first”
- **Intended learner:** A smart adult woman who already uses a conversational
  AI tool but lets the conversation carry the brief, decisions, current
  version and finish line.
- **Problem or question she brings:** “Why can I not just keep messaging it
  like a friend, and when do I stay, steer, stop, start a new chat or create a
  Project?”
- **Observable result she should be able to produce, explain or judge:** Bring
  one safe ongoing job; turn conversational discovery into a current working
  brief; choose Chat or longer-running Work deliberately; intervene in active
  work appropriately; separate a session from a Project; checkpoint the
  durable artifact and handoff.
- **Why this matters now:** Current products increasingly add cross-chat
  memory, scoped Projects, branching, artifacts and longer-running work modes.
  Older “one chat has all the context / a new chat starts from zero” advice is
  no longer a reliable universal model.

## 3. Existing-content reconciliation

- **Episode/canon:** Episode 2 teaches why a complete, specific assignment
  changes the result. The masterclass must not reteach prompting. It teaches
  how a changing conversation becomes and remains the governing assignment
  across work stages.
- **Concepts 101 / LIBRAiRY:** Concepts and Setup own durable definitions and
  lookup. Briefing 101 owns reusable brief anatomy. The class demonstrates
  operating the workflow and leaves the learner with a completed brief.
- **Class/register rows:** Merge or route to existing Basics rows for current
  context, saved history versus current context, memory, standing
  instructions, permissions, files, connectors and Projects. Expand
  `basics-projects` into the Project lesson rather than adding a duplicate.
- **Tool:** FAiRY may improve an individual prompt; it does not organize the
  work lifecycle or decide session/project boundaries.
- **Game/practice:** Dream Phone practises verification judgment, not workflow
  control. No new game is warranted.
- **NewsStand application:** None required for the pilot. A future dated
  product-change story may link to the durable class but must not become its
  source of truth.

**Decision:** **CREATE** one coherent masterclass pathway; **MERGE** existing
Basics mechanisms into its lessons; **CREATE** Lesson 4, *From Chatting to
Working*, as the first production pilot; **EXTEND** `basics-projects` for
Lesson 5 rather than duplicating it.

**Why the existing item cannot already perform this exact job:** No current
episode, LIBRAiRY passage, class row, tool, game or NewsStand story asks the
learner to operate one evolving task across discovery, briefing, bounded
execution, live intervention, independent review, checkpoint and handoff.

## 5. Class admission questions

- **What must be seen?** The same evolving job in a natural chat and in a
  current brief; the early interpretation persisting under scattered
  corrections; bounded execution from the brief; a checkpoint; four
  intervention choices; stay/new chat/clean review/Project decisions.
- **What does the learner do?** Complete her own one-page brief, classify six
  workflow scenarios and produce a checkpoint/handoff for a new session.
- **What controlled comparison makes the mechanism visible?** Same tool, same
  task and same facts. Only the organization changes: scattered corrections
  versus one current brief. Use a pre-authored fictional sandbox, not a
  theatrical uncontrolled failure.
- **What transfer proves the skill survives a new situation?** The learner
  applies the decision rules to six unrelated work scenarios and explains the
  governing boundary in each.
- **Which volatile details live in a reshootable appendix?** Chat/Work labels,
  Projects, branching, memory behavior, active-run controls, plan eligibility
  and exact web interface positions.
- **Why is a paragraph insufficient?** The skill depends on timing, state
  change, side-by-side comparison, live intervention judgment and producing a
  usable artifact.
