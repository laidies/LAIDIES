## Outcome

- **Product:** SUNNYVAiLE High (`sunnyvaile-high`)
- **Complete scope:** `/sunnyvaile-high.html`; High-owned room navigation and
  status surfaces; class discovery and `/learn/class.html?c=<slug>` handoff;
  quiz entry and `/learn/quiz.html` result/resume handoff; Report Card;
  Yearbook; LIBRAiRY 101 pointer; Book Fair doorway and `/bookfair.html`
  relationship; High→Book Fair→`/laidies-card.html` result/reward/Closet round
  trip; four visitor scopes; failure, accessibility, analytics and release
  proof.
- **User problem:** the building has several bounded local mechanics but no
  provenance-backed complete experience or cross-page contract. A learner can
  encounter many school features without one clear learning loop, while
  device-local scores and Clip code can be mistaken for durable records,
  currency, fulfilment or cross-device ownership.
- **Intended result:** the schoolhouse itself makes the learning path legible:
  choose demonstration/practice/reference; complete or honestly encounter its
  state; inspect explanation/result; retry or continue; and, only when
  authoritative systems exist, see reward/Book Fair/Closet propagation at the
  exact proved scope.
- **Evidence:** `EXPERIENCE-BRIEF.md`, `FUNCTIONALITY-MAP.md`, current
  `OPERATING-SPEC.md`, Repair 2 independent rejudge (90/100 bounded mechanism
  pass), current source inventory, Platform economic-ledger packet, and
  prevention rules BTB-088, BTB-133 through BTB-137.
- **Explicit non-goals:** authoring class scripts, quiz questions or learning
  claims; changing the shared economy/identity schema within the High lane;
  changing Closet code without its owner; selecting prices/stock; installing a
  service; publishing/deploying; or presenting unreviewed visuals as choices.

## Proposed direction

Run one building-experience championship around the recovered invariant
**“the schoolhouse is the learning interface.”** The incumbent must compete
against two materially distinct room/object approaches while holding fixed:

1. Classes remain a separate owner and `/learn/class.html` remains the class
   experience rather than being duplicated on the landing page.
2. Pop Quiz is the primary practice action; explanation/retry outrank score.
3. The 101 shelf remains in the LIBRAiRY with an exact learning-payoff handback.
4. Report Card/Yearbook are playful results at their exact persistence scope.
5. Book Fair/Clips/Closet remain a complete Platform build obligation; disabled
   stock is temporary visitor protection, not completion.
6. First-time, returning-no-Card, device-local Card and account-backed resident
   scenes are designed and judged independently.
7. Every visible room/object has a plain-language label and accessible
   equivalent; no hotspot hunting.

No external plugin or vendor is required for dossier recovery. Art generation,
media production, fulfilment and account/economy services are separate
capability decisions after their bounded briefs and approvals.

## Release and rollback

- **Affected products:** Classes, Pop Quiz, LIBRAiRY, Blend & Snap Study Pack,
  Book Fair, Closet/Resident Card, Identity/Rewards, analytics and release.
- **Release authority:** Portfolio/release manager after affected owners and
  independent judges sign the exact candidate.
- **Candidate identity:** record commit, build report, runtime hashes and
  environment in the final evidence packet.
- **Temporary controls:** unavailable class/Fair states remain honest during
  construction but do not close the build obligation.
- **Rollback:** restore the last independently accepted High shell and disable
  only the failing promoted capability; do not erase ledger history. Platform
  reverses economic effects with release/refund/revocation events and retains
  auditability.
- **Public verification:** clean `/sunnyvaile-high`, explicit `.html` fallback
  where applicable, class/quiz/Fair/Closet routes, custom 404, four visitor
  scopes and one controlled economy journey.

## Measurement and learning

- **Baseline:** latest accepted local evidence is a 90/100 bounded class
  admission-mechanism pass with 37 planned rows and zero live/video/admitted
  classes; current Book Fair stock is unavailable; account/economy/Closet
  authority is unverified.
- **Product signals:** correct first action chosen; class/quiz/reference route
  reached; explanation reviewed; retry; return/resume success; unavailable
  recovery; Fair fulfilment/refund; Closet read-after-write.
- **Prohibited analytics:** answers, scores, names, emails, Card fields,
  prompts, inferred ability, private catalogue/order details or service tokens.
- **Success:** first-time visitors can explain the High's job and choose the
  intended action; returning visitors resume without scope confusion; admitted
  learners demonstrate transfer; economy transactions converge without loss,
  duplication or fake fulfilment.
- **Failure:** navigation/visit rate rises while comprehension, explanation,
  transfer or propagation fails; any local state is interpreted as permanent
  identity, mastery or currency.
- **Review:** after the first representative admitted class/quiz and first real
  Book Fair vertical are publicly verified; then at source/admission freshness
  triggers and the regular product health review.
- **Learning scan:** reuse BTB-088 and BTB-133 through BTB-137. Initialization
  produced no new qualifying prevention rule beyond those canonical entries;
  no edit outside this dossier was made.
