## Stable promise and user outcome

The Bronze AiGE is SUNNYVAiLE's grown-up social-practice room: it helps a
visitor turn “we should talk about AI sometime” into a self-organised
conversation with her own people. `INFERENCE`

The useful result is a locally prepared invitation or calendar file, an
optional cocktail or equally complete spirit-free suggestion, a worthwhile
conversation cue, and an optional recorded track—not a booking, delivery,
venue, alcohol service, attendance record, account result or verified learning
outcome. `CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION OBSERVED`

The building must feel and operate like a distinct place in SUNNYVAiLE; its
room and meaningful objects are the interface rather than a decorative hero
followed by a generic card stack. `LOCKED LEDGER`

Not drinking is a complete path. Bronze may not turn alcohol choice,
participation, identity, accessibility or recovery into a requirement or
advantage. `INFERENCE`
