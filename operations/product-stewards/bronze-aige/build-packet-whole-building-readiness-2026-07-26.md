## Visitor-state execution contract

1. **First-time:** clean storage and no Card/account state; prove
   ten-second comprehension, all six stations, equal spirit-free access,
   result/next-step clarity and image/module/storage/audio failure recovery.
2. **Return without Card:** seed one valid drink receipt and valid coaster
   history; separately test corrupt, future, duplicate and storage-denied
   variants; prove useful continuity without identity.
3. **Device-local Card:** seed the canonical Card envelope separately; prove
   Bronze does not unlock, sync, merge or write Card state and remains useful
   if the Card disappears.
4. **Verified account:** use controlled accepted identity evidence only when
   the shared owner supplies it; until then prove the complete fallback and
   absence of account-backed Bronze claims.
5. **Transitions:** first → return; visitor → Card → same-device return; Card
   update/delete/revoke; sign-out; second tab/device; local/account conflict;
   and privacy/storage denial.

## Audio execution contract

1. KSVL owner binds `businesswomens` to the exact admitted source and states
   the current creator/rights authority in one non-contradictory record.
2. Content/audio owners reconcile canonical lyrics, as-recorded transcript,
   captions/alternative and track-to-Bronze job.
3. A human judge listens for intelligibility, mix/loudness, objectionable or
   contradictory content, correct title/artist and useful non-audio context.
4. Bronze and BWS controls use one shared ownership contract; explicit action,
   loading, playing, pause, blocked, error, ended, retry and competing-player
   states pass.
5. Safari, VoiceOver, reduced motion, 320/390/desktop and representative
   physical listening pass.
6. Source, exact artifact and authorized public origin serve identical
   admitted bytes/metadata; hold/removal propagates to Bronze, BWS and KSVL.

## Accessibility execution contract

1. Six objects and every dynamically enabled control have stable names,
   44px targets, visible focus and non-colour state.
2. One-open panel semantics, heading focus, Escape and initiating-control
   return work from pointer, keyboard and direct hash.
3. Every async result/failure uses persistent, concise programmatic status;
   errors do not disappear before recovery is understood.
4. 320px, 390px, 1280px, 200% zoom and text-spacing reflow have no core
   horizontal scroll, clipping or overlaid header/control obstruction.
5. Reduced motion removes flipbook/transition/smooth-scroll motion without
   removing information.
6. Images have correct alt/decorative treatment; image failure leaves the
   named station list and full result usable.
7. Audio has a complete non-audio explanation/continuation and never
   auto-starts.
8. Native Safari and VoiceOver run the first-time, returning, storage-failure,
   audio-failure and full-BWS journeys.
