## Corrected definition of a finished building

A building is not finished because its route loads, its local state is honest,
its service contract is documented or its deterministic tests pass. Those are
necessary technical foundations.

A launch-worthy building must also:

1. immediately feel like a distinct place in SUNNYVAiLE;
2. make its purpose and primary action understandable to a newcomer;
3. use the room and meaningful objects as the interface rather than defaulting
   to a generic web card grid;
4. have a complete visual composition, hierarchy, palette, typography,
   character/canon treatment and responsive behavior;
5. contain strong, accurate and worthwhile content or activity—not merely
   navigation or placeholder structure;
6. support the intended new, returning, mobile, desktop, accessible and
   failure journeys;
7. connect to real backend/state/media services wherever the product promise
   requires them;
8. provide an understandable result and next step;
9. pass independent product, brand, visual, UX/accessibility, content/learning
   and technical judgment; and
10. be shown to Ali only as a credible owner-review candidate, not as an
    unfinished page disguised as an approval request.

For consequential design work, the champion produces an incumbent and two
materially different challengers, runs red-team and blind scoring, and only
advances candidates meeting the 17/20 floors for product quality,
accuracy/trust and positive LAiDIES brand contribution.

## Active design wave

The active concurrency limit remains three:

1. **LIBRAiRY benchmark** — recover, compete, finish and document the reusable
   room/object interaction grammar.
2. **Visitor's Centre** — prove the grammar works for orientation and first
   arrival.
3. **Blend & Snap** — prove it works for a weekly, character-led café product
   rather than producing three visually similar rooms.

Each champion must complete:

`intent recovery → visual/content/UX research → incumbent + two challengers →
red team → blind scoring → build packet → specialist implementation →
independent judgment → exact local verification → owner-ready presentation`.

Backend, content, image, motion, audio, analytics and accessibility specialists
join when required by the building. They are not substitutes for the building
champion, and no specialist's isolated pass can declare the building finished.
