## Required building experience

The approved experience is a free 1999 video-store ritual:

`enter → hear Becky’s current truth → browse a real New Releases wall or aisle
→ pull a released tape → receive the rental-card response → open the exact
issue or illustrated Screening Room programme → continue/return`

The building metaphor must remain the interface. Generic cards, dashboards,
CSS substitute artwork, false commerce, invented current-week claims and
unadmitted motion films are prohibited.

## Acceptance rule

A future Chick Flicks visual candidate cannot pass because it looks nostalgic
or because its files exist. It must prove:

- the actual room/tape/rental objects remain the interface;
- Becky identity and every visible line have exact authority;
- each tape cover accurately represents the episode it opens;
- released, forthcoming, held and broken-cover states stay visibly distinct;
- no invented quotes, false weekly freshness, fake payment/account/history,
  or unadmitted motion-film promise;
- complete 1440/390/320, keyboard, focus, reduced-motion, broken-image and
  storage-failure behavior; and
- maker/judge separation with no inheritance from rejected building-wave work.
