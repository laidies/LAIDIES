# Screening Room product charter

**Status:** SPECIFIED — Cycle 6 dossier created; media and promotion remain HOLD  
**Parent:** Chick Flicks  
**Route:** `/watch.html?ep=<trailer|01|02|03|04>`

## Promise

The Screening Room is the comfortable listen mode for a LAiDIES episode. A
visitor hears the approved narration, sees an illustration or lesson card
that supports the words being spoken at that time, can read synchronized
captions below the picture, and can continue to the full issue.

It is currently an **illustrated listen-along**, not an approved motion-film
library. A review export, moving camera, animated loop or existing MP4 does
not change that status.

## Ownership

The champion owns programme selection, player truth, runtime asset admission,
new/returning/failure journeys, caption presentation, continuation, analytics
meaning and title-level release state. Episode Media Quality owns audiovisual
approval. Episode Experience owns learning/editorial purpose. Ali owns final
visual taste and public motion-film approval.

## Non-negotiables

1. The image shown must support the narration spoken at that clock position.
2. Character, costume, setting, era, palette and illustration system remain
   continuous unless an intentional, approved change serves the story.
3. Captions cover the as-recorded narration, remain synchronized, and never
   conceal the illustration.
4. Every runtime source is exact, local, packaged and title-bound.
5. Play is not completion, comprehension, reward or account progress.
6. Motion-film wording is title-specific and impossible until an independent
   SHA-bound audiovisual gate and Ali approval pass.

## Release gate

Each title passes separately. A title needs a complete normal-speed watch,
occurrence-level narration/visual matrix, identity/style/location review,
audio/caption coverage and sync, keyboard/mobile/Safari/VoiceOver evidence,
exact-artifact parity and owner approval. One passing title cannot promote the
others.
