## What five a day means

The daily minimum is five substantive **content units**, not five ideas copied
unchanged to six platforms. One strong source may become native variants, but
each published object must fit the channel's behaviour, crop, pacing, copy,
accessibility and audience job.

Every day covers these five jobs:

| Unit | Job | Default form | Primary outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Stop | Reach a person who does not know LAiDIES | short vertical hook, visual joke or sharp observation | qualified reach, hold/watch |
| 2. Teach | Make one AI idea useful on-platform | demonstration, founder explanation or mini lesson | completion, comment quality |
| 3. Save/send | Make something worth keeping or forwarding | carousel, checklist, script or comparison | saves and sends |
| 4. Join | Invite a real opinion or contribution | question, poll, response, remix or hot-seat prompt | replies and participation |
| 5. Visit/return | Lead to one exact working LAiDIES experience or ritual | feature invitation, episode/class/book hook or Wednesday callback | promised action and return |

The seven-job ladder in
`social/SOCIAL-CONTENT-LADDER-AND-SITE-PROMISE-GATE.md` remains canonical:
Stop, Wonder, Learn, Send/save, Join, Visit/use and Return. The five daily
units package those jobs at the newly directed volume.

## Plain-English visual explainer system — Ali direction 2026-08-16

LAiDIES should repeatedly turn one admitted AI concept into a connected
editorial package: a short visual explanation that earns attention, a
saveable carousel that makes the mechanism easy to revisit, and a deeper
article that supplies the evidence, limitations and useful next step. External
examples may inspire the communication mechanics; they are not factual,
editorial, visual or publication authority.

Every package must answer the same adult beginner's path:

1. **The real question:** lead with the recognisable work or daily-life reason
   the concept matters, not the technical term by itself.
2. **The plain definition:** explain the idea without requiring prior AI
   vocabulary.
3. **The visible mechanism:** show three to five connected cause-and-effect
   moves with a diagram, chart, object, comparison or worked example. The
   visual must carry part of the explanation, not merely decorate it.
4. **The concrete case:** demonstrate the concept in one credible adult,
   workplace or everyday situation.
5. **The boundary:** state what the concept is often confused with, what the
   explanation does not prove, and any material uncertainty or limitation.
6. **The useful landing:** leave the reader with one action, decision, check or
   better next question.

Channel derivatives have different jobs:

- **Reel/Short:** one question, one mechanism and one earned conclusion;
  readable captions, source labels for material numbers, and presenter footage
  only where human delivery improves understanding.
- **Carousel/document:** one concept per package; each page advances the
  explanation through definition, example, distinction and remember/check.
- **Article:** not a transcript or padded glossary. It supplies the full causal
  chain, dated sources, evidence-versus-inference boundaries, consequences,
  transfer example and useful action.
- **Newsletter/social text:** frame the reader's question and point to the
  exact admitted explanation; do not pretend the excerpt is the whole lesson.

The tone is plain English for capable adult beginners. Do not frame the reader
as a child, use “explain it to a five-year-old” positioning, interrupt an
unfinished lesson with a lead magnet, hide weak reasoning behind a familiar
analogy, or copy another creator's voice, artwork, claims or conversion tactic.
LAiDIES completes the useful explanation before inviting a save, reply, visit
or optional next step.

## Reach and “viral” standard

Virality is never promised. A reach candidate must have at least two of:
recognition, identity, surprise, utility, participation and performance. It
must also have a reason to build the right LAiDIES audience.

Agents produce three hook variants before selecting a candidate and record:

- what makes someone stop;
- the payoff delivered without a click;
- why someone would save, send, reply or remix;
- the LAiDIES connection;
- the exact source/canon evidence; and
- the primary metric and guardrail.

No engagement bait, fake controversy, borrowed authority, unsourced fact,
generic “AI girlies” language, trend-chasing without relevance or copied
viral format without a LAiDIES payoff.

## Daily production rhythm

1. **Sense:** pull episode, class, Library, building, NewsStand, audience and
   trend triggers.
2. **Select:** choose five units across the content portfolio and seven jobs.
3. **Source:** bind canon/facts, destination and rights.
4. **Compete:** draft three hooks and at least two creative treatments for
   high-reach units.
5. **Produce:** create master copy, artwork/video, captions, alt text,
   thumbnails/covers and native variants.
6. **Admit:** fact, canon, voice, Brand, accessibility, destination and
   publication gates.
7. **Publish:** only under confirmed account/publisher authority; record the
   exact object, URL and time.
8. **Learn:** inspect reach, hold/watch, saves/sends, replies, destination
   action, Resident Card conversion and return; state sample and uncertainty.

## Measurement and improvement loop

The Control Room audience tab reports:

- visitors and new visitors;
- verified new Resident Cards;
- new-to-returning mix;
- most and least visited pages;
- landing-to-meaningful-action and Card conversion;
- daily planned, ready and published content;
- platform reach, watch/dwell, saves, sends, replies and clicks;
- strongest/weakest content by comparable format; and
- ranked improvement opportunities with owner, experiment and measure.

Low traffic is uncertainty, not rejection. A page is not called “least
popular” until its exposure opportunity is known; a buried page and a rejected
page require different fixes.
