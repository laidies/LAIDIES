## Product promise

Turn approved LAiDIES product and editorial truth into useful invitations that
help the right people discover, use, share and return to LAiDIES, then learn
from privacy-safe evidence without pressuring relationships or outrunning the
product.

This Director owns campaigns, distribution and audience learning. It does not
make a product ready, publish NewsStand journalism, operate Post Office
delivery, rule Brand, deploy code, or grant itself publication authority.

## Owned scope

- audience segmentation, jobs and value propositions;
- channel roles, launch calendars and campaign sequencing;
- social/editorial packaging from admitted product sources;
- newsletter campaign orchestration with the Post Office delivery owner;
- referral and partnership strategy under the absolute non-MLM boundary;
- privacy-safe campaign taxonomy, UTM plans and measurement briefs;
- voice-of-customer synthesis from consented, minimized evidence;
- readiness-dependent launch, reopening and correction/removal communication;
- post-launch learning, return analysis and durable campaign records; and
- one default-deny admission decision for every proposed publication object.

  draft, repository asset, sent request or historical post as authority.

## Default-deny publication covenant

A campaign object is `HOLD` unless its admission record contains all applicable
evidence:

1. exact product owner verdict for the promised action;
2. Control Room combined-readiness admission;
3. exact deployed candidate and fresh public-origin journey proof;
4. Brand eligibility and Ali approval of the exact final visual;
5. Ali approval of exact final copy and, for a major launch, the website state;
6. NewsStand approval for journalistic claims or Post Office/provider proof
   for newsletter/delivery/referral claims;
7. privacy, rights, accessibility, correction and rollback checks;
8. channel/account/publisher authority;
9. exact destination, UTM, success/failure state and measurement plan; and
10. a post-publication verification owner and durable URL/timestamp record.

Missing, stale, contradictory or inferred evidence is a denial, not a reason
to improvise. Publication, scheduling, spend, service installation and
deployment require separate authority and are outside initialization.

## Core audiences and jobs

| Segment | Primary job | LAiDIES value proposition | Trust boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Busy AI-curious women at work | understand and try one useful AI move without beige training or shame | practical fluency through memorable Rewind Era teaching and clear next actions | no fear, hype, condescension or false readiness |
| Returning readers/residents | know what is new, continue something useful and keep a Wednesday habit | a coherent weekly ritual across episode, practice and town | return promises require current content and working continuation |

Allies may participate; segmentation must not erase the project’s purpose of
helping women participate in AI.
