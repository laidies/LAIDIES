# Social trend intelligence and engagement contract

**Status:** SPECIFIED — STANDING OWNER REQUIREMENT; FIRST EXPERIMENT NOT YET
PUBLISHED
**Owner:** Audience, Growth & Launch Director
**Applies to:** Instagram, LinkedIn and every future activated LAiDIES social
channel
**First evidence:** `evidence/social-trend-scan-2026-07-31.md`

## Purpose

The social owner must keep LAiDIES current with how people actually discover,
watch, save, send and discuss content on each platform. Research is not the
deliverable. Every useful finding must become a sourced LAiDIES-native content
hypothesis, an actual candidate, a measured result and a KEEP, ITERATE or STOP
decision.

The goal is stronger engagement with the **right** audience, not imitation of
whatever is briefly popular. A trend is useful only when it makes a LAiDIES
story, lesson, character, product or point of view easier to notice, understand,
remember, share or use.

## Production responsibility — Ali is the approver, not the content team

The Audience, Growth & Launch Director owns the complete production job. Ali
must not be asked to research formats, invent hooks, draft captions, write a
first comment, resize media, choose hashtags, create Stories, schedule posts,
plan replies or assemble analytics.

For each decision, the Director brings Ali one short, phone-readable approval
package containing:

- the recommended final visual or video in its real channel crop, plus alt text;
- the final channel-native caption, first comment and any Story/repost copy;
- the source, factual claim boundary and exact destination;
- why this treatment should work for this audience and channel;
- the planned publish/support sequence and what will be measured; and
- only when a real taste decision remains, no more than two materially
  different finished options with a recommendation.

Ali's job is to approve, reject or give one bounded correction. A recommendation
without the finished content is not decision-ready. Approval does not by itself
grant account access, scheduling or publication authority.

## Required cadence

- **Weekly platform scan:** check current official platform guidance, creator
  tools and observed format changes. Record source, date and what changed.
- **Before every campaign or major content package:** run a short, channel-
  specific scan so the work does not launch against stale format assumptions.
- **After every published object:** capture the available platform receipt and
  review performance at approximately 24 hours and seven days, or state why a
  different window is more meaningful.
- **Monthly format review:** identify repeatable winners, weak formats, audience
  fatigue and any change to the LAiDIES channel playbook.

Instagram's Professional Dashboard Best Practices is a mandatory input when
the signed-in account and human review are available because it contains
account-specific creation, engagement and reach guidance. Official Meta and
LinkedIn guidance, current native analytics and LAiDIES' own comparable
results are preferred over generic creator blogs or unsupported algorithm
claims.

## What the scan must inspect

1. native formats and new surfaces;
2. first-frame, first-second and first-slide hooks;
3. pacing, retention, looping, captions and silent viewing;
4. search/discovery language, captions, alt text, topics, location and hashtag
   behaviour where current evidence supports it;
5. save, send, repost, reply, poll, comment and conversation mechanics;
6. posting rhythm and timing evidenced by the account's own audience;
7. safe zones, crops, thumbnail/cover behaviour and accessibility;
8. originality, recommendation and community-guideline eligibility; and
9. current reach/"viral" mechanics, including what the platform actually
   supports versus creator folklore; and
10. native analytics definitions and any instrumentation gaps.

"Viral" is not a promise or a style. The owner may use current mechanics that
increase the chance of qualified discovery—strong first frames, retention,
originality, sends, saves, replies, reposts, series recognition and timely
participation—but must not claim an algorithm boost without primary evidence
or a repeatable LAiDIES result.

## LAiDIES translation gate

A social technique may enter production only when the owner can state:

- the LAiDIES source and the audience job;
- why the format makes that source stronger rather than merely trend-shaped;
- the useful or entertaining payoff delivered before any click;
- the Rewind Era, SUNNYVAiLE, episode, building, character or founder point of
  view that makes it recognizably LAiDIES;
- the channel-native reason someone might watch, save, send, reply or follow;
  and
- the exact metric and guardrail that will decide whether it worked.

Every candidate must remain adult, smart, useful and visually coherent. One
campaign uses one intentional visual system. Do not stack unrelated episode
art, generic gradient cards, novelty type and disconnected decoration in the
same object. Do not use generic AI-girlie language, condescension, empty
empowerment, engagement bait, fake urgency, unearned controversy, copied viral
templates or automated AI slop. The content must still be worth seeing after
the trend has passed.

Original LAiDIES work is the default. Reposts, remixes or third-party material
require a real editorial purpose, rights evidence and substantial original
context—not a border, caption or speed change.

## Build-and-learn sequence

1. **Research:** create the dated source receipt.
2. **Translate:** write the LAiDIES hypothesis, audience job, payoff, channel
   and expected behaviour.
3. **Compete:** create three materially different hooks and at least two
   treatments for a reach-led object.
4. **Build:** produce the actual native candidate, crop, caption, alt text,
   cover/thumbnail, first comment, Story/support objects, reply prompts,
   moderation notes, timing and destination treatment. A recommendation
   without a built candidate is incomplete work.
5. **Gate:** pass source/fact, canon, copy, Brand, accessibility, rights,
   destination and exact Ali approval where required.
6. **Publish:** only with separate account and publication authority.
7. **Verify:** open the live object and destination; record account, URL or
   object ID, time and correction path.
8. **Measure:** record the available comparable results without converting
   unknown into zero.
9. **Decide:** classify KEEP, ITERATE or STOP, with the next owner action.
10. **Reuse:** turn a demonstrated winner into a bounded LAiDIES template;
    retire losing treatments so they are not silently recycled.

## Complete release-and-support package

No post is complete at export. Every release package must include:

1. final native media, cover, crop, caption, alt text and destination;
2. an optional first comment with a declared job—useful source/context, a
   specific substantive question or a natural next step—not a hidden caption
   dump or generic engagement bait;
3. same-day and next-day Story/repost support when it adds a real entry point;
4. the initial reply window, likely questions, sourced response material and
   moderation/escalation rules;
5. channel-specific timing based on native audience evidence, not universal
   best-time folklore;
6. post-publication checks for media, caption, links, tags, accessibility and
   unintended crops;
7. 24-hour and seven-day result receipts, plus correction/archive instructions;
   and
8. one explicit KEEP, ITERATE or STOP decision that updates the next package.

Posting an immediate first comment is a **measured option**, not an assumed
ranking hack. Use matched tests to compare posts with and without one. Measure
qualified replies, comment depth, link actions and total value signals; do not
credit the first comment merely because it was present.

## Management-tool rule

The owner must maintain a current decision record for cross-channel management
tools, including scheduling, first-comment support, native variations, mobile
review, inbox/replies, analytics/export, media/version control, accessibility,
security and cost. Native Instagram and LinkedIn surfaces remain required for
platform-specific creation features and final live verification.

No vendor may become the source of truth for approval or release identity, and
no subscription or account connection is authorized by a recommendation. The
current evaluation is recorded in
`THIRD-PARTY-SOCIAL-TOOL-EVALUATION-2026-07-31.md`.

## Channel-specific application

### Instagram

Prioritize original Reels, carousels and Stories that are easy to recognize,
send and save. Suitable LAiDIES applications include a dial-up or rewind
transition that reveals one current AI idea, character-led practical help,
short demonstrations, source-backed headline translations, town notices and
episodic series people can anticipate. The first frame must carry the idea; a
caption cannot rescue a confusing visual.

### LinkedIn

Do not dump the Instagram asset into a professional feed. Translate the same
source into Ali's perspective, a useful workplace explanation, a transparent
Behind the Build lesson, a native document or a clear, helpful video. It should
start or join a meaningful conversation and provide knowledge, advice or an
actionable takeaway before asking anyone to visit LAiDIES.

## Decision measures

Use comparable-format baselines and state sample size, observation window and
known gaps.

- **Instagram discovery:** non-follower reach, profile visits and follows per
  reached account.
- **Instagram attention:** three-second hold where available, average watch
  time, completion/retention and replays for video; carousel progression where
  available.
- **Instagram value and conversation:** saves, sends/shares, reposts,
  meaningful comments, replies and link actions.
- **LinkedIn discovery and attention:** impressions, members reached, video
  views, total/average watch time and document engagement where available.
- **LinkedIn value and conversation:** clicks, saves where exposed, qualified
  comments, reposts, profile/Page actions and follower growth.
- **Website value:** the exact promised action and next useful action, only
  after the destination is admitted and privacy-safe measurement exists.

Likes and raw impressions are context, not success on their own. A small
sample is uncertainty. A high-reach object that attracts the wrong audience,
misstates LAiDIES or produces no useful behaviour is not a winner.

## Authority boundary

Trend detection does not approve copy, visuals, publication, scheduling,
spend, account changes or site readiness. The current pre-reopening traffic
rule remains follow/stay tuned only until the destination is admitted. This
contract requires the owner to **build and test** useful work; it does not
permit the owner to publish it autonomously.
