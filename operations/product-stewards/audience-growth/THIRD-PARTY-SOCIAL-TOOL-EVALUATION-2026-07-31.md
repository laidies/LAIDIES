# Third-party social operations tool evaluation — 2026-07-31

**Status:** DECISION-READY — BUFFER ESSENTIALS PILOT RECOMMENDED; NO PURCHASE
OR ACCOUNT CONNECTION AUTHORIZED
**Owner:** Audience, Growth & Launch Director
**Decision needed from Ali:** approve or reject the bounded pilot when ready

## Operating need

LAiDIES needs one phone-capable place to prepare and support Instagram,
LinkedIn and future channel releases without turning Ali into the content team.
The tool must help the Director carry the work from a finished content package
through scheduling, community response and measurement while preserving exact
approval and live-verification records in the LAiDIES operating system.

Required capabilities:

1. channel-native drafts for Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok and Threads;
2. scheduling, calendar and media/version organization;
3. first-comment and Story support;
4. a mobile workflow for review and comment response;
5. one inbox for comments and replies without hiding native context;
6. analytics, comparable date ranges, exports and custom UTM support;
7. alt text, safe crops, cover/thumbnail support and accessibility checks;
8. 2FA, recovery, role and least-access controls;
9. clear data export, cancellation and account-disconnection paths; and
10. a price and operating burden suitable for a one-woman company.

## Recommendation

Pilot **Buffer Essentials** with Instagram and LinkedIn in drafts-only mode.
The official Buffer pricing/help pages currently show:

- $6 USD per channel monthly for channels 1–10, or $60 USD per channel
  annually;
- unlimited scheduled posts and ideas;
- first-comment scheduling for Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn;
- a community inbox and mobile comment replies across the active channels;
- advanced analytics, longer date ranges, reports and custom UTMs;
- image alt text, 2FA, Notes, Canva, Google Drive/Photos and Zapier; and
- one user, which fits the current operating model.

At current list price, a two-channel monthly pilot is **$12 USD/month**. Verify
the exact checkout currency, tax and terms immediately before purchase.

Why this is the best current fit: it covers the most important one-person
publishing/support loop without paying for approval seats Ali does not need.
Ali's approval stays in the LAiDIES Control Room package; Buffer receives only
the approved final object after that decision. If a later mobile approval
workflow must live inside the vendor, reconsider Buffer Team rather than
making Ali perform production work.

## Native-platform requirement

Buffer does not replace Instagram's Professional Dashboard or LinkedIn's
native composer and analytics. The Director must still use native surfaces for:

- current account-specific Best Practices and recommendation guidance;
- features not exposed through third-party APIs;
- the final preview when layout, tags, audio or collaborator behaviour differs;
- live-post verification, corrections and account recovery; and
- the authoritative analytics receipt when vendor definitions differ.

## Immediate-first-comment rule

Buffer can schedule the first comment, but capability is not evidence of a
ranking benefit. A first comment may be used when it provides an exact source,
useful context, a substantive question or a clean next step. It must be tested
against comparable LAiDIES posts without one, and kept only if it improves
qualified conversation, link actions or another declared value measure.

## Bounded pilot acceptance test

Before any scheduling authority is granted:

1. connect only Instagram and LinkedIn with least access and 2FA;
2. create two non-public draft packages from an admitted LAiDIES source;
3. verify per-channel media, caption, alt text, first comment and Story support;
4. confirm Ali can review the exported phone package without entering Buffer;
5. exercise inbox/reply drafts without posting them;
6. export the calendar and analytics structures;
7. document disconnect, export, cancellation, recovery and deletion paths; and
8. record exact capabilities that still require the native apps.

The pilot passes only when the Director can produce the complete package and
Ali can make the decision without creating, rewriting or assembling it.
