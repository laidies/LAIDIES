## Identity and purpose

- **Product:** portfolio audience, growth, campaign and launch direction.
- **Product type:** internal portfolio service; no owned public route.
- **Audience:** LAiDIES prospective, new, returning, sharing, community,
  partner and future buyer audiences.
- **User job:** discover a relevant, truthful LAiDIES experience; receive value
  before an ask; understand the next action; choose whether to return/share.
- **Business job:** grow durable attention, trust, return and evidence of value
  without manipulative distribution or premature monetization.
- **Non-goals:** product readiness, journalism, provider delivery,
  deployment, Brand rulings, public identity decisions, raw audience
  surveillance, vanity-metric optimization or autonomous publication.

## Operating model

The Director is the translator between admitted product evidence and a
channel-specific invitation. The evidence path is:

`approved source → owner product verdict → Control Room combined admission →
claim/promise map → Brand/Ali approval → channel adaptation → exact
destination/public proof → authorized publication → live verification →
privacy-safe measurement → owner learning decision`.

Every boundary produces a separate status. A later boundary cannot repair a
missing earlier one.

## Admission states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `CAPTURED` | campaign idea is durably recorded; no source or scope approval |
| `SPECIFIED` | audience, promise, source, channels, gates and measurement are defined |
| `HOLD` | one or more required admissions are missing, stale or failed |
| `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT` | exact content/visual approved for bounded use; not scheduled or public |
| `READY TO PUBLISH` | every admission is current and publication authority is explicit; not yet public |
| `DEPLOYED` | owned destination candidate is deployed; campaign publication remains separate |
| `VERIFIED PUBLICLY` | exact channel object and destination work publicly, with URL/time evidence |
| `REMOVED` | object was taken down; it is not live evidence |
| `SUPERSEDED` | a named newer record replaces it |

`READY TO PUBLISH` is a campaign substate, not a replacement for the canonical
project statuses. It must never be reported as published.

## Campaign admission gate

For every channel object, complete all applicable rows in
`CAMPAIGN-ADMISSION-PACKET.md`.

### Product and release

- Name one exact action, destination and success state.
- Bind the current product owner verdict and evidence date.
- Bind the Control Room’s combined-state admission.
- Identify the exact release/artifact and public-origin proof.
- Test new/returning, mobile/desktop, logged-out/signed-in and failure states as
  relevant.
- Remove or narrow any promise whose proof is absent.

### Editorial, delivery and Brand

- NewsStand content requires its publication editor, claim/source,
  correction/retraction and currentness evidence.
- Newsletter claims require Post Office plus provider acceptance,
  confirmation, send/delivery and unsubscribe evidence appropriate to the
  claim. A submitted form is not a subscriber or delivered issue.
- Referral claims require an authoritative accepted/qualified lifecycle. A
  share sheet, copied link, page visit or local flag is not a referral.
- Visuals must be listed for the exact use in the canonical social allow-list.
- Ali approves exact final major-launch website state, copy and image/video.

### Channel and publication

- Record channel, account identity, publisher authority and final formatted
  object.
- Verify crop, overlay, alt text/captions, rights, link treatment and platform
  limitations.
- Record schedule/publication authority separately from content approval.
- After publication, open the actual post/message, verify its destination and
  record URL/message ID, timestamp, visible account and correction path.

## Segmentation and value proposition method

Each campaign names:

- primary segment and situation;
- audience job and current obstacle;
- promised value received on-channel;
- exact destination value;
- reason to act now that is true without urgency theatre;
- desired next/return behaviour; and
- excluded audience or harmful interpretation.

Use recognition, identity, surprise, utility, participation and performance as
creative levers. At least one useful payoff must exist before a click ask.

## Newsletter orchestration

The Director owns campaign brief, audience, approved source selection, CTA,
UTM, calendar, correction plan and learning question. The Post Office delivery
owner owns visible subscription truth and provider test coordination.
NewsStand editorial material remains under its own editor even when summarized
in a newsletter.

Before a send, require:

- final approved source and copy;
- provider audience/consent basis;
- test message, rendering, links, alt text and unsubscribe path;
- send authority and scheduled time;
- delivery/failure monitoring owner; and
- correction or stop-send procedure.

No subscriber list, email address, open-recipient record or reply body enters
this dossier.

## Partnerships and referrals

Partnerships must define audience fit, useful exchange, editorial independence,
data boundary, disclosure, rights, accessibility, workload, measurement and
exit. Contact-list upload, undisclosed paid placement and access to private
audience data are prohibited.

A share must be useful alone, have one-person energy, create no debt, avoid a
volume/status game and lead to an honest next step. There are no downlines,
recruiting commissions, invite leaderboards, cash-equivalent Butterfly Clips,
forced testimonials, “DM ten women” mechanics or community access tied to
recruitment.

## Measurement and customer evidence

### Measurement contract

- Use the shared generic event dictionary only after Platform/Privacy approve
  the implementation and delivery path.
- Campaign properties are categorical and allow-listed:
  `campaign_id`, `channel`, `content_id`, `destination_product_id`,
  `destination_action_id`, `creative_variant`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium`,
  `utm_campaign`, `device_class` and coarse `new_or_returning` only when the
  shared identity definition supports it.
- Never collect prompts, work situations, names, email addresses, handles,
  message/reply bodies, postcard notes, contacts, invite tokens, raw search,
  community content or full referrer URLs containing private data.
- Plausible/Clarity presence is not evidence of delivery, semantics or value.

### Decision measures

- **Discovery:** qualified reach/profile/destination actions.
- **Value:** saves, sends, lesson/tool starts and meaningful completions.
- **Return:** voluntary return to a current useful object within an approved
  aggregate window.
- **Trust:** source use, correction comprehension, opt-outs, complaints and
  consented qualitative themes.
- **Delivery health:** link, route, provider and channel failure categories.
- **Product signal:** repeated requests for templates, workshops, team
  resources or deeper help; never infer demand from impressions alone.

Every evidence packet states campaign/product version, date range, event
definitions, sample size, instrumentation gaps and observation versus
inference. Empty historical scorecards are templates, not baselines.

## Correction, removal and incident contract

Pause distribution when source truth, product readiness, visual approval,
provider state, destination, rights, privacy or public copy becomes wrong.
Record:

1. affected objects/channels and first known time;
2. exact claim or promise;
3. stop/pause/remove/correct decision and authority;
4. user-facing correction where needed;
5. source and derivative updates owned by their proper owners;
6. live verification of the change; and
7. measurement caveat and prevention rule.

A removed post remains historical evidence only.

## Acceptance and release

Initialization is accepted when:

- charter/spec/state/backlog and reusable/stale classifications agree;
- the grand-reopening ruling is `HOLD`;
- the packet can trace one approved source to channel and measurement without
  inheriting readiness;
- no registry, run queue, social, live route, shared analytics, external
  service or channel state was changed; and
- the product-steward checker still passes.

The first campaign remains blocked until the Control Room admits this owner and
supplies current product gates. No current campaign is `READY TO PUBLISH`.
