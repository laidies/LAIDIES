## Direct ruling being tested

Ali rejected the current LUMINAiRY portrait collection as too realistic,
painterly and dark. This ruling supersedes the previous use of dark,
painterly stained-glass portraits for this product.

## Candidate rendering language

- grown-up editorial-comic illustration, not oil painting or photorealism;
- clear inked contours, simplified facial planes and flat cel-shaded colour;
- bright, graphic stained-glass geometry rather than a dark cathedral scene;
- waist-up framing with the face as the visual priority;
- person-specific likeness, age, hair and clothing remain evidence-bound;
- no candles, Gothic gloom, black background fields or decorative props;
- restrained paper/halftone texture is acceptable only when it does not read
  as brushwork or realism.
