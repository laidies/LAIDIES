## Specialist contracts

### Editorial maker packet

For every proposed claim, deliver:

`claimId → exact text/selector hash → person/wing/kind → evidence excerpt and
hash → primary/authoritative source metadata → scope/caveat → verifiedOn →
recheckOn/trigger → rights/attribution → analogy and limit → correction owner →
affected consumers → proposed status`

No generic biography URL may support an unrelated quote, “first,” causal
claim or current role. Distinguish fact, interpretation, opinion, forecast and
dispute. Any uncertain component holds the smallest affected block.

### Shared handoff packets

- **KSVL:** guide/track IDs, exact origin/creator/rights status, playback and
  failure contract, removal trigger and return route.
- **Closet/Identity:** three preference keys/records, admitted-roster
  validation, device/account scope, migration/conflict/update/clear/delete/
  revoke behavior and prohibited ownership language.
- **Town Hall:** safe claim reference, consent/privacy/retention/moderation,
  authoritative receipt, no-reply wording, triage and propagation status.
- **Analytics:** approved event name, trigger, allowed enum properties,
  prohibited fields, dedupe rule, consent behavior, outage behavior, retention
  and dashboard question.

## Measurement and learning

- **Baseline:** no approved LUMINAiRY-specific analytics pull or representative
  comprehension result exists; all production claim blocks held.
- **Success signals:** visitors correctly distinguish wings; admitted
  source/learning routes are reached; corrections/holds are understood;
  door/modal/audio/storage failures are rare and recoverable; no privacy
  violations; source/freshness jobs meet cadence.
- **Failure signals:** profile-open count treated as learning; source opens
  without comprehension; stale/corrected content persists in a consumer;
  Closet mismatch; audio control without admitted track; focus/reflow failure;
  person selection in analytics; source monitor or correction propagation
  misses its SLA.
- **Review:** first 7 and 30 days after VERIFIED PUBLICLY, then quarterly for
  product health; current claims/source links follow their tighter cadence.
- **Decision:** expand admission only when the vertical slice passes. A
  failing dimension stays BUILDING/BLOCKED and triggers correction or rollback.
- **Dossier updates:** `state.json`, `backlog.md`, dated research/admission,
  integrated verdict, release/public evidence and learning record. Shared
  ledger/painpoint changes are queued through Control Room.
