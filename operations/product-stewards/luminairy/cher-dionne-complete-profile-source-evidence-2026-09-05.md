## Cultural-reference evidence

### University of California — “How Clueless used costumes to create its characters”

URL: https://www.universityofcalifornia.edu/news/how-clueless-used-costumes-create-its-characters

Why this source is useful: an institutional article built around an interview with costume designer Mona May. It explains that wardrobe was used to create character and describes Tai's later look as showing Cher's influence while retaining parts of Tai's earlier style. This supports a bounded discussion of influence as a recognizable pattern that another person adapts, rather than simple copying.

### Vogue — “Creating the Iconic Wardrobe of Clueless”

URL: https://www.vogue.com/article/clueless-costumes-mona-may-interview

Why this source is useful: an interview with costume designer Mona May. May explains that Dionne's black-and-white suit was designed to complement Cher without overpowering her. This supports treating the pair as a duo with distinct points of view, not as one lead plus a diversity add-on.

### BFI — “What would Jane Austen do?: Amy Heckerling on Clueless at 30”

URL: https://www.bfi.org.uk/interviews/what-would-jane-austen-do-amy-heckerling-clueless-30

Why this source is useful: a 2025 interview with writer-director Amy Heckerling. It discusses how the film meets young people through pop culture, fashion and shopping while challenging assumptions about Cher's intelligence. This supports using the reference for accessible pattern-making while avoiding the shallow equation of style with thoughtlessness.

## Allowed profile claims

- Cher and Dionne are fictional friends whose coordinated but distinct wardrobes make a useful LAiDIES teaching reference for trendsetting.
- Their paired treatment should show complementary points of view rather than making Dionne a supporting duplicate.
- The AI lesson is LAiDIES' editorial interpretation: make a repeatable pattern visible, useful and adaptable enough that other people can adopt it.

## Prohibited overclaims

- Do not claim Cher or Dionne invented a real technology, AI practice or management method.
- Do not put the LAiDIES lesson in either character's mouth as a quotation.
- Do not reduce trendsetting to novelty, personal branding, popularity, fashion consumption or being first.
- Do not imply that adoption proves a method is accurate, safe or appropriate.
- Do not collapse Dionne into Cher's sidekick or describe their value as visual diversity alone.
