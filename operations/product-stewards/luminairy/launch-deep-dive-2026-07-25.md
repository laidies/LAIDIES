The building must not allow beautiful devotional art to launder uncertain history, living-person roles or cultural references.

## Research, editorial and learning quality

The correct editorial distinction is explicit in the design: a PATRON SAiNT is a cultural/behavioural analogy, a MAiVEN is a real person whose account needs sources and a TRAiLBLAZER is history/industry context that needs scope and nuance. A card passes only when the reader can say what she learned, what source supports it, how the analogy helps (and where it stops), and what useful action/route follows.

Current biographical source links are a useful foundation, not a current-claim verification system. Living roles, employers, products, dates, quotes, historical priority and “first” claims are all freshness-sensitive. Evidence must stay distinct from LAiDIES inference and opinion; correction should propagate to episode, KSVL, cards and other consumer surfaces.

## Visual/brand, UX and accessibility

The design brief's door-as-interface and one-wing-at-a-time model meet the LAiDIES object-world standard better than generic card grids. It preserves identity: stained-glass portrait art is locked, but the room needs explicit state-on-arrival, readable door labels, keyboard/deep-link/back behaviour and mobile reflow. Existing design QA/candidate assets are evidence of construction direction, not Ali approval or accessibility clearance. Devotional language must remain optional, playful and respectful; it cannot pretend a fictional/cultural figure is a factual research authority.

## Freshness, correction, analytics and privacy

No automated freshness or corrections monitor is wired, and no privacy-safe analytics/VOC packet was available. Create a dated source registry, a correction log and a recheck cadence before scaling the roster. Later analytics should measure wing chosen, source/lesson route opened, accessibility failures and voluntary return—never names of picks, personal profile state, raw search terms that identify people or a “completion” count used to pressure collection.

## Launch blockers and improvement order

1. **FIX BEFORE LAUNCH:** claim/source/rights/freshness audit for every public person/content record.
2. **FIX BEFORE LAUNCH:** editorial gate proving each wing teaches a useful, source-linked outcome and links onward correctly.
3. **FIX BEFORE LAUNCH:** public desktop/mobile/keyboard/screen-reader test of doors, panels, cards, modal, audio and deep links.
4. **HIDE/LABEL FOR LAUNCH:** selections and MAiVENS-met are device-local, not durable identity/progress.
5. **POST-LAUNCH:** room-first visual reframe and quarterly source/correction upkeep after the truth system is live.
6. **REVENUE DEFERRED:** only original, rights-cleared optional objects after education and correction trust are established.
