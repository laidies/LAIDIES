## Stable promise and user outcome

The LUMINAiRY is a three-wing guide hall on Lantern Hill. A visitor meets
PATRON SAiNTS as clearly labelled cultural teaching devices, MAiVENS as sourced
accounts of women who shaped computing and AI, and TRAiLBLAZERS as sourced,
current accounts of people shaping present-day AI organisations, research and
products. **APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

The useful outcome is not collecting portraits. After one admitted encounter,
the visitor should be able to say who or what she met, what exact evidence
supports the account, what practical move or historical understanding it
offers, what remains uncertain or interpretive, and where to continue.
**APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

The room is not a leaderboard, credential, “greatest people” canon, endorsement
claim, devotional requirement or proof of learning from an opened modal.
**APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

## Place metaphor, feeling and ritual

The building should feel like a grown-up, luminous exhibition or guide hall:
three legible stained-glass doors, one open wing at a time, portraits/objects
whose status can be understood, and a source/next-route ritual that turns
admiration into usable understanding. **APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

The door is the interface, not decorative scenery. The visitor should always
know which wing is open, what that wing contributes, whether its material is
admitted or held, and how to leave or continue. **APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

The devotional vocabulary—wing, candle, saint, votive—must stay playful,
optional, specific and respectful. It must never imply religious authority,
coercion, adult/nightclub framing or generic “girlboss” hero worship.
**APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

The current stained-glass/portrait execution is implementation evidence, not
final sitewide-style approval; the sitewide illustration style decision
remains open under D-2026-07-26-050. **LOCKED LEDGER**

## Journeys

### Primary

Arrive → understand three wing jobs and current editorial status → open one
door → choose an admitted guide → read the exact scoped account and freshness
status → open its evidence → take one useful continuation → return with focus
and context intact. **APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

### Optional exploration

Compare wings, browse archive alcoves, flip an admitted Saint rule, explicitly
play a rights-cleared anthem, or choose one local guide per wing. None of these
actions becomes a reward, credential, public identity or learning claim.
**APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

### Return/resume

Restore valid device-local picks and the last useful route only at their proved
scope; explain content that became held, corrected, retired or unavailable;
never silently substitute a different person or stale wording. **LOCKED
LEDGER**

### Failure and recovery

Missing JavaScript, registry, receipt authority, source, track, storage,
network, modal readiness or correction service must preserve navigation and
show the smallest honest state. Retry must not double-count, duplicate picks,
advance audio or resurrect revoked content. **APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

## Desktop, mobile, accessibility, motion and audio

Doors and archive tabs use native controls, correct names/roles/states,
visible focus and complete keyboard operation. Open-wing state is announced
without stealing focus. **APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

An admitted profile modal must establish deterministic initial focus, trap and
wrap focus in both directions, close by Escape/backdrop/native control, restore
focus to the exact opener, prevent background scrolling and expose a truthful
opening/ready/failed/closed state. **VERIFIED USER/PRODUCT EVIDENCE**

At 320 CSS px and 200% zoom, the experience reflows without horizontal
essential-content loss. No essential meaning depends on colour, image, hover,
animation or audio. Reduced-motion removes smooth scrolling, transforms and
modal animation. **APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT**

Every portrait has useful alt treatment appropriate to whether the image
conveys identity, atmosphere or both; held content must not leak through alt
text, accessible names, metadata or no-script markup. **LOCKED LEDGER**

Audio starts only after explicit action, exposes play/pause/status in text,
honours reduced motion independently, never traps keyboard or screen reader
focus, and always has a readable source/lesson fallback. **LOCKED LEDGER**

Manual VoiceOver/screen-reader, Safari, native zoom and physical-device
evidence remains required; deterministic browser tests do not replace it.
