# Weekly Episode → Blend & Snap Study Pack source packet

**Version:** `EPX-STUDY-PACK-SOURCE-v1`
**Status:** SPECIFIED — REQUIRED WEEKLY RELEASE HANDOFF
**Producer:** Weekly Episodes — Engine & Production
**Receiver/product owner:** Blend & Snap
**Backfill:** `study-pack-source-packets/episodes-01-04.backfill.json`

Every admitted episode release supplies one checksum-bound source packet with:

1. canonical episode identity and core lesson;
2. key concepts with plain meanings and canon/source pointers;
3. one approved `rememberThis` line;
4. distinct work and life applications;
5. the Try-On brief;
6. a Cheat Sheet/reference brief;
7. concept- and character-card candidates with source, likeness/IP and rights
   status;
8. quiz learning objectives;
9. admitted episode image plus requested crop jobs and status; and
10. episode, pack, practice, reference, quiz and other truthful return links.

## Ownership boundary

- Weekly Episodes owns the producer packet's canonical teaching/editorial
  truth, source pointers, rights/likeness disclosures and version.
- Blend & Snap owns Study Pack composition, café/menu experience, component
  admission and the distinction between Study Sheet, Try-On, Cheat Sheet,
  Cards and Quiz.
- Trading Cards owns card-product art, card system, issuance, collection and
  acceptance. Card candidates in this packet are inputs, not cards.
- Brand & Experience owns the current visual lock. Canonical text, source and
  rights matrices may proceed; no final Study Pack or card art may be made,
  approved or integrated before the Brand ruling and the affected owner's
  independent gate.
- A packet does not make a component `available`. Blend & Snap must return a
  checksum-bound component disposition.

## Required status behavior

- Unknown or uncleared rights are explicit `HOLD`, never silently treated as
  fair use or approval.
- A third-party fictional character or living-person likeness is a teaching
  reference only until the applicable rights/likeness owner clears a specific
  treatment.
- Requested crops are production jobs. They are not approved images until
  their exact bytes pass the relevant image/Brand/accessibility gates.
- A missing component is `null` or `not_made`; no route or product is inferred.
- A canon, rights, image or link change supersedes the packet and requires a
  new checksum.

## Weekly lifecycle

At release Gate G, the Engine creates the complete packet from the ruled canon,
accepted episode package and current rights/source matrix. At Gate H, it binds
the exact public episode/return routes. Blend & Snap independently returns
`accept`, `hold`, `defer` or `not_applicable` per component and owns any later
Study Pack maker/judge packet.

The packet is part of the weekly release manifest inventory. It does not
authorize a homepage change, episode media change, site integration,
deployment or publication.
