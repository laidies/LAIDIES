   moving parts change. Generic CSS/rain/particle overlays do not count.

   or transition, never a loop.

   pan/zoom is allowed when it serves the line; shaking, jerking, snapping,

   music beat—not merely fill the clock.
10. Captions, visible text, titles, role labels and LAiDIES spelling/case must
    be exact; audio must be natural, intelligible, synchronized and clean.
