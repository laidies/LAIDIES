## 1. Identity, audience and outcome

- **Product:** the complete recurring weekly episode lifecycle and audience
  experience, from opportunity selection through measured public result.
- **Audience:** a smart, busy newcomer or returning learner who wants useful AI
  fluency without being patronized, misled or asked to become her own producer.
- **Job:** understand one consequential concept, use it within 24 hours,
  retrieve it later and know the next worthwhile step.
- **Distinct contribution:** an episode teaches through a serialized heroine,
  accurate plain explanation, a load-bearing Rewind Era analogy, a worked
  example, practice and a weekly return. Other products perform different
  lookup, instruction, news, tool, game, community or archive jobs.
- **Non-goals:** generating content volume, filling every surface, treating
  current products as a leaderboard, restaging an existing class/book, or
  calling a local export a release.

production orchestration, integration, evidence, correction and rollover.

Independent owners:

| Owner | Receives | Returns | Cannot decide |

| Chick Flicks | one immutable addition/correction/hold/removal/replacement manifest | checksum-bound accept/reject, format-specific availability and exact discovery/archive/watch/return proof | episode authorship or Episode Media Quality verdict |
| Screening Room | Chick Flicks-admitted listen/watch assets, captions, poster and fallback contract | player, accessibility, failure and public-byte evidence through Chick Flicks | editorial or craft approval |

| Blend & Snap | one checksum-bound Study Pack source packet with lesson, concepts, applications, practice/reference briefs, candidates, objectives, images and return links | component dispositions plus Study Pack composition, experience and acceptance | episode canon, final Brand direction or Trading Cards art/system |
| Library / Classes / NewsStand / KSVL / other product champions | structured impact proposal with concept owner and distinct learner/user job | select/update/link/defer/decline decision and its own acceptance evidence | automatic duplicate production |
| Platform / Release | checksum-bound candidate, route and rollback packet | exact artifact, deployment and public-origin proof | content or creative approval |

Maker and judge remain separate. A craft report is evidence; the Director
reconciles it and an independent gate accepts or rejects the integrated result.

## 3. Source hierarchy and re-entry

Before resuming an episode after intervening work:

1. read the latest Ali ruling and applicable ledger decisions;
2. recover the full approved examples, corrections and analogy relationships;
3. compare the last approved artifact with the current candidate;
4. identify genuine new evidence or changed decisions;
5. update the episode packet's authority matrix and checksums; and
6. stop if a proposed simplification, deferment or removal lacks Ali's
   approval.

Source order:

`Ali ruling/locked decision → episode packet/fidelity trace → approved
substance → ruled canon → current primary evidence → scripts/recording
reconciliation → manifest-bound media → derived surfaces → exact release/public
proof`.

Historical handoffs, drafts, filenames and stage stamps never outrank that
chain.

## 4. One durable packet per active episode

The packet records:

- episode number, title and current promise;
- version, packet checksum and source checksums;
- authority state of every candidate source;
- locked decisions and approved boundaries;
- unresolved owner decisions in dependency order;
- stage rows with owner, authoritative input, evidence, truthful status,
  blocker and next action;
- impact proposals and receiving-owner dispositions;
- release-candidate checksums and public proof;
- corrections and superseded versions;
- outcome evidence; and
- exact next-week rollover trigger.

Only one owner decision is surfaced at a time. Later decisions stay queued
until their dependencies are ready.

## 5. Lifecycle and hard gates

### Gate A — opportunity and curriculum

- Verify learner need, season prerequisites, duplication and freshness.
- Run the claim-register gate for the proposed episode and disposition every
  due/triggered claim before prose.
- Lock one practical outcome and neighbouring-episode boundary.
- Research changing claims from primary/official sources.
- Stop before prose when the premise or scope is unresolved.

### Gate B — concept and substance

- Explain the concept plainly.
- Trace every approved premise element as retained, technically corrected,
  Ali-approved deferment or Ali-approved removal.
- Define the worked example, practical Try-On and analogy limits.
- Map the concept into the recurring episode beats.
- Run the blind reverse brief plus concept-fidelity, technical truth,
  usefulness/transfer and episode-format vetoes.
- Ali rules the resulting Gate 1 card.

No canon, script, art or audio is authorized when Gate B fails.

### Gate C — canon and opportunity decisions

- Reconcile season continuity, title, patron saint, heroine outfit, facts,
  story beats, recurring segments and next-episode hook.
- Complete the episode opportunity scan after substance approval.
- Every conditional destination becomes `selected`, `deferred` with trigger,
  or `not_applicable` with reason.
- Send distinct-job impact proposals to affected product owners.

### Gate D — scripts and recording

- Written and spoken scripts derive from the ruled canon.
- Readable spelling and TTS performance spelling stay separate.
- Ali's listening changes are classified and reconciled.
- Final approved audio becomes the timing master.
- As-recorded transcript, normalized transcript, SRT and VTT bind to that
  exact audio.

### Gate E — cue and reference admission

- Story/continuity produces a complete beat and narration-to-visual job map.
- Each cue names characters, identity references, master visual-style hash,
  category references, location, outfit, semantic visual job and timing.
- Episode Media Quality independently admits each source image before motion.
- The locked visual system in
  `operations/episode-visual-system-lock.md` governs all episode people and
  category treatments.

### Gate F — motion, assembly and accessibility

- Each clip has a semantic event or deliberate-still rationale.
- Motion, edit, audio, captions and continuity receive independent verdicts.
- The full checksum-bound master is watched at normal speed and delivery size.
- Representative mobile/desktop, keyboard, reduced-motion, captions,
  failure/fallback and assistive-technology checks pass.

### Gate G — fan-out and release candidate

- Required core surfaces are complete.
- Re-run the claim-register gate; every affected narration, image, Study Pack,
  Library, class, quiz, NewsStand and site consumer has an exact disposition
  and verification state.
- Selected opportunities are complete under the receiving owner's authority.
- Canon-to-derived data drift checks pass.
- Search, article, listen/watch, practice, archive, email, social, KSVL,
  rewards and return paths are truthful and testable.
- Candidate manifest binds exact files and SHA-256 checksums.
- Rollback and correction routes exist.

### Gate H — deploy, public proof and learning

- Deployment/publication uses the approved exact candidate.
- Real public URLs and bytes pass the promised journeys.
- Sent/posted/played states use actual channel evidence.
- Outcome evidence is gathered without treating play/click/page load as
  learning or completion.
- Corrections create a new version and invalidate affected downstream proof.
- The learning scan updates a prevention control or records
  `no qualifying learning`.

## 6. Status model

Use the fixed working-agreement statuses. Additional Control Room execution
labels (`ACTIVE NOW`, `INITIALIZING/RECOVERY`, `NEXT`, `STALE`) describe task
activity, not product completion.

- `RUNNING`/`ACTIVE NOW` requires the real task ID, current action, exact write
  scope and evidence timestamp.
- `BUILT LOCALLY` does not imply independent review.
- `VERIFIED LOCALLY` does not imply deployment.
- `DEPLOYED` does not imply public verification.
- `VERIFIED PUBLICLY` applies only to the exact released version.
- A source change invalidates every dependent status whose checksum no longer
  matches.

## 7. Production manifest minimum

Every cue row binds:

`cue ID → narration window → spoken idea → visual job → source asset →
character/location/outfit/style references and hashes → motion intent →
audio/caption version → maker verdict → independent verdict → final occurrence
and checksum`.

Every release binds:

`approved canon version → final narration/audio/transcript/captions → cue
manifest → source images → clips → edit project → master export → derived
surfaces → deployed artifact → public verification`.

Missing or duplicate rows, rejected inputs, mismatched hashes and stale clocks
fail closed.

### Engine → Blend & Snap Study Pack source packet

Every admitted episode release includes the producer packet defined in
`STUDY-PACK-SOURCE-PACKET-SPEC.md`. It binds the core lesson, key concepts,
remember-this line, work/life applications, Try-On and Cheat Sheet/reference
briefs, concept/character card candidates with sources and rights, quiz
learning objectives, episode image/crop jobs and truthful return links.

The packet supplies canonical inputs; it is not Blend & Snap visual authority,
component admission, final Study Pack art or Trading Cards production
authority. Canonical content/source/rights matrices may proceed while final
art remains locked behind the Brand ruling. Blend & Snap returns independent
component dispositions. A canon, rights, image or link change supersedes the
packet.

### Engine → Chick Flicks immutable transaction

Every proposed addition, correction, hold, removal or replacement uses the
complete transaction in `EPISODE-RELEASE-MANIFEST-SPEC.md`. It binds episode
identity/status, canon checksum, checksum-bound issue artifact, exact
narration/caption/cue/image/motion assets, absent formats, the complete
dependency manifest, supersession, independent verdicts, rollback and the
Engine's exact episode-package public proof.

A filename, page, successful build, timestamp, export or isolated checksum is
not admission.

Chick Flicks returns separate checksum-bound dispositions for the reading
issue, narrated listen-along, admitted visual edition and motion-film edition.
Its public discovery/archive/watch/return proof is distinct from the Engine's
episode-package proof. An editorially valid issue may coexist with a held
media format.

Engine corrections invalidate stale Chick Flicks acceptance. A Media Quality
revocation holds only the affected media occurrence unless the editorial
package is independently held. Rollback selects one previously accepted whole
package and matching admission record; it never mixes candidates.

## 9. Audience, accessibility and failure journeys

The complete release tests:

- first-time learner without prior episode context;
- returning learner following season continuity;
- read, listen and any admitted watch branch;
- mobile, desktop, keyboard, reduced-motion and assistive-technology paths;
- missing audio/image/caption/network states;
- correction/superseded-version state; and
- every promoted downstream handoff and return.

The episode remains understandable without any one optional sensory channel.
A listen-along never becomes a motion-film claim. A play event, tape selection
or page visit never becomes a completion/reward claim.

## 10. Measurement, freshness and correction

Measure useful movement such as comprehension, Try-On completion under its own
contract, retrieval, correction discovery, meaningful continuation and
appropriate return. Treat low traffic as insufficient evidence.

Every changing claim records source, access date, recheck trigger and owner.
Recheck near recording and release. A material correction:

1. opens a new episode packet version;
2. marks affected versions `SUPERSEDED`;
3. invalidates dependent checksums/evidence;
4. updates every selected consumer;
5. preserves the correction trail; and
6. re-runs the affected public gates.

## 11. Control Room execution contract

Control Room may dispatch bounded work only when:

- the task is bound to this dossier/state and the active episode packet;
- the lane has exact non-overlapping write paths;
- all unresolved upstream decisions are excluded;
- maker/judge roles are separated;
- shared-file and service changes wait for the named integration owner; and
- the result returns as evidence, not automatic canon or release authority.

This task's portfolio transition is described in
`control-room-handoff-2026-07-26.md`. This owner does not edit
`registry.json` or `run-queue.json` for that transition.

Shared index, builder, route, analytics and release changes require one joint
Control Room lock naming both the Weekly Episodes and Chick Flicks owners.

## 12. Acceptance

The engine succeeds only when one episode passes the complete chain from
approved substance through verified public outcome with checksums and
receiving-owner evidence. A scheduler, prompt, manifest, draft, local file,
green component check or prepared release packet is not the complete engine.

Episode 5 is the first intended vertical proof after its Gate 1 approval.
