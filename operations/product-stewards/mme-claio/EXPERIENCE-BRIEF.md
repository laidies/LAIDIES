  one random authored reflection and a small move the visitor may keep, adapt
  or ignore. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

  diagnosis, tailored advice, crisis support, professional advice or current

## Stable promise and successful user outcome

The stable promise is: enter Mme CLAi-O's room, understand the honest boundary,
cut a randomized authored deck, receive one memorable reflection and decide
for yourself whether its small suggested move is useful.
`APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

The authoritative success is a valid governed card visibly rendered and
announced after the visitor activates the deck; animation start, a click,
storage write, badge write or route load is not completion.
`APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

The result must remain usable when art, audio, analytics, browser storage or a
network connection fails. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

## Place metaphor, feeling and ritual

- **Place:** an after-hours reading room built around a velvet séance table,
  crystal ball, fanned cards and a deck. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- **Feeling:** warm, funny, a little theatrical, direct and safe enough that
  drama never turns into authority or mockery of vulnerability.
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- **Ritual:** enter → read the boundary → cut the deck → watch a brief optional
  turn → receive the card in place → consider the move → optionally review,
  clear or draw again. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- **Signature:** late-night hotline language and a quiet 1999-commercial
  chyron may provide period flavour, but no fake callable number or inaccurate
  historic “exact disclaimer” claim may appear. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- **Ten-second comprehension:** “random authored reflection, not foresight;
  cut the deck; use real help and current sources for high-stakes matters.”
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
