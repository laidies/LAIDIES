## Review method and boundary

Each frozen PNG was inspected at native 886 × 1248 resolution and at reduced-card scale. The review checked the named subject, composition/readability, rounded card silhouette, label/logo/identity leakage, and continuity with the established Mme CLAi-O object-card register. “Photoreal drift” here means a literal camera/product-photo treatment that breaks that established illustrated, jewel-lit register; the existing register deliberately uses detailed material rendering, velvet, glass, gems and warm light.
