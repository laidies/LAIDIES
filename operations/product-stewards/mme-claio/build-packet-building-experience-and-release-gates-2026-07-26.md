## Outcome

- **Product:** Mme CLAi-O's Shop (`mme-claio`).
- **Complete scope:** canonical room/reading route, page-scoped CSS/enhancement,
  governed deck and art fallbacks, local count/history/Hotline Regular, clear
  and corrupt/denied-storage recovery, KSVL/back/charm handoffs, Cocktail
  Fortune redirect, BWS separation, Resident Card/reward boundary, analytics
  and exact release/public proof.
- **User problem:** a mechanically strong local activity still lacks a current
  provenance-backed experience decision, four-scope visitor proof and a
  reconciled shared reward/analytics/release contract.
- **Intended user outcome:** any visitor can immediately understand and
  complete one safe random reflection inside Mme CLAi-O's room, return usefully
  on the same device, clear it truthfully and never mistake local continuity,
  theatrical voice or a shared Card link for prediction, identity or a durable
  account reward.
- **Evidence:** `EXPERIENCE-BRIEF.md`, `FUNCTIONALITY-MAP.md`,
  `evidence-owner-entry-preflight-2026-07-26.md`, prior Repair 2 maker/judge
  records and current source/tests.
- **Explicit non-goals:** deck rewrite, prompt box/classifier, professional or
  crisis advice, prediction, account reading history, paid readings, physical
  deck, replacement shared identity/reward ledger, deployment or publication.

## Proposed direction

Run a controlled building-experience championship whose only free variable is
the room/object composition. Preserve the exact capability and safety contract
in all candidates:

1. **Incumbent:** current full room image with obvious labelled deck hotspot
   and redundant button.
2. **Challenger A:** the corrected brief's separate, obviously-clickable deck
   render composited into the room, with the result opening at the table.
3. **Challenger B:** a materially distinct capability-preserving room
   composition that keeps the deck physical and obvious without becoming a
   form, card grid or hidden-hotspot hunt.

The competition may use existing approved/eligible assets and isolated HTML/CSS
prototypes inside the Mme dossier evidence area. Any new visual generation
requires the artwork and independent visual-admission protocols. No candidate
may touch production paths before Ali's owner visual ruling.

No external tool, plugin, service or paid capability is proposed. Shared
Supabase/reward and Plausible/Clarity work uses existing platform surfaces only
after their owners accept the handoff.

## Invariants frozen for every candidate

- exactly 100 governed authored cards and current no-immediate-repeat rule;
- no typed visitor text, prompt analysis, classifier or model/provider;
- random/non-tailored truth and permanent humane high-stakes/current-fact
  boundary before and after draw;
- valid text result independent of art/audio/storage/analytics/network;
- same keys, ten-history maximum, last-three display, threshold five and
  strict timestamp/state sanitation until Platform explicitly accepts a
  migration;
- local/device persistence truth and no durable member-benefit language;
- scoped clear preserving unrelated valid badge siblings;
- obvious native deck control, keyboard/touch/focus/live-result/reduced-motion
  behavior and full mobile/desktop functionality;
- storefront hero/shared charm host, KSVL control and correct return handoff;
- Cocktail Fortune retirement recovery and BWS product separation.

## Exact analytics proposal for Platform/Privacy review

Use shared events where possible:

- `product_viewed`: `product_id=mme-claio`, route, device class and
  new/returning-device only;
- `meaningful_action_started`: `action_id=draw`;
- `meaningful_action_completed`: `action_id=draw`,
  `completion_source=governed_local_deck`;
- `meaningful_action_failed`: `action_id=draw|persist|clear`,
  controlled `failure_class`;
- `reward_granted` or a product-local equivalent only if the local-keepsake
  classification is approved, with `reward_id=hotline-regular` and
  `persistence_class=device_local`;
- `reward_grant_failed` only for a real authoritative local/account result,
  never merely animation or toast state.

Prohibited payloads: card name/copy, history, badge timestamp, identity,
email/account ID, inferred need, safety category, full URL query, typed content
or raw replay/session evidence.

motion and native screen reader:

1. empty/new local state;
2. returning valid state without Card;
3. same state plus valid device-local Card;
4. signed-in verified account plus local state;
5. signed-out return after account state;
6. second tab and second device;
7. fifth draw and duplicate/replay;
8. clear after sanitation with unrelated sibling;
9. denied read/write/remove and quota exhaustion;
10. malformed counts, oversized/unknown history and strict invalid/future
    badge timestamps;
11. missing card art, song media, analytics and offline/static network;
12. legacy redirect and BWS product boundary;
13. account import exclusion or, only if newly authorized, grant/display/
    dedupe/clear/revoke/delete/privacy lifecycle.

## Measurement and learning

- **Baseline:** no approved Mme product-specific production baseline.
- **Success signals:** safe completion, voluntary repeat, useful same-device
  return, boundary comprehension, successful scoped clear and no prohibited
  analytics/reward scope.
- **Failure signals:** abandoned/duplicate draw, storage false success, account
  scope confusion, high-stakes misconception, Card/Closet mismatch, missing
  result under optional failure, native accessibility defect or route drift.
- **Review:** 7 and 30 days after a verified public release, then after deck,
  safety, storage, shared reward, analytics, navigation or browser-support
  changes.
- **Decision after measurement:** repair a failing promise; do not add
  personalization, urgency, paid readings or durable rewards without a new
  evidence-backed owner decision.
- **Required dossier updates:** state, backlog, evidence, functionality map and
  qualifying painpoint/ledger entries after implementation and independent
  judgment.
