## Adopted launch decision

The candidate no longer asks for or routes arbitrary free text. Mme CLAi-O is
an honest random-card reflection: every draw is one random authored card, not
tailored to the visitor, not an analysis or answer, and not prediction or
professional advice.

The former finite-phrase safety classifier and its hidden stop path are
removed. A concise humane boundary is permanently visible and tells visitors
not to use a random card for emergencies, personal safety/abuse, health, legal,
financial or current-fact decisions. It names local emergency services, a
trusted person, qualified professionals and reliable current primary sources
without inventing a location-specific number.

## Bounded implementation

- Removed `#claioQuestion`, all page textareas, the `BOUNDARIES` regex list,
  `classifyBoundary`, `showBoundary` and draw-time prompt checks.
- Added visible random-authored, non-tailored, no-analysis/no-answer truth.
- Converted the safety state into a permanent component and enforced white
  heading/paragraph text over `#111735`; the computed color pair is about
  `17.53:1`.
- Preserved the governed 100-card deck, card art/slug aliases, non-repeat
  behavior, ten-entry local history, last-three display and five-draw local
  keepsake threshold.
- Count accepts only exact decimal safe integers `0..10000`; invalid negative,
  huge, decimal, exponential and non-numeric values reset to zero and are
  removed.
- History accepts only plain records whose exact card exists in the governed
  deck, rehydrates the authoritative `read` copy, discards unknown/corrupt
  current cards and clamps to ten.
- Badge storage accepts a plain object of valid shaped members, rejects
  invalid/future timestamps and rejects a non-device-local Hotline Regular
  member. Scoped reset preserves valid unrelated badges.
- Storage read/write/remove denial continues to leave the current-page reading
  usable and truthfully unsaved.
