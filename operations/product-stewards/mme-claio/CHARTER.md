2. No health, crisis, legal, financial, emergency, self-harm, abuse, substance-dependence or high-stakes decision is resolved by card copy; boundaries and escalation must be humane and direct.
3. Character warmth and drama never mock vulnerability, manufacture certainty, or substitute for a practical safe next step.
4. Deck repetition controls, history, call count and badges state device/account scope truthfully.
5. Cocktail Fortune is a migration alias, not a second competing reading product. Businesswomen’s Special remains its own BRONZE AiGE experience.
6. Reduced motion, keyboard, screen-reader, contrast and mobile readability are part of the reading—not later decoration.
