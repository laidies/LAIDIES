## Exact gate

A building candidate receives no Brand PASS unless the exact desktop and mobile
experience:

- feels like entering that specific place in colourful, vibrant SUNNYVAiLE;
- uses the environment and truthful physical objects as the primary interface;
- compares credibly with the Homepage colour/energy/world target;
- retains the place on narrow mobile and in failure states; and
- arrives complete enough that “Brand later” is not a repair dependency.

Neutral shells, generic boxed layouts, white functional bases, room images used
as backdrops and reskin proposals are **TECHNICAL EVIDENCE ONLY**. Scores,
tests, accessibility and truthful state cannot compensate. Technical evidence
may preserve capability hierarchy, copy, state machines and accessibility
findings; it cannot become an owner candidate, an integration dependency or an
Ali decision.

The Homepage comparison is a colour/energy/world benchmark. Its own stacked
content grammar is not automatically a building template.

## Visitor replacement admission criteria

The next Visitor candidate must:

1. make arrival unmistakably the SUNNYVAiLE Visitor's Centre, at Homepage-level
   colour and energy;
2. make the front desk a real operated counter, the map a physical wall/fold-out
   object and the directory a place-bound board, drawers, tickets or equivalent;
3. let the first meaningful action occur on a visible environment object;
4. attach deterministic text, state, focus and accessibility semantics to those
   exact objects;
5. preserve the authored place at 390px and 320px instead of stacking cards;
6. keep failure and empty states inside the same place;
7. show exact first-time, returning and recovery journeys in that environment;
8. pass independent building-as-experience and Brand-world veto review before
   any compensable scorecard; and
9. remain building-specific—the Visitor owner may not select the global visual
   system.

## Portfolio prevention rule

Every building admission packet must include a checksum-bound
`environment-is-interface` receipt with exact desktop/mobile renders, a
Homepage colour/energy/world comparison, identified physical objects for each
primary action, responsive/failure proof and an independent binary verdict.

Control Room must return any failed packet as **TECHNICAL EVIDENCE ONLY** and
remove it from owner-decision or integration queues.
