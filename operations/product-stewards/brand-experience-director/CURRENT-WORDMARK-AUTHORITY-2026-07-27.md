Do not browse for a visually plausible logo. Resolve the current authority
first. A current web candidate must:

1. name the exact authority source;
2. use the live Jost construction;
3. pass a retired-filename/reference scan;
4. compare the rendered mark with the current homepage; and
5. fail before owner review if the mark differs.

Historical videos and sealed artifacts are not silently rewritten. Any
deliberate exception requires an explicit owner ruling naming the artifact and
reason.
