## What this protects

This inventory prevents an idea, recommendation or blocker from disappearing between conversation, the shared idea log and a building backlog. The source census preserves every identifiable source entry and line. The tables below deduplicate that source material into initiatives with an accountable owner, sequence and next executable action.

A row marked **CAPTURED**, **SPECIFIED** or **BUILT LOCALLY** is not public and is not complete. A **LATER** row remains visible and must retain its return trigger; it is not discarded.

## Gaps newly made explicit in this reconciliation

1. **Experience-flexible learning is not a built system.** LAiDIES needs topic-by-topic support choices—essentials, use it, go deeper, judge it—without permanently labelling a woman beginner or advanced.
2. **The positive Daily idea needs an editorial product, not an affirmation.** Its job is one concrete act of curiosity or mutual advancement each day, with an anti-cringe gate and original language.
3. **Mutual advancement is a sitewide product and culture rule.** Credit, invitations, specific help, advocacy and opportunity sharing must appear in Community, LUMINAiRY, rewards and writing—not only one Daily card.
4. **Many partial foundations are real but not complete journeys.** Identity, continuation, messaging, cards and dashboard work must be reconciled rather than rebuilt or overstated.

## Capture-to-build rule

A material idea is not safely handled until all applicable steps exist:

1. faithful raw capture in the canonical idea log or an exact product source;
2. reconciliation against existing work so duplicates merge rather than overwrite;
3. one accountable registered product owner and affected owners;
4. this inventory records truth, priority, dependencies, next action and done condition;
5. the destination backlog accepts the build obligation;
6. implementation work enters the active queue when its trigger fires;
7. blockers carry an owner, unblock action and review date;
8. Ali sees only a decision-ready question when her judgment is genuinely needed; and
9. built, reviewed, released and publicly verified remain separate receipts.

Run `node scripts/build-portfolio-work-inventory.mjs --write` after material idea or routing changes. The check fails if any top-level Ali idea-log family or July 24 cluster is no longer mapped into the curated inventory.
