## Ruling

Accept Idea Inbox's `MERGE` recommendation. The idea belongs in the existing
LAiDIES app-strategy line as a cross-product **daily companion opportunity**.
It is not approved as a native app, notification feed, PWA build, wrapper or
new permanent-owner department.

The daily companion is also not a Town Entry mode. Town Entry retains the
website front-door and eventual consumer/presentation job; it does not inherit
Mme CLAi-O, NewsStand, notification, identity or delivery authority.

Control Room remains the provisional coordination owner only until an
opportunity audit proves whether this is one durable product. Source authority
stays separate:

- Mme CLAi-O owns the reading mechanic and safety boundary;
- NewsStand owns The Daily, The Breaking, quiet states, correction and
  retraction;
- Platform owns installability, cache/offline behavior, permissions,
  notification delivery, identity/state and privacy;
- Town Entry owns website entry and any admitted web/PWA presentation;
- Audience & Growth owns admitted packaging and measured return behavior;
- Brand & Experience and Ali retain app identity and public-creative approval.

## Exact return trigger

Do not commission the browser/PWA/native competition until all three are true:

1. Mme CLAi-O has an owner-approved, non-predictive daily-reading rule;
2. The Daily and The Breaking have admitted producer-to-reader, quiet-state,
   correction and retraction contracts; and
3. Platform can specify an opt-in, revocable, privacy-safe installability and
   notification proof without inventing a provider, account, permission or
   spend authority.

When triggered, the first cycle is an opportunity/advantage audit, not a
build. It must compare current mobile web, installable PWA and native on:

- user value and frequency;
- glanceable daily return, saved continuity, offline/background behavior,
  quick actions, deep links, sharing and system accessibility;
- permission, privacy and notification pressure;
- source freshness, quiet/unavailable states, correction and retraction;
- maintenance, app-store/release burden and exit/rollback;
- smallest reversible proof; and
- explicit benefits the mobile website already provides without native work.

The audit may recommend `WEB`, `PWA`, `NATIVE LATER`, `NO APP ADVANTAGE YET` or
a bounded staged combination. Only a materially distinct, evidence-backed
trade-off returns to Ali.

## Product-tree consequence

- No registry product or permanent owner is added now.
- No active-work, run-queue or launch-critical-path lane is opened.
- `docs/product/laidies-app-strategy.md` is prior strategy input, not a current
  operating specification; reconcile it against current product names, held
  states and evidence before any amendment.
- A permanent daily-companion owner is considered only if the audit proves a
  coherent durable product whose outcome cannot be owned through the existing
  source/product boundaries.
