## Controlling direction

1. The current/original Homepage remains the protected incumbent and is
   visually superior to every challenger shown so far.
2. Preserve its light gradient backgrounds and colourful, inviting character.
3. Keep the established LAiDIES colour families. For the next proposal only,
   test brighter, more electric 1990s versions of the existing button and
   accent-text colours. Do not make the page darker, create a new palette,
   use neon-on-black, add white boxes, reduce imagery or perform a wholesale
   redesign.
4. The next visual proposal must correct Miss Jeeves, Delta i Lai Nu and the
   FAiRY Godmother house using Ali's saved governing references.
5. Reorganization is permitted only when it improves the incumbent. One
   preferred exploration is a right-side Daily Column containing Breaking
   News, daily stories, a Mme CLAi-O daily reading, Daily AI Tip, Song of the
   Day, a site-specific Did You Know item and a funny AI-focused horoscope.
6. Before designing Daily AI Tip content or logic, recover the existing work
   from the “Initialize AIDB intelligence” conversation and durable records.
7. A helper/mascot may be explored for the column using Ali's supplied small
   blue creature only as a role/charm reference. It must be an original
   LAiDIES-owned design and must not copy OpenAI's character or asset.
8. The next owner return is one isolated full-page desktop/mobile comparison
   against the exact incumbent, with complete imagery and readable contrast.
   It must visibly improve the incumbent before Ali sees it.

## Exploration addendum

Ali permits the same isolated proposal to explore additional ideas for the
Daily Column and a slight reorganization of the rest of the Homepage, subject
to these boundaries:

1. Additional Daily Column modules or behaviours must create recurring visitor
   value, help people understand what is new or useful today and feel specific
   to LAiDIES. The column must not become clutter, generic dashboard UI or a
   content dumping ground.
2. Prefer one coherent Daily Column over scattered or repeated daily modules.
   Every proposed item must earn its space and specify editorial ownership,
   update frequency, freshness behaviour, empty/stale states, mobile placement
   and accessibility.
3. The proposal may identify and remove genuine duplication, consolidate
   repeated destinations or messages and improve hierarchy for first-time and
   returning visitors.
4. Small IA or placement changes are allowed only when they clarify the
   experience. This is not permission for wholesale restructuring, copy
   replacement, feature expansion or production/shared edits.
5. The visual comparison must annotate every proposed change with:
   - the exact duplicate or confusion it solves;
   - why the new placement is clearer;
   - which incumbent strength it preserves; and
   - the expected first-time and returning-visitor benefit.
6. The controlling visual direction remains unchanged: light-gradient
   incumbent, established colour families, electric/vibrant accents, complete
   imagery, readable contrast and unmistakable LAiDIES-world character.
7. The AI-focused horoscope should be tested as a concise Mme CLAi-O
   `AIstrology` or `Promptoscope` item: one funny observation about recognizable
   AI behaviour plus one useful action. It is entertainment/editorial content,
   never technical news, real prediction, mental-health guidance or inferred
   personal profiling. It must rotate, expose a clear date/freshness state and
   collapse cleanly with the rest of the column on mobile.
