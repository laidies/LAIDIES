## 2. Fit with KSVL intent

The concept fits KSVL because KSVL's approved job is to turn LAiDIES lessons,
people, activities and town identity into a repeatable sound world with a clear
route back to meaning. A strong music video can extend one admitted memory hook
into a scene, performance or journey and then return the visitor to the song's
owning destination.

The concept stops fitting when the video:

- becomes a decorative slideshow, generic third-party visual style or
  unrelated attention asset;
- substitutes visual spectacle for the song's actual words, lesson or
  destination;
- implies an episode motion film, public campaign or building state that its
  owner has not admitted;
- becomes a 29-title production quota;
- reopens the paused Deb workflow without evidence that the editing burden and
  destination are now better;
- treats a creator-confirmed playable master as proof of as-recorded words,
  caption timing, visual identity or public release; or
- is approved by its maker rather than a separate media/Brand/product judge.

## 7. Required maker and judge chain

The eventual chain, after song admission, is:

1. **KSVL catalogue/audio owner** — binds exact master, creator/provenance,
   word package and destination candidate.
2. **Song/recording reconciler** — creates the exact as-recorded transcript,
   caption timing and discrepancy record under the song-production protocol.
3. **Destination product owner** — specifies the video's user job, return path,
   non-goals and acceptance scenes.
4. **Brand & Experience Director** — supplies/admits governing visual grammar,
   invariants and any character/town depiction boundaries.
5. **Treatment competitors** — incumbent plus two materially different
   treatments; compare narrative/performance/journey value, production burden,
   accessibility and rollback. Rough exploration is not an Ali option.
6. **Video maker/editor** — works only inside a named isolated media lock and
   cannot judge her own candidate.
7. **Independent Episode Media Quality judge** — inspects the exact full-
   resolution/audio candidate, caption sync, edit quality, rights and technical
   delivery.
8. **Independent product/Brand/accessibility judges** — enforce 17/20 product,
   accuracy/trust and LAiDIES brand floors plus accessible equivalents.
9. **Control Room/release owner** — integrates only the admitted exact artifact
   and verifies destination, performance, rollback and public origin.
10. **Audience & Growth** — after product admission, prepares a default-deny
    distribution packet; it does not make the video ready.
11. **Ali** — chooses the consequential song/treatment and approves the exact
    public-identity visual; publication remains a separate authority.

## 8. Success measure

The pilot succeeds only if, with the exact song and destination:

- a newcomer can identify the song's core hook/job and where in SUNNYVAiLE to
  continue;
- the video improves unaided hook/source recall or destination comprehension
  versus the audio-only/liner-note baseline without teaching a wrong idea;
- captioned and non-audio viewers receive the complete intended meaning;
- the destination handoff works and is understood;
- independent product, trust and Brand scores each reach at least 17/20;
- human media review passes words-to-picture timing, edit rhythm, audio
  integrity and no generic/slideshow substitution;
- the candidate meets agreed size/performance budgets and has an exact rollback;
  and
- later distribution measures qualified completion, source/destination action
  and useful recall—not raw views, autoplay minutes or novelty.

Before an audience baseline exists, a small blinded comprehension comparison
using synthetic/consented reviewers is the admission measure. Publication
metrics do not decide creative truth.
