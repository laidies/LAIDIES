## Product promise

KSVL turns LAiDIES lessons into a repeatable sound world: original episode songs, town broadcast texture and clear routes back to the lesson. A first-time listener should know what is playing, why it belongs, how to control it and where to continue; a returner should find a dependable current/archival rotation without surprise audio or stale claims.

## Scope and sub-champions

- **Building steward:** radio arrival, broadcast/listen choices, listener comprehension, brand/editorial coherence and handoff to source lesson.
- **DJ Booth sub-champion:** album/mix interaction, queue, play/pause/seek/repeat, single-audio coordination, Spotify outbound links and mobile/keyboard use.
- **Catalogue & weekly rotation sub-champion:** source/metadata/artwork/track order, episode linkage, original-audio/rights record, freshness, archive and removal/replacement policy.

The steward does not create songs, clear rights, submit song requests, inspect listener data, deploy audio, or claim playback as verified without a real device/browser test.

## Journey contract

| Journey | Trigger | Honest completion | Persistence/result | Failure boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start listening | Press KSVL/mix/track play | Browser reports audible playback and current title/control state updates | May retain local player state only if labelled | Autoplay block, missing media, metadata/seek failure and pause must be visible/recoverable |
| Browse catalogue | Open radio/Booth/album | Track identity, source lesson and availability are clear | No completion/reward claim | Stale/missing/restricted track must not masquerade as playable |

| Song request | Submit request form | Service confirms receipt/held state | Current code may hold unsigned-in request locally; actual insert is external | No promise that DJ heard, selected or produced a song |
| Continue learning | Follow liner note/episode link | Linked source route opens | Source owns learning completion | Rights/metadata errors do not invent a lesson connection |

## Non-negotiable gates

Every published track needs an identifiable source, duration/format/metadata integrity, rights/ownership status, appropriate lyric/content boundary and a correct lesson/source link. Original LAiDIES music and short original hooks are preferred; borrowed lyrics/dialogue cannot become downloadable merchandise or decontextualized claims. No autoplay, surprise loudness, invasive listening telemetry, raw request text in analytics, or paid access to foundational learning.

## Cadence

Invoke on a track/metadata/player/episode/rights change, audio incident, weekly rotation, request-service change, listener safety issue or Ali/orchestrator request. Weekly rotation receives a provenance/freshness check; monthly review covers dead media, rights, source links, accessibility and catalogues; no trigger means no idle model call.
