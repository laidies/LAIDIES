## Intended outcome

Select an owner-review-ready page composition in which a first-time visitor
understands and operates the Resident Card from inside the vanity, every
required local control remains real and accessible, the four visitor scopes
are visibly truthful, and shared backend promises remain adapters/held states
rather than being reinvented.

## Competition contract

The allowed variable is the **room/object composition and disclosure order**.
All candidates must preserve:

- the same four visitor scopes and state authority;
- the same Resident Card fields and seven drawer jobs;
- local-first creation without required sign-in;
- the same provider/session definition of account-backed state;
- the same Closet producer/consumer contract;
- the same avatar, privacy, ownership, rewards and cross-device holds;
- the same No. 6 canon and MAiKEOVER on MAiN name;
- the same semantic controls and accessibility floor; and
- the same sitewide-style uncertainty.

Create three structurally distinct 1440×1024 desktop candidates and their
390×844 mobile translations:

1. **Incumbent refined — vanity plus adjacent open drawer.** Preserve the
   current straight-on room; an opened physical drawer expands within or
   immediately over its counter zone while the Card stays readable.
2. **Mirror focus — Card stage plus pull-out tool tray.** Keep the large mirror
   dominant; drawer selection brings one tool tray forward without sending the
   visitor downpage.
3. **Salon circuit — chair, vanity and reception as one room sequence.** The
   local Card action remains first; Finish shifts attention to a distinct
   reception zone without turning account claim into a prerequisite.

Each candidate must show the same four frames:

- first-time empty Card;
- returning-without-Card truth state;
- restored device-local Card with one drawer open; and
- verified-account-backed layout using clearly labelled fixture data only.

The fourth frame is UI-contract evidence, not proof that the service works.

## Functional prototype requirements

- Use a fixture adapter with exactly four visitor-state values; do not infer
  account state from a handle or local key.
- Preserve one real form control per choice. Drawer art/chrome cannot replace
  labels, groups, validation, focus or status.
- Only one drawer panel is expanded; focus moves deliberately and returns to
  its trigger on close.
- The Card remains legible while changing controls.
- Candidate mirrors are hidden and removed from the focus order when the
  avatar service is held or no candidates exist.
- Finish performs the existing atomic local envelope operation in a
  local-only prototype; account-backed frames use fixtures and visible
  `NOT SERVICE PROOF` marking.
- The reception desk states service availability. A held account path cannot
  accept or retain an email.
- Every motion effect has a no-motion state with the same information.

## Acceptance evidence

The championship passes only when:

1. all three candidates use the identical contract/invariant matrix;
2. every visible control and object has a real product job or is explicitly
   decorative;
3. no candidate invents a backend, entitlement, reward, public identity or
   cross-device promise;
4. a first-time user can point to how to start, see what changes and explain
   where the save lives;
5. returning-without-Card is not collapsed into first-time or member state;
6. device-local and account-backed Cards are visibly distinct;
7. desktop and mobile show the complete interaction rather than a cropped
   hero;
8. an independent full-resolution visual reviewer passes the hard admission
   checklist;
9. an independent accessibility reviewer passes the functional prototype; and
10. blind scores meet 17/20 for product quality, trust/accuracy and positive
    LAiDIES contribution.

## Integration plan

After Ali admits one direction, the champion writes a narrow implementation
packet that maps the winning regions to existing MAiKEOVER DOM/control IDs.
Frontend work may reorganize page-scoped HTML/CSS and presentation adapters,
but must preserve the tested local envelope and consume shared identity and
ownership interfaces supplied by Functionality & Platform. Any required shared
contract change becomes a durable handoff to that owner before implementation.

## Release, measurement and rollback

- **Release gate:** winning visual approved; exact functional candidate passes
  four-state local/fixture suite; shared states pass only when real services
  independently pass; artifact identity and public-origin proof are bound.
- **Measurement:** privacy-safe state class, drawer opened, local save result,
  Closet handoff result, account-flow availability and comprehension; never
  names, handles, email, selections, avatar data or tokens.
- **Rollback:** page-scoped candidate can revert to the last verified local
  route without deleting or migrating resident state. Shared services are not
  rolled back by this packet.
