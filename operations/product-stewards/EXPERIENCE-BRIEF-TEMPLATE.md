This brief is the authority from which the operating specification and build
packets are derived. Current code and current pages are observation evidence;
they do not become product intent merely because they exist.

Every governing statement must end with one provenance label:

- `ALI CONFIRMED`
- `LOCKED LEDGER`
- `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- `VERIFIED USER/PRODUCT EVIDENCE`
- `CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION OBSERVED`
- `INFERENCE`
- `UNKNOWN`

Only Ali-confirmed, locked-ledger and approved-brief/artifact statements may
govern canon. Evidence may challenge or support intent. Inference must be
tested or competed. Unknowns stay visible or return as one bounded decision.
