## Product promise

Blend & Snap is SUNNYVAiLE’s coffee-shop third place and the weekly place to **order** the Study Pack. JoJo knows a returning visitor’s usual on this device, tells her whether a current pack is fresh or collected, and hands her one coherent next step: review, practise, remember, then go next door to SUNNYVAiLE High to check understanding.

The building is not a generic link hub, a productivity demand disguised as a café, or a source of account-backed progress claims. The counter/menu and noticeboard must do the navigation work as readable, obvious objects in the room.

## Owned scope

- `blend-snap.html`: JoJo/café arrival, local “usual” and current-pack state, Study Pack order, past packs, noticeboard, and KSVL theme moment.
- **Study Pack sub-champion:** compact review/reference and its honest relationship to the Episode, Cheat Sheet, and Quiz. A complete Study Sheet route is not currently built.
- **Try-On sub-champion:** episode-specific applied exercise, prompt copying, local reflection save and return/debrief route.
- **Trading Cards sub-champion:** weekly concept-card packs, pack opening, collection/duplicate state, terminology and collection truth.
- The weekly handoff between Chick Flicks/episode, Blend & Snap, SUNNYVAiLE High/Quiz, My Closet, and community debrief.

## Boundaries

- Chick Flicks owns the episode’s audience promise and canon; SUNNYVAiLE High owns quiz assessment and any earned reward.
- Identity, Clubhouse Pass, cross-device collection sync and shared reward ledger remain shared-platform work. This champion may expose their truth and escalate defects; it may not create a competing reward store.
- A Try-On is an applied, bounded practice move—not a generic worksheet or a page that pretends to run the user’s AI task.
- Trading Cards are collection/memory reinforcement, not proof of learning or an earned account reward until the authoritative reward journey exists.

## Journey contract

| Journey | Honest completion | Current persistence / truth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter café | Visitor understands she can order the current Study Pack and follow the noticeboard | No completion required |
| Choose usual | A drink preference is selected | `localStorage` on this device only |
| Order current pack | She opens the order slip and chooses “Take it to a table” | Current-pack stamp is written when that issue link is opened; this is a navigation proxy, not proof she studied |
| Study Pack | She enters a released episode-specific review/practice/reference route | Study Sheet is planned; other components have distinct state |
| Try-On | She completes her real-task exercise and optionally saves a reflection | Local save and local weekly visit marker only |
| Trading Cards | She opens a pack, flips cards, and sees local collection/duplicate state | Local browser collection and last-pull state only |
| Quiz handoff | She follows to SUNNYVAiLE High for assessment | Quiz owns its outcome/reward; this building must not claim it |
| Return | She sees fresh/caught-up state appropriate to her device-local pack stamp | No cross-device or account claim |

## Quality rules

- The locked verb is **ORDER**. The room must retain the menu/counter as its interface, not regress to cards, text lists, or invisible image hotspots.
- Follow the LIBRAiRY model: real object-world renders composited into a room, controls obvious at rest, opening in place where the task benefits from it.
- “Your usual,” pack pickup, Try-On saves, cards and all rewards must state their actual persistence class. Device-local is useful and truthful; it is not Clubhouse Pass sync.
- Every weekly component needs a distinct job: Study Sheet = compact review; Try-On = practice; Cheat Sheet = reference/download; Cards = collect/remember; Quiz = check and earn beside the Pack.
- Do not create placeholder Study Sheets or make an episode pack look complete when its episode-specific components are absent or under review.

## Steward triggers

- A published episode, pack data, episode canon, study asset, content route or menu feed changes.
- A current-pack/return-state, local-save, card/duplicate, deep-link, media or accessibility failure.
- Any reward/persistence wording or Clubhouse Pass integration changes.
- Repeated relevant user feedback, privacy-safe analytics threshold, or monthly health review once wired.
- A new object/visual treatment for the café, cards or Try-On requires an independent brand/UX review.

## Escalation rules

- **FIX BEFORE LAUNCH:** false progress/reward/sync claim; an “available” weekly pack with missing required content; a dead core handoff; lost or misrepresented local reflection/card state; inaccessible primary order/practice action.
- **HIDE/LABEL FOR LAUNCH:** planned Study Sheet, unfinished card collection/sync, or any activity/visual state lacking its required evidence.
- **Ali decision:** major café art/brand direction, study-pack hierarchy, monetization, new economy rules, or any change to the coffee-memory metaphor.
