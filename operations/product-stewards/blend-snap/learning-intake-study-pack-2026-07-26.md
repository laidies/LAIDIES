## Format-specific admission

A Study Sheet must answer a question the learner has immediately after the
episode, be compact enough to scan at the café, preserve nuance and analogy
limits, and route to the durable owner for deeper lookup. It must include:

- one accurate core model;
- one important “do not collapse these things” distinction;
- one episode example plus one new transfer example;
- one analogy with its stopping point when genuinely helpful;
- source/freshness notes for changing or disputed claims;
- a “choose your next move” map distinguishing Try-On, reference and Quiz;
- accessible mobile and printable presentation.

It must not repeat the episode script, become the Cheat Sheet, contain the
Try-On exercise, award a result, or summarize a concept whose canonical truth
is still held.
