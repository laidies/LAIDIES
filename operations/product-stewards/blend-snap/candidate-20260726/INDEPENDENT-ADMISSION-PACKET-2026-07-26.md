`arrive → read verified Special → optionally choose usual → ORDER → read

- current Episode 04 Study Sheet is planned and has no route;

## Admission questions

- Does the room/object journey make the product understandable faster than the
  incumbent ledger without depending on global style?
- Is the distinction between current inventory and interaction samples
  unmistakable to an unfamiliar visitor?
- Does the four-state presentation teach the device/account boundary without
  turning the page into a platform-status dashboard?
- Does the failure state preserve dignity and a useful next action?
- Are the Review, Practice, Reference, Remember and Assess jobs distinct enough
  to guide choice?
