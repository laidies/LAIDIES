# Learning scan — Visitor’s Centre native Safari admission v1

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Scope:** Visitor-owned dossier/evidence only

## Reusable success

A tiny local HTTP fixture can preserve the exact route and all local assets
while replacing only the readiness-projection response. This proves Safari’s
real fail-closed UI without editing, rebuilding or temporarily corrupting the
shared projection artifact.

**Prevention rule:** For browser-native negative-path admission, intercept the
dependency at an isolated origin; hash the unchanged route and the fixture;
assert the receiver mode, canonical count and completion-claim boundary in the
browser itself.

**Behind the Build angle:** “How we proved a town directory stays honest when
its readiness feed is broken.”

## Surprise

Computer-control text injection into Safari’s Web Inspector can be truncated
or diverted by autocomplete even when a call succeeds.

**Prevention rule:** Keep inspector expressions short, split longer expressions
into small chunks with a pause, and verify the rendered console command and
result before using it as evidence.

## Accessibility limitation

VoiceOver could be switched on and restored to off, and Safari exposed the
route’s semantic tree, but the bridge did not expose a reproducible speech or
VoiceOver-cursor transcript.

**Prevention rule:** Treat accessibility-tree proof and VoiceOver speech/cursor
proof as separate claims. Never upgrade the former into the latter.

under the bounded Visitor-only lock; Control Room may promote these entries
under shared-ledger authority.
