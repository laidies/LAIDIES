## Product job

The Visitor’s Centre is SUNNYVAiLE’s complete tourist-information centre. It
explains what the learning world is, why it is a town, what every content format
and building does, and how to go directly to the right place.

The explanation order is locked:

1. what SUNNYVAiLE is;
2. why LAiDIES uses a town and Rewind Era memory to teach AI;
3. why episodes, news, books, activities, songs and future classes serve
   different learning jobs;
4. routes based on a visitor’s goal or desired content; and
5. every building’s exact name, purpose, current truth and direct route.

The Homepage contains only the compact version. The Centre owns the full
orientation, help and complete directory.

## Arrival room and map

- The masthead is a new responsive interior of a SUNNYVAiLE tourist-information
  centre—not a generic travel illustration or the map.
- Meaningful room objects include a counter, town information, displays,
  leaflets/tour materials, signs and practical visitor-desk equipment.
- Titles, labels, controls and dynamic information remain deterministic live UI.
- No admitted existing image currently performs this masthead job. The old lobby
  is rejected. The canonical town map remains a separate discovery object.
- The map can reveal building information or route directly, but it is never the
  only navigation method. Every building also has an exact-name accessible link.

## One orientation system, two entrances

The Centre maintains one canonical building/content map with two views:

- **Show me the town:** building-by-building orientation and direct routes.
- **Help me do something:** routes by goal, including learn, understand current
  events, ask/find, follow the Wednesday experience, practise, listen, connect
  and explore.

These are not two tours and must not duplicate descriptions, availability or
state. Visitor-facing labels may be refined during exact page review.

## Guided tour

- The tour is optional, contextual and non-blocking.
- A visitor can start, pause, skip, leave and resume without losing access to
  the page or route she selected.
- Destination explanations are short and contextual; returning visitors do not
  repeatedly receive first-time overlays.
- Tour state is device-local unless an account contract proves otherwise.
- Opening a stop does not prove that destination was used or completed.

- Use the same canonical header structure, LAiDIES identity, type hierarchy,
  eyebrow rules, spacing scale and control grammar as every public page.

## Visitor-state truth

- First-time, returning anonymous, device-local Resident Card and verified
  account-backed visitors receive the same complete usable core.
- Returning visitors may resume valid local tour state without forced onboarding.
- Resident identity never gates orientation. Personalization uses only verified
  shared identity fields and states its device/account scope.
- Route selection is a successful Centre handoff; it is not learning completion,
  reward, account creation or destination success.
