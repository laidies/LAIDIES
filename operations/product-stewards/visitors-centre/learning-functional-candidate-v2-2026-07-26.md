## Qualifying failure — page-level overflow passed while a heading clipped

The first 379-check attempt had no page-level horizontal overflow, but visual
inspection of the exact desktop screenshot showed `SUNNYVAiLE` clipping out of
its welcome panel and into the adjacent map composition. The grid and font were
rebalanced, the screenshots were regenerated and the exact candidate was
rehash-tested before independent admission.

**Prevention rule:** responsive evidence must combine page-level overflow
checks with visual inspection and component-bound checks for large headings,
labels and action groups. “The document did not scroll sideways” does not prove
that every component stayed inside its intended region.

**Possible Behind the Build angle:** “The page fit. The headline didn't.”

## Reusable success — exact parity stopped the prototype becoming a second truth

The initial prototype used accurate but shortened destination limitation copy.
An exact candidate-to-production-to-shared-directory comparison caught two
remaining Library wording differences after the larger reconciliation.
Candidate text was restored to exact current Centre-contract parity and the
suite now checks all 17 IDs, names, routes, states, summaries and limitations.

**Prevention rule:** an isolated prototype that consumes canonical directory or
admission truth must expose its fixture inventory to a deterministic parity
test. Plausibly equivalent wording is still a fork when another owner controls
the current claim.

**Possible Behind the Build angle:** “Our safe prototype was two commas away
from becoming a second source of truth.”
