## Learning scan

The reusable prevention rule is to sequence canonical-name propagation after
any checksum-bound route acceptance already in flight. First accept or hold the
frozen tuple; then treat naming as a separately hashed successor. This avoids
either admitting stale bytes or discarding authorized work. The legacy-browser
review adds a second rule: classify every failure by exact assertion count and
current authority source; a newer passing suite does not by itself prove that
an older failure is stale.
