# Visitor’s Centre — canonical name ruling

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Authority:** Ali  
**Status:** LOCKED — APPLY ACROSS ACTIVE AND PUBLIC SURFACES

The canonical building name is **Visitor’s Centre**.

Drop **Welcome Wagon** from the building name and from current visitor-facing
labels for the building. Do not present “Welcome Wagon” as an alternate
building, route name, navigation label, homepage CTA, metadata title, readiness
destination or onboarding-product name.

The route remains `/visitors-centre.html`. The building’s job remains
orientation: explain LAiDIES and SUNNYVAiLE, help a visitor understand what is
available and let her choose one useful destination.

Historical evidence, rejected prototypes and existing asset filenames may
retain the old wording when renaming would falsify provenance or break paths.
They do not establish current naming authority. Any visible sign or artwork
containing the old building name must be classified for correction during the
post-brand-lock asset audit.

Required active consumers include:

- product registry, dossier and state;
- public page title, metadata, headings, footer and fallback copy;
- homepage, Start Here and first-visit introduction;
- shared directory, quick navigation, tour and contextual-return labels;
- readiness projection source/runtime and Platform promise records;
- Resident Card/Closet destination labels;
- current design/build briefs and future visual prompts.

Acceptance requires a scoped active/public search with no remaining
visitor-facing instance that names the building “Welcome Wagon,” plus route,
metadata and representative mobile navigation checks.
