# Learning handoff: a current Tribune treatment of AGI and ASI

**Status:** CAPTURED / CREATE ROUTE — no article commissioned or published

**Source:** Ali direct request, 2026-08-10

**Concept owner:** Learning System & Concepts Director

**Surface owner:** NewsStand / The Tribune

## Distinct cognitive job

`AI Fundamentals 101` owns the durable foundation: what AGI and ASI mean, why
AGI has no universally accepted threshold, why ASI remains hypothetical and
how both differ from generative, agentic, embodied and general-purpose AI.

The Tribune should own the current sourced argument: who is claiming progress
toward AGI or ASI now; which definition and evidence each claim uses; where
credible researchers disagree; what incentives shape the claim; what evidence
would change the conclusion; and why the debate matters to LAiDIES readers at
work and in public decisions.

The article must deep-link to the exact admitted Fundamentals section for the
durable definitions. It may not create a competing definition or silently
rewrite the book.

## Required source and freshness floor

- exact current primary statements from each organization or researcher whose
  position is discussed;
- current peer-reviewed or primary research on operational AGI definitions and
  evaluation, including disagreements rather than one preferred ladder;
- dated separation of demonstrated capability, inference, forecast and
  advocacy;
- explicit treatment of benchmark limits, transfer, robustness, reliability
  and the absence of evidence for current ASI;
- a freshness trigger for material new definitions, evaluations, system claims
  or governance developments.

## Reader outcome

The reader can explain why “AGI is here” is not one testable statement until
the speaker supplies a definition, threshold and evidence; distinguish AGI
from agentic AI and ASI; identify what is demonstrated versus forecast; and
name the workplace or public decision affected by the claim.

## Acceptance boundary

NewsStand decides whether and when to commission, draft, review and publish the
article. Learning reviews concept integrity and learner transfer. Accuracy and
NewsStand owners review current claims and sources. Ali retains editorial and
public-release authority. This handoff creates no live link, draft, deploy,
publication or spend.
