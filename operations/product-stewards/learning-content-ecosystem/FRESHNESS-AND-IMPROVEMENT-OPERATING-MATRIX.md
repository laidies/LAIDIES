## Source tiers

1. **Authority:** official provider, original research/data, regulator,
   standard or actual product behavior. These can support a claim within their
   stated limits.
2. **Trusted scout:** repeatedly useful, transparent expert analysis such as
   AIDB or Ethan Mollick. These identify questions and evidence trails; they do
   not replace primary verification.
3. **Candidate lead:** creator post, reel, screenshot, anecdote, community
   discussion or competitor pattern. These may trigger a test or question but
   are never published as truth without independent verification.
4. **Rejected/unresolved:** provenance missing, claim overstated, conflict
   unresolved or result irreproducible. Preserve the lesson; suppress the
   claim.

Every source record states its distinct contribution, what it cannot
establish, last successful check, independent cursor, expected cadence and
fallback. A source being famous does not make each claim current or correct.

## Evaluation sequence

For each new item:

1. extract the exact claim, workflow, concept or opportunity;
2. resolve the original source and current primary evidence;
3. separate source fact, scout interpretation and LAiDIES inference;
4. test the behavior when the claim is practical;
5. search current canonical LAiDIES content and its consumer graph;
6. decide `UPDATE`, `LINK`, `MERGE`, `ADD`, `REPLACE`, `REMOVE`, `WATCH`,
   `NO_CHANGE` or `HOLD`;
7. choose the narrowest format whose job is genuinely missing;
8. record prerequisites, contradictions, downstream links and derivative
   opportunities;
9. create an executable work order, `NO_BUILD_REQUIRED`, or
   `LATER_WITH_TRIGGER`; and
10. dispatch under `AUTONOMOUS-CONTENT-EXECUTION-CONTRACT.md`.

## Improvement loop

Every product owner checks user friction, broken promises, health evidence,
freshness results, analytics when available, support/feedback, accessibility,
performance and repeated production pain. The result must become one of:

- an automatically executable reversible improvement;
- a bounded experiment with success/stop measures;
- a decision-ready Ali choice;
- a prerequisite build with owner and retry date;
- `LATER_WITH_TRIGGER`; or
- `NO_MATERIAL_OPPORTUNITY` with inspected evidence.

Analytics remain `UNKNOWN`, not zero, until an approved aggregate source is
connected. Lack of analytics does not stop correctness, accessibility,
freshness or observed-friction repairs, but it does prevent invented claims
about popularity or impact.
