## The ruled relationship

The Omnibus dates, Article 5 scope, Article 50 transition and any particular
organization's legal duties are dated legal facts. They remain in The Breaking,
with its legal-source, freshness and correction contract. This intake does not
turn a current EU law amendment into a general legal-advice lesson.

The small durable residue is a reading discipline, not a self-classification
method: a legal regime can have different clocks for entry into force,
application and transition; whether a clock applies turns on the exact article,
system category, role and jurisdiction. A headline about "the law" can therefore
be materially incomplete. That is already compatible with the existing
`Timely product/policy developments` concept-map row, which assigns current
policy facts to NewsStand and warns against stretching a dated story into
evergreen curriculum.
