## 1. Trigger and intended result

- **Working title:** Learn from Summer Adventure without copying Summer
  Adventure
- **Trigger:** Ali asked whether AIDB's engagement and training style contains
  useful patterns that LAiDIES should adapt.
- **Intended learner:** A woman who wants to use AI more capably but does not
  want a rigid course, an overwhelming curriculum or a gamified obligation.
- **Problem:** LAiDIES has strong episodes, Study Packs, buildings, practical
  tools and collectible systems, but the path between them can still feel like
  a catalogue rather than one purposeful learning journey.
- **Observable result:** The learner can choose a route that fits her available
  time and ambition, make one useful thing, understand what she practised, save
  the result appropriately and see a truthful next step.
- **Why now:** Weekly episodes and Study Packs are being standardized; building
  stamps, Butterfly Clips and Closet keepsakes are specified but not yet one
  complete account-backed learning loop.

## 2. What Summer Adventure actually does

The current public landing page presents 23 hands-on destinations across three
tiers, from using existing tools through agentic workflows. AIDB describes the
experience as self-directed, filterable by level and topic, and organized
around projects rather than a linear syllabus.

Observed product pattern:

1. **Choose by commitment.** Quick Trips are small; Excursions leave a useful
   object behind; Expeditions change a larger workflow or system.
2. **Give each destination one job.** The page explains the project, useful
   prompts, optional extensions and a stretch goal.
3. **End with an artifact.** Representative destinations create a reusable
   skill, personal context block, small app, agentic loop or business plan.
4. **Make progress visible.** Completed destinations receive passport stamps
   and titles.
5. **Pair completion with reflection.** The current experience uses a short
   "postcard" reflection; progress toward titles and a certificate is tied to
   reflected completions rather than a page visit alone.
6. **Release in a season.** New destinations and occasional challenges make
   the map feel alive without requiring one fixed sequence.
7. **Keep sharing optional.** A passport may remain private or be made public.

The learning strength is **choice + tangible output + reflection + visible
continuity**. The summer/travel skin is incidental.

## 3. Evidence boundary and critique

- AIDB and Summer Adventure are useful scouts and comparables, not LAiDIES
  instructional authority.
- The landing page's program count changed from earlier 22-destination copy to
  23 on 2026-07-29. LAiDIES must not hard-code volatile catalogue counts.
- A stamp and reflection establish participation, not comprehension, transfer
  or mastery. A certificate after a small number of completions must not be
  treated as evidence of capability.
- A catalogue of 20-plus options can create choice overload. LAiDIES should
  show one current route and a small number of clearly different alternatives,
  with the rest discoverable.
- Weekly drops can become FOMO. LAiDIES routes remain replayable, never expire
  and do not use broken-streak language.
- Tool-specific activities become stale quickly. Every product, model,
  capability, plan, region and permission claim needs a dated source and
  refresh trigger.
- Summer Adventure's terms label the program content for personal learning and
  prohibit redistribution, teaching it as a program or commercial reuse
  without permission. LAiDIES may study product mechanics; it must not copy
  destination text, prompts, branded titles, artwork or curriculum structure.
- The program warns learners not to enter confidential, customer, credential,
  source-code or other sensitive material. LAiDIES routes require a
  task-specific privacy check and must never turn a universal personal dossier
  into the default exercise.

## 5. LAiDIES adaptation

### The useful unit: one Town Route

A Town Route connects existing products around one learner result:

> choose a useful job → visit the right place → make or practise something →
> make the final call → save or use the result → receive the next honest route

Every admitted route must state:

- what she will leave with;
- expected time and effort;
- what she needs before starting;
- the exact building and existing activity used;
- a low-risk/private-information boundary;
- one required action and any optional stretch;
- what counts as meaningful completion;
- a two-question private reflection: **What changed? What will you do next?**
- whether anything is saved, where, for how long and who can see it; and
- the next useful route, including an honest stop state.

### Choice without a new curriculum

Use three plain commitment labels as filters, not three compulsory levels:

| Commitment | Learner job | Typical existing LAiDIES surface |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick Stop** | Understand or try one small thing now | Cheat Sheet, Trading Cards, one small Try-On |
| **Field Trip** | Practise and leave with one useful artifact | Full Try-On, High comparison/clinic, building tool |
| **Big Move** | Change a recurring workflow over more than one session | Class/workshop plus verified real-work application |

Names are working language only; Brand and Ali retain naming approval.

### Progress and ownership

- Reuse the existing building stamp for a meaningful building action.
- Reuse Butterfly Clips only where the economy contract admits the action.
- Save eligible artifacts to My Closet through the account-backed keepsake
  contract.
- Do not award progress for opening a page, scrolling, clicking "done" or
  entering a minimum number of characters.
- Do not require public sharing. If an optional show-what-you-made route is
  later admitted, Sorority House/Rooms owns the social job and its privacy and
  moderation contract.
- Treat a stamp as participation/relationship progress, never mastery.

## 6. Smallest useful pilot

**Pilot one route; do not build a 23-item map.**

Use the completed Episode 01 experience as the first candidate only after its
three core Study Pack items clear their existing admission gates:

1. **Start:** Episode 01 at The Chick Flicks.
2. **Remember:** Episode 01 Cheat Sheet at Blend & Snap.
3. **Practise:** the approved Episode 01 Three Tabs, One Task Try-On.
4. **Judge:** record which response fit the task and what needed changing.
5. **Keep:** optionally save the eligible Study Pack keepsake to My Closet;
   personal task text and pasted answers remain excluded by default.
6. **Continue:** offer the separate relevant SUNNYVAiLE High practice as the
   next route, not as a fourth Study Pack item.

This pilot tests whether connecting existing surfaces helps a learner finish a
useful sequence. It does not alter the locked three-item Study Pack.

### Pilot acceptance evidence

- Five-second comprehension: an unfamiliar learner can state what she will do,
  how long it may take and what she will leave with.
- No duplicate surface performs the same learner job.
- The completion event is a meaningful action, not a click.
- The learner can explain one difference she observed and one change she made.
- Account-backed Closet claims pass the existing end-to-end persistence
  contract before appearing.
- No private prompt/output is synced or shown publicly by default.
- Every current provider/model claim has an official source, checked date,
  applicable surface and refresh trigger.
- A learner can stop after any stage without guilt, lost progress or a broken
  route.
