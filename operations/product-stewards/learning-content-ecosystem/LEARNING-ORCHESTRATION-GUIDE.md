# LAiDIES learning orchestra — format roles, depth and handoffs

**Status:** CANONICAL INTERNAL OPERATING GUIDE

**Owner:** Learning System & Concepts Director

**Purpose:** make every LAiDIES format perform a distinct, useful part of one
learning journey. This guide controls routing and complement decisions; it does
not approve, publish or release an artifact.

## The learner promise before the format

Before choosing a format or writing copy, every substantial learning item must
answer:

1. **Why this:** what real problem, decision, opportunity or curiosity makes
   the concept worth teaching?
2. **Why now:** why is it useful at this point in the learner's journey or in
   the world?
3. **Where it lives:** in what recognizable part of her work, home, creative or
   civic life will she meet it?
4. **What changes:** what will she be able to explain, notice, do, diagnose,
   decide, challenge or create afterward?
5. **How she will use it:** name a concrete next situation in which the learning
   changes her action.
6. **What it connects to:** identify prerequisites, adjacent concepts,
   misconceptions, the canonical durable explanation, the experience this item
   extends and the next useful experience.

If those answers are vague, the item is not ready for a format decision.

## One concept, different jobs

| Format | Unique job | Appropriate depth | LAiDIES style | Must hand off to | Must not become |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Episode** | Create the need, stakes and memorable first encounter through character, story and consequence; show the idea changing what happens. | Enough mechanism for the story and mental model to be truthful; leave complete procedure, extended distinctions and assessed practice to their proper homes. | Cinematic, funny, emotionally intelligent, Rewind Era specific; never condescending. | Durable lookup when needed; class/lab for practice; relevant current application; Study Pack for retrieval. | A lecture, class transcript, generic explainer or exhaustive manual. |
| **Study Pack** | Help a learner retrieve, rehearse and apply an episode's already-ruled learning away from the film. | Compact: one accurate reference, one practice move, useful cards/printable support and an honest next step. | Tactile, collectible, clear, witty and usable at a glance. | Episode source, canonical reference, quiz/practice and Closet collection. | A second textbook, an unrelated worksheet bundle or competing canon. |
| **Pop Quiz / assessment** | Retrieve and discriminate; reveal whether the learner can recognize or use the idea, with explanatory feedback and replay. | Only concepts already taught or explicitly available in the question context; feedback explains why, not merely right/wrong. | Playful school ritual with fair questions and useful recovery. | Exact lesson/reference for misses; progress/reward record after a valid attempt. | First instruction, trivia for its own sake or a completion trap. |
| **LIBRAiRY entry or book** | Give the accurate, durable, revisable home for something a learner needs to look up, distinguish or revisit. | Entry: concise definition and distinction. Book/chapter: mechanism, examples, limits, sources and connected concepts. | Smart best-friend reference voice; precise, readable and rich without becoming academic sludge. | Related story for meaning; class for skill; dated NewsStand application where relevant. | An episode recap, class transcript, breaking-news archive or vague inspiration. |
| **Class / lesson / lab** | Build observable skill: explain the mechanism, compare, practise, diagnose, receive feedback and transfer to a new situation. | Deep enough to answer why X causes Y, show good/bad/changed examples, distinguish system layers and support independent use. A class may contain several resumable lessons. | Teach your smartest best friend: warm, rigorous, story-led, visual and never generic. | Prerequisite concept; Try-On/tool; assessment; durable reference; next-level class or real task. | A feature tour, list of tips, one long passive video or memorized interface recipe. |
| **NewsStand article** | Explain what changed in the real world now, what the evidence supports and how durable concepts apply. | Source-complete for the dated claim; enough evergreen context to understand the event, with links to—not duplication of—the maintained lesson. | Brisk, discerning, specific and transparent about evidence, uncertainty and dates. | Canonical concept/reference; relevant class/tool; correction and freshness path. | The textbook, a press-release rewrite or timeless recommendation. |
| **Promptoscope** | Deliver one funny daily AI horoscope that helps the reader notice a recognizable work-with-AI pattern. | One truthful observation and a bounded, low-stakes nudge; source-linked internally when derived from governed learning. | Comedic horoscope language, specific and quotable; never mystical product guidance or a disguised ad. | Optional relevant tip, activity or deeper learning when genuinely useful. | A course, prediction of real outcomes, card reading, news summary or unsupported rule. |
| **AI daily tip / “What the viral reel left out”** | Give one immediately usable move, the condition under which it works and the important thing the short claim leaves out. | Small but complete: action + why + boundary + human check + deeper destination. | Crisp, practical, mildly mischievous and receipts-forward. | Class, lab, tool or reference that teaches the full setup and mechanism. | A context-free hack, copied creator content or universal promise. |
| **Career / work-life AI mirror** | Help with a real workplace situation and use the parallel with AI to illuminate both human judgment and system behavior. | Practical situation, choices, wording/example, risks and a faithful AI connection; route complex skills to deeper teaching. | Experienced friend, not HR poster: useful, humane, funny where earned and never blamey. | Relevant AI concept, class/lab, community reflection or reusable template. | Claiming AI solves structural problems, therapy, legal advice or a shoehorned analogy. |
| **KSVL song** | Turn a ruled idea, distinction or behaviour into a mnemonic and emotional retrieval cue. | One accurate hook plus enough context to avoid teaching the opposite; metadata links to the source learning. | A real song with period-aware genre, rhyme, character and replay value—not an educational jingle unless intentionally comic. | Episode/class/reference source; lyrics with concept note and analogy limit; correct artist/album artwork metadata. | The sole explanation of a nuanced concept or filler derivative. |
| **Interactive tool / FAiRY help** | Complete an immediate real job while making the important inputs, transformation, assumptions and limits visible. | Task-complete with diagnosis, safe defaults, editability and a route to understand the mechanism. | Useful magic with receipts: warm, specific and honest about what it can and cannot do. | Relevant concept/class; saved artifact or next action; correction/help path. | A black-box answer oracle or substitute for judgment. |
| **Game / activity** | Rehearse a judgment, behaviour or sequence through consequence, feedback and replay. | Focused on one governed learning job; enough explanation to improve the next attempt. | Social, surprising and replayable; fun is part of the mechanism, not a skin. | Exact concept/lesson for recovery and a next challenge for mastery. | Unrelated engagement, opaque scoring or proof of mastery from a click. |
| **Community / Town Group Chat** | Let learners apply, explain, compare and support one another; use town life and comedy to make patterns visible. | Prompts and moderation support meaningful contribution without requiring private or sensitive disclosure. | Lived-in SUNNYVAiLE voice: generous, funny, specific and anti-competition. | Relevant learning, activity, resource or community response. | Fake social proof, unmoderated advice, compulsory sharing or duplicate instruction. |

## Depth and experience-level rule

`Beginner`, `working`, and `advanced` describe the assumed mental model—not the
importance or intelligence of the learner.

- **Beginner:** pre-teach the essential components, make the causal chain
  visible, remove hidden prerequisites and give a safe first transfer.
- **Working:** use realistic constraints, compare approaches, diagnose failures
  and require independent adaptation.
- **Advanced:** examine architecture, trade-offs, evaluation, uncertainty,
  governance and multi-step system design; do not add jargon merely to signal
  sophistication.

Every item declares its entry assumptions and exit capability. A learner may
enter LAiDIES at any level and should receive an honest route forward, not be
forced through basic content she can already demonstrate.

## Required concept package

Before production, bind one concept package containing:

- stable concept ID and canonical owner;
- the six learner-promise answers above;
- entry level, prerequisite and exit capability;
- mechanism and evidence boundary;
- adjacent concepts and named misconceptions;
- analogy mapping and limit, or a ruled no-analogy decision;
- distinct format jobs selected from the table;
- inbound links, outbound next steps and correction consumers;
- freshness trigger and correction owner; and
- an explicit **link / correct / update / extend / create / decline** ruling for
  every proposed surface.

## Anti-duplication test

Two treatments of the same concept are permitted only when all four are true:

1. each has a different learner job;
2. each uses its medium to do something the other cannot do as well;
3. each points to the other only where the handoff helps; and
4. one maintained concept truth and correction path governs both.

Changing the wording, colours, fictional wrapper or length does not create a
new learner job. When two items perform the same job, link, merge, update,
correct or retire one of them.

## Harmony and release gate

The binding evidence and enforcement rules live in
`operations/product-stewards/learning-content-ecosystem/CONTENT-QUALITY-ADMISSION-GATE.md`.
In particular, `format fit` means **feature adaptation**: every publishing
feature needs its own reverse brief, payload and rendered/playable proof. A
single generic derivative cannot satisfy several destinations merely because
the underlying concept is the same.

No substantial item advances beyond local build until its exact artifact proves:

- the learner promise and life/use case are specific;
- its format job, depth and voice match this guide;
- prerequisites and connected concepts are accurate;
- the next experience exists, is distinct and is honestly labelled for its
  current release state;
- search/index metadata uses the canonical concept identity;
- relevant inbound/outbound links are rendered and checked;
- duplication and contradiction scans are dispositioned; and
- every affected correction consumer is named.

The relationship graph may describe planned links, but a held or unbuilt
destination must not be presented as available.
