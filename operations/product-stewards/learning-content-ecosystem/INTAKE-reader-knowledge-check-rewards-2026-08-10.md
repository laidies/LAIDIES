## Ali's idea

LAiDIES learning content should help a reader check what she understands and
should connect meaningful learning progress to the town's rewards.

## Ruling

**ACCEPT**, with one non-negotiable separation:

- the learning system decides what evidence a check can support;
- the surface owner designs and admits the actual activity;
- the shared reward owner decides whether the admitted outcome may issue an
  existing reward; and
- a reward event never becomes evidence that learning occurred.

This is an extension of the existing learning and Butterfly Clip contracts,
not a new quiz product, local wallet or reward currency.

## Knowledge evidence ladder

| Level | Reader can… | Representative check | Truthful result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recognise | distinguish the concept from its most likely confusion | choose and explain which example fits | `CHECKED` |
| Explain | reconstruct the cause-and-effect mechanism in her own words | explain-back against a reasoned answer/checklist | `CONNECTED` when all required links are present |
| Draw | place the parts and relationships in a usable model | label, order or connect a diagram | `CONNECTED` when the required relationships are present |
| Apply | use the mechanism in a new ordinary or workplace case | unseen case with a reasoned choice or action | `APPLIED` |
| Retrieve later | recover and reconnect the idea after time or intervening learning | later-chapter or return check | preserves or changes the evidence state; never a streak |

`I don't know yet` and a wrong first answer must route to specific teaching and
feedback. They are not punishments. A successful retry after feedback is a
valuable learning outcome. No single book self-check may claim `MASTERED`.

## Reward boundary

- Reuse Butterfly Clips only through the shared, server-authorised economic
  ledger and an admitted action registry.
- A first admitted chapter checkpoint may qualify as one bounded meaningful
  learning event. It is deduplicated by reader, content ID and version.
- A distinct, capped improvement milestone may recognise successful repair
  after feedback; it may not be farmed through repeated guesses.
- A named knowledge collectible or merit-style milestone may recognise a
  larger connected-learning achievement only after the Closet/reward owner
  admits its meaning, asset and lifecycle.
- Do not pay for page views, scrolling, reading time, opening an answer,
  repeated clicks, streak preservation or raw quiz exhaustion.
- Do not remove currency for mistakes, rank readers by skill, expose private
  knowledge states on a leaderboard or lock core teaching behind identity.
- The feedback and reasoned answer appear whether or not the reader has a
  Resident Card. Identity is required only for a durable reward or saved
  progress when that path is actually built.

## Minimum activity contract

Every material chapter must provide:

1. one immediate misconception check near the teaching;
2. one explain-back or reconstruction activity;
3. one genuinely new application case;
4. reasoned feedback for every answer route;
5. an exact return link to the explanation that repairs an unmet link;
6. one cumulative Draw it / Explain it / Use it checkpoint; and
7. a later retrieval opportunity in a subsequent chapter or return check.

The activity may not test a term or mechanism above the chapter's
`ORIENT / TEACH / DEEPEN` state. A question bank is versioned with the concept
truth it assesses, so a corrected concept invalidates stale questions and
rewards.

## Privacy-safe evidence interface

The shared learning record may contain only accepted fields such as:

- `concept_id`, `surface_id`, `chapter_id`, `item_id`, `item_version`;
- `check_type` (`RECOGNISE`, `EXPLAIN`, `DRAW`, `APPLY`, `RETRIEVE_LATER`);
- `outcome_class` (`CHECKED`, `CONNECTED`, `APPLIED`, `REVIEW_AGAIN`);
- `attempt_band`, `assistance_band` and `new_or_returning`; and
- the admitted completion-event ID used by the reward ledger, when one exists.

Do not store the reader's raw free writing, drawing, prompt, workplace example,
name, email or private answer in ecosystem measurement. A surface that needs
such material requires its own explicit consent, retention and deletion
contract.

## Acceptance evidence before reward implementation

1. Learning owner admits the exact concept outcome and what the activity can
   and cannot prove.
2. Surface owner supplies an exact versioned activity with reasoned feedback,
   return routes and a calibrated known-bad fixture.
3. Unfamiliar-reader evidence shows the activity diagnoses and repairs at
   least one representative misconception without adding confusion.
4. Reward owner accepts one typed, bounded, idempotent event into the existing
   earning registry.
5. Platform proves grant, replay without duplication, failure without false
   balance, and same-account recovery through the authoritative ledger.
6. Closet/Resident Card shows the exact reason and bounded meaning without
   claiming mastery.
7. Public wording waits for live verification of the full event-to-wallet
   path.
