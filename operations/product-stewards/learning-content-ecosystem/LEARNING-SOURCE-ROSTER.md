# Learning source roster

**Status:** ACTIVE INTERNAL SOURCE ROUTER — NOT CONTENT AUTHORITY
**Owner:** Learning System & Concepts Director
**Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Operating rule

This roster identifies useful places to discover evidence, teaching patterns
and optional deeper-learning routes. Inclusion does not admit a claim,
commission an artifact or make an external source LAiDIES canon.

Before a source changes public learning content, the receiving owner must:

1. inspect the exact item, not only its channel, directory or author;
2. capture its title, author/instructor, publication or update date and stable
   URL;
3. distinguish the source's observed claim from LAiDIES inference;
4. return material factual claims to the strongest available primary evidence;
5. bind a freshness/correction trigger; and
6. route the learning through the existing concept, surface and prose-quality
   contracts.

Social feeds are discovery inputs only. Institutional reputation does not make
every item current, accurate, audience-appropriate or reusable.

## Surface routing

- **AI Fundamentals / LIBRAiRY:** use exact Stanford or MIT material only to
  check mechanisms, prerequisites and optional continuation; the book remains
  the durable LAiDIES explanation and cannot inherit an external course's
  sequence or audience assumptions.
- **Briefing 101 / Classes / practice:** Wharton may inform an existing
  purpose–context–test–revise practice when the receiving owner identifies a
  real learner gap. A prompt library is not a class commission.
- **NewsStand / AIDB / freshness:** Mollick social items are leads. The
  operative evidence is the original paper, product page, dataset, filing or
  official institutional record after current verification.
- **External links:** MIT and selected Stanford items may become optional
  deeper-learning routes only after exact-item accessibility, cost, level and
  freshness review. LAiDIES must explain why the link is useful and what prior
  knowledge it assumes.

## Cadence and correction

- Recheck every linked item immediately before first content use.
- Recheck directory/profile URLs every quarter or when ownership, access,
  pricing, syllabus or platform behaviour changes.
- A material source correction enters `freshness-signal-inbox.json`; it does
  not edit, retire or unpublish content automatically.

## Current disposition

`LINK / SOURCE-MINE / WATCH`, not `CREATE`. These sources strengthen existing
research and teaching inputs. They do not by themselves reveal a new canonical
concept or authorize an episode, class, book, tool, quiz, game, Study Pack item
or NewsStand publication.
