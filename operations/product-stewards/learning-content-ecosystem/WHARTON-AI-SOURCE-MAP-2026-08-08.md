## Decision

Wharton is a useful source **ecosystem**, not one prompt-library page. Adopt it
in layers:

1. use exact GAIL reports and their underlying papers for bounded prompting and
   learning-system claims;
2. use Wharton Human-AI Research and dated adoption reports for workplace,
   human-impact and governance evidence;
3. use course, webinar, podcast and product pages as teaching-pattern
   comparators or discovery leads unless the underlying evidence is inspected.

This is `SOURCE-MINE / LINK / WATCH`, not `CREATE`. No source below establishes
an unmet LAiDIES content job by itself.

## Findings that affect LAiDIES

### 1. Prompt advice must be tested as a claim

Wharton's GAIL reports make the strongest addition to the current system. They
show why a static list of supposedly universal prompt tricks is unsafe:
performance can change with the model, task, prompt variation, repetition and
the evaluator's definition of success. LAiDIES should use these reports to
strengthen the existing rule: state the job and criterion, test the exact
workflow repeatedly, inspect edge cases and revise or stop.

This supports existing Briefing 101, Try-On and verification jobs. It does not
justify a new prompt course.

### 2. Better learning is not merely better generated explanation

The AI-tutor summary points toward sequenced practice, productive persistence
and behavior-based learner evidence. That aligns with LAiDIES' distinction
between explanation, practice, retrieval and assessment. Before any numeric
claim or design mandate is adopted, the underlying study must be inspected.

This can inform LCE-005/LCE-010 measurement and progression work. It does not
authorize an automated tutor or replace unfamiliar-reader evidence.

### 3. Wharton is strongest for work, judgment and application

Wharton Human-AI Research provides better breadth than the Prompt Library for
future-of-work, organizational adoption, human judgment, governance,
creativity and practical business applications. Those are especially useful
for NewsStand, work-life mirrors, Episode 02's workplace territory and current
applications of the durable AI Fundamentals map.

The dated business sources remain applications of canon. They must not become
the evergreen conceptual authority.
