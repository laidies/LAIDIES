## Decision

> Episode explains → Study Sheet reviews → Try-On practises → Cheat Sheet
> supports later use → Cards strengthen retrieval → Quiz separately assesses.

The **Study Pack** coordinates Study Sheet, Try-On, Cheat Sheet and Cards. The
Quiz remains beside the pack under SUNNYVAiLE High. A pack is complete when
every component has an honest disposition and every selected component has
passed its own gate. `Not applicable` with a canon-backed reason is complete;
a placeholder is not.

No Episode 01–04 pack currently clears the complete content/learning contract.
That does not make all existing components unusable. It means Blend & Snap must
preserve their different statuses rather than letting a published episode or
attractive redesign lend approval to missing, overlapping, held or incorrect
children.

## Episode-specific Study Sheet build requirements

These are content briefs, not placeholder copy.

### Episode 01

- **Reader question:** What is generative AI, and what is the smallest safe
  first interaction?
- **Required relationship:** app/tool → model underneath → generated first
  pass → human judgment/verification.
- **Required distinction:** generative output is not ordinary search; the same
  task can produce different results across tools.
- **Misconception guard:** AI help does not remove the human decision or make
  a high-risk task safe.
- **Source constraint:** exclude the canon's still-pending adoption statistics
  and quotation unless their evidence is accepted for this derivative.
- **Continuation:** corrected Episode 01 Try-On; Open the Tab reference;
  separate Quiz after practice.

### Episode 02

- **Reader question:** Why does the same AI produce a useful answer one day and
  generic beige output the next?
- **Required relationship:** task + audience + context + constraints + example
  + revision = a usable brief, not a magic prompt.
- **Required distinction:** prompting is delegation; context is supplied
  information, not mind reading or guaranteed memory.
- **Misconception guard:** specificity improves the brief but does not
  guarantee truth or quality.
- **Continuation:** Episode 02 vague/specific Try-On; BRIEF Cheat Sheet;
  separate scenario-based Quiz.

### Episode 03

- **Reader question:** What can I use as a draft, and what must I verify before
  it borrows my name?
- **Required relationship:** draft → identify claims/assumptions → locate
  receipts outside the same chat → revise/qualify.
- **Required distinction:** fluent confidence, citation-shaped text and asking
  “are you sure?” are not independent verification.
- **Misconception guard:** Prompt Like Elle can expose gaps; it cannot verify
  its own output or replace the authoritative source/domain owner.
- **Continuation:** Prompt Like Elle Try-On; Receipts reference; separate
  evidence-triage Quiz.

### Episode 04

- **Reader question:** How did distinct contributions across two centuries
  become parts of today's AI field, and why does that history change who
  belongs in its next chapter?
- **Required relationship:** ordered contributions and their specific
  mechanisms, not one undifferentiated “women invented AI” claim.
- **Required distinction:** crediting a precursor or enabling contribution is
  not the same as claiming she single-handedly invented every descendant
  technology.
- **Misconception guard:** preserve the canon's exact guards for Ada, Hedy,
  Grace, ImageNet/AlexNet, AI winters and current bias research.
- **Continuation:** Founding Mothers timeline; LUMINAiRY field trip (not
  Try-On); corrected separate Quiz.
