# Episode 01 concept-card correction

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Status:** VERIFIED LOCALLY — researched and propagated  
**Owner:** Learning System & Concepts  
**Trigger:** Ali identified inaccurate, contradictory and patronizing Episode
01 card explanations.

## Decision

Episode 01's Trading Card Pack contains four concepts:

1. Generative AI
2. Model
3. Hallucination
4. Participation Gap

Invisible Load remains part of the episode's story and stakes. It is not a
concept card. The audience already understands the burden; defining it back to
women does not help them understand or use AI.

## Maintained explanations

### Generative AI

AI designed to create content—such as text, images, audio, video or code—using
patterns learned during training and the instructions and material it receives
now.

Memory line: **It generates. You still verify.**

### Model

A core component of an AI system that uses inputs to produce outputs. In
machine-learning systems, it is shaped through training. A model can be offered
through an app, an API or a coding tool, or combined with other models and
tools inside a larger product.

Episode 05 owns the maintained picture: the provider is the fashion house; the
model is the house's supermodel; an app is a boutique or department store where
people use one or more models.

### Hallucination

When generative AI produces false, erroneous or unsupported content and
presents it as part of an answer.

Episode example: every Burn Book entry was invented, and each was presented
with total confidence. Never describe the book as a mix of true and false
entries.

Memory line: **Confidence is not evidence.**

### Participation Gap

A measurable difference in how often groups use a technology. The May 2026
version of HBS Working Paper 25-023 estimates generative-AI adoption of 47.8%
for men and 39.3% for women among 318,924 respondents—a 22% relative gap. It
describes participation, not ability, and the estimate is not a claim about
every woman or man.

Memory line: **Women should not wait while AI is being shaped.**
