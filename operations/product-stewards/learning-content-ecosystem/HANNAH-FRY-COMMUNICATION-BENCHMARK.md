# Hannah Fry communication benchmark

**Status:** ACTIVE INTERNAL EXPLANATION LENS
**Benchmark ID:** `HANNAH_FRY_COMMUNICATION_LENS_V1`
**Owner:** Learning System & Concepts Director

## Purpose and boundary

This benchmark adapts communication mechanics previously analysed from Hannah
Fry's public science communication. It is not permission to imitate her voice,
persona, mannerisms, identity or the structure of a particular talk or book.
Her name is not evidence that a draft is clear or engaging.

The LAiDIES producer must translate the useful mechanics into the cognitive job
and scale of the destination. A class can support prediction, demonstration and
transfer. A book can create curiosity, make a hidden process concrete and route
a better next question without becoming a lecture. A reference, FAQ, practice
item, interactive or NewsStand explanation uses only the moves that improve its
own job. Promotional copy and microcopy declare the lens not applicable rather
than manufacturing teaching.

## Communication dimensions

1. **Human question:** begin from a reason the learner already has to care,
   choose, notice or challenge—not from terminology for its own sake.
2. **Useful curiosity:** invite a prediction, tension, surprise or question
   only when the answer will reveal the mechanism. A hook without explanatory
   payoff fails.
3. **Invisible process made concrete:** use the smallest faithful case,
   demonstration, diagram, comparison or sequence that lets the learner see
   what otherwise happens out of view.
4. **Familiar and technical movement:** connect ordinary experience to the
   correct abstraction and then return to the real system. A familiar example
   that never reconnects to the mechanism is decoration.
5. **Limitations and consequences:** retain where the explanation stops, what
   can go wrong, uncertainty, misuse and why the distinction matters.
6. **Humour and surprise:** use them only when they sharpen attention,
   distinction or recall without making the learner or subject childish.
   `NOT_APPLICABLE` is often the right choice.
7. **Better next question:** leave the learner able to ask, test or notice
   something more precise than before.

## Generation rule

Before drafting, the producer records which dimensions apply, why, the exact
experience or prose move planned for each, the destination-specific adaptation
and the no-imitation boundary. Full explanations apply at least six dimensions;
proportional formats apply at least two. The record fails if it merely says
"Hannah Fry inspired."

## Review and learning-loop rule

The producer and independent reviewer inspect the exact artifact for evidence
that the applied moves improve understanding and engagement while preserving
technical truth. They reject name-dropping, curiosity without payoff, a
familiar example with no technical return, pastiche and entertainment that
arrives before understanding. Any newly observed reusable failure follows the
shared learning-disposition route; feedback cannot automatically rewrite,
retire or unpublish content.
