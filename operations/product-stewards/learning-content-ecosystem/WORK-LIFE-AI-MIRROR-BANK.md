## The job of this bank

These are not generic confidence tips with an AI reference pasted on top. Each
seed starts with a real work situation, uses one accurate AI or workflow
principle to expose its structure, says where the comparison stops and gives
the reader a concrete next move.

The reader is capable. The problem may be incomplete context, unclear
ownership, a power dynamic, an unsafe workflow or a decision that has not been
made. It is not automatically her confidence, tone or attitude.

## Status key

- `PARENT FIRST` — build or accept the complete class, episode pass or
  reference entry before a Daily card.
- `DAILY CANDIDATE` — potentially fits one Daily screen after the parent and
  independent review pass.
- `NO DAILY` — too consequential or too nuanced for a short Daily treatment.
- Risk `L`, `M` or `H` means low, medium or high consequence; it is not a
  judgement about how difficult the situation feels.

## Release rule

No seed becomes a Daily card, class, Library entry, episode link or social post
until its exact parent, evidence, analogy limit, risk boundary and destination
pass. Parent correction, hold or expiry suppresses every derivative.
