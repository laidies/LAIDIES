# Autonomous content execution and release contract

**Status:** ACTIVE — standing operating rule
**Owner:** Control Room with Learning System & Concepts and the destination owner
**Authority:** Ali direction, 2026-08-02

## The rule

Ali review is an exception gate, not the start button.

When a content or improvement order is inside an accepted product contract,
has a named destination and owner, requires no unresolved taste, mission,
public-identity, spend, private-data or genuinely consequential policy choice,
the system must continue through build, independent review, owner acceptance,
release and exact public verification without asking Ali to say “continue.”

`CAPTURED`, `SPECIFIED`, `READY_TO_DISPATCH`, `BUILT_LOCALLY`, `REVIEWED`,
`RELEASED` and `VERIFIED_PUBLICLY` remain different states. Autonomy does not
permit a state to be skipped or inferred.

## Two execution paths

### Existing destination

If the destination page, product or feed exists and can truthfully perform the
assigned job, the receiving owner begins the order directly.

### Destination not ready

If the content cannot be useful or honestly released because its destination
experience is missing, incomplete or cannot display the required format:

1. create or reuse one prerequisite product/build order;
2. name the destination owner, exact missing capability and smallest useful
   vertical;
3. link every dependent content order to that prerequisite;
4. keep the content work active as `WAITING_ON_PREREQUISITE`, not an ownerless
   `BLOCKED`; and
5. return it automatically to dispatch when the prerequisite passes.

A missing page is a build dependency, not a reason to lose the content.

## Dispatch service levels

`READY_TO_DISPATCH` means executable now. It must acquire either a dispatch
receipt or an exact prerequisite within:

- **P0:** the current Control Room cycle, no later than four hours;
- **P1:** 24 hours;
- **P2:** 72 hours.

An overdue item becomes `EXECUTION_STALLED`. The next Control Room cycle must
either assign the free owner lane, record the collision and next retry time,
or create the missing prerequisite. Repeating the word `blocked` is not an
unblock action.

Every dispatch receipt records the work-order ID, owner, task/lane identity,
accepted scope, start time, next checkpoint and collision boundary. A
recommendation, handoff or validator PASS is not a dispatch receipt.

Dispatch is allowed to create the prevention-first producer contract; requiring
that contract before dispatch creates a deadlock. The contract is mandatory
before the primary artifact can truthfully claim `BUILT_LOCALLY` or enter
review. Each destination owner has a default work-in-progress limit of one
active primary content output, and active dispatches may not overlap paths.

## Review and release

Every primary artifact still passes its complete applicable governed chain: accuracy, anti-slop,
current best practice, LAiDIES voice, analogy integrity, usefulness/depth,
format fit, search/indexing, relationship linking, canon consistency,
accessibility, rights, product behavior and exact
rendered experience where applicable.

A possible song, tip, social post, newsletter item or other surface derivative
is parked unless it has its own distinct job and child work order. Parked work
cannot block the primary release. An applicable derivative has its own artifact,
gates and release proof and may not borrow the primary artifact's admission.

`Format fit` is not a generic design check. Every feature publishing the
concept must have a distinct, destination-appropriate treatment and its own
rendered or playable evidence under
`operations/product-stewards/learning-content-ecosystem/CONTENT-QUALITY-ADMISSION-GATE.md`.
Passing a class does not pass its NewsStand summary, daily tip, Promptoscope,
song or social derivative.

After all applicable gates and destination-owner acceptance pass:

1. bind the exact artifact and checksum;
2. use the destination's configured release path and rollback target;
3. release without an additional Ali continuation request when
   `aliDecisionRequired` is false;
4. verify the exact public bytes, navigation, search/index presence, related
   links and relevant downstream feeds;
5. mark `VERIFIED_PUBLICLY` only from that evidence; and
6. rollback or hold with an exact owner/action/time when public verification
   fails.

No automated process may publish from a draft, working file, expired evidence,
unaccepted asset or unconfigured release path.

## When Ali is required

Surface one decision-ready card only when the remaining choice materially
changes taste, mission, public identity, a meaningful spend, private-data
authority, rights posture or an irreducible consequential trade-off. The card
must provide:

- the exact question;
- the recommended option first;
- two or three mutually exclusive choices;
- a visual/rendered comparison when taste is involved;
- consequence, cost and deadline;
- what proceeds automatically after the answer; and
- a one-tap or short-answer response.

Do not send Ali owner work, routine sequencing, debugging or a request to
approve a recommendation that has not yet become a reviewable result.

## Freshness, correction and fan-out

A newly accepted signal opens one canonical transaction. It must identify the
maintained claim, every known consumer, required updates and release
destinations. The transaction cannot close until affected pages, episodes,
captions, classes, books, Study Packs, quizzes, tips, Promptoscope records,
songs, metadata, search/index entries and related-learning links are either
updated or explicitly ruled unaffected.

If the new evidence reveals an improvement rather than a correction, route it
through the portfolio opportunity inventory and create an executable order or
an evidenced `NO_BUILD_REQUIRED`/`LATER_WITH_TRIGGER` result. Recommendations
may not end as prose.

## What Ali sees

The Control Room separates:

- **Needs you:** only exact decisions and human-quality reviews;
- **Moving without you:** dispatched work with owner and next checkpoint;
- **Execution stalled:** overdue dispatches or prerequisites with owner,
  attempt history and next retry time;
- **Freshness health:** source recency, registered coverage, unmatched signals
  and open consumer corrections; and
- **Recently verified public:** exact releases and public proof.

Unknown data stays unknown. A healthy validator is not evidence that the
pipeline is moving.
