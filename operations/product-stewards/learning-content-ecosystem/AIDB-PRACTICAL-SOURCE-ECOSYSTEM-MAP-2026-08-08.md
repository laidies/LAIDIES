## Intake contract for a useful AIDB item

1. Preserve the exact AIDB edition and the named reference it exposed.
2. Resolve the exact original author/provider/research source and publication
   date; do not cite the scout summary when the original is available.
3. Classify the item as provider fact, primary research, practitioner
   experiment, attributed opinion, teaching pattern or tool discovery.
4. For a practical tool or workflow, record version, access/account, price,
   privacy/data use, prerequisites, platform constraints and a bounded test.
5. For teaching, map the learner question, concept/prerequisite, distinct
   surface job and what comprehension or transfer evidence would prove value.
6. Deduplicate against the concept map, work-life mirror bank, Classes,
   Library, Weekly Episodes, Study Packs and NewsStand before routing.
7. Return one ruling: `MERGE`, `LINK`, `CORRECT`, `EXTEND`, `CREATE`, `WATCH` or
   `DECLINE`. Only a proven unmet cognitive job may reach `CREATE`, and the
   destination owner still decides production.
