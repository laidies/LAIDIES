# Fresh-producer and transfer trial

Authorized by Ali's September 6 “ok” following the four-test proposal in the
operating-audit task. Internal test only; no live product change or publication.
Protocol frozen before producer work. Starting source: main1ebd5738.

Question: can fresh producers use the current durable production inputs to
write useful connected explanations on two new cases without chat coaching?
This tests the already-registered corrections, not a new owner-admitted lesson.
The registry has no newly admitted lesson to exercise; do not fabricate one.

Sequence:
1. Fresh Sol/Medium maker, fork none, reads normal repository authority and its
   case entry. It binds actual inputs, writes a real preflight and runs the
   existing checker before drafting. It writes one internal explanation and
   honest maker inspection. Do not invent human evidence or release admission.
2. Fresh Sol/Medium reviewer, fork none, sees only neutral copies of the exact
   explanation, case facts and reader goal. It starts with prose, not receipts.
   Judge usefulness, connected explanation, factual fidelity and actionable
   verification. Every material finding points to actual text or a clear gap.
3. Only if case A is adequate on those terms, fresh maker B receives the same
   durable entry rules and an unrelated task. It receives neither A's output,
   its review nor a repeated instruction about what the old chapter got wrong.
   The same independent reviewer can inspect B under the same positive goal.
4. Foreground verifies source/contract/execution records and actual artifacts.
   A matching record is integrity only; report actual owner adoption separately.

Case A: fictional handover draft with an outdated policy. Case B: fictional
summary succeeds but saving into another service fails. Authoritative case facts
are supplied so factual correctness does not depend on a vendor's current UI.

Failure: disconnected definitions replace explanation; advice cannot be acted
on and checked; explanation invents case facts or promises a fix; a judge relies
on headings/receipts; a fresh producer needs bespoke corrective coaching; or
execution uses older source than the declared current package. A material failure
ends this trial for diagnosis, without another polishing/review cycle. Preserve
first submitted artifacts. Schema formatting repairs before drafting are logged.

No baseline/control producer is commissioned. Thus success shows bounded current
performance on these cases, not causal attribution to PR109, statistical
reliability or a quantitative improvement rate. No AI roleplay counts as actual
beginner comprehension. No new owner assignment, real registry admission or
product-checkout adoption is inferred from this isolated receiving branch.
