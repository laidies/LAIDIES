# Learning-content ecosystem steward charter

**Status:** BUILDING — audit foundation created; no item-level editorial verdicts are implied.
**Portfolio role:** coordinate the learning progression across the LIBRAiRY, SUNNYVAiLE High/classes, weekly episodes and NewsStand.
**Decision owner:** Ali. **Portfolio owner:** Codex.

## Promise

LAiDIES' learning products should behave as one intentional ecosystem. A learner can begin with a story, a reference question, a practical class or a timely news development; she should be able to understand what she has learned, what is uncertain, what to do next, and where to go deeper without being made to repeat the same lesson in disguise.

## Format jobs

- **LIBRAiRY:** durable concepts and reference value.
- **SUNNYVAiLE High/classes:** sequenced instruction, mechanism, demonstration, practice and feedback.
- **Weekly episodes:** illustrated, captioned listen-alongs that use narrative, narration and visuals to make a concept memorable and show it in action. They are not approved motion films merely because motion assets exist.
- **NewsStand:** timely, source-qualified editorial reporting and argument; it is not scored down for not being an evergreen course.

## Non-negotiables

1. No item inherits approval from a neighbouring item, a rendered page, a visual treatment or a passing link.
2. Every material explanation must distinguish current fact, definition, inference, disagreement, forecast and scenario as appropriate.
3. Analogies clarify a mechanism and carry a stated limit when needed; they never replace the mechanism.
4. Repetition needs a distinct learning purpose: recognition/reference, narrative demonstration, sequenced practice, retrieval or current editorial context.
5. Current model, account, product, pricing, policy, statistics, privacy and safety claims require a dated source packet and recheck trigger.
6. A class, episode or quiz needs evidence that a learner can explain, apply and qualify the idea—not merely complete it.
7. NewsStand retains its editorial publication contract, source qualification, correction and freshness obligations.

## Scope and boundaries

**Owns:** the inventory, concept/progression map, cross-product overlap/gap/risk register, handoff design, evidence labels and review cadence.

**Coordinates with:** LIBRAiRY, SUNNYVAiLE High, Chick Flicks/episode-media quality, NewsStand, editorial/accuracy, UX/accessibility and platform analytics stewards.

**Does not own:** silently editing learning content, changing publication status, overriding a product's source-of-truth, creating a new shared canon, deployment, social publication or research claims without their evidence packet.

## Current trigger and exit condition

**Trigger:** AW-003 readiness program and Ali's instruction that the products complement rather than duplicate one another.

This foundation is complete only when its inventory resolves every current source item and names uncertainty. It becomes a product-quality audit only after item-level instructional and accuracy review is performed. No launch approval follows from this dossier.

## Cadence

- Reconcile when any mapped book/class/episode/story changes.
- Weekly: incorporate the current episode and linked NewsStand editorial signal.
- Monthly: inspect freshness triggers, dead handoffs, repeated concepts and user-confusion evidence.
- Quarterly: review progression gaps, changes in AI capability and whether format roles still make sense.
