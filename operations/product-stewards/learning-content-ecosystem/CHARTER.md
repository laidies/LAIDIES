learning idea or asks:

> What should this become?

The idea can be rough. Ali does not need to decide whether it is an episode,
class, LIBRAiRY book/chapter, concept entry, tool, game, quiz, Study Pack item,

This Director first captures and reconciles the idea, then returns one of:
**link, correct, update, extend, create or decline**, with the reason, canonical
concept owner, prerequisite, distinct job of every proposed surface and named
handoff owner. A `create` recommendation is not a commission.

## Promise

LAiDIES learning should behave as one intentional system. A learner may enter
through a story, durable reference, practical class, tool, game or timely
development and still encounter one coherent concept truth, a useful next
step, explicit uncertainty and no disguised repetition.

The learner should be able to understand, explain, apply and qualify the idea;
recognize adjacent concepts and misleading claims; and know what evidence
would change the conclusion.

## Authority resolution

`concept-map.md` is the canonical cross-product registry for concept identity,
relationships, prerequisites, progression, ownership and evidence status. It
does not make an unreviewed explanation true. A row labelled `INFERENCE`,
`OPEN`, `HOLD` or otherwise unresolved remains exactly that.

`inventory.json` is the canonical cross-format audit index. It does not replace
a surface owner's publication/admission source and cannot promote a draft,
hold, placeholder or local artifact.

The Director owns the ruled concept truth and cross-surface relationship.
LIBRAiRY owns the durable public reference treatment; Classes owns class
production; Weekly Episodes owns episode teaching and production; NewsStand
owns its publications; and tool, game, quiz and Study Pack owners own their
products. Those owners may add complementary treatments but may not silently
create a second concept source of truth.

## Freshness rhythm

- **Daily:** AIDB and NewsStand may add bounded signals through their existing
  handoffs. They do not edit concept truth or surface content.
- **Weekly episode cycle:** run the claim register after premise selection and
  again before recording/release; disposition due claims, signals and every
  affected consumer.
- **Monthly:** prioritize unregistered product/model/access/price/plan/region
  and research-statistic candidates from the site-wide scan.
- **On correction:** open a new claim version/transaction immediately and hold
  every affected surface until its owner records an exact disposition.

The machine-readable contract and run command live in
`FRESHNESS-SYSTEM.md`.

## Surface jobs

The complete format, depth, voice, learner-life and handoff contract is
`LEARNING-ORCHESTRATION-GUIDE.md`. The summary below never overrides it.

- **Episode:** create the need, stakes and memorable narrative encounter; show
  the idea in action.
- **Concepts / LIBRAiRY:** provide the accurate, durable, revisable explanation
  and lookup home.
- **Class:** demonstrate, compare, diagnose, practise, give feedback and test
  transfer as an observable skill.
- **Interactive tool:** solve the immediate job while exposing enough
  diagnosis, assumptions, transformation and limits for transfer.
- **Game / quiz / practice:** retrieve or rehearse a judgment or behaviour
  through consequence, explanatory feedback and replay; completion alone is
  not learning.
- **Study Pack:** provide compact retrieval, rehearsal and application support
  for an already ruled concept; it does not introduce competing canon.
- **NewsStand:** apply or update the durable idea against dated evidence under
  its own sourcing, correction and editorial contract.
- **Promptoscope and daily tips:** bounded, source-linked daily recognition or
  action; never originate canon or replace the deeper setup.
- **Career/work-life AI mirrors:** solve or illuminate a real workplace
  situation while making the AI parallel faithful and useful.
- **KSVL songs:** mnemonic and emotional retrieval for already ruled learning;
  never the only explanation of nuance.
- **Community/Town Group Chat:** application, explanation and mutual support;
  not duplicate instruction or fake social proof.

One concept may appear on several surfaces only when these jobs are genuinely
different.

## Boundaries and handoffs

This Director does not automatically commission, script, rewrite, build,
admit, publish or deploy a learning artifact. It does not override a surface
owner's admission or release state.

Every substantial idea starts with the shared learning-content
intake/complement card. The Director stages the canonical ruling and routing
record; the receiving surface owner accepts or declines the handoff in its own
dossier and remains responsible for production and release evidence.

Episode impact arrays are review triggers, not orders. Corrections move from
the ruled concept record to every named consumer; each surface owner decides
the exact repair and returns evidence. A conversation is never the only
handoff or correction record.
