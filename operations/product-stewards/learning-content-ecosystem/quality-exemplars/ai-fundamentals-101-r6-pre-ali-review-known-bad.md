# AI Fundamentals 101 R6 pre-Ali-review known-bad calibration

**Status:** REJECT — calibration only; never copy

**Incident:** BTB-467

This internal R6 candidate passed its producer’s exact-prose self-review, but a
role-distinct artifact-first review rejected it before Ali saw it.

## Defect 1: the author’s prose was treated as replaceable material

Ali’s locked source said:

> For example your Aunt Linda who insists that the data centre that went up in
> her friends city is what killed her friend’s cat. No Linda, the cat was 20
> and should have been dead for the last 5 years.

The candidate silently substituted a new agent-written punchline:

> No, Linda. The cat was twenty and had been living on audacity since the first
> Obama administration.

Matching the general voice does not authorize replacing the author’s exact
joke. Author-preserving editing must be checked against both the source and the
candidate, not inferred from a style checklist.

## Defect 2: the prose omitted a prerequisite promised by its own route

The bound teaching map required the Introduction to teach:

> input → AI system → output → consequence

and the reusable questions:

> What does it do? What does it use? What happens after the output?

The candidate contained neither, even though its producer receipt claimed the
opening delivered the connected system map. A contract can bind a section map
while the prose itself still fails to teach the promised prerequisite.

## Defect 3: a recap changed the set it claimed to summarize

The prose introduced a “confusing trio” of narrow AI, AGI and ASI, then the
adjacent recap silently substituted general-purpose AI for narrow AI. A recap
must preserve the exact distinctions taught immediately before it.

## Defect 4: the lookup route omitted first-class taught concepts

Predictive AI and recognition/classification received formal teaching and
transfer treatment but did not appear in the Concept Index. A substantial
book’s index must cover every first-class concept introduced in the chapter.

## Required reusable rejection

Reject substantial teaching prose when:

- it replaces locked authorial language without explicit authority;
- a promised prerequisite or outcome exists in the route but not the prose;
- a recap silently changes the set or axis it summarizes; or
- the Concept Index omits a first-class concept taught in the artifact.
