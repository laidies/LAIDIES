# Known bad — Career/Work-Life item that does not read as career advice

**Direct Ali ruling:** 2026-08-12

> **Delegate the outcome, not every keystroke.**
>
> A useful brief names the job, context, boundaries, output and acceptance
> test; a person still needs room for dialogue, judgement and development.

## Why this fails

- The reader cannot tell whether this is advice for a manager, an employee or
  someone using AI.
- It is generic delegation language rather than a recognizable career or
  work-life problem with a useful move.
- The intended AI connection is implicit, so the item does not perform the
  distinct Career/Work-Life job.
- The eyebrow label carries the category distinction that the content itself
  should establish. Ali could not identify it as a career tip.

## Prevention

Career/Work-Life must begin with a specific situation a professional woman
might recognize and provide useful career guidance on its own. Any AI
connection comes afterward and must improve the action or understanding. A
category label cannot rescue generic management copy or a pasted-on AI mirror.
