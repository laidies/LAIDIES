# Known bad — suspense headline hides the useful Daily point

## Rejected headline

> Shared AI work files carried passwords — and details the chat never showed

## Why it fails

`AI work files` is undefined at the point of entry. The second clause withholds the concrete finding as a reveal, so a factual research update reads like a teaser. A non-technical reader cannot tell who shared what, whether her ordinary private chat is affected, or what the article will help her do.

## Binding prevention

For a Daily report, name the human actor, the object and the finding directly. Define an unfamiliar object in the opening before it carries the explanation. Do not use constructions such as `details the chat never showed` to manufacture suspense from a bounded evidence finding.
