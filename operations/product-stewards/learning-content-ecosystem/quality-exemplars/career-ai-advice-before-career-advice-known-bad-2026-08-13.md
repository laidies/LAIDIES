# Known bad — AI advice wearing a Career / Work-Life label

**Direct Ali correction reproduced:** 2026-08-13

> Explain the work—not just the AI tool

The rejected candidate opens with using an AI tool, gives a script for
describing AI-assisted work, relies on an AI-disclosure study and ends with an
AI disclosure formula. A field labelled `guidanceQuote` appears before a field
labelled `aiParallelQuote`, but the reader-facing guidance is about AI from the
first sentence.

## Why this fails

- It is an AI-use tip, not independent career or life advice.
- Removing the paragraph labelled “AI parallel” does not leave a non-AI career
  tip; the remaining text is still about AI disclosure.
- The structured metadata lets the producer declare category compliance that
  the prose does not perform.
- It blurs Career / Work-Life with Paige's work-facing AI lane.
- The independent review repeated the producer's labels instead of testing the
  exact text without them.

## Prevention

Career / Work-Life must begin with a non-AI professional or life situation and
standalone advice from a credible non-AI source or accountable LAiDIES author.
The candidate must bind the complete body prefix before the explicit AI
connection. That prefix, including the headline, cannot rely on AI, model,
prompt, chatbot or named-tool framing. A validator must reject the known-bad
candidate unaided before it can be trusted on a successor.

The later AI connection must name one real mechanism, state where the parallel
stops and leave career judgement with the reader. A field name, eyebrow or
reviewer's assurance cannot rescue category-mismatched prose.
