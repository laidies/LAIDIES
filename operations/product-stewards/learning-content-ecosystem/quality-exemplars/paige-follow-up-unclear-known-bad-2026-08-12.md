# Known bad — Paige follow-up tip that the reader cannot follow

**Direct Ali ruling:** 2026-08-12

> **Make the follow-up do the remembering.**
>
> When your idea comes back wearing someone else's name, use the record—not a
> public accusation. In an approved AI tool, use only facts you may share and
> ask for a neutral recap: what you proposed, when, what was decided and who
> owns the next step. Check every line before sending.

## Why this fails

- The headline does not name a recognizable task or action.
- “Your idea comes back wearing someone else's name” asks the reader to decode
  the scenario before the tip has established what happened.
- The advice combines workplace attribution, documentation, privacy and AI
  drafting in one compressed card without a clear sequence.
- Ali's observed-reader result was: “does not make sense to me” and “I cannot
  follow what you are trying to say.” Internal editorial acceptance is invalid.

## Prevention

A Paige tip must make one useful AI action obvious without relying on the
headline metaphor. A nontechnical reader must be able to state the job, the
action and how to check it after one read. If the card contains multiple jobs,
move the full explanation to a destination and keep only one complete action
on the card.
