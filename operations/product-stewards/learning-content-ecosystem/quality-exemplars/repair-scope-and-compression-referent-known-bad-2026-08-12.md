# Known-bad exemplar — repair scope substituted and referent compressed away

**Incident:** BTB-474
**Authority:** Learning-owner admission from third independent `LCWO-013` review
**Required verdict:** REJECT
**Reuse:** Calibration only; never copy

## Rejected exact prose

> Disconnect accounts or folders this job does not need.

> Home test: invented weekend. Pass means a list that fits the weather and
> plans without real bookings or email.

## Why it fails

The required isolation boundary applied to the fictional **rehearsal**, but the
repair substituted the real **job**. In the transfer case, the live expense
system was needed by the job and therefore stayed connected during the
fictional test. The repair copied the requested sentence shape while preserving
the hazard.

The word-count repair also deleted the packing skill and packing-list nouns.
The remaining criterion had no subject, so a reader had to invent what “a
list” meant. A shorter sentence is not clearer if compression removes the thing
being tested.

## Prevention rules

When a review specifies the scope that makes a control effective, the successor
must retain that exact scope or prove a substitution against the original
failure scenario. Forward-test the same scenario before marking the defect
clear.

Compression must retain every operative referent: actor, object, action and
criterion. After cutting, a cold reader must be able to name what is being
tested and what passes without importing a missing noun from earlier versions.
