## Why this is known bad

- It exposes internal terms such as model, structure example, admitted and desk status instead of presenting a reader experience.
- It does not immediately establish the date, contents or hierarchy of a complete Daily newspaper.
- The lead assumes knowledge of a held technical incident and is not the clearest Daily story for the reader.
- Empty-state administration occupies more attention than useful content.
- A reader cannot readily tell what is news, what is a recurring column or why the pieces belong in one edition.
