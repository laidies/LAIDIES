# Known-bad calibration — opaque Library book contents

**Authority:** direct Ali rejection, 2026-08-08

**Reuse:** calibration only; never copy into a public contents route

The following proposed labels failed because a reader could not tell what the
chapter meant or what it would cover:

- The physical system
- How training works
- What the product can use
- What the system has now
- What stays true
- What may change
- What belongs with one job
- Meet the parts of the system

The defect is not merely tone. These are maker-facing abstractions. A contents
route must let an unfamiliar reader predict where her question will be
answered before opening the chapter. A visible title therefore names the
actual subject or distinction in plain language; a short deck may add payoff
or scope but cannot rescue a title that conceals the subject.
