## Exact rejected opening

> AI can now enter a meeting before anyone has agreed what the words *use AI*
> mean.
>
> One person means a chat product drafting the agenda. Another means software
> ranking job applicants. Someone else is proposing an automated
> customer-service workflow that can issue refunds. The slide still says **AI
> initiative**, as if these were one activity and one risk wearing a very
> confident blazer.

The rest of the introduction catalogued uses, risks and the book map, but did
not create a felt reason to care, an inviting LAiDIES point of view or a
curiosity-driven route into the mechanism.

## Why this must fail

- **Abstract stakes before felt relevance:** it begins in a generic meeting and
  reports several possible AI uses instead of showing what changes for the
  reader personally, professionally and publicly.
- **Institutional explainer voice:** the prose sounds like competent training
  material, not a warm, vivid LAiDIES invitation into SUNNYVAiLE.
- **Isolated-wit false positive:** “a very confident blazer” is a joke attached
  to a list. It does not make the mechanism clearer, carry the voice across the
  section or make the reading enjoyable.
- **Purpose asserted rather than earned:** lines such as “keep your judgement
  in the room” and “you need a map” announce the payoff without first making
  the reader feel the problem and want the answer.
- **Borrowed evidence:** a different chapter's engaging line cannot prove that
  this introduction is engaging.
- **No explanatory click:** the map is presented, but the opening does not
  produce a surprising change in how the reader sees an ordinary encounter
  with AI.

## Fail-capable producer guard

The introduction must earn all of the following from evidence inside the
introduction itself:

1. one recognisable human moment in which the reader can feel why not
   understanding AI costs her agency, opportunity or participation;
2. a curiosity gap whose answer requires the book's connected system map;
3. a LAiDIES/Rewind Era connection that performs a real teaching job and is
   carried back to the mechanism;
4. at least one concrete “this changes what I can do” consequence in daily
   life, work and the wider decisions being made around her;
5. an earned shift from “AI is happening to me” to “I can locate it, use it and
   have an informed say”; and
6. section-local evidence for voice, enjoyment, motivation and reader value.

One joke, one slogan, a list of use cases, a generic warning or evidence from a
different chapter cannot satisfy these conditions.
