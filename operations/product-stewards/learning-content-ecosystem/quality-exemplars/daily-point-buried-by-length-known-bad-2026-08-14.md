# Known bad — a Daily story that buries its point in length

**Authority:** direct Ali rejection, August 2026.
**Required verdict:** REJECT.
**Reuse:** calibration only; never copy.

## The rejected pattern

> “It’s very long. Like, what is the point of it? What are we trying to communicate? Why is it important?”

The story keeps adding background, qualifications, analogies, repeated summaries
and extra actions after the reader already has enough to understand the mechanism
and make the decision. Each paragraph can sound defensible in isolation while the
complete Daily story becomes harder to enter, harder to finish and too large to
share a newspaper with other stories and service columns.

## Why it fails

The point is not allowed to emerge from accumulated volume. A Daily lead must
answer what happened, make one invisible mechanism visible, preserve the material
evidence boundary and give the reader one useful consequence. Repeating the same
metaphor, warning or share instruction does not add depth. Necessary nuance stays;
duplicated explanation goes.

## What a successor must prove

- the headline and standfirst state the event and reader payoff;
- every section has one unique job in the selected Daily writing mode;
- the mechanism receives the most space but is explained once;
- evidence, limitation, work transfer, non-work transfer and action remain;
- repeated metaphors, conclusions and action sentences are removed; and
- the exact prose stays within the Daily story budget bound before drafting.
