# Known-bad exemplar — an unbuilt destination presented as a live route

**Incident:** BTB-473
**Authority:** Learning-owner admission from independent `LCWO-013` review
**Required verdict:** REJECT
**Reuse:** Calibration only; never copy

## Rejected exact prose

> Basics P8 explains extra powers and access; Build the Skill. Keep the Job.
> owns the full rehearsal.

## Why it fails

`Build the Skill. Keep the Job.` was only a specified, provisionally titled
intermediate lab and was not public. Present-tense reader copy made an internal
plan sound like a destination the reader could use. Correct metadata elsewhere
does not repair a false route in the prose.

## Prevention rule

Every reader-facing destination named in public prose must resolve to an exact
current public route and state, or be labelled truthfully as future and not
offered as the next action. A planned, specified, built-local or held surface is
not a live destination. Producer self-review must compare the prose with the
destination's actual release state, not merely confirm that the name exists in
an internal registry.
