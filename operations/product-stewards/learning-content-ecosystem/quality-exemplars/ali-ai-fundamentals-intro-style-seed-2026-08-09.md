# Ali-authored AI Fundamentals introduction style seed

**Status:** POSITIVE STYLE-AND-INTENT AUTHORITY / NOT FACT AUTHORITY

**Source:** Ali's direct draft supplied 2026-08-09.

This record preserves the load-bearing communication moves that the successor
must retain while separately checking factual claims.

## Governing opening promise

> “This book is really important. We made AI Fundamentals 101 so there is
> finally something that explains all the AI fundamental concepts, how they
> work, and how the whole end-to-end AI ecosystem is connected.”

## Relationship and point of view

> “But AI Fundamentals 101 is not your standard textbook or just a collection
> of definitions you can merely look up online. No, no. LAiDIES would never do
> that to you. This is not a florals for spring situation. We can (and we will)
> do better!”

## Three reasons to care

1. `From “ARGHH WTF!” to “AHH, That's Why!”` — understand how AI works well
   enough to interact with it, diagnose poor results and make it useful.
2. `From “OMG THE END IS NIGH!!!” to “Ugh, AS IF!”` — understand the basics
   well enough to evaluate headlines, hype, fear and purported hacks.
3. `From “I don’t care this has nothing to with me”` to informed participation
   in workplace, community and societal decisions about benefits, risks,
   infrastructure, control and access.

## Character of the humour

The voice speaks directly, has an opinion and uses specific comic escalation:
the crypto-bro aside, Skynet, Aunt Linda's unsupported data-centre theory and
the mock-scriptural instruction not to forsake AI after one poor result. The
humour grows from the argument and the relationship with the reader; it is not
an isolated witty sentence used to certify otherwise generic prose.

## Production ruling

- Preserve the three-part governing argument across the opening.
- Preserve a conversational speaker who is excited, funny, technically
  curious and visibly on the reader's side.
- Preserve LAiDIES-world routes when they perform real jobs: the LIBRAiRY owns
  the durable map, the NewsStand applies it to current claims and Classes add
  deeper guided learning.
- Verify or qualify claims about access, transformative impact, infrastructure,
  public consequences and historical quotations before publication.
- Edit for accuracy, clarity and Canadian English without flattening Ali's
  cadence into institutional prose.
