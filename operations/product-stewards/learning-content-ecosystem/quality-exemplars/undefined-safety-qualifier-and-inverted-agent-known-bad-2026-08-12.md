# Known-bad exemplar — undefined safety qualifier and inverted agent

**Incident:** BTB-475
**Authority:** Learning-owner admission from fourth independent `LCWO-013` review
**Required verdict:** REJECT
**Reuse:** Calibration only; never copy

## Rejected exact prose

> In a clean workspace… an unknown download stays out… Test a normal, awkward
> and should-stay-quiet request.

## Why it fails

“Clean” and “unknown” had no reader-observable boundary. A popular viral pack
could feel known, while no supplied product evidence established a place called
a clean workspace. The compressed modifier “should-stay-quiet” attached
quietness to the request rather than the skill, making confidential material
sound like an appropriate test.

## Prevention rules

Safety qualifiers must define the exact observable condition and disposition:
no live account or folder connected; a maker the reader cannot vet; do not run.
Popularity, familiarity and branding do not satisfy a source audit.

When compressing an instruction, name the actor whose behavior is being tested.
Forward-read every compound modifier for the opposite attachment. If either
reading could cause disclosure or unsafe action, use a full clause.
