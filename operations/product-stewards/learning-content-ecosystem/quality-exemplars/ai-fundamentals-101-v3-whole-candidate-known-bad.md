## Why the prose must fail

- It begins with a classification framework before the reader understands the
  familiar kinds of AI being classified.
- It asks a newcomer to hold five abstract axes at once, then adds agentic as a
  visually separate exception.
- It mixes tasks, modalities, training methods, capability breadth,
  licensing/access and operating patterns as if they were equivalent kinds of
  category. It also moves among product, model and system without holding the
  level of analysis steady.
- Classification, ranking and recommendation can be functions inside the same
  system; “general-purpose model in a specialist product” combines two system
  layers; AGI/ASI are disputed or hypothetical capability concepts rather than
  peers of generative AI, modality or access. The result is not technically
  coherent for an expert or understandable for a newcomer.
- It sounds like a competent institutional explainer rather than the reader's
  smartest, most enthusiastic, passionate and funny Rewind Era best friend.
- It lists examples and promised benefits but does not make the reader feel why
  the subject matters to her life, work and participation.
- Rewind Era and LAiDIES connections are sparse, decorative or absent rather
  than carrying accurate everyday-life teaching.
- A self-review used isolated phrases and evidence from other chapters to
  declare voice, motivation and enjoyment.

## Why the visual must fail

The Chapter 1 visual titled “One product can carry several truthful labels”
shows a crude dress beside five lavender text boxes. No AI product is shown;
no visible relationship maps dress properties to AI properties; “Agentic?” is
bolted on as a sixth yellow box; undefined access labels are presented as if
they explain themselves; empty space dominates; and the result is a generic
wireframe rather than a LAiDIES textbook illustration. It increases cognitive
load and detracts from the prose.

## Reusable prevention rule

1. Orient before classifying. Introduce familiar kinds in plain language and
   build the relationship only after each term has meaning.
2. Hold the level of analysis steady. Explicitly distinguish a task, model
   family, capability claim, system behaviour, product configuration and
   access/licensing condition before comparing any of them.
3. A substantial opening must carry the complete canonical LAiDIES voice and
   its felt reason to care, not one joke or one slogan.
4. An analogy is used only if it faithfully preserves the mechanism, is
   simpler on first encounter and improves observed explanation and transfer;
   otherwise omit it. Do not burden the reader with process language about
   where it stops unless a specific misconception requires that boundary.
5. A teaching visual must answer one learner question unaided and reduce cognitive
   load at intended size.
6. CSS/HTML-drawn shapes are forbidden as images or diagrams. Page layout and
   deterministic labels may use CSS; the visual itself must be a governed
   raster or vector asset.
7. No Ali review until prose and visuals have passed their independent gates.
   Ali is the taste and direction owner, not the QA department.
