## Problem

The 2026-07-29 correction used source-map language too directly in reader
copy. Several surfaces therefore defined terms without creating a useful
mental model, explaining why the distinction matters or giving the learner a
line worth remembering. Some longer Library copy also retained overconfident
provider advice and obsolete “show the reasoning” guidance.

## Intended outcome

Each maintained concept has five separate copy jobs:

1. an accurate plain-language definition;
2. the smallest useful mechanism;
3. why the reader should care;
4. one episode-native analogy whose limit is clear; and
5. one memorable line that does not impersonate the definition.

The Episode owns the encounter, the Trading Cards own retrieval, Vocab 101 owns
lookup, Concepts 101 owns depth and Classes own demonstration, diagnosis and
transfer. They share concept truth without repeating identical institutional
prose.

## In-scope consumers

- Episode 01 canon, article, current narration and cue data;
- Episode 01 Trading Card Pack and Study Pack term summaries;
- Episode 03 hallucination explanation where the same concept appears;
- Vocab 101 and the relevant Concepts 101/Handbook sections;
- Basics Class source and replacement narration;
- quizzes or answer explanations that reproduce the maintained definitions.

Archives, superseded audio and launch snapshots are excluded from current
copy, but superseded recording files must remain visibly blocked from reuse.

## Acceptance

- No reader-facing definition is copied verbatim from the research register.
- Definition, mechanism, analogy, consequence and memory line do distinct jobs.
- No card explains a woman’s lived experience back to her.
- No analogy is asked to carry a technical distinction it cannot support.
- No universal claim is made about reasoning models, free plans, accuracy or
  “show your work.”
- Current canonical consumers pass a stale-phrase scan.
- Trading Card and Storefront builds pass.
- Library consolidation and product-owner checks pass.
- Independent unfamiliar-learner and brand/editorial admission remain open and
  are reported truthfully.
