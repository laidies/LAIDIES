# Research-institution AI learning source map

**Status:** VERIFIED SOURCE ROUTING — NO CONTENT COMMISSION

**Checked:** 2026-08-08

**Owner:** Learning System & Concepts Director

## Decision

Add a bounded institutional source family, not an institutional reading list.
The best additions have different jobs:

1. Helsinki and OpenLearn benchmark nontechnical adult progression;
2. Cambridge and selected technical courseware check mechanisms and lifecycle;
3. Carnegie Mellon checks learning design, practice and feedback;
4. the Alan Turing Institute, Data & Society and Oxford supply responsible-use,
   work and governance research leads.

Institutional reputation is not claim-level evidence. Every public use still
requires the exact item, author, date, access conditions, learner level,
licence and strongest underlying evidence. Current disposition is
`LINK / SOURCE-MINE / WATCH`, never automatic `CREATE`.

## Surface reconciliation

- **AI Fundamentals / LIBRAiRY:** Helsinki, OpenLearn and Cambridge are
  comparators for progression and explanation; Harvard/Berkeley are bounded
  mechanism checks. None becomes the LAiDIES source of truth.
- **Classes:** Carnegie Mellon may improve an admitted class's sequencing,
  practice and feedback contract. It does not commission a teacher course or
  substitute for learner transfer evidence.
- **NewsStand:** Turing, Data & Society and Oxford are dated research leads.
  NewsStand still owns exact-paper sourcing, correction and publication.
- **External continuation:** Helsinki and selected OU-developed OpenLearn items
  are the strongest initial candidates. Turing may qualify for governance.
  Link only after exact-item access, cost, level, accessibility and freshness
  review, with a plain explanation of why the learner would go there.

## Smallest current gaps

1. Inspect one exact Helsinki introductory route and one OU-developed OpenLearn
   route at learner level before proposing either as a public continuation.
2. Verify captions/transcripts, mobile usability, account/cost and licence for
   any exact course before recommendation.
3. Do not infer learning effectiveness from course availability; compare the
   actual teaching sequence and, where consequential, seek learner evidence.
4. Keep technical and governance sources distinct from nontechnical learning
   routes so prestige cannot conceal an audience mismatch.
