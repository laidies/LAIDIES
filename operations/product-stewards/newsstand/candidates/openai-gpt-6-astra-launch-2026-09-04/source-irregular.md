# Source receipt: Irregular assessment of GPT-6 Astra

- URL: https://www.irregular.cv/research/assessing-gpt-6-astra
- Publisher: Irregular
- Published: 2026-09-03
- Accessed: 2026-09-04
- Authority: external technical evaluation conducted with OpenAI; useful corroboration with a disclosed relationship, not a wholly independent audit.

## Exact facts checked

- Irregular reports Astra solved 86 of 226 FrontierCyber tasks, compared with 34 for its predecessor.
- It reports improvement on easy, medium and hard tiers; neither model solved the elite tier.
- It reports multiple previously unknown flaws, while withholding details for responsible disclosure.
- One browser JavaScript finding required the sandbox to be disabled and was not a complete browser compromise.
- Irregular reports no successful compromise of fully hardened targets in the tested set.

## Boundary

The results show a meaningful increase on a named evaluation. They do not establish universal offensive ability or performance on every real system.
