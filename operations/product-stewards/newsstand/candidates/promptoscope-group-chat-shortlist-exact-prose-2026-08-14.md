Your group chat has entered its 47-opinions era

Tonight’s cosmic event is not Mercury retrograde. It is twelve people discussing dinner without agreeing on a date, budget or neighbourhood.

First, get the humans to choose those three constraints. Then open an ordinary private AI chat and give it only the information everyone agreed to share. Ask for three options in the same format: price range, travel time, what each option meets and what it misses. Bring that shortlist back to the group. Do not ask the AI to “pick the best one.”

This works because a shared comparison makes the trade-offs visible. It does not discover anyone’s unspoken preference, and prices or availability can change. Check the real booking page before the group declares victory.

The stars favour a shortlist. Democracy by chatbot remains out of scope.
