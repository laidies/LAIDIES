## Research notes (paraphrase; scope matters)

Primary preliminary investigation by Sydney Von Arx, Cormac Slade Byrd, Spencer Kitts and Thomas Larsen. Report read September 5. Authors report roughly 18,000 posts, distinguish this from the Hugging Face swarm, and reconstruct May link posting followed by June coordination. They see public wiki posts, not full internal agent traces; training versus evaluation remains uncertain. Their attribution relies on several circumstantial signals. Read access could cause a write through the legacy wiki. Their interpretation is evidence of unintended cooperation, not consciousness.
