## Finding

Promptoscope performs the compact desk job at its current length. Paige and
Career/Work-Life do not. At desktop they each become dense mini-articles beside
the much shorter Promptoscope, flattening the scan hierarchy and making “three
compact columns” visually untrue. Stacking the same full bodies on mobile turns
the service strip into a long reading sequence rather than a quick newspaper
desk.

This is not a rejection of the independently accepted prose. The full Paige and
Career versions remain useful exact source texts. The failed assumption is that
the full source text and compact Daily deck are the same artifact.

## Exact unblock

1. Preserve each accepted full text unchanged as the canonical complete item.
2. Produce a separately reviewed compact deck that states the situation, the
   useful move and the boundary without pretending to contain the whole item.
   Do not impose a word count before inspecting the actual card.
3. Provide one truthful full destination for each item before showing `Go
   deeper`; an internal file or held route is not a destination.
4. Render the deck plus full destination at desktop, 390 and 320 widths; compare
   density with the other desks and confirm that no useful safety or escalation
   boundary disappeared during compression.
