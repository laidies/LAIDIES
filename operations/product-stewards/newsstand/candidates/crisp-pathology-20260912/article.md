# CRISP matched a senior specialist during fast surgery checks.

## The Story

During surgery, a pathologist — a specialist who examines tissue to help identify disease — may need an answer before the next step. A frozen section is tissue quickly frozen, sliced and examined during surgery. Usual preparation preserves it in chemicals and wax first, giving it a different appearance.

CRISP analyses those images. In an observational study, meaning researchers watched normal care, it beat other AI models. A separate reader study found it matched the senior specialist overall.

## The LAiDIES Read

This was a comparison in a difficult setting: the image must be read quickly while surgery is underway. CRISP gives a prediction; the pathologist still interprets the tissue and informs the surgical team. The paper describes it as decision support, not a replacement for the specialist.

## What This Means for You

This does not show that CRISP improves treatment or patient outcomes. The study observed performance and included a simulated deployment analysis. The authors say they still need a study that changes care and follows what happens to patients.

## Cocktail Party

“CRISP matched a senior tissue specialist and beat the other AI models compared. That is still different from proving better care.”
