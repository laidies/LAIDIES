## Reader job

Explain Hugging Face before explaining the deal: it is a widely used place where
people and organizations find, share, evaluate and run AI models and datasets—
closer to a public workshop and catalogue for AI builders than to a consumer
chatbot. NVIDIA is the dominant supplier of the specialized chips used for much
AI work. The useful question is therefore not only “who bought whom?” It is
whether the platform continues to make competing models, clouds and hardware
equally practical after the transaction.

For a nontechnical reader, the immediate effect is limited: her ChatGPT or
Claude account does not change because an agreement was announced. The durable
consequence is market structure. The owner of a key layer can shape defaults,
visibility, integration effort and investment even while formally preserving
choice. NVIDIA explicitly promises openness and hardware choice; readers should
treat that promise as something to test over time rather than as either proof of
neutrality or evidence of bad faith.
