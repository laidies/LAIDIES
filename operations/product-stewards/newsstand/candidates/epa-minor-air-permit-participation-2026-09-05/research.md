## Verified scope

The proposal would revise 40 CFR 51.161’s federal public-participation minimum for **State and local minor New Source Review (NSR) programs approved through State Implementation Plans**. Minor NSR regulates construction of new minor stationary air-pollution sources and minor modifications to existing stationary sources. [Federal Register PDF p. 1, summary; pp. 5–6]

If finalized as proposed, it would remove notice and comment as a *federal minimum* feature for those minor-source programs and leave State/local agencies discretion over whether and how to include participation. It does **not** require any State to revise its existing program: an agency may retain its present participation rules. If it chose a revision, it would use the SIP process, including notice/hearing on the draft SIP submission and EPA review/approval. [PDF pp. 6, 9, 11]

Data centres are not named in the proposal. Whether a particular data-centre facility is affected depends on the facility’s emissions classification and the relevant State/local program. Do not say all data centres, or all air permits, are exempt.

## What the proposal does not change

The proposal says it does not revise:

- public-participation requirements for major PSD or nonattainment NSR permits;
- Title V and federally enforceable State operating-permit programs;
- public participation for preconstruction authorizations in Indian country, including its existing Tribal minor-NSR requirement;
- participation requirements for developing, revising, and approving SIP submissions. [PDF p. 7]

## Evidence limits and safe reader question

The original text establishes a proposed federal-minimum change, its limited program scope, and the closed federal comment period. It does not establish which State/local programs will change, whether a particular data-centre project is a minor source, or whether/when a final action will issue.

**Bounded reader job:** distinguish a proposal about minimum public participation for a category of State/local *minor* air permits from an immediate nationwide change to every data-centre permit. Any future story needs a current final-action check and a concrete State/local example before claiming an effect on a specific community.
