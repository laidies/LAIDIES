## Pilot A — The attachment is still on Deb’s desktop

**Lane:** AI memory hook
**Possible parent:** Context/memory lesson; only after the parent is accepted
**Cast:** Mayor Deb, Paige, Miss Jeeves, JoJo

> **MAYOR DEB:** Why can’t it finish the announcement? I explained the whole
> thing yesterday.
>
> **PAIGE:** In this chat?
>
> **MAYOR DEB:** In my heart.
>
> **MISS JEEVES:** Your heart is not in the project folder.
>
> **MAYOR DEB:** It should check both.
>
> **JOJO:** Deb, did you attach the notes?
>
> **MAYOR DEB:** They are on my desktop.
>
> **PAIGE:** Your computer desktop or the actual desk under the broken printer?
>
> **MAYOR DEB:** This town asks too many follow-up questions.

**Learning handback:** Context does not travel because you remember providing
it somewhere else. Put the right source in the right conversation or project,
then check what the tool actually received.

**CTA candidate:** `Give the next move enough context →`

## Pilot C — Unsupported claim in aisle three

>

>

>

enters your work, find the source, date and scope.

**CTA candidate:** `Catch it with the Receipts Pass →`

## Pilot selection checklist

- [ ] Names, roles, relationships and buildings match accepted canon.
- [ ] The thread is visibly labelled fictional before the first message.
- [ ] No avatar or character art is generated until the character owner names
      the approved exact asset.
- [ ] A linear transcript preserves speaker order and meaning.
- [ ] The design is readable at 320 CSS pixels without cropped bubbles,
      horizontal scroll or tiny timestamps.
- [ ] The visual grammar is one coherent Rewind Era messenger treatment—not a
      screenshot of Instagram, iMessage or another creator’s work.
- [ ] An AI-linked pilot names the exact accepted parent and remains suppressed
      if that parent is held, corrected or unavailable.
- [ ] A town-only pilot points only to a real accepted destination; otherwise
      it ends without a CTA.
- [ ] Real community messages, resident names, private inbox data and fake
      engagement counts are absent.

## Recommended first visual pilot

**Pilot A** is the strongest first test because it proves the two jobs Ali
described: recognizable town-character comedy and a useful AI memory hook. The
recommendation is not acceptance; Pilots B and C remain necessary to prove the
format can support continuity and pure comedy without forcing every chat to
teach.
