What does a ChatGPT pet actually do?

The Story

OpenAI’s September 11 desktop update added a quick chat to the floating Pets controls on macOS and Windows. OpenAI calls that small new chat Quick Chat. The useful part is the small control panel: you can type or speak a new request while another app is open, then use its bell to see whether a chat is running, needs your input, is ready, or is blocked.

The pet is optional animated appearance around that panel. OpenAI says choosing one changes its appearance, not how ChatGPT completes tasks. If you want the floating controls without an animal, choose Mini in Settings > Pets; Mini means the floating controls without a pet.

The LAiDIES Read

Say you are planning a birthday dinner in a notes app and want ChatGPT to suggest a grocery list. Paste the meal details into a quick chat from the floating controls while you use another app. That is a convenience feature. It is not a new assistant, and it does not make the grocery list more accurate.

The status is useful when you have stepped away from the main window: Needs input means the chat needs your answer, approval, or another decision. Ready means it has finished with unread activity. Those labels describe the chat’s progress; the pet is simply the visible companion beside them.

What This Means for You

Choose a pet if you like having it on screen, or Mini if you only want the controls. One important limit: a chat started from those floating controls is outside a project. If you need the files and instructions saved in a project, start the chat from that project in the main app instead.

On the web, a pet can appear inside supported ChatGPT Work chats, but OpenAI says it does not have the desktop floating overlay or activity tray. Pets may also depend on your account and workspace being allowed to use them.

Class Notes

When a new interface looks magical, separate the visible decoration from the action it actually gives you. Here, the action is starting and following a chat; the limit is where that chat gets its context.
