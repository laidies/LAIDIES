Ten clues. No timer. Hints welcome. Looking things up counts.

## models
answer: MODELS
clue: These learn patterns during training; a chatbot product can be built around one of them.
hint: The plural of a word also used for a small representation of something larger.
explanation: AI MODELS use patterns learned during training to make predictions or generate results. A chatbot product adds other parts around a model, such as its interface and connected tools. Changing the product's settings does not necessarily change the model underneath.

## tokens
answer: TOKENS
clue: Arcades took these. Language models process text in pieces with the same name.
hint: Small units; in text, one may be only part of a word.
explanation: TOKENS are the pieces a language model processes. A text token can be a word, part of a word or punctuation. A token limit is therefore not a word limit: a short paragraph can use more tokens than its word count suggests.

## prompt
answer: PROMPT
clue: The request you give an AI. Also what an actor needs when the next line has departed.
hint: A cue to begin or continue.
explanation: A PROMPT gives the AI a request or instruction. For an invitation, say who it is for, which details must stay and what tone you want. There is no need to locate a secret incantation; the missing date is usually more useful.

## input
answer: INPUT
clue: Your question or an uploaded photo goes into the AI system as this.
hint: The information supplied at the beginning, rather than the answer returned.
explanation: INPUT is information supplied to a system. It can include your instructions and, where supported, an image or other file. If you want help reading a label, a clear photo may supply details a vague description leaves out. Check that the tool can use that format.

## output
answer: OUTPUT
clue: The answer or image an AI system returns, whether brilliant, baffling or wrong.
hint: What comes out, rather than what you put in.
explanation: OUTPUT is the result a system produces. It names what came back, not how reliable it is. If an AI rewrites your invitation, compare the date, place and meaning with your original before sending it. Fluency is not a fact-check.

## training
answer: TRAINING
clue: The learning process that adjusts an AI model using examples.
hint: Practice intended to build an ability.
explanation: During TRAINING, a model's internal numbers are adjusted using examples and feedback. That differs from supplying a document for the current answer. A chatbot using your document does not, by itself, prove the model has been retrained on it.

## source
answer: SOURCE
clue: The report, study or original announcement you trace a claim back to.
hint: Where the information came from.
explanation: A SOURCE lets you inspect the basis for a claim. Open it and check whether it actually supports the sentence beside the link. A real document can still be cited for something it never says. The link has arrived; the evidence may not have.

## search
answer: SEARCH
clue: What a connected web tool does to find pages, and what you do for the remote.
hint: Look for something rather than recall it from memory.
explanation: SEARCH can bring retrieved information into an AI answer. It does not make every result reliable or prove the final answer reflects the pages accurately. For a changed opening time, check the venue's own current notice rather than stopping at the AI's summary.
