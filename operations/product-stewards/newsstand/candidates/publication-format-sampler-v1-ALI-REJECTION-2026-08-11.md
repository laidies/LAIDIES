## Ali's verdict

All nine examples fail. Do not salvage the prose or admit the template set.

### The Breaking

- The OpenAI/Hugging Face item was a later update to an event that might originally have been breaking; the update itself was not Breaking.
- It lacked the background a newcomer needed to know what the story was about.
- “If your organization is testing an AI agent” was not LAiDIES voice, not a plausible reader action and not explained in plain language.
- The Claude replacement felt clickbaity and did not establish that the story was genuinely Breaking for LAiDIES readers.
- Ali had not encountered it in AIDB, which should have triggered a stronger editorial-relevance check rather than automatic selection from an available internal packet.

### The Daily

- Ali could not tell what she was looking at.
- Internal model, desk, eligibility and empty-state scaffolding had been presented instead of a coherent dated newspaper.
- The hierarchy and difference between news, recurring columns and administrative status were unclear.

### The Big Question

- The style was poor and did not explain the issue.
- It was not investigative journalism.
- It presented assertions and abstract debate rather than reporting that uncovers evidence, contradiction, responsibility or consequence for a real reader.
- It meant little to someone encountering the subject without internal context.

### Straight Answers About AI

- “Can I trust an AI answer if it includes sources?” was a weak premise; readers already know AI can invent citations.
- `Open it / match it / date and scope it` was too obvious and incomplete.
- The answer did not teach the additional verification work a reader can actually perform when a source exists but may still be weak, misused or misleading.

### Dear Miss Jeeves

- The diagnosis and advice were questionable and dated.
- Rule files and instructions can still be ignored; presenting clearer instructions as the main solution fails to address that.
- Long-running jobs cannot always be restarted in a clean session.
- The answer did not address persistent controls, external state, checkpoints, automated rejection tests, task decomposition, handoffs, context management or product limitations.

### Remaining service columns

- Ali rejected the complete set. The shorter columns do not inherit acceptance merely because their individual defect was not discussed separately.

## Root cause

The producer worked backwards from available drafts and a field registry. It did not first prove that each topic belonged in its format, that the reader question was real, that the reporting method could deliver new value or that the advice matched current long-running AI work.

The templates described article anatomy but not journalism. They made it possible to fill fields without:

- selecting a consequential and genuinely current premise;
- conducting original investigation;
- establishing what a non-technical reader already knows;
- identifying what the reader still cannot do after ordinary advice;
- testing advice against the actual failure in a long-running workflow; or
- proving the finished prose sounds and feels like LAiDIES.

## Invalidation

- The producer self-review attached to V1 is invalidated.
- V1 is a negative exemplar only.
- The V1 template candidate may not commission new work.
- No successor example may be drafted from V1's structure or wording.
- AIDB coverage is not a publication authority, but absent AIDB pickup is a relevance warning that requires an explicit reason to continue.

## Required successor proof

Before another example is shown:

1. select one format only;
2. prove the topic belongs in that format before drafting;
3. document the reader's real prior knowledge and unresolved question;
4. name the reporting or teaching work that will produce value beyond a competent summary;
5. for investigation, identify documents, data, affected people, responsible organizations, contradictions and unanswered questions to pursue;
6. for advice, reproduce the real failure and test the proposed action against it;
7. compare AIDB and other trusted analysis after an independent primary-source read;
8. reject the premise when the evidence, urgency, investigation or reader payoff is weak; and
9. bind and visibly pass the combined Hannah Fry / Feynman-style / AIDB
   explanation and editorial-reasoning benchmark; and
10. show Ali one internally admitted example, not another nine-format batch.

No new prose is authorized by this rejection record.
