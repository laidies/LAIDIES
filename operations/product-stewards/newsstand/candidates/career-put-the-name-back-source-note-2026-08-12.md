## Workplace evidence

Lean In's current recognition page says women can be judged more harshly and receive less credit even at the same performance level. Its current workplace-ally guidance gives a specific in-meeting move: when someone carries off a woman's idea, reconnect the contribution to the woman who first surfaced it. The newer recognition guidance also recommends brief milestone recaps that name contributions and outcomes.

This supports a calm attribution-and-record move. It does **not** establish why a particular colleague repeated an idea, prove deliberate theft, or mean a woman must solve a structural bias problem alone. A dated agenda or recap records sequence and contribution; it cannot establish motive. Power, repeated patterns, retaliation risk or material career consequences may require a manager, sponsor, employee-relations channel, union representative or qualified adviser rather than a clever sentence in a meeting.
