L2@P0: Preamble — Why This Agreement Exists
L3@P0: This Memorandum of Agreement exists for one reason: children come first.
L4@P0: Students are not products. Teachers are not beta testers. And schools are not a source for data
L5@P0: collection or experiments. When artificial intelligence tools enter a classroom, they bring
L6@P0: enormous potential, and equally enormous responsibility. This agreement sets one of the
L7@P0: highest standards for how that responsibility must be met.
L8@P0: Likewise, the National Academy for AI Instruction is not a data company. The Academy is a
L9@P0: national initiative dedicated to equipping educators with the knowledge, skills, and support to
L10@P0: use artificial intelligence effectively and responsibly in teaching and learning.
L11@P0: This is a binding Memorandum of Agreement between the National Academy for AI Instruction
L12@P0: (referred to throughout as "the Academy") and any AI Provider that the Academy engages to
L13@P0: provide AI-powered services to educators or students. Every requirement in this agreement is
L14@P0: mandatory. Vendors who cannot meet these requirements are not eligible to serve the Academy
L15@P0: or to achieve the Standard. The Standard is a seal of excellence and a public commitment to
L16@P0: the safety, privacy, and well-being of children, families, and educators. It signifies that an AI
L17@P0: Provider has met the Academy’s highest standards for responsible, trustworthy, and educationcentered artificial intelligence.
L18@P0: Memorandum guiding principle: When in doubt, protect the students, families, and educators.

L21@P0: Academy but are available to any school district that desires the same principles to apply when

L23@P0: create a clear, simple, and expeditious process through which any school district can request

L41@P1: Key Definitions
L42@P1: As used in this Standard:
L43@P1: • "Student Data" means any information that is directly related to an identifiable student,
L44@P1: including but not limited to: names, student ID numbers, grades, disciplinary records,
L45@P1: prompts entered into AI Provider Educational Product, AI-generated outputs tied to a
L46@P1: student, behavioral patterns, device identifiers, location data, memory files, audio or
L47@P1: visual data, and metadata. This definition is intentionally broad. When in doubt about
L48@P1: whether something is Student Data, treat it as Student Data.
L49@P1: • "Covered Data" means Student Data plus any data belonging to or generated by
L50@P1: educators, administrators, or an EDU Customer (including the Academy) when using AI
L51@P1: Provider Educational Products. Covered Data does not include Telemetry Data (as
L52@P1: defined below).
L53@P1: • “Telemetry Data” means technical and operational data generated through the use or
L54@P1: operation of the AI Provider Educational Products that has been de-identified so that it
L55@P1: cannot reasonably be used to identify, relate to, describe, or be linked to an individual
L56@P1: student, or educator. Telemetry Data may be associated with an EDU Customer solely
L57@P1: for security, reliability, debugging, capacity planning, service health, aggregate
L58@P1: performance monitoring, and to make product improvements. Telemetry Data may not
L59@P1: be used, directly or indirectly, for generative-model training, student profiling, individual
L60@P1: student user personalization of AI outputs, advertising, marketing, behavioral inference,
L61@P1: or the creation or enhancement of profiles concerning individual users.
L62@P1: • "AI Provider" means the company providing the AI-powered service, including all
L63@P1: subsidiaries, affiliates, contractors, and sub processors.
L64@P1: • "Training" means any use of data to develop, fine-tune, update, improve, or otherwise
L65@P1: alter an AI model's weights, parameters, or behaviors.
L66@P1: • “AI Provider Educational Products” means services or features that use generative
L67@P1: artificial intelligence technologies to generate outputs that are primarily designed and
L68@P1: marketed for use by students, educators and administrators, with authenticated usage
L69@P1: under an agreement between the educational entity and the AI Provider. For the
L70@P1: avoidance of doubt, general-purpose productivity, collaboration, communication, search,
L71@P1: cloud, development, or workplace-assistance products, including products that may be
L72@P1: licensed or used by educational entities but are not primarily designed for educational
L73@P1: purposes do not constitute an AI Provider Educational Product.
L74@P1: • “EDU Customer” means an educational entity located in the United States that licenses

L78@P2: PRINCIPLE 1 | No Student or Educator Data for Training — Ever, With
L79@P2: One Narrow Safety Exception
L80@P2: • "Academy" means the signing institution, including its students, educators, staff, and
L81@P2: administrators.
L82@P2: • "Breach" means any unauthorized access to, disclosure of, alteration of, or destruction
L83@P2: of Covered Data.
L84@P2: • "Zero Data Retention (ZDR)" means that inputs (prompts) and outputs (responses) are
L85@P2: not stored, logged, or retained beyond the immediate session required to deliver the
L86@P2: service.
L87@P2: • "Synthetic Data" means data that is artificially generated to simulate patterns, scenarios,
L88@P2: or edge cases. Under this agreement, Synthetic Data may be used only if it is not
L89@P2: created from, seeded with, tuned on, or reasonably reversible to Covered Data.
L90@P2: • "Agentic or Action-Taking Feature" means any AI capability that can initiate, approve,
L91@P2: transmit, modify, or execute actions on behalf of a user, online or offline, rather than only
L92@P2: generating informational output.
L93@P2: • "Memory Files" means anything an AI Provider Educational Product saves about a user
L94@P2: between sessions, stored facts, preferences, profiles, or conversation summaries, so it
L95@P2: can "remember" them later. All Memory Files are Covered Data under this agreement.

L134@P3: Narrow Safety and Security Exception
L135@P3: Covered Data may be used for safety and security purposes only under all the following
L136@P3: conditions:
L137@P3: • Safety and Security Functionality. The exception applies only to processing that is
L138@P3: reasonably necessary to detect, prevent, investigate, mitigate, or remediate harm to
L139@P3: users or threats to the security or integrity of the AI Provider Educational Products,
L140@P3: including self-harm risk, child sexual abuse material, grooming, threats of violence,
L141@P3: bullying, malicious activity, security incidents, vulnerabilities, or unauthorized access.
L142@P3: Safety or security functionality may be technically integrated into a broader model,
L143@P3: system, or product that performs other functions, provided that the processing of
L144@P3: Covered Data under this exception remains strictly limited to the safety or security
L145@P3: purposes described in this paragraph.
L146@P3: • Purpose-Limited Use. Covered Data processed under this exception may be used
L147@P3: solely for the applicable safety or security purpose and may not be used for general
L148@P3: model training or improvement, user profiling, personalization, advertising, behavioral
L149@P3: inference, market research, development of unrelated products or functionality, or other
L150@P3: commercial purposes.
L151@P3: • Minimum Necessary. The AI Provider may process only the minimum amount of
L152@P3: Covered Data reasonably necessary to accomplish the applicable safety or security
L153@P3: purpose and must use reasonably available technical and organizational measures to
L154@P3: minimize the Covered Data processed.
