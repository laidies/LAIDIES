A report says OpenAI may slow AI. That is not a slowdown yet.

The Story

Bloomberg reported September 11 that Sam Altman told an OpenAI staff meeting the company could potentially pace its most advanced AI work with several other labs. The people familiar with the private meeting were unnamed.

That is a report of a discussion, not evidence that OpenAI has slowed work. It names no participating labs, agreement, timeline, or ChatGPT change.

The LAiDIES Read

One company promising to slow down would limit only that company. A coordinated pace would require other labs to choose and keep the same rule. Reuters, reporting Bloomberg’s account, said an Anthropic spokesperson was interested in working with the industry on release pace; interest is not a deal.

What This Means for You

So the useful answer is: no announced slowdown yet. Watch for a named agreement, a direct company statement, or a specific product change. OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki separately wrote September 6 that OpenAI would withhold further scaling as needed; that earlier post does not confirm the reported meeting.
