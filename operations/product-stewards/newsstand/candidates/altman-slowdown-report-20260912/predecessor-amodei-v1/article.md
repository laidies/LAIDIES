# Anthropic proposes outside AI safety evaluators. That is not an industry slowdown.

## The Story

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a proposal to slow the pace of improving AI capabilities while safety work catches up. His first step is one Anthropic says it will take itself: invite an outside evaluation team with ongoing access similar to employees who assess risk.Amodei says that team would have desks, badges, laptops, and mostly comparable access to relevant tools and workspaces. He says privacy, legal, and contract exceptions would remain.

## The LAiDIES Read

The useful change to watch is not a promise that AI has slowed. It is whether outsiders can check a company’s safety claims from inside the work, then say publicly what they found. Amodei says reviewers should be able to publish key findings without Anthropic editing them, with narrow redactions for security, legal, commercial, or third-party confidential information.

## What This Means for You

That is a proposed accountability tool, not a shared industry rule. Amodei calls for other companies and governments to coordinate; neither source announces an agreement or a slowdown. If this develops, look for a named evaluator, published findings, or a specific cross-company commitment.

## Cocktail Party

“Outside access can test a safety promise. It does not make every company promise the same thing.”
