# LUMINAiRY profile candidate

Name: Hannah Fry
Role: Keeper of the Explanation

About: Fry makes the mathematics inside automated decisions visible enough for ordinary people to question. The 2026 Leelavati Prize recognized her contribution to increasing public awareness of mathematics. In Hello World, she asks who should decide a criminal case—a person or an algorithm—and who should decide who receives a life-saving liver. Penguin describes the book as examining algorithms' power, limitations and whether they improve on human decisions. LAiDIES calls her Keeper of the Explanation because she turns a hidden system into questions a reader can use: What is it trying to achieve? What does it miss? Who carries the consequence?

Lesson: Make an invisible technical system understandable by showing where it touches ordinary decisions.
