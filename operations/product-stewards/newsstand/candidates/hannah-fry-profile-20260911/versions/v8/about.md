Fry translates mathematics for the public without losing its scope or importance. The 2026 Leelavati Prize recognized her contribution to increasing public awareness of mathematics. In Hello World, she asks who should decide an accused person's fate—a person or an algorithm—and who should decide who receives a life-saving liver. Penguin describes the book as examining algorithms' power, limitations and whether they improve on human decisions. LAiDIES calls her Keeper of the Explanation because she turns a hidden system into questions a reader can use: What is it trying to achieve? What does it miss? Who carries the consequence?
