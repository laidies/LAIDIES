# LUMINAiRY profile candidate

Name: Hannah Fry
Role: Keeper of the Explanation

About: Fry’s contribution is making the mathematics inside automated decisions visible enough for ordinary people to question. In Hello World, she asks who should decide a criminal case: a person or an algorithm. The point is not that one is automatically fairer; it is that the trade-offs matter when a system shapes healthcare, justice or other consequential choices. The 2026 Leelavati Prize recognized her contributions to public awareness of mathematics. LAiDIES calls her Keeper of the Explanation because she turns a hidden system into questions a reader can use: What is it optimizing? What does it miss? Who carries the consequence?

Lesson: Make an invisible technical system understandable by showing where it touches ordinary decisions.
