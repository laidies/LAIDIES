Fry’s contribution is making the mathematics inside automated decisions visible enough for ordinary people to question. In Hello World, she asks who should decide a criminal case: a person or an algorithm. The point is not that one is automatically fairer. Each can fail differently, and those differences matter when a system shapes healthcare, justice or other consequential choices. Cambridge recognized her public communication with the 2026 Leelavati Prize. LAiDIES calls her Keeper of the Explanation because she turns a hidden system into questions a reader can use: What is it optimizing? What does it miss? Who carries the consequence?
