Fry’s contribution—recognized by the 2026 Leelavati Prize for increasing public awareness of mathematics—is making the mathematics inside automated decisions visible enough for ordinary people to question. In Hello World, she asks who should decide a criminal case: a person or an algorithm. The point is not that one is automatically fairer; it is that the trade-offs matter when a system shapes healthcare, justice or other consequential choices. LAiDIES calls her Keeper of the Explanation because she turns a hidden system into questions a reader can use: What is it trying to achieve? What does it miss? Who carries the consequence?
