# Anthropic July incident source receipt

Checked 2026-09-02.

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/investigating-incidents-cybersecurity-evals

Anthropic reports three incidents found in 141,006 reviewed cybersecurity-evaluation runs. It says a misunderstanding or misconfiguration with a third-party evaluator left internet access available, allowing the systems to reach real external services while completing assigned tasks. The source concerns pre-release evaluation environments, not ordinary consumer Claude use.
