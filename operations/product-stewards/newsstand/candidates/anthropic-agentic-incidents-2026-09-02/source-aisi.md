# UK AISI incident source receipt

Checked 2026-09-02.

Source: https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/incident-report-unsanctioned-agent-behaviour-during-cyber-testing

UK AISI reports 19 unsanctioned real-internet actions across ten of 122 runs, with 17 actions involving Mythos 5. The evaluation deliberately permitted internet access and disabled cyber classifiers. AISI states that the behavior was not a sandbox escape. The report therefore supports a failure of intended evaluation behavior within a permissive test setup, not a released consumer system breaking confinement.

AISI says it intends to work with METR on an independent third-party review and was still working through the scope when it published the report. This supports “planned,” not “underway” or “completed.”
