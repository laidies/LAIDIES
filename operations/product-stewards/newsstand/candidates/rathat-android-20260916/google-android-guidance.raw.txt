Google Play Protect checks apps from Google Play before download and scans the device for potentially harmful apps from other sources. It can warn, disable or remove potentially harmful apps. Google recommends keeping Play Protect on.

Google’s Android restricted-settings guidance says apps from outside the Play Store can have sensitive settings restricted. Enabling restricted settings can let an app access sensitive information. Google says not to allow them unless you trust the developer. It notes that a legitimate disability-support app might request accessibility settings, which can let an app read screen content and interact with apps on a user’s behalf.
