# Write the finish line before asking for another draft

Your AI draft sounds polished, but it still hides the decision or leaves the
next step fuzzy. Before asking for “a stronger version,” write three things the
finished work must let its reader find or do. For a briefing note: name the
decision in the first paragraph; make every figure match the approved source;
show the next action, owner and date.

Give those checks and the draft to an AI tool your workplace approves, using
only material you are allowed to share. Ask for one revision that meets the
three checks. This helps because “better” is an opinion the tool has to guess
at; visible results give the revision a target.

The checks do not prove the facts are right. Compare the new draft with each
check yourself, then verify every figure and claim against its approved source.
The AI does not get to award itself the trophy.
