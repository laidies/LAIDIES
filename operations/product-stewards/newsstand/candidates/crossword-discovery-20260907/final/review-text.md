# Crossword puzzle 01 and two LAiDIES house ads

Ten clues, a few familiar faces, and no timer. Try what you remember, open a hint, or take a little detour through SUNNYVAiLE. Looking things up is part of the fun.

## TOOLS
Clue: A connected calculator or search service can be one of these in an AI system.
Hint: Think of equipment that lets you do a particular job.
After a correct answer or reveal: A calculator is a TOOL. An AI can write out a sum, or call a calculator to work it out: those are different routes to an answer. When the number matters, ask which route it used and check the result.

## MEMORY
Clue: In an AI product, saved information that may be brought back into a later conversation.
Hint: You have one too, although yours may still contain every word of a Spice Girls song.
After a correct answer or reveal: MEMORY is information saved for later use. It can save you repeating a preference, but you may not want an old detail following you into a new task. Look at what your product has saved and how to change or remove it. Saved information is not necessarily included in every answer.
Before solving, an optional hint link: [Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-8)
After solving, the link reads: [Read about context and memory](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-8)

## AGENTS
Clue: Mulder and Scully were FBI ones. AI ones can work through steps towards a goal.
Hint: The answer describes a role, not a guarantee of good judgment.
After a correct answer or reveal: An AGENT can work through steps towards a goal: search, inspect the results, then decide what to try next. It may use TOOLS along the way. A goal is not a blank cheque—decide which actions need your approval before it starts.
Before solving, an optional hint link: [Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-14)
After solving, the link reads: [Follow the agent loop](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-14)

## PROJECTS
Clue: At school, these involved cardboard and a deadline. In some AI products, they group related work.
Hint: A place to keep material for one ongoing piece of work.
After a correct answer or reveal: A PROJECT can keep related chats, files and instructions together in products that offer this feature. Useful when one piece of work has sprawled across six conversations. Before asking for the final draft, check that the current brief is available to the AI—not merely filed somewhere in the project.
Before solving, an optional hint link: [Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#working-with-ai-101::%40chapter-6)

## CONTEXT
Clue: You tell the AI “make it shorter”. The earlier draft it can actually see is part of its ___.
Hint: Think of what is on the desk right now, not everything in the filing cabinet.
After a correct answer or reveal: CONTEXT is the information available for the current answer. A document elsewhere in your account may not be included. If an important detail is missing, provide it or check that the relevant file is available.
Before solving, an optional hint link: [Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#working-with-ai-101::%40chapter-5)

## SKILLS
Clue: Making a mixtape took these. In AI software, the word can also mean reusable task instructions.
Hint: Know-how you can use again.
After a correct answer or reveal: In some AI systems, a SKILL is a reusable set of task instructions and resources. For recurring meeting notes, it might specify the format and checks each time. Before using one, read what it tells the system to do; the name on the package is not the instructions inside.
Before solving, an optional hint link: [Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-15)
After solving, the link reads: [Read about reusable AI skills](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-15)

## GRAPH
Clue: A network drawn as dots and connections, such as people linked to their friends.
Hint: Here we mean relationships, not a bar chart of sales.
After a correct answer or reveal: A GRAPH shows things and their connections. In an AI workflow, those things might be tasks: draft an email, check it, send it. Follow the connections before running the workflow. Does the route include your approval before sending, or go straight from the AI’s draft to somebody’s inbox?
Before solving, an optional hint link: [Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-15)
After solving, the link reads: [Read about workflow graphs](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-15)

## LOOPS
Clue: Groundhog Day has a famous one. In a program, these repeat a sequence of steps.
Hint: Round again: check, act, then check again.
After a correct answer or reveal: Search, inspect the result, adjust the search, repeat. That repeating sequence is a LOOP; it is one way an AGENT can work through a task. Groundhog Day would make a terrible project plan, so give the task a stopping point: a time limit, a budget or a question it must bring back to you.
Before solving, an optional hint link: [Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-14)
After solving, the link reads: [Follow the agent loop](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-14)
