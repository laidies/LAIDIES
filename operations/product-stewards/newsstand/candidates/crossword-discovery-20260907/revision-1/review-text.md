# Crossword puzzle 01 and two LAiDIES house ads

Ten clues, a few familiar faces, and no timer. Try what you remember, open a hint, or take a little detour through SUNNYVAiLE. Looking things up is part of the fun.

## TOOLS
Clue: A connected calculator or search service can be one of these in an AI system.
Hint: Think of equipment that lets you do a particular job.
After a correct answer or reveal: TOOLS let a system do a specific job, such as calculate or search. Generating text about a calculation is different from calling a calculator. Which tools are available depends on the product and its permissions.
[Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-14)

## CLAIO
Clue: Mme ___: visit her in SUNNYVAiLE for a reading, not a weather forecast.
Hint: Her name gives a familiar fortune-teller sound an AI twist.
After a correct answer or reveal: Mme CLAi-O offers a reflection and a suggested move, not a prediction. You decide whether the message is useful for your situation.
[Find a hint in town](/games/madame-claio.html)

## MEMORY
Clue: In an AI product, saved information that may be brought back into a later conversation.
Hint: You have one too, although yours may still contain every word of a Spice Girls song.
After a correct answer or reveal: MEMORY is information retained for later use. Saving something does not guarantee it will be used in every answer. Check what your product saves and lets you change or remove.
[Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-8)

## AGENTS
Clue: Mulder and Scully were FBI ones. AI ones can work through steps towards a goal.
Hint: The answer describes a role, not a guarantee of good judgement.
After a correct answer or reveal: AGENTS can pursue a goal through steps, sometimes calling tools along the way. They still need limits: deciding what to try next is not the same as having permission to do anything.
[Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-14)

## PROJECTS
Clue: At school, these involved cardboard and a deadline. In some AI products, they group related work.
Hint: A place to keep material for one ongoing piece of work.
After a correct answer or reveal: PROJECTS can group related chats, files or instructions in products that offer them. Organisation helps you find things; it does not prove every file reaches every answer. Check what the product actually includes.
[Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#working-with-ai-101::%40chapter-6)

## PAIGE
Clue: Her AI and productivity tip has a regular place in this newspaper.
Hint: Her name sounds like something you turn in a newspaper.
After a correct answer or reveal: PAIGE is the NewsStand character behind the practical tip. Visit her column for an idea to try in your work; the published date belongs to that particular column.
[Find Paige in Useful this week](/newsstand#ns-feature-desk-title)

## CONTEXT
Clue: You tell the AI “make it shorter”. The earlier draft it can actually see is part of its ___.
Hint: Think of what is on the desk right now, not everything in the filing cabinet.
After a correct answer or reveal: CONTEXT is the information available for the current answer. A document elsewhere in your account may not be included. If an important detail is missing, provide it or check that the relevant file is available.
[Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#working-with-ai-101::%40chapter-5)

## SKILLS
Clue: Making a mixtape took these. In AI software, the word can also mean reusable task instructions.
Hint: Know-how you can use again.
After a correct answer or reveal: SKILLS ordinarily means learned abilities. Some AI systems also use the name for reusable instructions and resources for a task. The label alone does not tell you what the system can do or how well it will do it.
[Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-15)

## GRAPH
Clue: A network drawn as dots and connections, such as people linked to their friends.
Hint: Here we mean relationships, not a bar chart of sales.
After a correct answer or reveal: A GRAPH represents things and the connections between them. For example, dots could be people and lines could mean “works with”. That can help a system represent relationships; it does not make the information correct.
[Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-15)

## LOOPS
Clue: Groundhog Day has a famous one. In a program, these repeat a sequence of steps.
Hint: Round again: check, act, then check again.
After a correct answer or reveal: LOOPS repeat a sequence. An AI system might act, inspect the result and try another step. Repetition alone does not improve an answer: the check must be useful, and there needs to be a stopping point.
[Find a hint in the LIBRAiRY](/library.html#ai-fundamentals-101::%40chapter-14)

Good to know: These are ideas to recognise, not a test of which AI subscriptions you own. Product features and settings vary; the linked lessons explain the distinctions.
