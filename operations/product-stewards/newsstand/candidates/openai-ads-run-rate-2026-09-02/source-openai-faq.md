# OpenAI advertising FAQ source receipt

Checked 2026-09-02.

Sources:

- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt
- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001245-ads-manager-availability

OpenAI's current help content says eligible Free and Go users may see ads, while Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts do not have ads. It describes an Ads-Free route for some Free users with lower usage limits and reduced feature access. It says ads are labelled and separate from answers, and advertisers cannot alter or rank answers. These are current provider rules, not an independent audit. The availability table concerns advertisers' access by country; it does not establish that every user in those countries receives ads.
