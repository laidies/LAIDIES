# OpenAI advertising announcement source receipt

Checked 2026-09-02.

Source: https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/

Published August 31, 2026.

OpenAI says ChatGPT Ads reached a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate in fewer than 200 days. It says tens of thousands of advertisers use the product and advertiser access extends to more than 40 countries. These are company-reported figures and are not described as audited financial results. The article defines run rate as a pace projected over a full year and does not convert it into revenue already collected, profit or a guaranteed forecast.
