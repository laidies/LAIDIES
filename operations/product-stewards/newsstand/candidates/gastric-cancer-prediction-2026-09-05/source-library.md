<h3 id="ch-3-3-4-supervised-unsupervised-and-the-spectrum-between">3.4 — Supervised, Unsupervised, and the Spectrum Between</h3>
<p>The training we've described so far — showing the system labelled examples and letting it learn the pattern — is called <strong>supervised learning.</strong> Supervised because a human provided the "right answers" (the labels).</p>
<ul><li>Spam filter: supervised. Humans labelled emails as spam/not spam.</li><li>Face grouping: supervised. Humans labelled photos as same face/different face.</li><li>Customer churn prediction: supervised. The historical data showed which customers did and didn't cancel — those are the labels.</li></ul>
