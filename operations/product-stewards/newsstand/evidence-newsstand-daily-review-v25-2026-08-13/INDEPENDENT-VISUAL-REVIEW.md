## Verdict

`ADMIT_PRIVATE_DIRECTION_REVIEW`

The longer Career / Work-Life successor creates no blocking visual defect. At
desktop it keeps readable type, a clear two-line headline and proper
subordination to the Daily lead. It does not clip, crowd adjacent desks or turn
the service rail into a mobile stack.

The complete v25 result retains:

- one Daily newspaper first, with Catch Me Up and archive secondary;
- clear lead, service-desk and paper-index hierarchy;
- an intentional mobile continuation using a partial next desk plus `Swipe for
  all four` rather than accidental clipping;
- one continuous newspaper-like article instead of pastel cards;
- no visible overlap, document-level horizontal overflow or cut-off text at
  1440, 390 or 320; and
- the LAiDIES printed-paper texture, confident ink and vivid controlled accents
  without a generic software-card page.

## Blocking defects

None found in the exact v25 pixels.

## Non-blocking observations

- The Career desk is at the visual-density ceiling for this compact desktop
  rail. Future candidates may not use it as permission to shrink type or add
  another line.
- The 390/320 rail intentionally shows only the first desk and part of the next.
  The label and crop communicate the interaction, but public release still
  needs the separately required real-device touch check.
