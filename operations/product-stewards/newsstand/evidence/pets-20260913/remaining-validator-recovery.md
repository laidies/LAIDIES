## What the 227 failures mean

They do **not** establish that 227 published stories are false.  The migrated internal validator now requires a replayable checksum chain that much historical content predates: a private envelope matching the stored `envelopeSha256`, an accepted independent admission record with that same hash, the exact issue snapshot, and that issue's own bound ordinary candidate.  The current required release suite does not list this legacy validator.  A failure is therefore a recovery queue, unless a current candidate/admission gate independently fails.

The current ordinary-candidate validator does not define a general `publisherType` taxonomy.  It checks the actual candidate's bound HTTPS source, date, source-evidence binding, claim map, review chain, and source IDs.  The old eleven-value list in `validate-newsstand-stories.mjs` was likewise not a current policy source.  The two Pets product values are evidenced only by its bound candidate:

`operations/product-stewards/newsstand/candidates/chatgpt-pets-20260913/admitted-v4/ordinary-candidate.json`.

That is enough to validate that candidate through its own current gate; it is not authority to copy every observed historical label into a global allow-list.  A durable publisher-type contract must be introduced separately, with an owner and negative tests.  Until then, unknown historical types are a **resolver/policy gap**, not a factual-data repair instruction.
