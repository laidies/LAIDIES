`DOMContentLoaded` for `/newsstand.html`. The write occurs independently of
NewsStand's canonical dataset/access result, so an unavailable or load-failed

## Prevention rule

A cross-product progress or reward event must bind to the product owner's
named authoritative user result. Route arrival may count only when that is an
explicitly ruled completion event. Unavailable, failed and storage-denied
states require their own tests and may not paint or propagate success.

## Required handoff

- NewsStand owner names the acceptable current/quiet completion result.
- Tour/Rewards and Functionality/Platform owners reconcile the event,
  read-after-write behavior and downstream Homepage/Closet consumers.
- Control Room assigns the shared-script and consumer locks before repair.
- After integration, record the result in
  `operations/painpoints-log.md` if the independent/public evidence confirms
  the cross-product failure and reusable fix.
