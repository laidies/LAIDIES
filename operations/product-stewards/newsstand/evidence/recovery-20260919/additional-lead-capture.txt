L4: # Anthropic wants Claude to analyze your bank account and financial data
L5: 
L6: By
L7: ###### cite0†Mayank Parmar L8: 
L9:   * September 16, 2026
L10:   * 08:35 PM
L11:   * cite1†4 L12: 
L13: cite2†Image: Claude†www.bleepstatic.com L14: 
L15: Anthropic is testing a new personal finance feature called "Claude Money" that will allow you to connect your bank accounts directly to Claude and "understand your money."
L16: 
L17: AI companies coming after your finances is not a new thing, as OpenAI has a similar feature, and Anthropic appears to be catching up.
L18: 
L19: cite3†Image: Claude Sonnet†www.bleepstatic.com L20: As cite4†spotted†x.com by TestingCatalog on X, Anthropic is testing the feature for the Claude app on iOS, where a new Money section has appeared alongside Chats, Code, Artifacts, Dispatch, and Cowork.
L21: 
L22: "Understand your money with Claude," the page reads. "Link your bank accounts and ask Claude about spending, plans, and more."
L23: 
L24: There's also a "Get started" button for connecting an account, although the feature does not appear to be rolling out to most users.
L25: It's worth noting that Claude Money references showed up in the app ahead of the expected announcement, which is why details around it are quite limited.
L26: 
L27: We don't yet know which banks it will support, how accounts will be connected, or if the feature will be locked to the United States (or certain states in the country).
L28: ## Claude Money looks a lot like ChatGPT Finances
L29: 
L30: As I mentioned, OpenAI already cite5†offers†plaid.com a similar feature called Finances in ChatGPT, which lets you connect bank accounts, credit cards, brokerages, and other financial accounts through Plaid.
L31: 
L32: When you connect your finances to ChatGPT, it can use your data to answer questions about spending, bills, subscriptions, savings, net worth, and investments.
L33: OpenAI insists it does not train AI models on your personal data, and that it currently supports more than 12,000 financial institutions in the U.S.
L34: 
L35: Anthropic could follow a similarly limited rollout for Claude Money, and I wouldn't be surprised if the feature never rolls out in Europe due to local privacy laws.

L4: # New RatHat Android malware uses AI to automate device control
L5: 
L6: By
L7: ###### cite0†Bill Toulas L8: 
L9:   * September 17, 2026
L10:   * 05:50 PM
L11:   * cite1†1 L12: 
L13: cite2†Image: Android†www.bleepstatic.com L14: 
L15: A new Android malware called RatHat has been discovered, targeting users with an AI-powered subsystem that helps operators remotely navigate compromised devices.
L16: 
L17: Zimperium zLabs researchers analyzed the malware and believe it is linked to threat actors from China after finding it using LLM prompts written in Chinese.
L18: The researchers say the malware is distributed through malvertising, SMS, and phishing sites promoting APK downloads from outside Google Play.
L19: 
L20: Like most Android malware families, RatHat relies on abusing Android's Accessibility permissions to perform highly privileged actions on infected devices.
L21: Most notably, it enables Developer Options and Wireless Debugging to gain a local shell-level execution context without requiring an external computer, similarly to mechanisms seen in the cite3†ToxicPanda and cite4†RedHook Android malware families.
L22: 
L23: ADB access lets RatHat install a Go-based agent (liblocal-service.so) that runs commands with ADB shell privileges, creates battery-restriction bypasses, and manages persistence.
L24: The same agent also restores the malware if it's removed or stopped, providing a persistence channel independent of the malware itself. This back-up goes both ways, with the malware restoring the agent if it's removed.
L25: 
L26: cite5†Image: The Go agent's keylogging activity†www.bleepstatic.com The Go agent's keylogging activity
L27: Source: Zimperium
L28: 
L29: A second agent (libmedia_codec.so) also acts as an FRP reverse-proxy client, establishing a persistent tunnel to the attacker.
L30: The malware displays HTML overlays for targeted banking and cryptocurrency applications to capture account credentials.
L31: 
L32: It can also intercept SMS messages and notifications, including one-time passwords; record text-change events; extract URLs from browser address bars; and capture lock-screen PINs, passwords, and unlock patterns.
L33: 
L34: cite6†Image: Password- and PIN-stealing overlays†www.bleepstatic.com Password- and PIN-stealing overlays
L35: Source: Zimperium
L36: ## AI-guided navigation
L37: 
L38: Zimperium reports that RatHat uses an AI-powered user interface-automation engine that serializes the live Android Accessibility tree into XML and sends it to a popular AI assistant not named in the report.
L39: 
L40: The AI tool is then tasked with:
L41: 
L42:   * Identifying the center coordinates of a named interface element
L43:   * Determining an element's actual on-screen text
L44:   * Returning navigation instructions such as 'SCROLL_DOWN' and others
L45: This AI-powered subsystem makes RatHat's interface automation more adaptable than automation based entirely on fixed scripts, allowing remote navigation without requiring the operators to perform real-time interaction.
L46: 
L47: "RatHat uses AI to intelligently navigate and control the device interface in real-time, making its operations more adaptable and harder for security software to detect than traditional, scripted automation," comments Zimperium.
L48: cite7†Image: RatHat's AI prompts†www.bleepstatic.com RatHat's AI prompts
L49: Source: Zimperium
L50: 
L51: The researchers warn that RatHat will actively thwart removal attempts by intercepting the uninstall confirmation screen, canceling the procedure, and displaying a fake Google Play overlay containing a bogus error message.
L52: Zimperium also highlights multiple anti-analysis techniques embedded in the malware, including APK container tampering, a bloated (61MB) Android manifest file, and invalid DEX pseudo instructions, all aimed at confusing or breaking analysis tools.
L53: 
L54: Android users should never download APK files from outside Google Play unless they explicitly trust the publisher, avoid granting Accessibility permissions to apps, and regularly scan their devices with Play Protect.

L16: 
L17: Physicists and mathematicians are going beyond the classic equations of fluid dynamics to understand turbulence — often with the help of AI.
L18: 
L19: By
L20:
