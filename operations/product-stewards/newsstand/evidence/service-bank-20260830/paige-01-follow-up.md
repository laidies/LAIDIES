# Turn meeting notes into a follow-up you can actually send.

Make the draft keep track of what was agreed—and what wasn’t.

Before you ask AI to write a follow-up email, separate your notes into decisions, actions and unresolved questions. Use only information you are allowed to put in that tool.

Try: “Draft a short follow-up from these notes. Include each agreed action, its owner and its deadline. If a name or date is missing, write ‘to confirm’. Keep proposals separate from decisions. Do not add commitments.”

That last instruction matters: a tidy email can quietly turn ‘we could launch in October’ into ‘we will launch in October’. Compare the draft with your notes before sending. Check names, dates and promises first; polish the greeting afterwards.
