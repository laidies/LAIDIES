# Mme CLAi-O’s cards have a practical move on them.

A reading comes with something to consider doing next.

Each card in Mme CLAi-O’s reflection deck includes a reading, a message and a practical move. The Mini Backpack, for example, asks you to remove one commitment before adding another.

The NewsStand can feature a card, and you can explore the deck at Mme CLAi-O’s. These are authored reflections, not predictions about your future.
