# Why does “make it professional” make me sound unbearable?

Give the tool an example of your voice and a specific job to do.

Dear Miss Jeeves, every time I ask for a professional email, I get “I hope this message finds you well” followed by three paragraphs nobody needed. How do I make it sound like me?

— Trapped in Corporate in Calgary

Dear Trapped in Corporate,

“Professional” leaves a lot for the tool to decide. It might mean concise and courteous to you, but produce an email full of formal introductions and impressive-sounding filler. The instruction names a broad style without showing what you want.

Give it a short, privacy-safe example of your own writing. Then name the changes: “Write this in my usual style. Put the request in the first sentence. Keep it warm and direct. Use ordinary words. Do not add a greeting longer than the request.”

If the draft is already close, ask it to change the offending sentences only. You might write: “Replace ‘I am reaching out to inquire’ with ‘Can you tell me’. Keep the rest unchanged.” Compare the revision with your original; dates, names and commitments should not change during a tone edit.

An example gives the tool something concrete to follow. You still choose what sounds like you. If a sentence would make you wince saying it to a colleague, it does not become professional because an AI put it in an email.
