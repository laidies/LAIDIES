# When someone takes credit for your idea.

A script for putting your contribution back into the conversation.

You explained the idea yesterday. Today a colleague presents it as theirs. If you can respond calmly, make your contribution specific: “I’m glad we’re taking this forward. When I proposed the pilot yesterday, the problem I wanted to solve was the handover delay. I can walk us through the first step.”

If the meeting has moved on, follow up privately: “In today’s meeting, you presented the pilot without mentioning my contribution. I developed the proposal we discussed yesterday. Can you clarify that in the follow-up?”

Use details you can support. If this keeps happening, keep the relevant drafts and messages, and discuss the pattern with a manager you trust. You do not have to improvise a confrontation with someone who can retaliate against you.

Dorie Clark’s work on professional visibility informs this column; Amy Gallo’s guidance addresses credit-taking specifically. The scripts here are written by LAiDIES, not quotations from either author.
