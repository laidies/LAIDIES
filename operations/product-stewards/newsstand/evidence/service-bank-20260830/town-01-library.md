# Four books to help you find your next answer.

The LIBRAiRY’s opening set includes explanations, practical methods, a dictionary and sourced questions.

The LIBRAiRY’s opening set brings together AI Fundamentals 101, Working with AI 101, the AI Dictionary and Straight Answers About AI.

Start with Fundamentals when you want to understand how the pieces fit. Open Working with AI when you have a task to do. Use the dictionary for a term you have just encountered, and Straight Answers for questions about AI’s wider effects. The books do different jobs, so you do not need to read all four in order.
