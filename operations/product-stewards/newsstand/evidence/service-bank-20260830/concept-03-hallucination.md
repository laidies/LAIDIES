# Hallucination: a convincing answer with an invented detail.

A response can be mostly useful and still include a false citation or date.

In AI, a hallucination is false or unsupported information presented in a generated answer. It does not have to be an entirely invented story. One nonexistent study in an otherwise sensible summary is enough.

A language model generates its response; it does not automatically verify every sentence against a reliable record. Search and supplied documents can give it evidence to use, but the answer can still misread that evidence or add an unsupported detail.

When a claim matters, open the cited source and find the passage that supports it. Check that the author, date and conclusion match the answer. A real link to a real paper is not enough if the paper does not say what the answer claims.
