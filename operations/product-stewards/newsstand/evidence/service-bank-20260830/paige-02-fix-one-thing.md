# Fix the paragraph without rewriting the whole thing.

Tell the tool what to change—and what to leave alone.

You like the draft, apart from the opening paragraph. Asking for “a better version” gives the tool permission to change far more than you wanted.

Try: “Revise only the first paragraph. Make the request clear in the first sentence and remove the apology. Keep the rest exactly as it is. Do not change names, dates, figures or commitments.”

Keep a copy of the original and compare the two. The instruction narrows the job; it does not guarantee that every other word stays put. If the tool still changes unrelated text, ask for the replacement paragraph by itself and insert it into your saved draft.
