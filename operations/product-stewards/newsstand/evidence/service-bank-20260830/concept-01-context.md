# Context: the information available for this answer.

Your chat history and what the model can use right now are not necessarily the same thing.

Ask an AI to write an invitation and it can produce something plausible. Tell it that this is a casual lunch for six colleagues, not a customer event, and you have changed the material it can work with. That material is context.

Context can include your message, instructions, earlier conversation, material from files and results returned by tools. What actually reaches the model depends on the product. A document sitting in your account is not proof that its contents were used.

The context window is a different thing: it is the limit on how much material the model can process at once, measured in tokens. Context is the material; the window sets a capacity limit. If an important detail has disappeared from a long conversation, restate it and check that the next answer follows it.
