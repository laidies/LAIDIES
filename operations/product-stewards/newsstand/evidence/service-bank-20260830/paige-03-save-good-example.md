# Save the example, not just the prompt.

A useful result gives you a concrete standard for the next attempt.

When an AI-assisted email finally sounds like you, save a version with private details removed. Keep it beside the instruction that produced it and a short note about what you changed.

Next time, try: “Use this example for length and tone, not for its facts. Write a new email from the notes below. Do not carry over the example’s names, dates or promises.”

An example makes ‘friendly but direct’ less vague. It can also contaminate the new draft with old details, which is why the final check should compare facts with today’s notes, not with the sample.
