# NewsStand Daily service templates — candidate for Ali review

**Status:** CANDIDATE ONLY. These templates do not accept any existing example,
authorize autonomous drafting, create a dated Daily issue or permit publication.

The four desks have different reader jobs. A source may support more than one
NewsStand destination only when each treatment creates a distinct useful
outcome. It may not be copied across desks to fill the paper.

## Paige's Practical AI Tip

**Use when:** a current source or maintained LAiDIES concept supports one useful,
bounded work-facing AI action a reader can try today. Target: 90–180 words.

| Section | Content | Analysis | Delivery | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The work moment | One recognisable task, decision or frustration | Select a situation where the proposed action changes the work rather than merely sounding sensible | One direct opening sentence in ordinary workplace language | Begin with an abstract AI principle, product announcement or generic productivity claim |
| Try this | One exact action, setup or short prompt pattern | Make the action small enough to use today and proportionate to the source evidence | Imperative wording; include the material or context the reader must supply | Offer a vague instruction such as “be specific,” “check the receipts” or “use AI responsibly” without a new method |
| Why it helps | The cause-and-effect reason the action can improve the result | Explain what information, constraint, comparison or verification step changes the AI process | One or two plain sentences | Claim the wording is magic, universal or guaranteed |
| Keep it honest | The important privacy, accuracy, workplace-policy or capability boundary | Identify the most likely way the action could mislead or fail | Short adjacent warning, not legalistic boilerplate | Bury the limit, ask for confidential material or imply the AI has verified its own answer |
| Check the result | The observable human check before the output is used | Give the reader a real acceptance test rather than “review carefully” | One concrete check or comparison | Repeat the same instruction as Try this or make the AI the final judge |
| Go further | An exact current LAiDIES destination only when it adds depth | Preserve one durable concept owner and avoid turning the tip into a mini-class | Optional single link | Link to an unfinished, stale or merely related destination |

**Rotation rule:** Paige may revisit a broad topic only when a new method or
materially different situation produces a new reader action. A recently used
lesson cannot be paraphrased to fill another date.

## Career / Work-Life

**Use when:** credible career or life guidance is useful before AI enters the
item, and a faithful AI connection later helps the reader understand or apply
the same underlying principle. Target: 150–300 words.

| Section | Content | Analysis | Delivery | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The real situation | One specific professional or life tension | Choose a problem involving judgement, relationships, visibility, boundaries, decisions or growth—not an AI workflow wearing a career label | Human opening that remains useful if every later AI sentence is deleted | Open with prompting, AI disclosure, tool use or a technology headline |
| The useful advice | The source-backed career or life principle | Explain why the guidance fits this situation and where context matters | Advice first, in plain language | Use generic confidence language, therapy claims or a slogan without practical meaning |
| What that can sound like | Concrete wording, decision rule or next action | Convert the principle into something the reader could realistically say or do | One specific example | Give a canned script that ignores power, culture, confidentiality or personal judgement |
| The AI connection | The same underlying mechanism as it appears in an AI interaction | Prove the connection: name what is genuinely shared between the human advice and AI use | Explicit transition after the complete non-AI advice | Bolt on an unrelated AI tip merely to qualify for LAiDIES |
| Where the comparison stops | The human, organizational or technical difference that the analogy cannot carry | Prevent the AI parallel from flattening a complex career or life problem | One candid boundary | Suggest AI can resolve fairness, relationships, workplace politics or structural problems |
| Try this today | One small non-destructive move | Select an action that remains useful even if the reader never uses AI | Close with a concrete choice or note to make | End with motivation alone or require the reader to share private information |

**Source rule:** the non-AI section binds a credible original practitioner,
research or accountable LAiDIES author. The AI connection binds its own current
source or maintained concept when it makes a volatile product or capability
claim.

## Promptoscope

**Use when:** a funny horoscope frame makes one normally non-work AI-use pattern
memorable. It is a practical comic column, not a prediction and not Paige in a
different outfit. Target: 90–180 words.

| Section | Content | Analysis | Delivery | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The comic forecast | One recognisable home, family, school, hobby, shopping, travel or everyday AI moment | Find the truthful tension that earns the horoscope joke | One playful opening with a clear real-world situation | Use an interchangeable zodiac line, claim personal knowledge or start with a work task |
| The earthly move | One exact thing to tell, show or ask the AI | Choose an action that changes the available context, constraint or comparison | Direct instruction in ordinary language | Become a generic “write a better prompt” tip or require a long formula |
| Why the stars changed | The real non-mystical mechanism | Explain why the action changes the result without dropping the comic frame | One plain cause-and-effect sentence | Attribute the result to luck, Mercury or a magic phrase |
| The human reality check | One safety, accuracy or real-world verification step | Select the check most relevant to the situation | Short specific check | Add generic caution unrelated to the example or let the AI verify itself |
| The sign-off | One earned final joke that lands the practical lesson | Reinforce the situation or limit rather than introduce a new claim | One short Rewind-era or horoscope-flavoured line | Copy Paige's serious cadence, imitate Mme CLAi-O's deck or let the joke obscure the action |

**Distinctness rule:** if the item still works unchanged in a workplace memo, it
belongs to Paige. If it relies on a fixed authored card or reflective reading,
it belongs to Mme CLAi-O.

## Mme CLAi-O's Reading of the Day

**Use when:** the deterministic dated selection points to one exact card in Mme
CLAi-O's governed deck. The reading is fixed, non-predictive and never generated
from reader data. Target: 60–140 words.

| Section | Content | Analysis | Delivery | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today's card | Exact governed card title | Verify the immutable deck ID and dated selection before any prose is assembled | Prominent card name | Invent, rename, combine or regenerate a card |
| The reading | Exact fixed authored card language | Preserve the approved meaning and sequence | Display the governed wording without paraphrase | Expand the reading with model-generated interpretation or current-news filler |
| The small move | Exact governed reflection or action | Keep the move proportionate and non-clinical | One clear question or action | Turn reflection into diagnosis, therapy, financial, legal or medical advice |
| What this is | The non-predictive, non-personalised boundary | Make clear that no reader data produced or tailored the selection | Friendly adjacent disclosure | Imply divination, prediction, personalization or knowledge of the reader |
| Visit Mme CLAi-O | Exact current route to the full governed experience | Verify the destination and failure state on the publication date | Optional single continuation | Link to an unavailable route or imply the Daily card grants a personalized reading |

**Selection rule:** the issue stores the exact card ID, deck version and dated
selection method. A card may not be silently regenerated or swapped after the
issue is admitted.

## Song of the Day

connection to the day's paper or mood. Target: 35–100 words.

| Section | Content | Analysis | Delivery | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| On today's request line | Exact song title and artist | Verify the metadata, release identity and current availability | Prominent title and artist | Guess a version, remix, artist credit or release identity |
| Why it belongs today | One specific connection to the paper, town moment or useful mood | Earn the selection through meaning rather than era alone | One lively sentence | Choose a track merely because it is from the Rewind Era or force a teaching metaphor |
| Press play | One currently lawful listening destination | Check the exact route on the issue date | One clear action with provider boundary | Host unlicensed audio, reproduce lyrics or imply universal regional availability |
| Availability note | Any material provider, region, account or version limit | Preserve the difference between an editorial pick and owned LAiDIES media | Compact adjacent note when needed | Hide sign-in, cost, region or removal uncertainty |

## Did You Know?

**Use when:** one currently verified fact helps a visitor understand or use
LAiDIES better. This is site guidance, not general AI trivia. Target: 35–100
words.

| Section | Content | Analysis | Delivery | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The useful surprise | One exact current LAiDIES feature, route, behavior or boundary | Select something genuinely helpful or delightful that readers may miss | One plain, specific fact | Promote an unlaunched feature, inflate availability or publish generic AI trivia |
| Why you might care | The visitor job it makes easier or more enjoyable | Connect the fact to a real action, decision or discovery | One short payoff sentence | Treat novelty alone as usefulness |
| Try it here | One exact current destination or action | Verify the public route and its failure state on the issue date | Direct link with concrete label | Link to a placeholder, stale route or destination that does not perform the claim |

## Town Notes

**Use when:** a dated, real LAiDIES operational or community notice changes what
a visitor can do, attend, find or expect. Target: 40–120 words.

| Section | Content | Analysis | Delivery | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The notice | Exact dated change, event, opening, closure or availability state | Establish that the notice is current and visitor-relevant | Answer first with date and affected place | Turn internal work, local code or an idea into a public announcement |
| Who it affects | Visitors, residents or a narrower named group | Bound the audience and avoid universal claims | One clear sentence | Leave readers guessing whether the notice applies to them |
| What to do | Exact route, time, requirement or alternative | Give the smallest useful next action | Direct action with timezone or eligibility where material | Send readers to an unavailable destination or hide a material limitation |
| Check-back point | End date or next verification trigger when the notice is temporary | Prevent an expired notice from looking current | Compact dated footer | Leave temporary information without expiry or correction ownership |

## Today's Curiosity / Mutual-Support Move

**Use when:** one small, optional action helps a reader explore, compare,
practice or support another person without pretending to be a news story.
Target: 50–140 words.

| Section | Content | Analysis | Delivery | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today's invitation | One specific low-friction question, comparison, observation or act of help | Choose an action with a real learning or community payoff | Warm optional opening | Issue a vague “be curious” challenge, guilt the reader or manufacture engagement |
| How to try it | Exact steps, object or person involved | Keep the move safe, private and possible today | One or two concrete sentences | Require public posting, an account, private data or another person's participation without consent |
| What to notice | The pattern, difference or human response worth observing | Turn the activity into a useful reflection rather than busywork | One focused question | Predetermine the “right” discovery or turn anecdote into evidence |
| Share or continue | Optional current LAiDIES route only when a real intake/continuation exists | Preserve consent and honest product state | Optional single action | Pretend a feedback/community mechanism exists or block completion behind sharing |

## SUNNYVAiLE Desk

**Use when:** one brief fictional town item adds delight or continuity to the
NewsStand without masquerading as sourced reporting. Target: 50–160 words.

| Section | Content | Analysis | Delivery | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fiction label | Exact visible `SUNNYVAiLE FICTION` disclosure | Separate world-building from all sourced news at first glance | Place before the headline and body | Hide the disclosure in metadata, a footer or an ambiguous town voice |
| The town item | One canon-consistent event, exchange or observation | Serve a named humour, character or continuity job | Brief newspaper-style item in the established LAiDIES voice | Invent a real-world claim, product availability, user activity or public event |
| The real boundary | Any real LAiDIES destination or status mentioned by the fiction | Verify that the joke cannot create a false functional promise | Plain adjacent clarification when needed | Let fictional copy imply that a route, reward, character interaction or community feature is live |
| Optional continuation | Exact current entertainment or town destination when one genuinely extends the item | Preserve the distinction between story world and site function | One optional link | Send readers to an unfinished route or attach a factual source list to make fiction look reported |

**Separation rule:** fictional town material never occupies The Breaking, a
news-story slot, The Week's Biggest Story or a sourced update. Search and archive
results retain the visible fiction label.
