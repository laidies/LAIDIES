# NewsStand layout research — 6 September 2026
Status: recommendation; private pilot preserved, not approved or deployed.

## Evidence
Ali rejects arbitrary catch-up colour grouping, butter-yellow panels, tiny town illustrations, Mme CLAi-O text floating beneath art and oversized empty space. The private three-column pilot restores art but still leaves unused height below shorter features; it is not a sufficient final solution.

NN/G, Common Region (2020): shared backgrounds/boundaries imply relationships; decorative containers can create clutter. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/common-region/
Design Museum interview with Guardian creative director Alex Breuer (2018): readability/navigation guided print and digital; distinct editorial formats have distinct pace; colour identifies meaningful sections. https://designmuseum.org/exhibitions/print-in-the-digital-age-redesigning-the-guardian-newspaper/qa-with-alex-breuer
Guardian design guide: typography for signposting, pillar/highlight/architecture colour roles. https://design.theguardian.com/
These are design principles and practitioner precedents, not tests of LAiDIES readers. No public user research was claimed.

## Recommended composition
1. Mme CLAi-O gets one bounded horizontal newspaper feature: prominent portrait card in a fixed left image area, complete reading in the right text area, action beneath the text. No CSS floats or text wrapping under art. Height follows content, not neighbouring tiles. Phone stacks image then reading.
2. Below it, What's New and Did You Know use two illustrated columns with large images above headlines, aligned starts and a navy dividing rule. No butter-yellow panel and no stacking beside a stretched Mme column.
3. Catch-up is a compact chronological list: small edition/date, headline, existing summary and link, one consistent reading surface, navy horizontal rules. No alternating column backgrounds or equal-height story slots. Preserve all content and reader links.
4. Colour has a stable job: vivid pink/coral/orange pop-art section heads, cyan accents and controls, navy readable text. Avoid random full-panel colour alternation; no butter-yellow, cream replacement or purple body text. Preserve existing approved artwork and masthead geometry.

Next: make one representative two-section pilot with actual current content; inspect desktop390/320 and longer reading variants before independent review and scoped release. Source selection and article changes are outside this layout correction.
