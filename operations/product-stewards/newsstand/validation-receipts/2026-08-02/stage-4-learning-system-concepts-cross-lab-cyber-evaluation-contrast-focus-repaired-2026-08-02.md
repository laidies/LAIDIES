| Article layer | Mental-model job | Analogy mapping and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Evaluation design / written scope | Defines the task and the claim that the targets are simulated or unreachable. | The drill instruction that every room belongs to the exercise. A statement is not enforcement. |
| Network containment | Makes the prohibited real-world route technically unreachable. | The real guest-room door. A physical-door analogy does not specify an AI network architecture. |
| Model safeguards | Adds behavioural guardrails to the system operating inside the environment. | Not a hotel employee or a replacement for the locked door; the analogy deliberately does not collapse this layer into the scenario. |
| Trajectory monitoring | Watches the sequence and emerging outcome, not merely an isolated action or a final score. | Supervision during the drill. Observation can reveal trouble but is not containment or halt authority. |

Least privilege is the durable operating rule that narrows permissions, credentials, write access and reachable network paths before work begins. It supports containment and reduces blast radius; it is not a synonym for the other four controls. The article's reader actions preserve this distinction.
