## Reader-learning ruling

**PASS.** The account box gives the reader a useful, non-technical decision
tree without erasing the facts that make the change uneven:

- **Free:** Terra remains available; Luna does not, and OpenAI did not announce
  a larger Free allowance.
- **Go:** Terra, not Luna; the subscription price and quota budget are
  unchanged, while Terra uses fewer credits in Codex and ChatGPT Work.
- **Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise:** Terra or Luna; their named
  subscription prices and quota budgets are unchanged, while those models use
  fewer credits in the same two product paths.
- **API users:** the direct published token-rate reduction applies under a
  different usage meter.

This explicitly avoids the misleading shortcut “paid plans get more.” Go is a
paid plan but cannot choose Luna; Free has Terra access without a newly stated
allowance; and neither the subscription price nor the nominal quota changed.
The answer therefore names *who* may see an effect and *where* before stating
the possible effect.

The limit answer is accurate and suitably bounded for a newcomer: lower credit
consumption can mean an existing paid allowance lasts longer **only** for the
eligible Terra/Luna use in Codex or ChatGPT Work. It does not turn into a claim
of an increased general ChatGPT message limit or a guaranteed number of extra
tasks. That is the right level of explanation for a Breaking item; a numerical
task estimate would invent an account-specific result that the source does not
provide.
