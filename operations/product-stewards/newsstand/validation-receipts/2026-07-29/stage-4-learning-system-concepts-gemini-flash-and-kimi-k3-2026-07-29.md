| Element | Disposition | Reason and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Gemini 3.6 Flash / 3.5 Flash-Lite names, prices, GA status, Antigravity default, migration rules and Flash Cyber access | **DATED — NEWSSTAND ONLY** | These are release facts with a required freshness sweep. They do not belong in an evergreen lesson. |

### Exact learning destinations

1. **High `high:gemini-same-question-two-answers` — EXTEND LATER, not link.**
   Its distinct future job is a controlled, current comparison: one safe task
   across the chosen Gemini models, then a reader judgment of output, elapsed
   time, total cost and failure/retry behaviour. Its current status is
   `not-scheduled`; no draft may link to it or imply the class exists.
2. **`library:whos-who` and `episode:05` — DECLINE AS CURRENT DESTINATIONS.**
   They concern provider/model/app layers, but are respectively held and
   unruled. Using them would turn a current release into unsupported curriculum
   and violate the no-held-link rule.
3. **Concepts 101 / Vocab — DECLINE NEW ITEM.** The general decision rule is
   adequately captured as a future controlled-practice outcome; a separate
   book entry would duplicate the dated story without a distinct durable
   reference job.

The Breaking draft remains appropriately self-contained: it tells the reader
to test rather than switch and names the checks. No additional learning copy is
required for this exact draft, and no unavailable destination should be added.
