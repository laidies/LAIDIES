## Why this cannot pass

The compound has a material source change after its editorial draft was
completed. The exact article correctly rejects the sensational conclusion that
government has ordered a pause, but now overcorrects: it tells readers the
story is at “employee advocacy” level while OpenAI and Anthropic have publicly
supported the initiative. Its claim map does not contain this development, and
its headline/reality-check boundary needs to be redrawn rather than patched
silently.

The 1,310-signature count is verified only as a time-bounded live reading. It
must be checked again immediately before any later release decision. The
statement's language about personal *comments* must not be expanded into an
unqualified assertion that all signatures are personal-capacity statements.
