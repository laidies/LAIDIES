# The Weekly — subproduct dossier and operating specification

## Promise, audience and job

The Weekly is a durable synthesis that connects the week’s evidence without
repeating the headline cycle. It helps a newcomer build a coherent map and a
returning reader see what accumulated, conflicted or changed direction.
Frequency is weekly only when a synthesis has earned publication.

The current publication is `hold`. Its sole candidate, a Health-in-ChatGPT
story, is held for independent accuracy/editorial approval.

## Editorial and source contract

- Synthesize across developments; do not concatenate Daily items.
- State the durable question, source chronology, material agreement and
  disagreement, demonstrated present state, uncertainty, consequences and
  what to watch next.
- Use primary/official evidence plus independent or authoritative context
  proportionate to risk. Health/privacy claims cannot clear on vendor evidence.
- Preserve the news format: narrative synthesis and receipts, not artificial
  lessons, quizzes or an evergreen-course shell.
- `maxAgeHours` is 192. A missed recheck blocks current presentation.

## Reader contract

The cover and selector must say **The Weekly**. Existing Wednesday artwork is
archive art only and cannot imply approved Weekly identity. Newcomers receive
enough background to enter the story; returning readers receive synthesis
rather than repetition. Held copy never leaks through search or a saved hash.
Corrections remain adjacent; retraction removes the body and preserves history.

## Ownership and plumbing

The Weekly editor owns synthesis and continuity with Daily/Breaking coverage.
Accuracy and the relevant high-stakes specialist independently approve claim
maps. Parent and Platform own canonical data, artifact and rollback. The weekly
episode/newsletter production system is a dependency, not publication
authority for this paper.

## Experience, measurement and revenue

The physical weekly paper can support a richer long read, source timeline and
“what changed since last week” orientation. It must reflow at 390 px, 200%
zoom, reduced motion and native screen readers. Measure accurate recall,
relationship understanding, source use, correction clarity and useful return.
Print/collectible editions are a later possibility only with rights,
fulfilment, accessibility and an editorial firewall.

## Acceptance and holds

Acceptance requires independent approval of a representative synthesis,
approved Weekly visual or deliberate text-only treatment, source-to-reader and
correction/rollback proof, exact artifact, native accessibility, owner visual
approval and authorized public verification. Current verdict:
**SPECIFIED — EDITORIAL/OWNER/RELEASE HOLD**.
