# The Breaking — subproduct dossier and operating specification

**Status:** SPECIFIED — QUIET LOCALLY; PUBLICATION AND RELEASE HOLD  
**Parent:** SUNNYVAiLE NewsStand  
**Machine edition:** `breaking`  
**Route:** `/newsstand.html`

## Promise, audience and job

The Breaking is the rare interruption for a consequential AI change that a
reader should know before the next Daily. It serves smart newcomers and
returning readers who need an immediate choice-changing explanation, not an
alarm feed. A qualifying model release is Breaking when capability, access,
price, speed, limits, privacy, safety or workflow meaningfully changes; a
feature release needs independent urgency or consequence.

Frequency is conditional, never scheduled. A clear day is success. The current
record is `quiet`, with zero Breaking stories.

## Editorial and source contract

- Apply D-033 through D-040: explanation floor, no filler, reality-check and
  model-release choice analysis.
- Require a timestamped claim map, primary/official sources, access/cost and
  predecessor comparison when relevant, uncertainty, affected readers,
  practical action and watch points.
- Interested-party evidence may establish what a vendor announced, never the
  entire truth of safety, performance or impact.
- Incidents, allegations, health, legal, political, privacy or safety claims
  stay on mandatory human hold.
- `maxAgeHours` is 24. An overdue check blocks current presentation.

## Reader contract

Newcomers first see why the interruption qualified and what changed. Returning
readers see a dated current story or a calm, dated quiet state. Direct routes,
search and Back/Forward must all re-run the same access decision. Corrected
stories keep a dated correction; retracted stories keep only the withdrawal
notice. No empty red banner, countdown or urgency theatre is allowed.

## Ownership and plumbing

The private radar may discover candidates; it cannot publish. The subchampion
owns edition classification and draft quality. Accuracy independently owns the
claim map. The parent owns canonical data and reader integration. Platform owns
artifact, deploy, rollback and public monitoring. Current automation is a
manual legacy RSS/LLM review artifact disconnected from the public dataset.

## Experience, measurement and revenue

Use the NewsStand’s Paige/rack/newspaper grammar, but prominence exists only
while a qualified story is current. The Breaking must be first in status and
keyboard order without trapping focus. Measure comprehension, useful action,
source use, correction clarity and trust; never optimize alarm clicks. No
sponsorship, affiliate or paid placement may influence qualification,
headline, sourcing or prominence.

## Acceptance and holds

Acceptance requires an independently scored qualified and clear-day fixture,
producer-to-reader/correction/rollback proof, exact artifact, native
accessibility and authorized public verification. Current verdict:
**SPECIFIED — QUIET; RELEASE HOLD**. No story, publication, owner approval or
public freshness is claimed.
