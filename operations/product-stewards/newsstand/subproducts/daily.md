# The Daily — subproduct dossier and operating specification

**Status:** SPECIFIED — QUIET LOCALLY; PUBLICATION AND RELEASE HOLD  
**Parent:** SUNNYVAiLE NewsStand  
**Machine edition:** `daily`  
**Route:** `/newsstand.html`

## Promise, audience and job

The Daily is an edited briefing of consequential developments since its
previous issue. It is for a smart newcomer who wants enough context to
understand today’s change and a returning reader who wants a compact,
non-repetitive update. It is not the retired TODAY raw feed.

Cadence means “checked daily,” not “must publish daily.” A clear day is valid.
The current record is `quiet`, with zero Daily stories.

## Editorial and source contract

- Every item states prior context, what happened, how it works, why it matters,
  who it affects, what changes or does not yet change, uncertainty and watch
  points.
- Build the primary-source read before comparative reporting or AIDB input.
  Attribute any borrowed analysis that materially shapes the LAiDIES read.
- Use an everyday analogy and a Rewind Era/SUNNYVAiLE analogy only when each
  improves understanding; state where it stops matching.
- Routine feature releases belong here only when they materially change a
  reader’s choice. Low-consequence churn is rejected, not padded.
- `maxAgeHours` is 36. An overdue check blocks current presentation.

## Reader contract

Arrival explains the briefing’s time window and newest meaningful change.
Returning readers can distinguish a new issue from a checked-but-quiet day.
Stories expose dates, sources, evidence versus inference and practical
implications. Search/direct/history paths share the parent access gate.
Correction and retraction behavior is identical across the rack and archive.

## Ownership and plumbing

The radar prepares candidates; the Daily editor selects and synthesizes.
Accuracy independently approves claim maps. The parent writes only approved
records to `content/newsstand-stories.js`; Platform proves artifact, release
and rollback. The existing manual `hot-goss` workflow produces a private,
seven-day artifact and is not a Daily backend, scheduler or publication path.

## Experience, measurement and revenue

The Daily should be the dependable return habit, with compact scanability that
does not remove explanation. Measure whether readers can accurately state what
changed and choose test/switch/wait/ignore; also source use, corrections and
voluntary return. Never log raw archive queries without an approved privacy
design. Any membership support funds reporting, not privileged facts or a
weaker free explanation.

## Acceptance and holds

Acceptance requires a representative current issue plus a quiet-day fixture,
independent 17/20 editorial/trust/brand floors, source-to-reader and
correction/rollback proof, exact artifact, native accessibility and authorized
public verification. Current verdict: **SPECIFIED — QUIET; RELEASE HOLD**.
