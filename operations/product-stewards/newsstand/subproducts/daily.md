## Promise, audience and job

The Daily is the complete dated SUNNYVAiLE newspaper. It is for a smart
newcomer who wants today's consequential change explained plainly and a
returning reader who wants a useful daily habit. Its sourced-news desk may be
quiet, but the dated paper still publishes whatever recurring service desks are
fresh and admitted, with governed empty states instead of invented filler. It
is not the retired TODAY raw feed and not one long article wearing a masthead.

The current complete-newspaper direction is binding in
`NEWSSTAND-EDITORIAL-PRODUCTION-STANDARD.md` and
`DAILY-NEWSPAPER-EXPERIENCE-BRIEF.md`. This dossier's former “checked daily,
not must publish daily” wording was superseded by Ali's August 3 decision and
must not be used as production authority.

## Editorial and source contract

- Before full prose, every item passes the representative-proof production
  sequence in `NEWSSTAND-EDITORIAL-PRODUCTION-STANDARD.md`: exact route and
  story mode; one reader question and payoff; factual headline; answer-first
  opening; newcomer background; causal outline; evidence boundary; useful
  applications and landing; proportional length.
- Every item then states prior context, what happened, how it works, why it
  matters, who it affects, what changes or does not yet change, uncertainty and
  watch points in the order required by its selected story mode.
- Build the primary-source read before comparative reporting or AIDB input.
  Attribute any borrowed analysis that materially shapes the LAiDIES read.
- Use an everyday analogy and a Rewind Era/SUNNYVAiLE analogy only when each
  improves understanding; state where it stops matching.
- Routine feature releases belong here only when they materially change a
  reader’s choice. Low-consequence churn is rejected, not padded.
- `maxAgeHours` is 36. An overdue check blocks current presentation.

## Reader contract

The route opens on today's complete Daily newspaper. It does not open on a
Paige hero, a rack selector, an article shell or a dashboard. Breaking appears
above the Daily only when it genuinely qualifies. Weekly and Big Picture are
obvious alternate papers without displacing today's issue. Returning readers
can distinguish a new issue from a sourced-news-quiet day. Stories expose
dates, sources, evidence versus inference and practical implications, while
recurring desks remain visibly part of the same paper. Search, topics, archive,
direct and history paths share the parent access gate. Correction and
retraction behavior is identical across the issue and archive.

## Ownership and plumbing

The radar prepares candidates; the Daily editor selects and synthesizes.
Accuracy independently approves claim maps. The parent writes only approved
records to `content/newsstand-stories.js`; Platform proves artifact, release
and rollback. The existing manual `hot-goss` workflow produces a private,
seven-day artifact and is not a Daily backend, scheduler or publication path.

## Experience, measurement and revenue

The Daily should be the dependable return habit, with compact scanability that
does not remove explanation. Measure whether readers can accurately state what
changed and choose test/switch/wait/ignore; also source use, corrections and
voluntary return. Never log raw archive queries without an approved privacy
design. Any membership support funds reporting, not privileged facts or a
weaker free explanation.

## Acceptance and holds

Acceptance requires a representative complete issue plus a sourced-news-quiet
fixture, producer proof before prose, independent editorial/trust review,
observed cold-reader comprehension, exact same-viewport full-page desktop and
mobile inspection, Ali approval of that complete newspaper, source-to-reader
and correction/rollback proof, native accessibility and authorized public
verification. Current verdict: **PRODUCTION REPAIR — RELEASE HOLD**.
