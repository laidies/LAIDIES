## 1. Career / work-life — seed 22

The parent is sound. Episode 02 explicitly frames prompting as delegation and
teaches task, audience/context, constraints and what good looks like. Its
canonical source calls a prompt "a delegation" and says that a useful brief
specifies the task, audience, context, tone, constraints and success. Seed 22
keeps the human limit: a person also needs autonomy, dialogue and development.
It is low consequence and its proposed result is concrete, so it is a good
Daily-scale service column rather than a class substitute.

The bank's own release rule nevertheless requires the exact parent, evidence,
analogy limit, risk boundary **and destination** to pass before a Daily card.
The bank says nothing in it is public. The existing Daily derivative schema
cannot represent `career_work_life`; it only permits `paige_tip` and
`promptoscope`. This review cannot convert a prose seed into an admitted
Daily record or silently extend that schema.
