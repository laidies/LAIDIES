# Promptoscope source map — group-chat shortlist

Checked: 2026-08-14 America/Vancouver
Public status: private candidate evidence only

## Reader job

Help a nontechnical reader turn a circular personal group-chat decision into
one comparable shortlist without pretending AI can discover the group’s hidden
preferences or make the decision for them.

## Current primary sources

1. OpenAI, **How do I create a good prompt for an AI model?**
   https://help.openai.com/en/articles/4936848-how-do-i-create-a-good-prompt-for-an-ai-model-like-gpt4
   Checked 2026-08-14. The current page recommends a clear task, necessary
   context, a preferred output shape and, when useful, asking for multiple
   options. The Promptoscope candidate uses three human-chosen constraints and
   asks for three options in the same format.

2. OpenAI, **Retiring group chats in ChatGPT**
   https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12703475-group-chats-in-chatgpt
   Checked 2026-08-14. New ChatGPT group chats stopped being available from
   2026-07-09 and existing rooms are moving to read-only. The candidate
   therefore says to use an ordinary private AI chat and bring the shortlist
   back to the human group. It does not direct readers to a retired feature.

## Claim boundary

- The AI receives only the date, budget and neighbourhood the humans already
  agreed to share.
- The requested output is three options using the same comparison fields. It is
  a shortlist, not an instruction to choose for the group.
- Price, travel time and availability can change; the reader checks the real
  booking page.
- The candidate makes no claim that AI knows unspoken preferences, that a
  particular restaurant is available or that an AI group-chat product exists.

## Freshness triggers

Recheck if either official OpenAI page materially changes, if the candidate
names a provider-specific feature, or if its action expands beyond a bounded
shortlist.
