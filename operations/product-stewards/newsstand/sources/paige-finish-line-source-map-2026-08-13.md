# Paige source map — name the finish line before another AI revision

**Checked:** 2026-08-13 PDT

**Candidate lane:** Paige's Practical AI Tip

## Current primary sources

1. Anthropic, **Prompt engineering overview**

   https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview

   The current guide says prompt refinement should start with a clear definition
   of success and a way to test against it. It also warns that not every failure
   is best solved by rewriting the prompt.

2. Anthropic, **Define success criteria and build evaluations**

   https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/test-and-evaluate/define-success

   The current guide says useful success criteria are specific and measurable,
   and that the evaluation method should match the kind of result being judged.

## What those sources support

- A reader should decide what a successful result must visibly do before
  repeatedly changing prompt wording.
- Concrete acceptance checks give both the AI revision and the human review a
  more useful target than an adjective such as “better,” “stronger” or “more
  polished.”
- The human remains responsible for deciding whether the real work passes.

## LAiDIES adaptation for this compact tip

For one ordinary work draft, write three observable finish-line checks before
requesting one revision. Example checks for a briefing note: the decision is
named in the first paragraph; every figure matches the approved source; the
next action has an owner and date. Ask the AI to revise toward those checks,
then compare the result with each check yourself.

This is a bounded practical adaptation of the sources, not a claim that three
is a scientifically optimal number. Three keeps the Daily action small enough
to try without turning the card into an evaluation course.

## What the sources do not establish

- Criteria do not make an AI answer accurate, private, fair or suitable for
  use.
- An AI tool cannot be the final judge of its own output.
- This method does not repair a wrong model, missing source material,
  contradictory instructions or a task that should not use AI.
- The source guidance is written for AI-system and prompt development. The
  compact workplace example is LAiDIES' clearly labelled practical adaptation.

## Privacy and workplace boundary

Use only an AI tool and material permitted by the reader's workplace. The
example does not require confidential material. A revision can still alter a
fact while satisfying visible structure checks, so figures and claims must be
checked against their approved sources.
