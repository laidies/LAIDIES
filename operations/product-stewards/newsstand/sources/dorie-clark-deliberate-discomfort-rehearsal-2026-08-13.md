## Primary career source

- **Dorie Clark, LinkedIn:** [“Why Successful People Choose Discomfort Over Safety”](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/doriec_we-often-assume-successful-people-are-more-activity-7470929021355126784-_F1F)
- **Observed date state:** public post displayed as three weeks old when checked on 2026-08-13.
- **Exact useful claim:** Clark says people who advance are not necessarily more confident; they may be more willing to choose discomfort deliberately, including difficult conversations, sharing ideas publicly and taking risks before they feel fully ready.
- **Evidence class:** named practitioner's observation and advice. It is not a controlled study, a guarantee that discomfort produces advancement, or evidence that every workplace rewards risk fairly.

## Current AI-tool source

- **OpenAI Help Center:** [ChatGPT Voice](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001274)
- **Observed current facts:** Voice supports spoken back-and-forth inside a ChatGPT conversation. Options and availability vary by plan, workspace settings, region and app version. OpenAI warns that ChatGPT can make mistakes.
- **Current data boundary:** the page says Live and Advanced Voice audio clips are stored with chat history and retained for 30 days; deleting a chat deletes associated clips within 30 days, subject to stated security, safety and legal exceptions. Standard Voice audio is deleted after transcription unless the user opted to share audio for training. Workplace settings and policies can impose additional limits.

## LAiDIES adaptation

LAiDIES may connect Clark's non-AI advice to one narrow rehearsal exercise: use an available voice-capable AI as a practice partner for a bounded difficult conversation. The user supplies the role, desired outcome and one likely objection, without names or confidential details. The AI raises one objection at a time so the user can practise a clear response.

This adaptation does **not** claim that AI can predict the other person, understand workplace power, decide whether a situation is safe or fair, replace human advice, or make a dangerous, discriminatory, legal or otherwise high-stakes conversation safe.
