ritual, but compete a NewsStand-specific environment rather than cloning the

direct official feeds are the incumbent; Inoreader and self-hosted
changedetection.io are challengers. Any account, spend, credential or external
data authority requires Ali plus Platform/Privacy approval. No plugin or

same representative states:

2. all-clear/quiet state;
3. corrected story;

Every direction must keep Paige, the exact four mastheads, real live

Blind judges score product, accuracy/trust and LAiDIES brand; each floor is
17/20 before Ali sees an owner-ready candidate.

### NS-P0-2 through NS-P0-6 — publication authority

Build one private deterministic corpus with:

- one valid and at least two reject/quiet cases per publication;
- Breaking clear-day and Daily quiet-day cases;
- Weekly multi-development synthesis and Tribune thesis/counterargument cases;
- source receipts with exact retrieval time, lawful retained bytes/hash,
  claim bindings, source type and recheck deadline;
- hard-hold, disputed, source-changed, future/stale, duplicate/escaped-key,
  cross-publication relabel and candidate-self-authority attacks;
- independent decision identity bound to exact candidate/source/schema/policy
  hashes; and
- published → corrected → retracted → rollback transaction receipts.

The compiler may emit a proposed public record only. It must not edit
`content/newsstand-stories.js` until the release manager presents an
independently accepted decision and holds a canonical-data lock.

### NS-P0-7 — reader and four visitor scopes

For each of first-time, returning without Card, device-local Card and verified
account scope, run:

`arrival → paper state → paper/quiet result → story or archive → source/next
route → return → changed freshness/correction → revisit`.

Also run first→return, visitor→Card, Card→account, sign-out, second tab/device,
storage denied/corrupt, direct referral hash, source outage, invalid dataset
and offline/static-media failure. Card/account state must not alter publication
access or imply saved/synced history unless a separately accepted contract is
implemented.

### NS-P0-8 — propagation and shared-system repair

- Homepage: use the same admitted data; collapse The Breaking on clear day;
  route The Daily to the exact full explanation; never cache a corrected/
  retracted headline independently.
- Visitor's Centre/directory: preserve current-or-limited copy and exact four
  jobs.
- Tour/rewards: replace route-load success with the ruled authoritative
  NewsStand stop event, or record an explicit Control Room decision that
  arrival is the step; test unavailable desk, duplicate and storage denial.
- Story handoffs: exact Library Accounts 101 and Episode 3 routes must return

- Legacy consumers: inventory and migrate/remove promoted `hot-goss.html`,

## Measurement and learning

- **Baseline:** four local reader desks and a bounded fail-closed review router;
  no complete recurring producer/publication operation, approved final
  environment, NewsStand event contract or current public proof.
- **Success signals:** first-use job comprehension; eligible story/source
  opening; correction understanding; useful next action; honest quiet-state
  acceptance; voluntary return; zero cross-paper/source/correction drift.
- **Failure signals:** filler/alarm clicks, candidate self-approval, stale
  body exposure, false Card/sync/reward claim, raw query capture, broken
  handback or a green upstream job without exact public evidence.
- **Review date:** after one exact local candidate and again after an authorized
  public interval.
- **Decision after measurement:** improve explanation/discovery/return,
  recalibrate publication policy, or hold/retract; never weaken the evidence
  floor to increase volume.
- **Dossier updates:** state, backlog, functionality map, exact evidence,
  independent verdict and any qualifying painpoint learning after each cycle.
