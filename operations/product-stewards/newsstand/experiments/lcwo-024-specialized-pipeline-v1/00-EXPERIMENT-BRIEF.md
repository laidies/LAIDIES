## Stages and hard boundaries

1. **Evidence desk** produces `01-EVIDENCE-BRIEF.md`. It may resolve facts,
   uncertainty, mechanism and freshness only. It may not draft prose.
2. **Explanation architect** produces `02-TEACHING-SPINE.md` from the evidence
   brief plus the two explanation benchmarks. It may not read the predecessor,
   writing lock or story template.
3. **Daily publication editor** produces `03-DAILY-DRAFT.md` from the evidence
   brief, teaching spine and exact Daily template. It may not read the
   predecessor or add claims.
4. **LAiDIES line editor** produces `04-LAIDIES-EDIT.md` from the Daily draft,
   evidence brief and writing lock. It may improve voice and clarity but may not
   add facts, change the mechanism or decorate weak teaching.
5. **Independent comparator** reads the predecessor and final successor first,
   then the governing evidence/template/voice sources. It produces
   `05-INDEPENDENT-COMPARISON.md` and must prefer the predecessor, successor or
   neither with exact reasons.

The foreground NewsStand editor owns reconciliation. A specialist cannot mark
its own output admitted, release-ready or published.

## Test acceptance

The experiment succeeds only if the independent comparator finds:

- zero new factual claims outside the evidence brief;
- one clear reader question and one visible cause-and-effect mechanism;
- technical language introduced only after ordinary meaning;
- distinct workplace and non-work examples using the same mechanism;
- a practical action that follows from the evidence rather than generic advice;
- Daily format fit, non-clickbait headline and recognizable LAiDIES voice;
- fewer material reader-facing defects than the 888-word predecessor; and
- no known defect repeated from the current NewsStand rejection registry.

The successful private proof returns to Ali for calibration review. It does not
authorize a page build, issue assembly, deployment or autonomous publication.
