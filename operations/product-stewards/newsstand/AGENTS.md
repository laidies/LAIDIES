# NewsStand scoped production contract

These instructions apply only to NewsStand reporting, newspapers, reference
columns and service desks. They add to the short repository router. Do not load
episode, visual-generation or unrelated product rules for editorial drafting.

## The outcome

NewsStand helps a woman with no technical background understand what happened,
why it matters, what it does and does not mean for her, and what useful idea or
action she can carry forward. Accuracy without understanding fails. Clarity
without factual discipline also fails.

A Daily story is one item inside the Daily newspaper. It is not the whole
edition. The Daily may contain multiple earned stories plus distinct service
desks; a quiet desk uses its honest empty state rather than filler.

## Route before drafting

The reader's question selects the destination:

- **Breaking:** what changed so urgently that waiting for the next Daily would
  materially disadvantage the reader?
- **Daily:** what happened today, what does it mean and why should I care?
- **Weekly:** what did at least two developments add up to across the week?
- **The Big Picture:** what larger consequential pattern, mechanism or power
  question does broader evidence reveal?
- **STRAiGHT TALK:** what is the maintained answer to a recurring AI question?
- **Dear Miss Jeeves:** why does this frustrating AI-use problem keep happening
  and what can the reader realistically do?
- **Service desks:** one exact job only—Paige teaches a practical AI action;
  Career/Work-Life gives career-first guidance with an earned AI connection;
  Promptoscope makes one AI-use pattern funny and memorable; Term of the Week
  teaches one term; Mme CLAi-O provides an authored reflection, not technical
  advice.

One source may contribute to several destinations only when each output answers
a different question and performs additional work. Never multiply one idea
across desks to fill space.

## Use four narrow production roles

1. **Evidence editor:** produces a compact fact pack from current primary or
   official evidence. It states what happened, what establishes it, what it
   does not establish, important limits, source dates and open questions. It
   contains no public-facing flourishes.
2. **Teaching editor:** receives the fact pack, not the whole operational
   history. In ordinary language, she answers the human question, reconstructs
   the mechanism step by step and explains the consequence. Technical terms
   appear only after their meaning is clear.
3. **Format editor:** shapes that explanation for the selected destination.
   The destination controls length, order and emphasis; internal checklist
   labels do not become compulsory public headings.
4. **LAiDIES line editor:** adds warmth, rhythm and earned Rewind Era character
   after the explanation works. She may cut and clarify but may not change the
   facts, enlarge the claim or replace the mechanism with a joke.

For a substantial article, keep these as separate passes with a small handoff.
Do not ask one drafting prompt to research, teach, format, decorate and certify
the result simultaneously. A compact service item may combine roles only when
its factual and teaching job is genuinely small.

## Shared teaching spine—not shared visible sections

Every substantial story must leave the reader able to answer:

1. What is the human question and the short answer?
2. What actually happened?
3. How did it happen, one connected step at a time?
4. What does this change—or not change—for ordinary work and life?
5. What larger learning, useful action or better next question should remain?
6. What is still uncertain or likely to change?

This is a thinking sequence, not a mandated set of headings. Use a workplace or
non-work example only when it makes the mechanism clearer. Do not force both
into every story or compact column.

## Choose the honest reporting mode

- **Plain-Language Explainer:** the source framing is fair; the reader needs an
  announcement, research result or capability explained without jargon.
- **Headline Reality Check:** an exact public headline or report creates a
  materially wrong fear, belief or action when compared with the underlying
  evidence. Name and fairly summarize the reporting, correct the likely
  misunderstanding early, then show what the evidence actually supports.

Do not search for errors when none exist. AIDB is a useful detection lens for
claim-versus-evidence mismatches; it is neither authority nor the public voice.

For every Reality Check, include the transferable media-literacy lesson: what
the headline encouraged the reader to believe, why complex evidence can be
compressed into an attention-winning claim, and what questions reveal the real
scope. Keep the research method, result and meaning together in the narrative;
do not scatter them into orphan sections.

## Producer preflight

Before independent review, the producer reads the exact prose from beginning
to end and repairs it if any answer is no:

- Would a newcomer know within the opening why this is being shared?
- Are every unfamiliar object, action and audience explained before jargon?
- Can the reader follow cause and effect without consulting another tab?
- Does a risk story distinguish ordinary useful AI use from the narrower risky
  action, so the article informs rather than frightens readers away from AI?
- Does the article say what the evidence does not prove and who is directly
  affected?
- Is the larger learning specific to this story rather than generic “check the
  receipts” advice?
- Does every section advance the reader's understanding?
- Is the LAiDIES voice helping the explanation rather than decorating it?

If the prose fails, repair the prose. Do not create another framework artifact,
receipt or reviewer cycle first.

## Checks and review

Mechanical checks may enforce source links, dates, content IDs, route names,
word ranges and required factual boundaries. They may not require one exact
heading, sentence or synonym when several clear versions are possible.

Independent review begins only after the producer preflight is clear. The
reviewer reads the article before maker records and answers the human question
in her own words. Never invent an `OBSERVED_HUMAN` result. If no real unfamiliar
reader has been observed, state that limitation plainly.

Ali's review is used to establish a small approved exemplar set, not as the
permanent Daily publishing queue. Once a format has accepted exemplars and the
producer reliably clears the same newcomer standard, routine Daily production
may proceed through its authorized editorial and release path without waiting
for Ali on every story.

Candidate, checked, committed, deployed and publicly verified remain different
states. No NewsStand item is live until the exact public article and newspaper
surface have been verified.
