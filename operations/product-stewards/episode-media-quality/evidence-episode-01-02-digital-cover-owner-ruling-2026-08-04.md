- Both files are project-produced title cards already checksum-bound in the
  issue-static family and selected as the canonical issue covers.
- Episode 01 carries the exact canonical title, contains no person, likeness or
  third-party logo, and uses the existing issue's adapted reference. This is
  not a legal-clearance conclusion or broader marketing authority.
- Episode 02 carries the exact canonical title and uses original object-led
  imagery without a celebrity likeness, real-band branding or album cover.
- Independent full-resolution review accepted these exact E1/E2 bytes for the
  three narrow digital-cover jobs and rejected the prior VHS incumbents.
- The source cue sheets must not use these files as ordinary cue images. The
  public cover-only builder may use them only as the single static cover named
  by the derived-edition manifest.
