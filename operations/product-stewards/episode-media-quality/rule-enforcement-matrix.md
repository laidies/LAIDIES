Diagnosis: the system has rich rules but relies on prose, source-level cue tables, thumbnails, sampled measurements and a final owner watch that never becomes a blocking machine-readable acceptance gate. That is how wrong identity/background/style, narration-image mismatch, timing drift and fake motion can survive technically valid export checks.
