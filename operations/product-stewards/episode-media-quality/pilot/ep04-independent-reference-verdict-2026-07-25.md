## Cue rulings

### Cue 15 — establish the LUMINAiRY: **FAIL admission**

The scene is legible, the visible `LUMINAiRY` and `LANTERN HILL` lettering
reads correctly, and the rear-view Heroine makes the location/job immediately
understandable. It is not an approval: the only nominated building authority
is visibly a materially different façade and composition (central, symmetrical
frontage with different tower arrangement, entrance and sign placement). It
cannot authenticate the pictured building. The Heroine is seen only from the
rear and her clip/costume continuity is not bound to a cue record. The two
doorway figures are unbound identities.

**Required before reconsideration:** bind an exact approved exterior reference
that genuinely matches this scene, or replace the scene; bind the Heroine
continuity fields and mark the doorway figures as non-identifying decoration
or supply their identity references. A visually attractive near-match is not a
setting reference.

### Cue 16 — explain the MAiVENS: **FAIL admission**

The blue/gold corridor is coherent with the nominated MAiVENS-wing category
reference and visually gives the narration a purposeful place. But it is a
gallery whose point is *who the women are*, so every readable portrait is a
material identity claim. No left-to-right/depth portrait map or per-person
identity source is bound. The naval-uniform portrait in particular reads as a
different, male-presenting person at this scale; it cannot be called Grace
Hopper (or anyone else) from resemblance. The all-caps `THE MAIVENS` treatment
also prevents a character-level check of the locked `MAiVENS` brand spelling.

This fails the no-ambiguous-ensemble/known-MAiVEN rule, rather than merely
leaving a cosmetic annotation unfinished. No motion may be added to repair
it.

**Required before reconsideration:** SHA-bound map for every visible readable
portrait with its exact identity reference and likeness review; remove or
replace every portrait that cannot clear that test. The outcome may be an
environment-only hall, but it may not imply named women through unbound faces.

### Cue 17 — deliberate still / Ada introduction: **FAIL admission**

The image has a clear intentional-still composition: the Heroine looking up
and the large portrait are readable, and the blue/gold room continues from
Cue 16. The frame does not clear the actual subject matter. The foreground
portrait is only *intended* to be Ada by filename and resemblance; the Ada
stained-glass candidate is itself an in-world rendering, not a person/period
identity dossier. The Heroine's partial face, butterfly clips and outfit also
have no cue-level continuity binding. The several background portraits retain
the Cue 16 ambiguity.

**Required before reconsideration:** the same portrait map plus an Ada
identity/period dossier; bind the Heroine's exact reference/costume rules and
state why this five-second hold is the appropriate breathing/transition beat.
It may remain an intentional still only after those image gates pass.

### Cue 18 — London, 1843 transition: **FAIL**

`LONDON, 1843` is legible and the scene immediately communicates an era shift,
but a still cannot satisfy the required semantic transition event. A pan, zoom
or a transition-card hold would not cure that. More importantly, the frame
presents a huge apparently working brass machine beside Ada. The canon guard
is specific: her 1843 Note G was for Babbage's **unbuilt** Analytical Engine.
The repository contains no SHA-bound primary/authoritative reference set for
Ada's appearance, the 1843 setting, documented Engine drawings/models or a
permitted visual interpretation. Therefore the frame risks turning a teaching
guard into a false historical claim. The generic punched-card and paper
imagery cannot authenticate the object or note.

**Required before reconsideration:** an evidence-bound Ada/London/Engine
dossier with an explicit allowed/not-evidenced object list, and a declared
graphic transition plan from the admitted MAiVENS frame to the vetted period
frame: rose-window/light change → stained-glass transformation → white bloom
→ `LONDON, 1843` resolves → period scene. It needs timing, permitted moving
elements and true-still controls. Do not make the present still move.

### Cue 19 — Ada / mechanical calculator: **FAIL**

The clip is not an admissible animation source. Its sampled frames show
substantial changes in Ada's face/posture/card framing and the camera crop,
while oversized glowing musical notation dominates the left half of the image
and changes shape/scale across the five seconds. That is neither a documented
historical mechanism nor a declared, narration-specific semantic event. It
also conflicts with the proposed historical evidence gate and the cue's
teaching job: viewers may reasonably read a working device emitting music,
rather than Ada's insight that a machine could manipulate symbols.

The old clip's dynamic appearance is not clearance: there is no known-still
control, threshold, human normal-speed motion verdict, Ada identity dossier,
period/object dossier, or reviewed repeat strategy for the 9.70-second
placement. The contact sheet corroborates the curator's PENDING/FAIL finding;
it does not rehabilitate the clip.

**Required before reconsideration:** do not loop or re-edit this source. After
the Ada historical dossier clears, create a replacement as either (a) a
deliberate still with the documented object/Note G story made legible through
objects, or (b) one tightly declared action that expresses that idea and
passes true-still-calibrated normal-speed judgment. No oversized literal music
notes, face/body drift, or camera motion standing in for the action.

## Reference sufficiency and history ruling

The curator's `PENDING`/`FAIL` treatment is upheld. I found
`operations/reference/real-people/README.md` but no Ada Lovelace or Grace
Hopper dossier directory, and no traceable reference package for the other
visible MAiVENS. The available stained-glass portraits can be a category/
in-world-artifact source after admission; they are not a substitute for the
identity authority required by the locked visual system. Likewise, the canon's
accurate narrative guard is not a visual source.

No source is ruled correct merely because it is polished, existing, named
`canonical`, or appeared in an earlier selection manifest.
