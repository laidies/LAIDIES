## Non-approval rule

This bundle makes candidate references traceable for an independent Image Quality Judge, Story Editor/historical reviewer, and Motion Quality Judge. It does **not** admit a source, verify a likeness, approve a location, clear motion, or turn legacy final-selection language into current approval. `PENDING` means “identified and available for named review”; `FAIL` means an admission requirement is absent or contradicts the current contract. No item is independently `PASS` here.

Authority order: person-specific identity → master people-rendering style → category/environment reference → scene/era brief → prompt prose. A filename, local render, legacy contact sheet, existing loop, or curation slug cannot move an asset upward in that order.
