## Non-negotiable reading

1. The 1843 item is a translation of Menabrea with Lovelace's notes, including
   Note G's procedure for Bernoulli numbers. It is not evidence that a complete
   Analytical Engine was operating in a room beside Lovelace.
2. The complete Analytical Engine was not completed in Babbage's lifetime;
   documented trial pieces, plans and notebooks exist. A visual may therefore
   show an explicitly **proposed / unbuilt / diagrammatic** Engine, but may not
   present a working historical machine as fact.
3. The National Portrait Gallery records a 1836 portrait and its rights
   restriction. It is a source for a limited, dated portrait reading—not an
   unlicensed image source, not proof of Ada's exact 1843 clothes, workspace or
   pose, and not a licence to copy the painting.
4. A filename, a rendered stained-glass asset, resemblance, or a legacy
   selection sheet does not bind a person’s identity. Any unbound readable
   portrait is removed/replaced rather than named from appearance.
