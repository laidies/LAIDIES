Three genuinely viable rulings

Option 1 · lowest edit count

Yellow plaid everywhere

Restore B05 and B14 to the older fixed host-signature outfit.

  • Changes: 2 beats; B05 includes motion; B14 is still.
  • Continuity: strong within the trailer.
  • Brand: familiar Clueless-coded signature, but less distinct from weekly outfit language.
  • Throwaway: discards both later explicit corrections.
  • Risk: directly reverses “no Episode 4 yellow plaid” at B14.
Option 2 · recommended

Multicolour everywhere

Make the newer trailer-only dress the fixed signature for all nine appearances.

  • Changes: 7 beats; B39 includes motion; six are stills/strips.
  • Continuity: strongest; removes both immediate B04→B05 and B14→B15 reversals.
  • Brand: creates a distinct trailer identity while episode looks keep rotating.
  • Throwaway: highest immediate art replacement cost.
  • Risk: requires a new exact tuple and complete independent re-review.
Option 3 · no media change

Declare a deliberate two-look exception

Keep B05/B14 multicolour and all other appearances yellow, with a bounded authority receipt.

  • Changes: no source media; one formal ruling.
  • Continuity: weakest; outfit switches occur across adjacent beats with no declared story event.
  • Brand: preserves all existing art but makes the host identity feel accidental.
  • Throwaway: none now.
  • Risk: likely moves the same issue into audience-facing review.
