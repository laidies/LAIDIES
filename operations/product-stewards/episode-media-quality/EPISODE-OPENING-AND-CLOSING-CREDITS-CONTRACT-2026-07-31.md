## Why the visuals wait

The recurring credits are the identity of the series. Rebuilding them before
the character and building canon is final would either repeat work or freeze
retired identities into every episode. Production may resume only when all of
these inputs exist as exact checksum-bound allowlists:

1. **Character canon:** the Heroine and recurring named cast, including correct
   face, age, hair, wardrobe and role identity.
2. **Building and location canon:** approved exteriors and interiors, correct
   building-to-function mapping and deliberate time-of-day intent. SUNNYVAiLE
   is not permanently night-time.
3. **Visual-system canon:** the accepted LAiDIES/Rewind Era rendering language,
   palette, typography and motion treatment. Rejected pixel-art, painterly or
   draft comic sources do not inherit approval.
4. **Episode identity:** correct episode number, title, icon, cover and any
   episode-specific motif that varies inside the recurring shell.
5. **Logo identity:** the admitted master and episode logo-animation family,
   with exact use and reduced-motion behaviour.
6. **Audio and credit metadata:** the exact intro/outro masters, rights, artist
   and band identity, production roles, loudness target and episode-specific
   credit lines.

## Product role

The credits must feel like a real recurring show—not a template pasted onto a
file and not a summary of the episode.

- The **opening** creates recognition, establishes the LAiDIES cast and town,
  names the exact episode and gets into the story without overstaying.
- The **closing** provides an earned wind-down, legible production and music
  credits, the correct episode identity, a warm SUNNYVAiLE sign-off and one
  useful next step.
- The recurring shell stays recognizably the same across episodes. The episode
  icon, title, motif and selected beat may change without changing character
  identity or inventing a new building.
- Credits do not replace episode cover art, portable listening metadata,
  captions, the player poster, or the site masthead.

## Production sequence

1. Freeze the character, building/location and visual-system allowlists.
2. Bind final audio, credit text, episode identities and rights metadata.
3. Run one bounded design competition for the shared opening/closing language;
   old renders may inform the brief but have no release priority.
4. Build and independently accept one representative opening and closing at
   delivery size before generating episode variants.
5. Produce episode-specific derivatives from that accepted shell.
6. Enter every occurrence into the universal site-video registry and review it
   at normal speed against its exact purpose, audio clock and neighbours.
7. Test the checksum-bound final episode in the real desktop/mobile player,
   including captions, audio, crop, controls, reduced motion, fallback and the
   transition into and out of the episode body.
8. Bind only the exact admitted master to website, portable listening/video,
   YouTube and correction-propagation packages.

## Acceptance requirements

### Opening

- One clear show-start arc: identity, town/cast energy, exact episode title,
  then a clean handoff to the episode.
- No duplicate logo reveal, placeholder credits, illegible names, wrong cast,
  wrong buildings, unexplained wardrobe change, awkward speed-fit, prolonged
  still zooms or generic montage.
- Motion is purposeful and readable at normal speed; no object or character
  disappears behind an incorrect layer.
- The intro music and picture end together without clipping narration or
  creating an empty pause.

### Closing

- Exact episode title/number and complete approved production, music and source
  credits are readable long enough to use.
- The sign-off feels warm, memorable and recognizably LAiDIES without becoming
  a second lesson, a sales pitch or a wall of text.
- One honest next step may appear after the credits; it must match what is
  actually available and may not imply the next episode is released early.
- Audio resolves cleanly, the player reaches a stable end state, and portable
  derivatives retain the correct cover and metadata.

## Automatic failures

- Rendering before the character/building checksum allowlists are final.
- Treating v32, v7 or any other existing build as approved because it is newer.
- Using a retired, unapproved or visually inconsistent person or location.
- Compressing a rejected sequence to fit the song rather than redesigning it.
- Static images at changing zoom levels presented as character animation.
- Credits that cannot be read on mobile or are cropped by a player surface.
- Missing/wrong episode number, title, artist, band, cover or production role.
- Passing the credits separately while the assembled episode transition fails.

## Definition of done

Opening and closing credits are done only when the shared design, exact
episode variants and assembled episode masters all pass the universal
occurrence-level media gate and the real visitor/player journeys. Until then,
the correct status is **DEPENDENCY HOLD**, not “built,” “latest” or “ready.”
