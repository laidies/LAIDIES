# Product operating-specification backfill

**Status:** ACTIVE PORTFOLIO WORK

The initial deep dives and charters established ownership and launch gaps, but
they were not written against one uniform operating-specification schema.
FAiRY Godmother is the first product reconciled to the new
`OPERATING-SPEC.md` contract.

The portfolio orchestrator must now backfill every registered building and
subproduct. No missing detail may be inferred merely from current UI or code.

## Order

1. Products with safety, identity, reward, external-service or hero-promise
   risk.
2. Products promoted by the homepage, Visitor Centre, weekly episode or other
   entry surface.
3. Learning products participating in the shared content ecosystem.
4. Remaining shops, games, publications, activities and supporting services.

## Backfill cycle

For each registry item:

1. read locked owner decisions and existing dossier;
2. inspect the exact page, code, assets, data, service and public evidence;
3. research the user job, subject and relevant best-in-class patterns;
4. classify observed truth, intended behaviour, unbuilt aspiration and
   conflict;
5. create/reconcile `OPERATING-SPEC.md`;
6. run affected parent/subchampion and shared-guild review;
7. identify bounded owner decisions rather than guessing;
8. update state/backlog/registry dependencies; and
9. only then admit implementation build packets.

## Completion rule

A product is specification-complete only when the experience, mechanics,
learning/content, visual/media, frontend/backend/state/reward, analytics,
dependencies, upkeep and acceptance sections are evidence-backed or
explicitly marked `UNKNOWN`, `NOT BUILT`, `OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED` or another
canonical status.
