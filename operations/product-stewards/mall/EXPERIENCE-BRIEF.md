## Stable promise and user outcome

The Mall is SUNNYVAiLE MAiN Street No. 9: a Rewind Era reference concourse in
which a visitor can search or walk a directory of intentional departments,
understand each destination's truthful state, enter a useful reference
experience and return to Centre Court. It is not a claim that a route is
stocked, transactional, fulfilled, account-backed, reward-bearing or
editorially admitted merely because its storefront exists. `LOCKED LEDGER`

The visitor outcome is: find the cultural object, person, programme, film,
song, food, style, memory or novelty she was looking for; understand the
specific reference job of the destination; complete only the action that the
destination can prove; and receive a clear return or next step. `APPROVED
BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

The Mall organizes cultural vocabulary and routes to relevant LAiDIES
experiences. It does not replace the Library, High, episodes or NewsStand as
the authority for factual AI teaching. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

## Place metaphor, feeling and ritual

The experience is Centre Court → diegetic directory → continuous storefront
corridor → named shop interior → counter/pile/tray/bench/call-sheet ritual →
return through the Mall. The room and its meaningful objects perform the
product job; the Mall must not collapse into a generic card grid.
`ALI CONFIRMED`

truth: nostalgia invites the visitor in, while status, source, limitation and
fallback make the experience trustworthy. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

The existing atrium, fountain, directory, unit numbers and continuous corridor
are the approved structural foundation. The final sitewide painterly-town
versus adult-comic relationship remains an owner decision and cannot be
settled by the Mall alone. `LOCKED LEDGER`

Every department needs an exact purpose, canonical route, owner, source trail,
rights/trademark/likeness disposition, checked-on date, correction route,
current availability, primary action, fallback and admission verdict in the
readiness register and its child dossier. `LOCKED LEDGER`

Reference lists are preview inventory until editorial and rights admission
passes. A local list, asset or working interaction does not promote its claims
or imagery. `LOCKED LEDGER`

No held destination may remain enterable from the Mall. No missing or retired
destination may silently fall back to the homepage. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

No Gift Shop concept may imply sales history, stock, restock, availability,
production, price validity, affiliate relationship, checkout, delivery,
shipping, return or refund until that exact lifecycle is proven.
`LOCKED LEDGER`
