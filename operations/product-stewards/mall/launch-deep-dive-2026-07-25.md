# Mall Building — launch deep dive

**Status:** REPORT READY — evidence-based building assessment; not implementation, shop approval, commerce approval, fulfilment proof, or public-verification claim.

## Intent

The Mall is LAiDIES' Rewind Era reference concourse. Centre Court, a physical directory, searchable references and ten storefronts make cultural context navigable and pleasurable. Its strongest job is discovery: a visitor remembers a thing—Cher, a Walkman, Dunkaroos, a song, a film—and finds a relevant place or explanatory thread. It should enrich AI learning with shared cultural language, not replace the learning itself or pretend every reference is a retail offering.

## Navigation, discovery and shop readiness

The shell provides complementary discovery modes: directory search for intent-led visitors, a visible two-column directory for scannability, and a storefront corridor for browsing. Unit 11 turns a no-result into an optional community suggestion rather than a dead end. This is a promising way to keep a large town navigable.

The truth risk is the statement that every storefront is a real entrance. A route can resolve while still having thin, stale, inaccurate, inaccessible or unmaintained content. Mall stewardship must maintain a shop register—job, owner, source/currency, current state, primary action, linked lesson, accessibility/technical verdict and retirement/fallback—rather than relying on the shell's inventory count.

## Visual and brand quality

The atrium/fountain/directory/concourse grammar is strong object-world design: it creates a navigable place without defaulting to cards. Shop artwork, unit numbers, corridor controls and a deliberately vacant shop support a coherent world. The risk is visual inventory outrunning substance. Each storefront must earn its real estate with a specific reference/discovery job and a clear reason it belongs in LAiDIES.

## UX, mobile and accessibility

The markup includes labelled directory search, focus-visible store cards, a responsive corridor, mobile instruction and reduced-motion rules. These are useful foundations, not a complete verdict. Open proof includes keyboard operation of search and corridor arrows, focus after search/no-result, drag versus scroll conflicts, screen-reader outcome announcements, directory/storefront information parity, touch target size, horizontal overflow, missing shop/error recovery, contrast and real-device Safari. The broader fallback QA does not make an all-shop Mall pass.

## Commerce, affiliate and claim truth

The Mall is not yet established as a commerce platform. “The Gift Shop is open” is a route-level fact, not evidence of a cart, price, stock, affiliate relationship, checkout, shipping, refunds or fulfilment. Any future commercial or affiliate content must disclose material relationships and preserve independent editorial/reference selection. A cultural reference must never be framed as endorsement, availability or affiliate recommendation without evidence.

Unit 11's community suggestion model should state what happens to a nomination, protect privacy and avoid promising a new shop, publication or reward. It is feedback intake, not user-generated commerce inventory.

## Analytics gap

Plausible/Clarity embeds are not a Mall discovery system. Measure only privacy-safe structural outcomes: entry, browse/search, no-result, successful shop handoff, Unit 11 handoff and technical error. Search strings can reveal private interests and should not be collected by default. Pair aggregate signals with qualitative evidence about whether a visitor found the cultural context/learning route she sought; raw clicks and shopping intent are not proof of value.

## Freshness, upkeep and revenue

First build a small durable shop register and periodically recheck sources, cultural/factual framing, links, rights, imagery, claimed availability and downstream lesson relevance. Retire or label a stale shop rather than leaving it decorative. Once discovery and claim truth are proven, test an intent-led guide; only after that consider shop-specific ethical revenue. Revenue must be disclosed, independently curated, accessible, fulfilable and reversible, with no pressure or purchase gate on core learning.

## Verdict

The Mall shell is **BUILT LOCALLY** with a coherent discovery model and a substantial route inventory, but it is overall **SPECIFIED**. It should not be promoted as a commerce/affiliate destination or as a fully verified reference catalogue until the shop readiness, discovery accessibility, rights/freshness and claim-truth gates pass.
