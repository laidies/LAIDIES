## Product promise

The Mall is SUNNYVAiLE's Rewind Era reference concourse: a visitor can browse or search memorable cultural references, discover how each relates to LAiDIES' learning world, and enter a real, honestly scoped shop. It is not a fake shopping mall, a product catalogue, or an affiliate funnel unless a future shop earns that identity with real value and fulfilment.

## Non-negotiables

1. Every storefront is a real entrance with a specific job; no decorative empty store is represented as an experience.
2. References are curated explanatory context, not a substitute for factual AI teaching or a licence to use unverified/copyrighted material carelessly.
3. Search and directory help a visitor find a meaningful route; they do not turn cultural references into engagement bait.
4. “Open,” “new,” “vacant,” “leasing,” “shop,” “product,” “affiliate,” “available,” and “buy” language is true at the point of interaction.
5. Unit 11/community nomination is optional, privacy-respecting and never promises publication or a reward.
6. Revenue follows a trustworthy reference experience and real fulfilment; core learning remains accessible without purchase or affiliate pressure.
