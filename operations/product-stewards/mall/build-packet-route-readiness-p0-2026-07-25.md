## Problem and evidence

The Mall promised ten enterable destinations but had no machine inventory
showing what each route actually was. Search did not announce results or move
focus, reduced-motion CSS could not override JavaScript's forced smooth scroll,
Unit 11 implied a suggestion made the Mall expand, and “Gift Shop is open” plus
stock/hold language outran disconnected commerce.

Evidence: `launch-deep-dive-2026-07-25.md`, current route/runtime inspection,
the Hyvor Burn Book page, Gift Shop catalogue source and deterministic rendered
tests.

## Intended outcome

A visitor can search or walk every destination, understand preview/held/external
states, recover from no result, and never mistake a cultural route, community
comment or device-local interest for publication, stock, reservation, purchase,
fulfilment or reward.
