# Mall operating specification

**Status:** BOUNDED LOCAL PASS — independent Repair 1 rejudge 92/100; individual
shop substance, rights/currentness, community moderation, commerce,
fulfilment, deployment and public outcomes remain **UNVERIFIED**.

## Identity and purpose

- **Product:** The Mall, SUNNYVAiLE MAiN Street No. 9.
- **Type:** building and Rewind Era reference-discovery concourse.
- **Audience:** a new or returning visitor looking for a cultural reference,
  object, person, programme, film, song, memory or related LAiDIES experience.
- **User job:** search or browse, understand each destination's current state,
  enter a useful route and recover cleanly when the wanted reference is absent.
- **Distinct contribution:** one physical, playful directory for LAiDIES'
  cultural vocabulary; it provides context and routes, not factual AI teaching.
- **Non-goals:** a fake retail catalogue; an affiliate funnel; implying that a
  route, sample list, working price, local hold or community comment is stock,
  checkout, fulfilment, publication, moderation or a reward.

## Experience model

- **Metaphor:** arrive at Centre Court, read the directory, walk a continuous
  storefront corridor and enter a named unit.
- **Ten-second comprehension:** “Search or walk the shops. Most reference
  departments are previews; the Gift Shop till is closed; Unit 11 opens an
  optional third-party community discussion.”
- **New user:** arrival → directory/search or corridor → truthful destination
  status → selected shop or honest no-result route.
- **Returning user:** the same register defines what is open, preview-only,
  held, changed or retired; no “new/open” badge exists without dated evidence.
- **Identity:** browsing and local Gift Shop holds require no account. A local
  hold is not stock, reservation, purchase, ownership or cross-device state.
- **Accessibility:** all discovery works by keyboard and pointer; search
  results are announced atomically; focus moves to results/no-result; the
  corridor has labelled controls and visible focus; 320 CSS px/400% and
  reduced-motion paths lose no action or information.
- **Failure:** no result, missing destination, blocked local storage, unavailable
  community provider and disconnected checkout remain distinct and honest.

## Mechanics and rules

- **Inputs:** optional directory query; browse/arrow/scroll actions; optional
  local Gift Shop hold; optional external Burn Book discussion handoff.
- **Core actions:** filter directory, reset search, enter a route, walk the
  corridor, inspect a Gift Shop item and store/remove a device-local interest.
- **Authoritative discovery completion:** a listed destination resolves from the
  same exact artifact and exposes its stated purpose/status plus a Mall return.
- **Search completion:** a normalized query produces the ruled matching routes
  or one announced no-result with an optional, accurately described next step.
- **Unit 11:** it is vacant. Its current route opens Hyvor-powered community
  comments requiring Hyvor sign-in. Suggestions are not guaranteed review,
  moderation, publication, a new shop or a reward.
- **Gift Shop:** checkout is disconnected. Prices are working labels; previews
  are source art, not product proofs; local holds are private device memory only.
- **Edge cases:** punctuation/case/empty queries, repeated searches, focus after
  zero/one/many results, keyboard corridor movement, storage denial/corruption,
  missing scripts/images/routes, unavailable external community/provider links.

## Content and learning

- **Format job:** organize cultural reference context and route to deeper
  LAiDIES experiences. Reference lists are previews, not verified teaching.
- **Outcome:** a visitor can find the intended department and distinguish
  cultural shorthand from evidence about AI.
- **Mental model:** “shop” is the location metaphor; availability, purchase and
  fulfilment exist only where separately proven.
- **Accuracy/rights:** every shop needs named source/currentness/rights state,
  an owner, correction path and recheck trigger before its catalogue is promoted.
- **Analogy limit:** nostalgia can invite recognition but cannot replace the
  mechanism, evidence, nuance or consequences taught by Library, High, episodes
  or NewsStand.
- **Next experience:** the selected shop, then the smallest relevant LAiDIES
  concept/activity where a verified relationship exists.

## Visual, voice and media

- **Direction:** approved LIBRAiRY-derived object-world: full Centre Court
  arrival, diegetic directory and one continuous storefront corridor.
- **Canon:** ten reference departments, vacant Unit 11 and a separately held
  Gift Shop; Mall No. 9.
- **Voice:** witty, specific and plain about preview, local, external and held
  states. Avoid “open,” “stock,” “buy,” “available” and “the mall expands”
  unless the exact outcome is proven.
- **Assets:** current approved Mall storefront/interior sources only. Candidate
  art is not owner-approved by code presence.
- **Motion:** no essential information in motion; reduced-motion removes smooth
  scrolling and decorative transforms.
- **Owner decision:** any new Mall/Gift Shop art or campaign visual still needs
  Ali's taste approval.

## Technical and operational contract

- **Routes:** `/mall.html`; ten `/mall/*.html` canonical departments;
  `/shop.html`; `/community/burn-book.html`.
- **Modules:** `content/mall-v2.css`, `content/site/mall-v2.js`,
  `content/mall-shop-v2.css`, `content/site/mall-shop-v2.js`,
  `content/shop-v2.css`, `content/site/shop-v2.js`.
- **Data:** `route-readiness-register.json` is the machine-readable readiness
  and claim inventory. Page source remains the runtime source.
- **Backend/providers:** Hyvor is external Unit 11 discussion infrastructure.
  Printful/Gumroad are future possibilities only; no checkout is connected.
- **Persistence:** `laidies_puffies_board` Gift Shop holds and
  `laidies_mall_wish` are device-local only.
- **Rewards:** search, wishes, nominations, visits and local holds grant none.
- **Privacy:** do not send or log raw search strings. Hyvor has its own sign-in;
  the Mall must disclose the handoff before navigation.
- **Reliability:** every register route must exist in source and artifact; local
  failure stays non-destructive; external/provider failure retains a Mall route.
- **Fallback:** hide or label a destination whose purpose/source/status cannot
  be proven; never substitute a decorative shell for a working experience.

## Analytics and customer evidence

- **Events:** aggregate Mall entry, browse/search result class, no-result, route
  handoff, Unit 11 handoff and technical failure.
- **Safe properties:** product version, result-count bucket, route ID, viewport
  class and failure category. Raw query text is prohibited.
- **Baseline:** **NOT WIRED**; Plausible/Clarity presence is not a discovery
  success baseline.
- **Measures:** relevant-route completion, no-result recovery, keyboard/mobile
  completion, claim comprehension and broken/stale-route rate.
- **Cadence:** before release, after any route/source/claim change, weekly for
  episode-linked content, and immediately after rights/privacy/commerce issues.

## Dependencies and ownership

- **Parent champion:** Mall building steward.
- **Subchampions:** ten reference departments, Gift Shop and Unit 11.
- **Guilds:** editorial/learning, rights/legal/commercial, UX/accessibility,
  frontend, privacy/security, brand/visual, quality automation and release.
- **Upstream:** shop sources, approved assets, community provider contract and
  any future commerce/fulfilment contract.
- **Downstream:** town directory, episodes/classes using cultural references,
  Closet local holds and future revenue research.
- **Conflict:** Mall may label/hide unsafe claims but cannot activate community,
  identity, reward or commerce systems owned elsewhere.
- **Freshness owner:** Mall champion maintains the register; subchampions own
  their source/date/rights evidence.

## Acceptance and release

- **Product quality:** all register destinations have a useful purpose, truthful
  state, primary action and fallback.
- **Trust:** no cultural, availability, moderation, commerce or fulfilment claim
  exceeds its evidence; quality/trust/brand floors are each at least 17/20.
- **UX/accessibility:** deterministic source and rendered tests cover search,
  reset/no-result, result focus, directory/corridor parity, route/return links,
  keyboard, focus, 320/400% reflow and reduced motion.
- **Commerce/community:** held until real provider, privacy, moderation,
  disclosure, stock/price/checkout/fulfilment/refund evidence exists.
- **Release:** exact artifact and then bounded public-origin verification are
  separate required states. No local test is deployment or public proof.
- **Current status:** local P0 shell/register repairs may proceed. Independent
  judgment, representative editorial/rights review, deployment and public proof
  remain open.
