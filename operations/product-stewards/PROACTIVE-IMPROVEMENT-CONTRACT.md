# Proactive improvement contract

**Status:** ACTIVE — portfolio-wide operating rule
**Owner:** Control Room, enforced through every permanent product owner
**Trigger:** Ali's 2026-07-26 direction that agents identify and advance
improvements instead of waiting for her to supply every idea

## Required owner behaviour

Every owner cycle must inspect its complete product and produce one of:

1. an evidence-backed improvement opportunity;
2. evidence that a previously recorded opportunity should be closed; or
3. `NO MATERIAL OPPORTUNITY` with the evidence inspected.

An opportunity is not a vague idea. It must include:

- the observed user problem or unmet job;
- evidence and its date;
- affected new, returning, Resident Card, mobile, desktop, accessible and
  failure journeys;
- expected user and portfolio value;
- the smallest useful experiment or build;
- owner, dependencies, collision boundary and acceptance measure;
- privacy, trust, rights, cost and maintenance risks; and
- whether Ali must decide anything.

## Autonomy boundary

Owners should implement reversible, isolated, already-authorized improvements
without waiting for Ali. They should prepare and bring forward a
decision-ready recommendation only when the choice involves Ali's taste,
mission, public identity, meaningful spend/data authority or an irreducible
trade-off.

Owners may not manufacture busywork, optimize vanity metrics, silently expand
public promises, publish externally, spend money, expose private data or
override another owner's contract in the name of proactivity.

## Control Room presentation

The Control Room maintains one ranked opportunity queue in the internal
dashboard. Every row states:

`OPPORTUNITY | EVIDENCE | EXPECTED VALUE | NEXT ACTION | OWNER | STATUS |
MEASURE | ALI DECISION`

The queue is ranked by public harm, user value, dependency leverage, learning
value, reversibility and effort. Every five-minute launch audit reconciles
material changes; after launch the daily check and Monday owner-health audit
perform the same duty.
