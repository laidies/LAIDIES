state and safe route back. A local preview, room visit or Resident Card is never
presented as a submitted post, community identity, moderation decision or
durable reward.

- Visible privacy, terms, moderation, reporting and return routes.
- Coordination with the Sorority House steward, Safety/Privacy, Identity and

## Required operating evidence

Every change must preserve exact hash/history behaviour, keyboard/focus
continuity, provider fail-closed states, no provider contact on unapproved local
origins, and honest identity/reward language. Promotion additionally requires a
named human moderation and incident path, authorized real-provider journey
evidence, native accessibility, owner approval, privacy-safe measurement and
public-origin verification.
