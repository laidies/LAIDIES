# Butterfly Clip earning and redemption contract

**Status:** SPECIFIED — exact launch values remain balancing fixtures until
Ali admits the first catalog
**As of:** 2026-07-31
**Owner:** Functionality & Platform Director with Closet, Book Fair, FAiRY and
source-building owners

## Required resident result

A resident can do something meaningful, see the exact Butterfly Clips it
earned and why, use the same balance everywhere, spend it on a real admitted
reward, see what was spent and on what, and receive or recover/refund the
promised result.

The wallet must show:

- available clips;
- pending/reserved clips;
- lifetime clips earned;
- lifetime clips spent;
- lifetime clips refunded/adjusted;
- a plain-language itemized history; and
- each spend's offer name, amount, time, destination, receipt/result and linked
  refund/correction when applicable.

Available balance is not a substitute for lifetime history. Spent clips do not
disappear from the resident's story.

## Earning registry

Every eligible action has one registry record with owner, purpose, typed
completion event, dedupe/cap, provisional amount, abuse rule and visible result.

The first balancing fixtures should use small understandable bands:

| Meaningful action | Provisional band | Limits |
|---|---:|---|
| Complete an episode's admitted learning checkpoint | 2 | Once per episode/version |
| Complete a real Try-On | 3 | Once per Try-On/version; quality-independent |
| Complete a Pop Quiz attempt | 1 | First admitted attempt; improvement/milestone bonus is separately capped |
| Complete a class | 6–10 | Based on admitted size; class completion, not page view |
| Complete the Wednesday Tour | 6–8 | Once per released route/version |
| Complete a purposeful town trail | 1–2 | Once per trail/version |
| Make an approved useful community contribution | 1–2 | Daily/weekly cap; moderation result required |
| Welcome a distinct resident through an accepted private invite | 8–12 | Reciprocal, idempotent and capped; no public-handle self-award |

These are technical/balancing fixtures, not public prices or promises. Ali
admits exact launch amounts as a versioned registry without code scattered
across pages.

Never pay for:

- page views, reloads, clicks or time-on-page;
- pressing Play, raw listening minutes or repeat streams;
- a raw share action without an admitted outcome;
- unmoderated comments, duplicate messages or engagement spam;
- daily streak preservation; or
- actions LAiDIES cannot honestly verify.

## Which reward to use

- **Butterfly Clips:** flexible spendable value for a meaningful action.
- **Stamp:** relationship/progress at one building.
- **Badge or charm:** a named achievement or milestone.
- **Trading Card/collectible:** a specific owned discovery or completion result.
- **FAiRY Play:** a metered service allowance, not general currency.

A completion may grant a modest clip amount plus one specific collectible when
both have distinct jobs. It should not spray every reward type at once.

## FAiRY Play redemption

After the first grant → wallet → real fulfilment → spend/refund vertical passes,
the catalog may offer **one-way Butterfly Clips → one extra FAiRY Play**.

Rules:

- Plays never convert back into Clips and have no cash value.
- A small baseline/earned Play allowance remains so the core tool is not
  effectively paywalled.
- The offer is a versioned catalog item; a provisional test price may use an
  8–10 clip band until real earning pace and service cost are measured.
- Redemption reserves Clips, grants the exact Play once, then commits spend.
- If the Play cannot be granted, the reservation releases or the spend refunds.
- Using the Play emits a typed service outcome; it cannot mutate the wallet
  directly.
- The Wallet shows `Extra FAiRY Play` as the spend item and the FAiRY surface
  shows the resulting allowance/receipt.

## Ledger and safety contract

- One append-only economic ledger is authoritative.
- Only narrow server RPCs grant, reserve, spend, release, refund, revoke or
  adjust.
- Every producer emits an authorized typed completion ID; the browser never
  self-awards.
- Replays return the original result without duplication.
- Concurrent spends allow at most one success.
- A fulfilled offer creates an entitlement/allowance receipt before spend is
  final.
- Corrections append compensating records; history is never rewritten.
- Direct client writes, negative balances and silent expiry are forbidden.
- No private text, email, listening history or community-message content is
  stored in the economic record.

## First acceptance vertical

1. Complete one admitted learning fixture.
2. Grant one exact clip event once.
3. See available, pending and lifetime totals in My Closet.
4. Reserve one real Book Fair digital object.
5. Deliver it into the correct Closet collection.
6. Commit the spend and show the named line item.
7. Replay the flow without duplication.
8. Force timeout/failure and prove release/refund.
9. Restore the same result in a second signed-in context and deny account B.
10. Only then add the FAiRY Play catalog conversion and repeat the atomic
    reserve/grant/commit/refund proof.

No building may invent a local wallet, payout or FAiRY exchange while this
vertical remains incomplete.
