The case/Play ledger does not begin until typed outcomes and verified identity
exist. Personality cannot precede or override routing, safety, retrieval or
answer validation.

For AI explanations and advice, the content gate also requires:

- a correct mental model rather than a slogan or decorative analogy;
- no obsolete “today's AI versus AGI” contrast that denies current systems can
  plan, use tools, pursue goals, work across steps or switch domains;
- clear separation of present capability, definition, evidence, uncertainty,
  expert disagreement, forecast and scenario;
- enough reasoning for the user to explain and adapt the idea and recognize a
  misleading claim; and
- a deliberate handoff to Library, High/classes, episodes, NewsStand or a
  relevant game when that format does the deeper job better.
