# FAiRY practical career script bank release — 2026-09-02

Status: **DEPLOYED / PUBLICLY VERIFIED for the bounded Worker release**.

## Visitor result

FAiRY now has five additional source-bound career routes: continue a real
professional conversation without extracting or chasing; help a leader invite
decision-relevant dissent and close the loop; test a possible career direction
with a small reversible experiment; request specific feedback from an informed
observer and take a bounded processing pause; and discuss a written job offer
as a whole package without fabricated leverage.

Each route requires a matching visitor situation and selects one exact
server-owned AI preparation job. The model cannot replace it with a generic
career exercise. Public answers remain original LAiDIES wording; named-expert
credit remains held until support can be bound to the exact generated answer.
