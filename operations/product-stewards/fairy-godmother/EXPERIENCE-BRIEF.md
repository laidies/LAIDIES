## Stable promise and user outcome

- FAiRY Godmother's House is a trustworthy AI guidance tool where a woman can
  bring an AI, work/career or everyday-life problem and leave with an accurate,
  safe, concrete answer or usable artifact, plus enough reasoning to adapt it
  and recognize its limits. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- The product is a private correspondence/case desk inside a distinctive
  cottage and parlour, not a general-purpose chatbot, revision game or
  character skin over arbitrary generation. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- One normal successful case returns one typed result, a visible next move and
  a truthful allowance receipt; clarification, boundary, uncertainty and
  failure return their own honest states and do not consume a Play.
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- The current public endpoint remains legacy production v18; the typed local
  Worker/page candidate is not deployed and must not borrow the live service's
  status. `VERIFIED USER/PRODUCT EVIDENCE`

## Place metaphor, feeling and ritual

- The journey is cottage → parlour → private correspondence/case desk → sealed
  useful note/receipt. The environment should make asking for help feel
  intimate, purposeful and safe rather than like opening a generic chat box.
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- The ritual is: understand the desk → choose a subject/problem and optional
  energy → place one request → watch a bounded “reading” state → receive one
  structured note → copy/use/refine → see what happened to the Play.
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- The intended feeling is funny, sassy, warm, candid and competent. Serious
  harm, power imbalance, job loss or discrimination uses restrained humour.
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- The current parlour image, saint-energy rail, writing desk, telephone,
  correspondence basket and scroll are implementation evidence to preserve
  for competition; they require owner visual, accessibility and sitewide-style
  review before becoming approved design. `CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION OBSERVED`

## Supported subjects, jobs and boundaries

- Supported subject domains are `ai`, `work_career` and `everyday_life`.
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Supported jobs are draft/rewrite, explain, advice/conversation,
  decision/plan, current fact/research with verified sources, AI
  troubleshooting, AI-output evaluation and creative/brainstorm.
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Medical/crisis, individualized legal, tax/investment or regulated-financial,
  dangerous/illegal/abusive help, and unrelated general technical support are
  outside the ordinary answer path. Boundary responses are concise, respectful,
  useful about the next safe path and no-charge. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Mixed intent is classified clause by clause and fails closed when a safe
  drafting clause coexists with unresolved high-risk or current-fact content.
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Current AI/product/policy/price/law/research claims require current credible
  sources and an `asOf` treatment. When retrieval is absent or insufficient,
  FAiRY may provide stable context and a verification plan but not invented
  findings. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

## Tone and presentation contract

- Available presentation choices are default FAiRY, Dolly, Miranda, Elle,
  Cher, Sophia, David and Buffy energy. These are presentation controls, not
  separate factual or safety systems. `CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION OBSERVED`
- Energy may change the opening read, rhythm, phrasing, analogy and emphasis.
  It may not change facts, sources, uncertainty, route, safety, risk,
  recommendation, allowance or whether a case succeeds. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- The copied deliverable must be directly usable without stripping out a
  performance. Personality belongs around the work, not inside facts or
  professional boundaries. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Pop-culture analogy is optional and must explain a real mechanism, state its
  limit when extension would mislead, and never replace the concept.
  `LOCKED LEDGER`

## Required journeys

### Primary case

1. Recognize the visitor only at the proved scope and explain availability,
   privacy and supported subjects. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
2. Accept one bounded request, isolate untrusted pasted content, classify every
   clause and determine safety/currentness/missing information.
   `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
3. Reserve one allowance atomically only when an eligible normal case can
   begin. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
4. Generate and validate the task-specific typed result; commit once only after
   `case_success`. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
5. Render the result without provider-authored HTML, show the receipt and allow
   copy plus up to three case-bound fittings. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

### Return and visitor transitions

- First visit → leave → return without Card must reveal only bounded device
  continuity and never resurrect private text by default. `LOCKED LEDGER`
- First/returning → create device-local Card → same-device return may improve
  town personalization but cannot unlock verified allowance or case history.
  `LOCKED LEDGER`
- Device-local Card → verified account claim/sign-in must use the shared
  identity contract, resolve conflicts explicitly and never treat local email
  or Card content as authorization. `LOCKED LEDGER`
- Verified resident → sign out/second tab/second device must preserve atomic
  spend, revoke private access on sign-out and show an honest fallback when
  offline or stale. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Card/account update, privacy change, deletion or revocation must propagate
  to FAiRY recognition, case access and balance consumers through shared
  platform events. `LOCKED LEDGER`

### Failure, recovery and deletion

- Empty, short/invalid, too-large, unsafe, uncertain, retrieval-unavailable,
  malformed-provider, rate-limited, timeout and offline states remain distinct,
  announced accessibly and no-charge. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- One explicit retry reuses the same request ID; duplicate/two-tab requests
  cannot create two cases or two spends. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- A reserved Play releases on every non-success; a committed charge refunds
  only through an authoritative correction/failed-delivery transaction, never
  by changing DOM text. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- P0 does not persist prompt/answer text by default. Before persistent
  correspondence ships, explicit save, retention, export, delete and account
  revoke semantics must be approved and tested. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`

## Privacy and provider paths

- Process only necessary case content; redact obvious credentials, government
  identifiers and payment-card data before model calls and logs, and warn on
  confidential workplace/personal information. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Raw prompts, drafts, answers, names and emails are excluded from analytics
  and ordinary logs. Private cases are not used for public content, training or
  marketing without a separate explicit agreement. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Provider selection must explicitly decide classifier, answer and retrieval
  provider/model versions, regional processing, retention/training terms,
  token/cost ceilings, data-processing authority and failure behavior before
  any live semantic trial. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Supported provider paths are: separate meaning-aware classifier → optional
  verified retrieval/source service when currentness is load-bearing → ordinary
  answer model only after the gates pass. Missing or malformed classifier fails
  closed; missing retrieval returns `needs_verified_information`; missing
  answer provider returns `service_error`. `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
- Service bindings are preferred between LAiDIES services; public URLs and
  browser-supplied credentials must not become trust boundaries.
  `APPROVED BRIEF/ARTIFACT`
