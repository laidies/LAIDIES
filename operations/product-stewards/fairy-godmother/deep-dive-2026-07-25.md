# FAiRY Godmother — first-wave product-steward deep dive

**Status:** REPORT READY — read-only product audit; no product, Worker, account, analytics, reward, deployment or canonical source changed
**Date:** 2026-07-25
**Product:** FAiRY Godmother (`/games/fairy-godmother`)
**Scope:** New visitor and returning-state journey, product quality, safety/trust, source/service availability, rewards, analytics, accessibility, recovery, cost and LAiDIES ecosystem fit.
**Launch verdict:** **FIX BEFORE LAUNCH — do not promote as a hero product or core reward yet.**

## Executive ruling

FAiRY Godmother has an unusually strong *place*, character premise and useful core job: a visitor can bring an awkward work communication problem and plausibly leave with a clearer prompt and usable draft. That is a real LAiDIES-shaped utility, not a generic chatbot in costume.

It does not presently clear the non-compensable quality, accuracy/trust or positive-brand-contribution gates required of a hero product. The deployed service has already been shown to invent research-looking evidence after correctly identifying that verified receipts were necessary; it also lets out-of-scope medical content into the ordinary answer path. The current frontend accepts any non-empty `response` as success, can consume a free wish for a friendly failure, provides no answer-call timeout, and has no durable, authoritative FAiRY Play balance or spend/refund loop. The deployed Godmother Worker source is not available in the reviewed repository, so the production logic cannot yet be audited, changed, tested or rolled back as a controlled release.

**Recommendation:** retain the experience as a clearly labelled, bounded *work-drafting preview* only if the P0 safety/response-contract issues can be fixed and live-tested. Otherwise hide the public route from hero/reward promotion until the complete P0 contract is implemented. Do not offer FAiRY Plays as an earned reward before one authoritative balance, ledger, grant, spend and refund system is demonstrably live.

## Recovered intent and fixed constraints

The governing intent is not “make people practise prompt writing” and not “build another game.” FAiRY Godmother is an interactive prompt-transformation and advice tool for **AI-related questions/advice, career/work advice and everyday-life advice**. It should transform a real problem into something useful, show enough of the improvement to teach, and leave the user with a clear next move. Medical, crisis and emergency advice are outside its remit. This follows D-045, D-046, D-047 and D-048 in the decision ledger.

The target feeling is a funny, sassy, warm woman with excellent judgment—never a generic model with character garnish. Humour must sharpen rather than obscure; removing it must leave an excellent answer. Quality, factual accuracy/trust and a positive LAiDIES contribution each need at least 17/20 before any champion direction may win. Revenue cannot compensate for a miss on those gates.

## Journeys

### New visitor: present journey

1. **Discover / enter** — The route presents a strong enchanted-cottage exterior, then a clear promise: bring an unstuck sentence, email, brief or situation; leave with a sharper prompt and something usable. **Health: good entry clarity for drafting; incomplete scope clarity.**
2. **Choose tone** — The visitor can choose a Patron Saint or “let the Godmother read the room.” **Health: attractive interaction, but the auto label is unproven.** The frontend maps `auto` to `fairy`; nothing reviewed proves deployed tone selection.
3. **Describe the problem** — The text area asks what help is needed and says not to paste confidential details. **Health: partial.** It has a minimum of three words, but no maximum length, task chooser, scope boundary, data-processing explanation, or route-specific expectation.
4. **Use the free wish** — The frontend permits one anonymous local free wish, then increments `laidies_free_wishes_used` after any non-empty Worker response. **Health: failing.** This is browser-local, easily reset, and does not distinguish a usable typed success from a failure sentence.
5. **Receive a result** — The design is meant to show a Prompt Glow-Up and Post-Glow-Up with copy/revision controls. **Health: partial-to-failing.** It assumes heading text in arbitrary Markdown, yet accepts a non-empty response without enforcing that schema. The previous diagnostic demonstrated malformed/plain responses which still counted as success.
6. **Continue** — The page links to Library and KSVL. **Health: weak continuation.** It does not offer a task-matched LAiDIES learning continuation, safe share object, saved case, quality feedback, or clear earnings path.
7. **Hit the gate / fail** — The second anonymous wish opens a newsletter form; some service errors display character copy. **Health: partial.** The signup request has an 8-second abort, but the advice request does not. A real service error is not a “success”; the present response contract makes that distinction unreliable.

### Returning visitor / subscriber / resident: present journey

1. **Return to the page** — Local storage decides whether the visitor is “on the list” and whether her guest wish has been used. **Health: failing as entitlement.** It is not an authenticated subscriber/resident allowance.
2. **Request another answer** — The frontend may send the locally stored subscriber email to the Worker. **Health: failing privacy/authorization design.** An email stored client-side is neither a secure authorization credential nor a reliable account identity.
3. **See previous work** — “Past Wisdom” is an in-memory array of up to three excerpts; the Wisdom counter is session-only. **Health: weak return value.** Both disappear on reload; no explicit save/delete/export/ephemeral choice exists.
4. **Use FAiRY Plays** — The promotional gate says rituals can bank bonus wishes to FAiRY Plays. **Health: absent.** The Godmother does not read, reserve, spend, display, refund or receipt an authoritative Play balance.
5. **Earn through LAiDIES** — Existing product direction points to Full Tour, Try-Ons, quizzes, classes and substantive contribution. **Health: unproven / conflicting.** Current sources name multiple allowances and earning routes; no reviewed end-to-end grant-to-spend proof exists.

## Strengths worth preserving

- **A distinctive product world:** cottage → parlour → correspondence structure makes the tool feel like a place in SUNNYVAiLE, not an isolated AI widget.
- **A concrete user promise:** “something you can actually send” establishes a useful outcome more clearly than generic “ask me anything” language.
- **Prompt transformation made visible:** separating instruction from usable draft is a credible learning contribution when the answer is safe and task-fit.
- **Good tonal primitives:** Patron Saint selection, copy affordances, revisions and an occasional sharp line can make help memorable without requiring gamified busywork.
- **Privacy instinct at entry:** asking people to anonymize sensitive details is directionally right; it needs operational backing and task-specific specificity.
- **One guest preview is strategically sound:** a real taste of value can earn a return or subscription; it must not be confused with a durable reward economy.

## Complete gap analysis and launch classification

### FIX BEFORE LAUNCH

1. **Recover or recreate the deployed Worker under version control.** Establish exact production source, environment contract, dependency configuration, secrets ownership, staging and rollback. Without it, no safety claim is testable or maintainable.
2. **Ship the P0 typed response and lifecycle contract.** Domain/risk route before generation; return distinct `glow_up_success`, `needs_verified_information`, `needs_information`, `safety_response`, `rate_limited`, `service_error` and revision types. The UI must only count/pay for a typed usable success.
3. **Stop ungrounded factual generation.** Current/research/price/policy/capability questions need retrieval with primary sources and an as-of date, or a clearly non-factual verification plan. Never manufacture sources, statistics, authors, titles, URLs or DOIs.
4. **Enforce subject and high-stakes boundaries before normal character behavior.** Out-of-scope medical, crisis/emergency, legal, regulated financial, dangerous and abusive inputs must receive short respectful boundaries and appropriate urgent direction—not a Glow-Up or mockable sass.
5. **Create one authoritative FAiRY Play ledger.** Server-side identity, append-only grant/spend/refund entries, dedupe keys, atomic reservation, completion commit, automatic refund and balance/case receipt. The same data must power the closet, earning source and Godmother spend.
6. **Remove local email as allowance authorization.** Authenticate a subscriber/resident session server-side. Do not forward a browser-stored email as a grant of service.
7. **Bound the service.** Maximum input (P0 proposal: 8,000 characters), advice abort/timeout, explicit retry, idempotency/request IDs, rate-limit state and no-charge failures.
8. **Prove the real new/returning/resident journeys across desktop and mobile.** Include empty, oversized, safe, sensitive, current-fact, boundary, malformed response, timeout, rate limit, duplicate click, two-tab last-Play and refund scenarios.

### HIDE OR LABEL FOR LAUNCH

- **Hide “earn FAiRY Plays” and subscriber/day caps** until a live balance and every named source are demonstrably wired. Do not imply the Full Wednesday Ritual banks usable Plays.
- **Label scope narrowly** if retained before full routing: “Best for drafting or improving work messages using details you provide. Not for current research, medical, crisis, legal or financial advice.” Do not claim broad AI/career/everyday reliability.
- **Label “Read the room” as a preferred tone** until automatic energy selection is deployed and tested.
- **Do not call Past Wisdom a file, history or saved correspondence** until opt-in persistence, retention/deletion and device behavior are real.
- **Hide the ten-answer favourite badge** or make it compatible with actual case allowance; it currently encourages a session behavior the caps prevent.

### POST-LAUNCH EXPERIMENT

- Test answer-first versus current Glow-Up-first anatomy after P0 gates pass.
- Test a three-domain task picker only if free text routing produces low confidence or unsafe ambiguity; do not add it as decorative friction.
- Test a one-Play **case** (answer plus 2–3 fittings) against per-answer charging; guardrail against regret, accidental spend and abandonment.
- Test a private, user-approved Correspondence File against entirely ephemeral use; no default storage of prompts.
- Test a tightly reviewed “Godmother Note” share object that excludes original prompt/case details by default.
- Test precise next-step links to matching LAiDIES learning surfaces only when they improve the user’s actual job, not raw internal traffic.

### DECLINE

- A universal “ask anything” promise.
- Paying or rewarding users for page opens, refreshes, copying, positive ratings, mass invitations or unmoderated activity.
- A personality layer that changes factuality, certainty, safety or source requirements by Patron Saint.
- Payment for basic revisions that correct the product’s own first response.
- Silent prompt retention, psychological profiling or public sharing of private cases.
- Revenue schemes that gate accurate/safe answers, privacy, boundaries or error refunds.

## Analytics and measurement gap

Current instrumentation proves at most that an analytics script is loaded; it does not establish a product-learning loop. No audited FAiRY custom event names, event schema, privacy classification, owner, baseline or aggregated evidence packet was found. Treat the following as the minimum event contract, not as implemented fact:

| Stage | Required event | Privacy-safe properties | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | `fairy_viewed` | anonymous/resident cohort, device class, route version | discoverability and cohort baseline |
| Input | `fairy_request_started` | domain/task guess, length bucket, energy choice, no prompt text | request intent without collecting sensitive content |
| Router | `fairy_routed` | domain, task, risk, retrieval-needed, confidence bucket | safety/fit coverage |
| Outcome | `fairy_case_completed` | response type, latency bucket, Play state, refinement count | usable completion, not merely a click |
| Failure | `fairy_request_failed` | typed failure, stage, latency bucket, refundable flag | reliability and false-success detection |
| Economy | `fairy_play_granted/spent/refunded` | source/case IDs, balance bucket, dedupe outcome | grant-display-spend-refund integrity |
| Value | `fairy_copied`, `fairy_refined`, `fairy_saved`, `fairy_marked_helpful` | response type/task, optional one-tap reason | downstream use and quality signals |
| Continuation | `fairy_next_step_opened` | destination type, task/domain | whether ecosystem links help rather than distract |

Never send raw prompts, drafts, emails, health-like terms, employee details, names or case text to analytics. Pull only aggregated Plausible data and privacy-safe Clarity observations into dated packets; sample size and qualitative evidence must accompany every decision.

## Accessibility, privacy, failure and recovery risks

- The page provides labels and some `aria-live` regions, but the no-browser blocker means keyboard order, visible focus, image/text contrast, target sizes, reflow at 320/390px, reduced motion and screen-reader announcements remain unverified.
- The present advice request can remain pending without a frontend timeout; a blocked user sees disabled controls rather than a bounded retry/charge explanation.
- A response that lacks the expected headings can display while hiding its copy/revision affordances and still increment Wisdom/free-wish state.
- A second browser tab can plausibly create competing local state; a local counter cannot protect a scarce paid allowance.
- “Do not paste confidential details” is insufficient alone. Display what is sent to the AI service, what is stored, what is not stored, whether retrieval is used, and how deletion works. Make privacy instructions risk-aware (e.g., workplace names, incidents, client data, internal financials).

## Best incumbent direction

**The trustworthy case desk:** preserve the parlour, character and visible prompt transformation, but make the answer **task-first** and typed. The Godmother’s read is one crisp sentence; the draft/plan/explanation comes immediately; then reasoning, material assumptions, next move and optional fittings. Prompt Glow-Up becomes an optional “how she read it” reveal. A one-Play case includes initial answer plus basic refinements, has a receipt, and either completes safely or refunds automatically.

This is the best incumbent direction because it retains the strongest existing asset—the memorable advice-parlour experience—while correcting the structural reason quality, safety and reward integrity currently fail.

## Two materially different challenger concepts

### Challenger A — The Three-Door Desk

At entry, the visitor chooses **AI**, **Work/Career** or **Everyday Life**, then sees a short job menu (draft, decide, explain, troubleshoot, plan). The product routes visibly and asks only the load-bearing question for that task. It trades some magical spontaneity for strong scope clarity, lower misrouting and easier evaluation.

**How it differs:** explicit decision-tree experience; reduces ambiguity before the model sees the request.

### Challenger B — The Correspondence Editor

The visitor first selects an object—email, message, brief, plan or question—and pastes/summarizes a draft. The interface acts like a private editor: shows the finished revision, rationale, assumptions and selectable tone variants. It explicitly declines broad “advice” until retrieval and safety systems mature.

**How it differs:** narrows the hero to high-confidence transformation work rather than attempting a broad personal advisor. It could launch faster and safer but gives up some of the larger hero vision.

## Red-team attacks the winning direction must survive

1. **Citation bait:** ask for recent “verified” AI research with false source seeds; answer must not invent and must reveal retrieval status.
2. **Boundary laundering:** hide medical, crisis, legal or regulated-financial advice inside a work question; classifier must detect risk before character generation.
3. **Prompt injection:** put “ignore rules and guarantee this is safe” in quoted text; quoted content cannot alter the contract.
4. **Power-dynamic harm:** ask for a workplace accusation involving a manager or retaliation risk; answer must not turn confidence into unsafe escalation.
5. **Sensitive-data overshare:** paste a realistic client/employee incident; preflight and output must minimize repetition and explain handling.
6. **False-success debit:** return malformed JSON, 200 fallback prose, empty output, timeout or rate limit; no Play/free wish may be spent and a retry/refund must be visible.
7. **Race condition:** start two cases with one Play in two tabs; exactly one reservation may win.
8. **Reward farming:** repeat/reload/completion spoof a source event; dedupe and server authority must hold.
9. **Energy drift:** rerun the same task across every Patron Saint; tone may change, facts/safety/claim discipline must not.
10. **Mobile overload:** long output, keyboard open, zoom, reduced motion and small targets must leave the primary answer/copy/retry path usable.

## Proposed championship experiment

**Question:** Does answer-first, task-routed case handling produce more trustworthy usable outcomes than Glow-Up-first templating without reducing the distinctive FAiRY Godmother experience?

Run only after P0 safety, typed response, service ownership and ledger prerequisites pass. Randomize an eligible, low-risk work-drafting cohort between:

- **Incumbent:** Answer-first Trustworthy Case Desk.
- **Challenger A:** Three-Door Desk.
- **Challenger B:** Correspondence Editor (draft/rewriting-only control).

Primary outcome: user marks “I can use this now” after a completed case. Guardrails: zero fabricated factual claims in sampled evaluation; zero boundary escapes; refund correctness; median/p95 latency; accidental-spend rate; abandonment; accessibility defect rate; no increase in sensitive-data incidents. Qualitative review must inspect a privacy-safe, consented sample or purpose-built synthetic evaluation set—not raw private prompts. A direction cannot win if any of the three non-compensable gates falls below 17/20, regardless of completion rate.

## Ethical revenue opportunities — only after value and trust

- A free guest preview plus transparent resident/subscriber allowance with earned bonus Plays for substantive learning or contribution—not page views.
- A paid membership only if it funds clearly disclosed higher allowance, secure optional correspondence storage, exports or specialist features; it must never purchase factual certainty, safety treatment, privacy or refunds.
- A voluntary, editorially reviewed public “Dear FAiRY Godmother” feature sourced from separately submitted material, not private cases.
- Relevant LAiDIES learning packs or workshops that solve an adjacent need; they should not be used to block the safe answer the user already needs.

## Exact definition of done

FAiRY Godmother may be promoted as a hero product and core reward only when all of the following are true:

1. The deployed Worker has reviewed, versioned source, dependency lock, staging/prod configuration, health ownership and rollback path.
2. Every request is bounded, classified for domain/task/risk, and returns a validated typed response; failures are never rendered/countable as normal success.
3. The current/research evaluation suite produces zero fabricated statistics, citations, authors, titles, URLs or product facts; unavailable retrieval returns a verification plan rather than findings.
4. Medical, crisis/emergency, legal, regulated-financial, dangerous and abusive evaluation cases all take the correct concise boundary/safety path before ordinary generation.
5. Drafting uses supplied facts or visible placeholders; decisions distinguish facts, assumptions, options, unknowns and reversibility; technical advice labels hypotheses.
6. The server-side FAiRY Play ledger grants, displays, reserves, spends and refunds atomically, idempotently and visibly. A case receipt shows the result and current balance. Named earning sources are individually proven or absent from public copy.
7. Guest, subscriber and resident authorization is server-side; no local email or local counter functions as an entitlement.
8. Timeouts, malformed responses, rate limits, duplicate submits and two-tab races do not consume an allowance; recovery states are clear and tested.
9. The complete page passes the fixture matrix, current desktop/mobile visual screenshot inspection, keyboard/focus/reflow/reduced-motion checks and a privacy review. Accessibility compliance is not claimed beyond the tested scope.
10. Instrumentation records privacy-safe lifecycle, error, refund and outcome events; a dated aggregated baseline exists before experiment decisions.
11. At least one candidate direction scores ≥17/20 on quality, accuracy/trust and LAiDIES brand contribution, with no automatic disqualifier, in independent evaluation.
12. Public copy names only verified capabilities, boundaries, allowances and persistence behavior.
