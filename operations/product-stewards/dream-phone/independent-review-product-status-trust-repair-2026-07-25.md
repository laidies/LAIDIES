# Dream Phone independent product/status/trust review

**Review date:** 2026-07-25  
**Reviewer role:** independent judge; not the maker  
**Candidate:** exact local working-tree sources identified below  
**Overall verdict:** **FAIL — DO NOT ADVANCE**  
**Launch status remains:** **PUBLIC EXPERIMENT PRESENT, NOT LAUNCH-APPROVED / HIDE OR LABEL**  
**Owner product-model decision:** **UNRESOLVED — not decided by this review**

## Decision

The bounded repair materially improves status truth, advice boundaries,
session/reward truth, parked-engine isolation and fail-closed handling for a
missing or structurally malformed ledger. The two tracks are visibly labelled
and the current visuals remain recognizably LAiDIES.

The candidate nevertheless fails independent judgment. One of the two
evidence-admitted rounds contains a material chronology error that conflicts
with its cited official source. The runtime also admits stale evidence and
ledger/runtime source mismatches. Cross-entry copy still calls the activity
live, describes it as an AI hallucination game and uses unbounded “advice”
language. The game does not meet its own learning contract, and rendered
keyboard journeys expose focus and result-announcement defects.

Quality, accuracy/trust and brand are non-compensable. All three are below the
required 17/20 floor.

## Exact gate evidence

### 1. Product intent and unresolved-model boundary — PASS for preservation; FAIL for current quality

- `CHARTER.md`, `OPERATING-SPEC.md`, `state.json` and `backlog.md` consistently
  preserve Ali’s major product-model choice as unresolved.
- The booth exposes two current tracks and never executes
  `dream-phone-game.js`; source inspection and the deterministic test both
  confirm that the parked patron-saint engine is not loaded or invoked.
- Just Call’s first-screen and in-track copy identify it as a prewritten,
  playful reframe with no knowledge of the user’s situation.
- The claim game provides clause-level reveal copy and an official background
  link, but its user action is still a whole-claim binary verdict. Contacts may
  all be opened without a trade-off, and the player is not asked to identify
  decisive evidence, explain a conclusion or apply the method to an unfamiliar
  claim.
- The final perfect-score copy says, “That’s the whole skill.” The operating
  specification explicitly says the score is not mastery proof, and the maker
  records no representative transfer evidence.

### 2. Claim accuracy and official-source parity — FAIL

The Sky Dancers admission is supported: the CPSC record states a recall of
about 8.9 million units, 170 strike reports and 150 reported injuries.

The Mortal Kombat/ESRB admission is not accurate as written:

- ESRB Part 8 says Sega and Nintendo were summoned in December 1993.
- It then says the industry formed a trade association **four months after**
  the first hearing and the ESRB was officially formed **five months later**.
- The runtime reveal instead says the trade association formed four months
  after the hearing and ESRB formed **one month later**.
- A runtime clue and the Markdown claim ledger say ESRB formed **five months
  after the first hearing**.

Those are not equivalent chronologies. The official ESRB timeline separately
confirms only the 1994 founding. The admitted round therefore fails exact
source support and must return to `HOLD` until one chronology is accurately
worded and independently rechecked.

Official records checked:

- https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2000/cpsc-galoob-toys-inc-announce-recall-of-sky-dancers-flying-dolls
- https://www.esrb.org/about/prologue-and-part-1-doom-to-the-power-of-ten/
- https://www.esrb.org/about/part-8-twenty-five-years-later/
- https://www.esrb.org/history/

### 4. Cross-entry status and promise truth — FAIL

Corrected entry points include the booth, Fun Pack, welcome tour, SUNNYVAiLE
directory and content registry. Other reachable records remain contradictory:

- `content/site/site-index.json` labels the game `status: "live"` and says
  “the AI tells you a wild 90s fact” and that the user practises spotting an AI
  hallucination.
- `index.html` town-map metadata says “Calls, advice and Easter eggs.”
- `index.html` full town directory repeats “Calls, advice and Easter eggs.”

Those claims conflict with the local candidate’s exact boundary:
experimental scripted deck, static prewritten reflection, not authoritative
fact-checking, not personalized/professional advice and not launch-approved.
The existing town checker does not catch this semantic contradiction.

### 5. New, returning, storage, motion and responsive journeys — mixed

**PASS**

- new users see the experiment and advice boundaries;
- reload honestly resets session call history;
- storage-method denial does not break Just Call;
- no reward badge is written in the tested call;
- 390px booth, 390px game and 1280px states had no horizontal overflow;
- reduced-motion computation found no active transition or animation inside
  the visible Just Call track, and the game disables smooth scrolling,
  animation and transitions;
- the bad-evidence state is understandable and provides a route back.

**FAIL / insufficient**

- desktop evidence has a very large blank interval between orientation and the
  phone instrument;
- mobile Just Call presents a very long role directory before the user reaches
  the end; this is visually coherent but weak for fast returning use;
- the claimed 200% result is a font-size/640px approximation, not a browser
  zoom or assistive-technology audit;
- no VoiceOver/screen-reader, contrast or cross-browser evidence exists.

### 6. Keyboard, focus and dynamic announcements — FAIL

- Keyboard caller activation and Recent Calls close/Escape restoration pass.
- Opening Just Call leaves focus on the document body rather than moving it to
  the new track’s heading or first action.
- Activating Back hides the currently focused Back button; focus remains in a
  hidden ancestor.
- Activating a game verdict leaves focus on the document body after the
  verdict controls are disabled.
- `#reveal` has no `aria-live`, alert/status role, focus target or deliberate
  focus move. A screen-reader user has no proved announcement of correctness,
  clause feedback, source or Next action.

The maker browser test covers the history popover but does not cover these
primary track/result transitions.

## Bounded repair packet

This packet does **not** choose Dream Phone’s final product model, redesign its
visual system, deploy it, promote it or create account/reward plumbing.

### P0 — required before this independent gate can be rerun

1. **Return `mortal-kombat` to `HOLD`.**
   - Remove it from the playable admitted deck immediately.
   - Reconcile the exact hearing, trade-association and ESRB dates against the
     official sources.
   - Make JSON ledger, Markdown ledger, runtime claim, clues, reveal, lesson
     and link state one qualified chronology.
   - Obtain a fresh independent accuracy admission.

2. **Make admission genuinely fail closed.**
   - Validate required schema fields and parseable dates.
   - Reject a round when `reviewBy` has passed, the correction state requires
     review, the source is missing, or any claim is not admitted.
   - Enforce runtime/ledger parity for round ID, exact bounded propositions and
     all displayed source URLs. Prefer generating the playable runtime from
     one reviewed data source instead of maintaining duplicate truth.
   - Add deterministic and rendered cases for missing, malformed, stale,
     correction-required, unknown, duplicate, held, source-mismatched and
     claim-mismatched records.

3. **Repair every reachable entry contract.**
   - Inventory all Dream Phone references, including `index.html` and
     `content/site/site-index.json`.
   - Use the same experimental/scripted, non-authoritative, non-personalized
     boundary and hide-or-label status everywhere.
   - Add semantic contract assertions so “live,” “AI tells you,” “advice” and
     hallucination-learning claims cannot silently return.

### P1 — required for quality and accessibility gates

4. **Stop overstating learning.**
   - Remove “That’s the whole skill” and any score/mastery implication.
   - Add a bounded transfer action: identify the supported/unsupported clause,
     name the decisive evidence and state what evidence would change the
     conclusion.
   - Run the representative unfamiliar-player transfer test required by the
     operating specification. Do not call the result learning efficacy before
     it passes.

5. **Repair primary focus and announcement flows.**
   - Move focus deliberately when entering/leaving a track.
   - After verdict, focus a labelled result heading or result container and
     announce the result and availability of clause feedback.
   - Move focus to the new round heading after Next and to the final summary
     after completion.
   - Add rendered keyboard assertions for open, back, verdict, next and final;
     add a screen-reader check for the result.

6. **Complete bounded responsive/accessibility evidence.**
   - Test actual browser 200% zoom, 390/430/1280/1440 layouts, contrast and one
     supported screen reader.
   - Address the desktop dead space and mobile first-value/directory burden
     without selecting a new product model or unapproved visual direction.

## Conditions for re-review

Re-review only an exact, newly hashed candidate after P0 and P1 repairs pass
their deterministic and rendered tests. The independent judge should verify
the official propositions again, replay all adversarial ledger variants and
inspect the repaired focus/announcement states.

Even a passing bounded repair would not resolve Ali’s major Dream Phone
product-model choice and would not authorize deployment, launch promotion or a
claim of public verification.
