## Recovered promise

Dream Phone is a playful 1990s phone-booth experience. Its approved product
class is a game/reflective toy rather than an authoritative advice or
fact-checking service. If it claims to teach, the game must rehearse a
transferable behaviour through meaningful choice, consequence, feedback and
replay.

The exact major product model remains unresolved. The public implementation
currently contains two doors—scripted Just Call reframes and an experimental
claim-checking game—while an older patron-saint engine remains parked in
source. A prior internal proposal, Hotline Desk, would combine a direct call
with a three-call “what help do I need?” game. It was the strongest internal
direction but did not pass Council for Ali review or implementation.

## The experience job that is already safe to govern

Regardless of the selected model:

1. The booth must communicate its status and boundary before reliance.
2. The first meaningful action must be obvious within ten seconds.
3. The phone/town metaphor must clarify the action rather than replace it.
4. Any caller output must be explicitly scripted; no personalized,
   professional, medical, legal, financial or therapeutic authority.
5. Any learning claim must decompose claims, distinguish confidence from
   evidence, expose source limits and show what evidence would change the read.
6. A result must name what happened, why, and a useful next action.
7. Replay must create new practice or a deliberately new lens, not only answer
   memorization.
8. Local/session state and account-backed state must never be conflated.
9. Failure must be visible, recoverable and never replaced with invented
   advice, evidence or success.

## Learning intake and complement card

- **Canonical concept owner:** source evaluation / evidence judgment belongs
  with the LIBRAiRY learning system; exact owner link remains to be reconciled.
- **Dream Phone format job:** short behaviour rehearsal.
- **Distinct learner action:** inspect a claim, seek specific evidence, commit,
  compare reasoning and state what would change the conclusion.
- **Not its job:** durable reference, sequenced instruction, breaking-news
  adjudication or general-purpose advice.
- **Complements:** LIBRAiRY for durable reference; High/episodes for sequenced
  teaching; NewsStand for timely sourced claims; FAiRY for immediate advice.
- **Admission gate:** no new deck or teaching unit until the canonical concept,
  misconception, outcome, evidence owner, freshness rule and complement choice
  are recorded.
- **Transfer gate:** unfamiliar representative players can identify unsupported
  clauses and explain a better evidence request; correctness/clicks alone do
  not pass.

## Accessibility and failure invariants

- Semantic names and keyboard operation for every pictured phone control.
- Visible focus and deliberate focus movement/restoration after mode, call,
  verdict and reveal changes.
- Programmatic result announcements without duplicated emoji noise.
- 200% zoom, reflow at 320 CSS px, 390/430 mobile and 1440 desktop.
- Reduced motion; no meaningful state communicated by animation/color alone.
- Evidence missing/stale/corrected/malformed: entire deck fails closed with
  retry and exit.
- Unknown number: honest busy state and immediate recovery.
- Storage denied/corrupt: core journey remains usable; no saved claim.
- External source unavailable: keep the authored result bounded, disclose the
  missing source and prevent fresh admission until rechecked.
- Analytics/third-party script failure: core product remains usable.
- No free-text work problem, answer or identity is sent to analytics.
