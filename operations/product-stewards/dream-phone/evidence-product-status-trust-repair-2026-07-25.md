## Tested behaviours

- New user sees `Experimental · scripted` and the advice boundary in the
  auto-opened Just Call track.
- Keyboard activation produces a caller result.
- Closing Recent Calls by its close button or Escape restores focus.
- Reload resets session-only call history.
- No `laidiesSecretBadges` reward is written during the tested call.
- Mobile 390px and desktop 1280px have no measured horizontal overflow.
- The 640px/200%-text approximation has no measured horizontal overflow.
- Reduced-motion game CSS disables smooth scrolling, animations and
  transitions.
- Just Call still works when Web Storage methods throw.
- The game loads exactly the two admitted ledger rounds.
- Keyboard activation reveals a scripted clue.
- A malformed ledger produces `Deck unavailable`, loads no claim and disables
  both verdicts.
