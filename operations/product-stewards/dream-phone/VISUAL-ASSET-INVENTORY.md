## Visual acceptance rule

A Dream Phone successor fails before review if it:

- replaces the recognizable pink booth with a generic retro/operator console;
- treats the building as a decorative picture beside a generic app panel;
- omits `dream-phone-dialer-product-v4-transparent.png` as the primary operated
  Just Call object;
- substitutes flat text rectangles for the 25 image-bearing caller cards;
- invents a caller, identity, description or response instead of recovering the
  authored roster and bundle source;
- repeats exterior crops instead of establishing one outside-to-active-phone
  threshold;
- omits the documented hot pink, purple, yellow, sky-blue and teal 90s colour
  punctuation;
- substitutes CSS-drawn/glyph phone art or a fake portrait fallback;
- bakes critical controls, status, rules or boundary text into raster art; or
- claims visual approval from a checksum, asset name or maker inspection.

Ali remains the acceptance owner for finish-versus-park direction and any later
public visual release.
