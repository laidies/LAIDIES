## Decision

Repair 2 is a substantial and credible correction. The only playable round is
now Sky Dancers. Its two bounded propositions match the official CPSC recall
record. Mortal Kombat is back on `HOLD`, and every retained chronology string
now preserves ESRB's two separate intervals: the trade association formed four
months after the first December 1993 hearing; ESRB was officially formed five
months after that.

The repaired candidate also passes the submitted missing, malformed, stale,
correction-required, URL-mismatched, claim-mismatched, duplicate, unknown and
held-only rendered fixtures. Cross-entry descriptions now consistently call
Dream Phone experimental and scripted, deny personalized/professional advice
and saved rewards where relevant, and no longer call the claim deck live or an
AI hallucination detector. Primary focus movement/restoration, result status
semantics, clause-level source/limits, transfer reflection and no-mastery copy
all pass the exact rendered journey.

I am not advancing the bounded gate because the runtime's date validator is not
a real calendar-date validator:

```js
Date.parse("2026-02-31T00:00:00Z")
```

returns a normalized March timestamp rather than `NaN`. Consequently,
`checkedAt`, `nextReviewBy`, admitted-round `reviewBy` and source `checkedAt`
can contain impossible dates and still pass `dateValue()`. A future-looking
impossible `reviewBy` can therefore bypass the evidence freshness contract.
The adversarial suite does not contain this case. For a product whose promise
is evidence admission and freshness, that is a bounded P0 trust-control defect,
not merely test polish.

This verdict does not reopen the corrected facts, select Dream Phone's product
model or authorize visual redesign, deployment, promotion, analytics,
assistive-technology claims, rewards or public verification.
