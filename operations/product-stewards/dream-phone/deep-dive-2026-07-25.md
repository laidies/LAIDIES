# Dream Phone Product Steward Deep Dive

**Date:** 2026-07-25
**Steward scope:** Dream Phone only
**Status:** REVIEW COMPLETE FROM CURRENT PUBLIC SOURCE; RENDERED-FLOW EVIDENCE BLOCKED
**Public route checked:** `https://laidies.ai/games/dream-phone`
**Overall launch verdict:** **HIDE OR LABEL** the activity from primary launch promotion until the trust, product-model and QA gates below pass. If it remains directly reachable, label both modes as experiments and remove reward/persistence implications that are not proved.

## Executive ruling

Dream Phone is publicly live, not merely parked. On 2026-07-25, the public HTML and the four relevant public scripts were byte-for-byte identical to the current local files:

- `games/dream-phone.html`
- `games/dream-phone-game.html`
- `games/dream-phone-game.js`
- `games/dream-phone-bundles.js`
- `script.js`

That is a critical source-of-truth conflict. The current operating and review documents say Dream Phone is parked, should not be implemented, and did not pass Council. The production route instead promotes two working doors—**Just Call** and **Play the Game**—and the homepage promotes “Make the call.” This is not a harmless documentation lag: the public product has bypassed the last recorded product gate.

The strongest part of the public experience is **Just Call**. It has a coherent nostalgic object, strong caller voices, immediate output, remixable advice and genuinely delightful Easter eggs. It is close to a useful branded toy. It is not yet a trustworthy learning product: advice is randomly selected from static bundles without collecting the user’s situation, the caller directory is too broad, the page does not distinguish entertainment from decision support, and its rewards claim more persistence than this audit can prove.

The separate **Play the Game** route has a more defensible learning loop than the older patron-saint deduction code: the player sees a claim, chooses whom to call, receives partial testimony, commits to “For Real” or “As If,” then gets an explanation and source. That loop practises corroboration, denominator-seeking and resistance to confident repetition. However, its factual deck has a severe trust defect: several historical claims cite Wikipedia or Snopes rather than the original evidence, one user-facing statement says every fact is “real and sourced,” and the interface gives no per-clue provenance before the verdict. A fact-check game cannot ask the user to distrust weak evidence while using weak or secondary evidence as its own authority.

The product should therefore not be presented as a finished flagship activity for the SUNNYVAiLE reopening.

## Recovered intent

The durable product intent is:

> Dream Phone is a playful hotline that helps a woman identify the kind of work help she needs and gives her a useful, funny, caller-specific next move, with an optional nostalgic game that rehearses judgment through choice, consequence, feedback and replay.

The intended two moods are:

1. **Quick Call:** “I need one useful take now.”
2. **Play the Game:** “I want a short, replayable clue experience with a meaningful reveal.”

The intended design principles are:

- value before directory browsing;
- caller advice so distinctive that “of course Receipts would say that” is true;
- three useful clues, visible progress and a reveal worth the effort;
- one shared production-quality phone/object world;
- remix cards that transform the active result, not generate unrelated novelty;
- Jenny/867 as an undisclosed discovery;
- no false backend persistence claims;
- mobile comprehension without a manual;
- a return reason stronger than visual nostalgia alone.

### Delight versus learning

The distinction is non-negotiable:

| Surface | Honest primary job | What it may claim | What it must not claim yet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Just Call | Delight + lightweight reflective advice | “A funny caller-specific way to get unstuck or reframe a moment” | That it diagnoses the user’s real need, produces reliable professional advice, or teaches a skill merely because advice appears |
| Fact-check game | Rehearsal of source evaluation and claim decomposition | “Practise checking specifics, disagreement, denominators and provenance before deciding” | That all conclusions are authoritative while citations are secondary, missing or unverified |
| Patron-saint deduction game embedded in source | Nostalgic deduction/delight | “A logic game about eliminating possibilities” | Career/AI learning; its clues are fictional traits and do not rehearse the product’s public fact-check promise |
| Rewards/Easter eggs | Surprise, memory and return delight | “A local discovery” when storage is local | Durable member progress or cross-device ownership without a proven account journey |

Decision D-2026-07-25-045 is the governing test: a game teaches only when the player makes a meaningful choice, receives a consequence and feedback, and can replay to improve judgment. Delight is valuable by itself; it does not need a false educational label.

## Current public product: step-by-step evidence

These steps are source-verified production states, not screenshot-verified states.

### Step 1 — Entry and promise

**Current production:** The page title and metadata promise two tracks. The visible entry offers **Just Call** and **Play the Game**, plus a Jenny hint. The homepage separately promotes Dream Phone as “Advice line · 3–5 min” with “Make the call.”

**Health:** **FAIL — trust/status.**

**Strength:** The two-door choice is immediately legible in source and preserves fast utility versus playful depth.

**Gap:** The current public entry does not show the recorded “Glow-up in the works” state. It presents both tracks as available despite the Council record saying no concept passed for implementation. “Just Call” also diverges from the durable “Quick Call” naming.

### Step 2 — Just Call orientation

**Current production:** The user can type a number, press image-mapped phone controls, choose a caller card, use the heart for a random caller, view recent calls, and discover `867-5309`, `*67`, `*69` and Deb.

**Health:** **PROMISING, NOT VERIFIED VISUALLY.**

**Strength:** The phone behaves like an object, not merely a themed form. Multiple routes to the same action support exploration and nostalgia.

**Gaps:** The instructions are long and reveal too many interaction mechanisms at once. The user has not supplied a need, problem or context, so “straight answer” overstates what the system knows. The full directory appears to include roughly twenty roles, creating scanning cost before first value.

### Step 3 — Caller selection and answer

**Current production:** Each caller rotates through three static object-based answer bundles. Each bundle has a base output plus `secret`, `speaker` and `hangup` variants. The selection is random/rotating by caller and not based on user input.

**Health:** **REVISE.**

**Strength:** Caller voice differentiation is unusually strong. Product, Research, Operator, Founder, Comms and other roles have memorable 1990s/Y2K analogies tied to practical moves.

**Gaps:** The product cannot truthfully say it found the “right personality for the kind of answer you need” because it never learns the need. The content often sounds specific and authoritative despite being random. Advice about leadership, evidence, finance or AI can be useful, but the page lacks a clear entertainment/reflection boundary and lacks a “bring your actual situation” input.

### Step 4 — Remix

**Current production:** “Share a Secret,” “Speaker Phone” and “Mom Says Hang Up!” rewrite the active bundle into private insight, meeting-ready language or a decisive next step.

**Health:** **STRONG MECHANIC.**

**Strength:** This is the clearest product grammar in Dream Phone. The same idea is transformed for a different social context, which makes the cards more than random decoration.

**Gap:** The variants are prewritten, so the user does not practise the transformation herself. This is a tool/demonstration, not yet a learning loop. A later learning version could ask the user to choose which remix fits her situation and explain why.

### Step 5 — Just Call reward and return

**Current production:** `867`, `*67`, `*69`, Deb and using all remix cards can unlock badges. `unlockSecretBadge` writes to local storage under `laidiesSecretBadges`, renders a toast, and schedules member reward sync. General member sync converts most Dream Phone discoveries into `secret_badge` events and selected achievements into `merit_badge` events.

**Health:** **FAIL PENDING END-TO-END PROOF.**

**Strength:** Discoveries fit the phone fantasy and create replay motivation.

**Gaps:** The reward toast can imply durable ownership immediately, while sync depends on auth, schema acceptance and later member-sync success. The current source contains both “preview/parked” reward copy and runtime code that changes catalog messages to “unlocked.” The public journey from anonymous unlock → sign in → server event → new device restore was not proved. Return history and bundle rotation are session memory only, not a designed returning-user state.

### Step 6 — Play the Game entry

**Current production:** The two-door entry routes to `/games/dream-phone-game.html`, labelled “Beta.” It promises: an AI makes a wild 1990s claim; the user calls around town; no caller simply hands over the answer; the user decides “For Real” or “As If.”

**Health:** **GOOD CORE PROMISE; HIDE OR LABEL AS BETA.**

**Strength:** The objective, commitment and tone are clear. It is more coherent than the unrelated patron-saint deduction game still embedded in `dream-phone-game.js`.

**Gap:** There are two different “Play the Game” models in the repository/public bundle:

- the visible standalone fact-check game;
- the patron-saint “who is secretly in your corner?” deduction engine loaded on the main page but described in source comments as parked.

This duality increases maintenance risk and makes the canonical game model ambiguous.

### Step 7 — Calls and evidence gathering

**Current production:** Each round presents a composite claim and several contacts. Contacts reveal one clue at a time. The player chooses how many calls to make before committing.

**Health:** **PROMISING LEARNING LOOP.**

**Strength:** Caller reliability varies; rumor, repetition, official records and specific numbers create useful evidence differences. The player controls evidence cost and can act early or investigate.

**Gaps:**

- Caller lines are scripted to support the answer, so the user is recognizing authored patterns, not actually checking evidence.
- The interface does not show source/provenance per clue before the verdict.
- There is no notebook action such as “mark corroborated,” “ask for denominator,” or “open original source.”
- Some clue lessons rely on “everyone agrees/disagrees,” which can teach consensus as truth unless carefully bounded.
- The deck is static in page source and has no publication date, review owner, expiry or evidence status.

### Step 8 — Verdict, consequence and feedback

**Current production:** The player commits to “For Real” or “As If.” A reveal labels each part of the compound claim, explains what was true/inflated/made up, gives a lesson and links to one source.

**Health:** **MECHANIC PASS; TRUST FAIL.**

**Strength:** The game decomposes compound claims rather than treating a whole statement as simply true or false. That is a valuable real-world skill.

**Gaps:**

- The binary button labels flatten mixed claims even though several rounds contain one true clause and one false clause.
- One cited source per round is insufficient when multiple subclaims are adjudicated.
- The current deck includes secondary sources such as Wikipedia and Snopes. Those can be useful discovery sources, but the game’s own standard should prefer primary/official evidence.
- “Every fact here is real and sourced” is too broad. The audit did not verify every claim/source pair, and the interface includes period-accurate fabricated myths by design.
- Some historical casualty/medical claims require especially careful wording and primary sourcing.

### Step 9 — Replay and returning state

**Current production:** The fact-check game has rounds, score dots and a final state; the patron-saint game can start a new randomized game. Just Call rotates bundles in current memory and keeps call history only in current runtime state.

**Health:** **REVISE.**

**Strength:** Both modes have natural replay hooks.

**Gaps:** There is no source-visible content freshness system, spaced return model, saved “rounds seen,” mastery progression, transparent score meaning, or reason to return after exhausting the small static deck. A returning user may see repeated advice/facts without acknowledgement. Rewards are a substitute for designed progression.

## Full gap analysis

### 1. Product coherence

- Public implementation contradicts recorded Council and parking decisions.
- “Quick Call,” “Just Call,” “advice line,” “fact-check game,” “patron-saint deduction” and “help you need today” describe at least three different products.
- The visible fact-check game does not match the recorded Hotline Desk “call three people, reveal what kind of help you need today” concept.
- The strongest current utility and strongest current game do not share a meaningful content model beyond a phone metaphor.
- The entry asks the user to choose a mode before proving what either is good for.

### 2. Game design

The fact-check game has choice, consequence, feedback and replay, but:

- evidence calls are mostly reveal taps rather than strategic questions;
- there is no cost/benefit consequence for over- or under-investigation beyond score;
- mixed claims are forced into binary verdicts;
- feedback reveals the authored answer rather than requiring the user to cite evidence;
- replay teaches the fixed deck quickly and may become memory rather than transferable judgment.

The patron-saint game is a sound elimination puzzle, but:

- it is disconnected from the public fact-check promise;
- clues concern fictional character traits, not a career/AI behavior;
- “secretly in your corner” is delight, not a learning claim;
- “Just tell me who” removes the core loop without explaining the trade-off.

### 3. Content and deck freshness

- Advice bundles and fact rounds are hard-coded static JavaScript/HTML.
- No content version, reviewed date, evidence owner, expiry, jurisdiction or correction record is user-visible.
- No deck service, CMS or controlled publishing pipeline exists for Dream Phone content.
- No seen/unseen round selection or de-duplication is visible.
- Just Call has three bundles per caller, which creates breadth but shallow repeat depth.
- Several bundles make general professional claims without caveats or escalation guidance.
- The fact deck uses mixed source quality and has not been verified claim-by-claim in this audit.

### 4. State, persistence and rewards

- Current-call state, history and per-caller bundle index are in-memory and disappear on reload.
- Game state appears page/session local and is not resumable.
- Secret badges are local-first in `laidiesSecretBadges`.
- Member sync exists in shared `script.js`, but Dream Phone does not show authoritative sync state at the moment of unlock.
- Sync failure can occur after a celebratory toast; the user may not know the reward is device-local.
- No proof was obtained for anonymous-to-auth migration, cross-device restore, duplicate prevention, deletion, offline recovery or schema support for every Dream Phone reward type.
- Discovery badges and merit badges share storage but are later split by an ID set, which is fragile taxonomy-by-code.

### 5. Backend and third-party dependencies

- Base activity content is static and does not need a backend.
- Durable member rewards depend on the shared auth/Supabase client, `member_reward_events`, accepted reward types and sync scheduling.
- Page-level analytics load Plausible.
- Both pages load Microsoft Clarity session recording/heatmap code.
- The game’s external source links depend on third-party availability and may change.
- Google Fonts are external presentation dependencies.
- No private data was inspected and no backend mutation was performed.

### 6. Analytics

Plausible and Clarity are loaded, but no Dream Phone-specific product events were found. Pageviews and recordings cannot answer whether the product is valuable or educational.

### 7. Accessibility

Source-positive signs:

- language and viewport metadata exist;
- main buttons use semantic `<button>`;
- several dynamic areas use `aria-live="polite"`;
- the dialer supports keyboard Enter;
- image alt attributes are present in many caller/reveal surfaces;
- some controls have `:focus-visible`.

Unresolved or likely risks:

- phone hit zones rely on transparent percentage-positioned controls; accessible names and focus order must be visually/semantically verified;
- emoji in button text may create noisy announcements;
- dynamic focus is not deliberately moved when changing mode, receiving an answer or revealing a result;
- `scrollIntoView` and animated transitions do not visibly honor `prefers-reduced-motion` in the audited Dream Phone code;
- selected state is often conveyed by class/color without a proven `aria-pressed` state;
- eliminated/disabled suspects use opacity/grayscale and pointer blocking, but keyboard and screen-reader state need testing;

- no fresh 390px or 1440px capture was possible;
- no VoiceOver, keyboard-only, 200% zoom or reduced-motion test was completed.

Current positive behavior:

- unknown dialed numbers produce a busy signal;
- repeated power-ups explain they were already used;
- missing badge integration degrades without crashing;
- image load failure on reveal hides the image;
- source links remain optional to finishing a round.

Missing recovery:

- no visible retry for failed external scripts, reward sync or analytics;
- no explicit offline state;
- no “save locally only” explanation;
- no recovery of an interrupted game after reload;
- no stale-source warning if an external citation changes or disappears;
- no correction mechanism on a disputed fact;
- no clear route for beta feedback despite “tell us what feels off” copy;
- no proof that errors in shared `script.js` leave both modes usable.

Positive:

- Dream Phone is deeply native to SUNNYVAiLE’s MAiN Street and 1990s/Y2K object world.
- Callers can embody LAiDIES roles such as Research, Product, Operator and Founder.
- The fact-check concept aligns with Episodes 2/3 themes: specificity, evidence, receipts and resisting polished-but-unverified claims.
- Easter eggs and voice can create the “I can’t believe she made this” reaction.

Risks:

- It currently competes with episodes/curriculum instead of clearly reinforcing one transferable behavior.
- The product publicly contradicts the project’s own approval and truth labels, which harms the brand more than charming copy can repair.
- A fact-check game with weak citations directly violates LAiDIES’ “practise what it teaches” standard.
- The large role directory can feel like an internal taxonomy dump rather than a welcoming public product.
- Static advice can sound glib around consequential work decisions.
- The phone/song/sparkles/characters/easter eggs risk outweighing the user’s actual job if the first useful action is not immediate.

## Challenger concept 1 — The Receipts Switchboard

**Job:** Practise asking the right evidence question, not guessing whether a fun historical claim is true.

Flow:

1. A short workplace/AI claim arrives at the switchboard.
2. The player chooses one of three operators: **Ask for the source**, **Ask for the number/denominator**, or **Ask what would change the decision**.
3. The chosen line reveals a specific piece of evidence and closes another line.
4. The player marks each clause: supported, contradicted or still unknown.
5. The receipt prints with the best next verification step and a primary source.

Why materially different: the player chooses an evidence operation, not a personality or a verdict. The consequence is information gained/lost. Replay can vary claim structure and evidence quality, making the skill more transferable.

Brand advantage: the phone becomes literal information routing; “Receipts” is mechanism, not decoration.

Risk: requires a rigorous content/evidence pipeline and careful avoidance of current-news claims unless freshness is operationally supported.

## Challenger concept 2 — One Call Before the Meeting

**Job:** Turn one real work moment into a rehearsed next sentence.

Flow:

1. Choose the moment: **I need to recommend**, **ask**, **push back**, **show proof**, or **close**.
2. Choose the audience: peer, boss, client or room.
3. One caller answers with a short script and the principle behind it.
4. The user chooses a remix: warmer, firmer or shorter.
5. The phone asks the user to pick the version she will use and stores it locally as a temporary note, not a profile.

Why materially different: it is a focused rehearsal tool, not a browsing toy or fact game. Value is measured by whether the user leaves with a usable sentence.

Brand advantage: turns the strongest existing caller voices into a high-frequency work companion.

Risk: unrestricted text would create privacy and safety complexity. The first version should use structured choices only and avoid storing sensitive content.

## Red-team attacks

1. **“It is a random quote machine wearing a beautiful phone.”** True for current Just Call unless a need choice changes the answer.
2. **“The fact-check game asks me to trust its facts on faith.”** True when one secondary link supports a multi-part reveal.
3. **“You say it is parked, but it is on the homepage.”** True and damaging to operating credibility.
4. **“The reward says unlocked, but it disappeared on my other device.”** Plausible until the sync journey is proved and status is shown.
5. **“I won because I remembered the deck, not because I learned research.”** Likely after repeat play with a small static deck.
6. **“Everyone agreeing is not evidence.”** Correct. Consensus clues must be paired with provenance and specificity.
7. **“A wrong historical detail could undermine the entire LAiDIES evidence promise.”** Correct; accuracy is a non-compensable gate.
8. **“The phone book is your internal content taxonomy exposed to users.”** The breadth of roles creates this risk.
9. **“The nostalgia is doing all the work.”** The phone, song and Easter eggs are memorable, but first value and learning transfer are not yet proved.
10. **“Clarity recordings capture more than the product needs.”** Possible until masking, consent/notice and retention are verified.
11. **“The source includes two game engines; nobody knows which product is canonical.”** Correct.
12. **“Beta is being used as permission to ship an unverified learning claim.”** The label does not lower the trust bar.

## Championship experiment

### Test: Receipts Switchboard versus current fact-check game

**Question:** Does an evidence-action game produce stronger immediate transfer than a binary truth-guessing game without reducing delight?

**Participants:** 24–40 representative first-time users; exclude anyone who has seen the deck.

**Conditions:**

- A: current fact-check round, corrected to authoritative sources;
- B: Receipts Switchboard using the same underlying claim/evidence.

**Primary outcome:** On a new, structurally similar claim immediately afterward, the user independently chooses the most diagnostic evidence action and explains why.

**Non-compensable gates:**

- zero material factual/source errors;
- at least 90% correctly understand what the game asks within 20 seconds;
- no participant believes the activity is live AI or personalized advice;
- no privacy-sensitive free text;
- keyboard/mobile completion passes.

**Secondary outcomes:** time to first meaningful action, completion, evidence-source opens, confidence calibration, stated usefulness, delight, voluntary replay.

**Do not use as success:** pageviews, time-on-page alone, badge unlocks, or raw verdict accuracy on a memorized deck.

**Winning threshold:** B improves transfer by at least 20 percentage points, stays within 15% of A’s delight rating, and passes every non-compensable gate. If neither passes the comprehension/trust gates, hide the game and keep only a corrected Just Call experiment.

## Ethical revenue possibilities

Revenue is only eligible after repeated proof of useful value, accurate content, honest persistence and accessible completion.

Acceptable later possibilities:

- a paid, reviewed “meeting line” or role pack with structured scenarios and transparent update dates;
- an employer/team licence for private, structured rehearsal decks that do not collect employee-sensitive free text;
- a physical/printable Dream Phone card deck paired with free digital source notes;
- sponsored theme packs only when sponsors cannot influence verdicts, source selection or caller advice, and sponsorship is unmistakable;
- membership that funds fresh human-reviewed decks, archive access and cross-device state—not correctness or essential sources.

Decline:

- paywalling sources, corrections or safety boundaries;
- selling user dilemmas or session-recording data;
- manipulative streaks, loot-box rewards or artificial scarcity;
- affiliate recommendations disguised as caller advice;
- sponsors inside fact verdicts;
- charging for “AI personalization” when output remains static/random.

## Exact definition of done

Dream Phone may be called **VERIFIED PUBLICLY** and promoted for launch only when all of the following are true:

1. Ali/Product Council approves one named product model and its exact public promises.
2. The public entry, homepage, town, Clubhouse, episode links and metadata use the same status and naming.
3. Only one canonical Play the Game model executes in production.
4. A first-time user can state each mode’s job and choose one within 20 seconds on 390px mobile and desktop.
5. Just Call returns a caller-specific, need-relevant result after no more than two deliberate choices.
6. Just Call clearly states its entertainment/general-guidance boundary.
7. Every fact-game subclaim has an evidence-ledger record, authoritative source where available, reviewer, review date and correction path.
8. The game never presents unknown/mixed evidence as binary certainty; feedback distinguishes supported, contradicted and unresolved clauses.
9. Choice, consequence, feedback and replay are visible, and a transfer test—not completion alone—supports any learning claim.
10. Fresh content is versioned; seen-round behavior and expiry are defined.
11. Anonymous/local, signed-in, sign-in-after-unlock, sync success, sync failure, duplicate, reload and cross-device reward journeys pass.
12. The UI says “saved” only after authoritative save confirmation and says “saved on this device” when that is the truth.
13. Dream Phone-specific analytics answer the approved value question without collecting sensitive input.
14. Clarity masking/privacy controls and retention are verified.
15. Keyboard-only, VoiceOver, 200% zoom, reduced-motion, contrast, focus management and dynamic announcement checks pass.
16. Fresh accepted screenshots cover entry, first value, remix, game evidence, verdict feedback, reward success/failure and returning state at 390/430/1440.
17. Broken image/script/source-link, offline, storage-blocked and backend-failure states have usable recovery.
18. No console errors, broken assets, horizontal overflow or blocked core controls occur in the exact release candidate.
19. The exact deployed bytes are rechecked against the approved artifact.
20. The public route passes the same bounded new-user and returning-user journeys after deployment.
