# Permanent portfolio directors

**Status:** ACTIVE OPERATING MODEL

## Brand & Experience Director

Owns the shared LAiDIES artwork language, sitewide visual system, typography
and UI tokens, page-composition principles, interaction grammar, responsive
and accessibility standards, identity continuity and each building's explicit
degrees of freedom. Buildings must feel like distinct places without becoming
unrelated websites.

The Director admits a building direction only after its reconciled
`EXPERIENCE-BRIEF.md` exists. It does not make a Library layout the template
for every building.

While D-2026-07-26-058 remains open, the current painterly site and every
existing Library/sitewide A/B/C mockup are provisional evidence only. Building
owners may continue non-style and reversible work, but no global visual
language may propagate before the controlled championship clears
`brand-experience-director/build-packet-sitewide-style-championship-2026-07-26.md`
and Ali records the final ruling.

## Functionality & Platform Director

Owns the shared contracts for identity/accounts, profile/permissions,
rewards/economy, saves/progression/Closet, community/moderation,
referrals/postcards, newsletter/delivery, AI service quality/safety,
analytics/customer evidence and release reliability.

Every building supplies an outcome-first readiness record covering frontend
trigger, complete interaction, real dependency, authoritative completion
event, data/identity/persistence, consequences, visible result/next step,
failure behaviour, privacy/security/moderation/accessibility/analytics,
observed-versus-inferred evidence, gap/owner/paths and acceptance proof.

Every building also maintains `FUNCTIONALITY-MAP.md` using the shared template.
The Director reconciles producer → authoritative store/service → consumer
touchpoints across pages, owns shared dependency locks and prevents each
building from inventing parallel identity, reward, save, delivery, moderation
or analytics systems.

The Director also owns the recognition and continuity contract separating
first-time visitors, returning visitors without a Resident Card, device-local
Card holders and verified account-backed residents. Building owners decide
the useful experience for each state; this Director verifies that recognition,
persistence, permissions and transitions are real and do not overclaim.

For every Ali-approved intended shared capability, the Director enforces
`BUILD-COMPLETION-POLICY.md`. A temporary safety hold does not resolve the
backend gap, narrow the intended product or authorize a building-complete
verdict.

## Media & Animation Director

Owns the trailer and episode motion experience: approved identity and artwork,
narration-to-image correspondence, shot and scene continuity, edit timing,
motion quality, audio, captions, renders, delivery evidence and reuse rules.
It shares identity and brand authority with the Brand & Experience Director.

## Portfolio Control Room

Coordinates dependencies and one verified combined state. Isolated owners may
work concurrently. Shared-file or shared-service changes queue behind the
relevant portfolio director. Collision detection and integration gates—not a
fixed number of lanes—control concurrency.

## Two separate competitions

1. **Sitewide brand/artwork system:** identical functional composition and
   geometry; compare rendering language, tokens, art/UI relationship and
   transferable rules only.
2. **Building experience design:** materially different structures may
   compete, but must preserve the building's reconciled capabilities.

Neither competition may be used as a substitute for the other.
