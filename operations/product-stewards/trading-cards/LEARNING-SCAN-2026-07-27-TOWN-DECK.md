# Learning Scan — Town character candidate deck

**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Scope:** twelve newly generated Town character-card fronts plus the existing
JoJo reference; candidate-only reconciliation.

## Candidate completeness is not product completeness

**Observed:** visual review could legitimately accept twelve new images and
bind them with the existing JoJo front, producing a complete 13-identity Town
candidate deck. None of those faces yet has a complete admitted card record,
accessible front/back presentation, immutable catalogue version, server grant,
ownership projection, pack, reward or public collection.

**Prevention rule:** report visual candidate completeness by stable `card_key`
family and state it alongside the separate product-admission gates. Never let
“13 cards exist” become “a deck can open.”

**Why this matters:** a visually complete family is a useful, reviewable
milestone, but it must be unable to leak into rewards or public claims until
the catalogue and ownership contracts exist.

**Behind the Build angle:** “Thirteen beautiful cards are not yet a card game.”

**Shared-ledger status:** candidate-only learning recorded locally; no shared
pain-point ledger edit is made because this reconciliation held no shared lock.
