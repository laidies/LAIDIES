# Ali visual rejection and no-invented-quotes ruling — 2026-07-27

**Status:** `REJECTED / STALE / DO NOT ADMIT`

This ruling supersedes every earlier visual, catalogue, rendered-consumer,
technical-shape, or isolated-product acceptance that depended on the current
Town character fronts.

## Locked rule

- No character card may contain invented dialogue, a quotation, a slogan
  presented as character speech, or a speech bubble.
- **Exact approved exception — JoJo:** `THE USUAL?` is approved on JoJo's card
  because it is a natural café greeting tied directly to her established Blend
  & Snap role and the locked coffee/memory metaphor. This exception does not
  authorize any other invented character line or speech bubble by itself.
- **New owner-authorized proposal process — 2026-07-28:** Ali has asked Codex
  to propose a first funny, role-specific line for each Town character. These
  lines are `CANDIDATE COPY` until Ali accepts or revises them; proposal is not
  character canon or public authority.
- **Exact approved exception — Mayor Deb:** `LOOP ME OUT.` is approved on
  Mayor Deb's card.
- A character-card front may identify the character by canonical name. Any
  teaching copy belongs on the real-text back and must be sourced from approved
  canon rather than invented as the character's voice.
- A technically valid manifest, catalogue, viewer, or independent receipt
  cannot override Ali's visual and character judgment.

## Town-card palette direction — 2026-07-28

Use each character's established colour family in a stronger electric/vibrant
1990s print-pop treatment. Avoid soft candy/pastel versions of those colours.
This does not mean neon-on-black, a new palette, glitter/foil everywhere,
cyberpunk chrome or dark-page treatment. Standard fronts use crisp black ink,
controlled halftone, warm paper-white relief and saturated character-specific
colour.
