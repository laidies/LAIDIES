## Episode 05+ weekly handoff

Before publication, the Episode owner submits:

1. episode number, title, slug, release state and canonical source hash;
2. exactly five proposed learning units, each labelled glossary term,
   framework step, history anchor or practice principle;
3. plain front title/hook, short back explanation, exact source locator and
   fact-verification receipt;
4. provisional alt-text seed and prohibited visual misconceptions;
5. pack key `pack:concept:sSS:eEE:vN` and immutable card keys;
6. card status `candidate` until editorial, visual and Platform admission;
7. correction owner and revalidation date.

The Trading Cards owner rejects duplicate concepts, metaphor-only teaching,
unverified facts, generic motivation presented as a concept and any pack whose
five units do not cover the episode's actual learning job.
