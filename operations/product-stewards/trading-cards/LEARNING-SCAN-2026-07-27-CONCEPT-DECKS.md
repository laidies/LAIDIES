# Learning scan — Episode 01–04 concept-card fronts

**Result:** qualifying reusable learning recorded as BTB-197.

The visual system itself was stable across all four packs. The material
failure was semantic: an attractive panel flow could still teach an unsafe
shortcut. `YOUR 20%` initially showed an unreviewed draft reaching Send and
then showed an arrow that visually skipped Final. Independent card-level
review caught both defects. The accepted v3 uses one docked, mandatory
`DRAFT → REVIEW → FINAL → SEND` sequence.

Prevention now required: instructional artwork with a consequential sequence
must bind every arrow tail and head to the intended stage, explicitly reject
or omit unsafe bypasses, and receive a full-size independent semantic-flow
review rather than only a style/text check.
