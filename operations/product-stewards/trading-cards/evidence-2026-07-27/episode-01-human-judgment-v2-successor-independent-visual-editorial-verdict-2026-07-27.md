## Independent review

The card correctly presents the locked front title and hook: **`YOUR 20%`** /
**`YOU STILL DECIDE`**. It visibly removes the prior front's three independently
valid rows: `DRAFT` now has only the large teal arrow toward the magnified
`REVIEW` document, and there is no separate top-row direct `DRAFT → SEND`
button route.

The premium black, hot-pink, turquoise and holographic 90s card grammar is
consistent with the supplied reference. The hand, pen, magnifier, documents,
button and decorative marks are coherent; intended labels are legible; no
unintended pseudo-text or extra instructional claim was observed.

However, the required **single mandatory `DRAFT → REVIEW → FINAL → SEND` flow**
is not yet visually unambiguous:

1. The `DRAFT → REVIEW` relationship is clear.
2. The teal arrow leaving the `REVIEW` label descends rightward but does not
   terminate on, dock with, or otherwise identify the lower-left `FINAL`
   document.
3. The pink line from the `FINAL` document to the `SEND` button is clear, but
   because `REVIEW → FINAL` is unbound, a viewer can read the downward teal
   arrow as a review-to-send progression with `FINAL` merely adjacent.

That ambiguity fails the narrowly prescribed teaching sequence. It is no
longer the former unsafe direct-draft bypass, but this front still cannot
truthfully claim a clearly mandatory four-stage flow.

## Exact repair required

Create a successor front that changes only the flow geometry: visibly dock the
outgoing `REVIEW` arrow into the `FINAL` document, then visibly dock a single
`FINAL` arrow into `SEND`. Keep the exact title/hook, no direct send path, the
candidate-only boundary, and the existing visual family. Reseal the exact PNG
and submit it to a new independent review.

## Learning scan

For instructional flow art, an arrow that merely points near the intended next
step is not a valid dependency marker. Each required step must have one
unmistakable inbound and outbound relationship at normal viewing scale.
