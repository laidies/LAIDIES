## Product judgment

### First-time visitor result — PASS

The landing state gives a plain, useful instruction—meet the Town and turn one
over—and a conspicuous preview boundary before the roll call. It does not ask a
visitor to create a Card, sign in, open a pack, collect, earn, trade or infer
that a card belongs to her. The honest next result is discovery, not reward.

### Character front/back job — PASS

All 13 Town records are present as recognisable character fronts and readable
rendered-copy backs. Each back provides the actual product value: role/place,
one concise action-oriented teaching move and a specific boundary. The set is
coherent rather than a collection of decorative portraits:

- Deb supplies a boundary; Mme CLAi-O reflection without prediction; SunnyV a
  learning hook without music-rights or completion claims; and the FAiRY
  Godmother a plan without a promised service/reward.
- Jeeves and Paige teach source checking without claiming authority/current-news
  verification; Cosmo, Becky, June and Penny stay within their place-specific
  service/age/ownership boundaries.
- Paulette remains an explicit Town cameo/energy reference—not a licensed
  real-person identity claim. Matron Lumen remains a Town lantern-keeper—not a
  PATRON SAiNT, MAiVEN or TRAiLBLAZER. JoJo remains a reference-bound preview,
  not evidence of remembered account history or a reward.

This preserves Town-only canon and makes the full family legible without
crossing into non-Town character families.

### Usability and honesty — PASS for isolated candidate

The independently accepted consumer has all 13 cards at desktop, 390, 320 and
200%-reflow proxy; every front loads after it is reached, every back reads
without clipping, and keyboard turn/return plus `aria-pressed` and accessible
names work. Reduced motion and forced catalogue failure preserve a useful,
truthful fallback. The preview copy explicitly denies pack, ownership and
release, and the failure state also denies a collection change.

## Held scope

This acceptance does **not** admit a catalogue record for public use, create a
pack or odds table, grant/open/replay a card, create a private ownership event,
project to Closet, publish a collection, deploy a route or verify public
origin. Native assistive-technology/physical-device judgment and production
integration remain held.

## Exact next action

Keep this candidate frozen. Open a separately scoped release-admission only
after an authoritative server grant/open/replay/correction and private
ownership/Closet contract exists, and after native accessibility plus exact
artifact/public verification can judge that integrated result. Do not convert
this preview acceptance into pack or ownership authority.

## Learning scan

**Closed opportunity:** a first-time visitor can now get the card experience
without a fake collection mechanic. Prevention rule: a preview must state its
non-ownership boundary before interaction and in its failed-data state, so
visual delight cannot silently become an entitlement promise.
