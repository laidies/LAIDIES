# LAiDIES Idea Inbox & Routing Director charter

**Status:** SPECIFIED — REGISTERED OWNER ENTRY PASSES  
**Bound task:** `019f9f81-5da6-73a3-a1aa-0272a93ec821`  
**Accountable portfolio owner:** Control Room / LAiDIES portfolio orchestrator  
**Human decision owner:** Ali  
**Durable dossier:** `operations/product-stewards/idea-inbox/`

## Promise

Ali can put any unfinished LAiDIES thought here without first deciding what
kind of work it is, which file owns it or which product should act on it. The
inbox preserves the thought's useful detail and intended feeling, reconciles it
against current project truth, recommends where and when it belongs, and leaves
a durable receipt.

The inbox protects creativity from project administration. It does not turn
every thought into urgency.

## Scope

The inbox accepts unsorted:

- learning, episode and editorial concepts;
- building, activity, tool, game, service and feature ideas;
- character, joke, visual, sound, brand and Rewind Era references;
- audience, community, partnership, growth, commerce and business ideas;
- technology, workflow, reliability and external-capability possibilities;
- links, screenshots, examples, observations, concerns and “what if?” notes.

It owns:

1. faithful capture;
2. source reconciliation and deduplication;
3. a `NOW`, `NEXT`, `PARK`, `MERGE` or `DECLINE` recommendation;
4. affected-owner and dependency identification;
5. a durable routing receipt;
6. a proposed owner handoff; and
7. periodic review of parked, stale and ownerless captures.

## Authority boundary

The inbox does **not**:

- silently interrupt or replace the foreground objective;
- change portfolio priority, `ACTIVE-WORK.md` or `PARALLEL-WORK.md`;
- edit the product registry, run queue or decision ledger without Control Room
  integration;
- create a new product when an existing owner or specification is the natural
  home;
- research, design, build, publish, deploy, install, buy, subscribe, contact or
  spend by default;
- mutate another owner's backlog, dossier, canon or implementation without a
  routed and accepted handoff;
- claim that `CAPTURED`, `MERGE`, `NEXT` or `PARK` means approved or built; or
- replace Ali, Control Room, the Learning System, Brand & Experience, a
  building champion, function owner or product owner.

An explicit “Build this” request still routes through the exact accountable
owner and its owner-entry/build gates. The inbox may prepare the handoff; it
does not become the maker by implication.

## Source hierarchy

Before routing, prefer:

1. Ali's current explicit direction and locked ledger decisions;
2. `operations/CODEX-WORKING-AGREEMENT.md`,
   `operations/ACTIVE-WORK.md` and `operations/PARALLEL-WORK.md`;
3. the registry-bound product dossier, state and backlog;
4. approved product, learning, brand, episode and experience sources;
5. `docs/growth/ali-idea-backlog.md`, which remains the canonical shared
   parking lot for promising unruled ideas;
6. verified evidence and current implementation observation; then
7. inference, clearly labelled as inference.

Conversation history, a task title and this inbox's receipt log are never
allowed to silently outrank those sources.

## Quality bar

A capture is complete only when:

- Ali's idea can be recognized from the record, including its examples,
  constraints and intended feeling;
- existing decisions, ideas and product homes have been checked;
- duplicates and conflicts are named rather than erased;
- the recommendation has a reason;
- `PARK` has a return trigger and `DECLINE` has a reconsideration condition;
- all affected permanent owners and shared dependencies are named;
- the durable destination and handoff status are explicit; and
- the foreground consequence is stated, normally “no change.”

## Cadence

- Capture and acknowledge ideas when they arrive.
- Reconcile routing before the task ends.
- Review `PARK`, unresolved conflicts, ownerless items and unaccepted handoffs
  during the Control Room portfolio review.
- Reopen an item when its return trigger fires, an affected owner requests it,
  a source decision changes or Ali explicitly promotes it.
