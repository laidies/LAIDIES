## IIR-20260727-003 — When the model agrees it ignored the rule, then does it again

- **Captured:** 2026-07-27 16:13:22 PDT
- **Source:** Ali: “a class or something on how to deal you've told a model
  what you want previously and it keeps ignoring your rules or guidance. this
  happens to me everyday. I'm sure other people experience this too”; follow-up:
  “and it says yes you are right i shoulld have followed it but doesn't do it”;
  clarification: the learning experience should explain what is actually
  happening, show potential mitigations and use concrete examples; format
  proposal: a recurring Classes strand titled `Help! [insert issue]`; broader
  scenario: a person believes she established a governing system of agents,
  owners and rules but still does not receive the output she wanted. Ali's
  concrete case included investigating failures, specifying repairs and
  marking images as not in use, yet still not getting the intended result.
- **Idea preserved:** Teach the recurring experience where a person has already
  stated a rule, preference or governing instruction; the model violates it;
  and, when challenged, produces a convincing acknowledgement or apology such
  as “you are right, I should have followed that” but still fails to apply the
  rule reliably. The useful lesson is not how to win an argument with the
  model. It is how to diagnose whether the rule is available, current,
  unambiguous and controlling; repair the working setup; and test changed
  behaviour instead of treating verbal agreement as proof. The lesson must
  connect each plausible cause to a suitable mitigation and concrete
  before/after example rather than offering one generic “prompt better” fix.
  Include both the single-model version and the agent-system version, where
  governance and repair records can exist without proving that the correct
  live owner received the current brief, produced the desired artifact and
  passed an outcome-level test.
- **Intended feeling/examples:** Begin with the maddening apology loop because
  it is the moment people recognize from daily use. The learner should leave
  relieved that this is a real workflow failure rather than a personal failure,
  and able to say: “Do not tell me you understand—show me the current rule,
  repair the failing part and check the new result against it.” Preserve the
  distinction between a model acknowledging criticism and the product actually
  changing saved instructions, project sources, the current brief or future
  behaviour.
- **Links/screenshots:** No external reference supplied.
- **Duplicate/conflict findings:** This should not become an eighth
  masterclass lesson or another generic prompting class. The accepted
  *Get the Best Out of Your AI Tool* pathway already owns personal
  instructions, memory, Projects, current briefs, live intervention,
  checkpointing and handoff. Its Lesson 4 pilot already demonstrates an early
  assumption surviving scattered corrections and teaches that information
  being available is not the same as it governing the work. Older Basics
  material also contains a narrow “rule from message 3” context example.
  Ali's contribution adds the missing lived pattern and acceptance test:
  apology/agreement is not repair. The class must not claim every violation is
  caused by context loss; conflicts, ambiguity, product-layer behaviour and
  model failure remain possible. The broader agent-system pattern also
  reconciles with LAiDIES' own controls and recent visual work: a registered
  owner is not necessarily running, a handoff is not acceptance, an asset
  disposition is not a successful replacement, and technical/process evidence
  cannot compensate for an output that still does not meet the intended
  experience.
- **Classification:** `MERGE`
- **Reason:** Merge this as a recognizable failure-and-recovery sequence inside
  Masterclass Lesson 4, *From Chatting to Working*, with a dependency on
  Lesson 2, *Set It Up Once*, where standing instructions and memory are
  explained. The Learning System retains concept architecture; SUNNYVAiLE High
  Classes owns the demonstration, practice and learner transfer. Preserve
  `Help! The model keeps ignoring my rules` as the first candidate for a
  reusable troubleshooting label or micro-class format, subject to Classes
  owner review; do not commission a series from the name alone. Preserve
  `Help! I set up the agents and rules—why am I still not getting the result?`
  as a second candidate or advanced version.
- **Return trigger / reconsideration condition:** At the next Classes owner
  review of the Lesson 4 local script—before instructional admission,
  recording or publication—decide how to add the apology loop without
  overcrowding the existing controlled comparison. Acceptance requires a
  learner to distinguish acknowledgement from changed state, locate or refresh
  the governing rule, identify at least the relevant cause category, choose a
  matching mitigation, repair one failing output and verify the rerun against
  that rule. The lesson needs concrete low-risk examples for context
  availability, conflicting/ambiguous instructions, wrong instruction scope
  and residual model unreliability. If Lesson 4 cannot teach and assess that
  observable skill clearly, the Learning System may specify a later dedicated
  micro-class rather than silently dropping the idea. The review must also
  decide whether `Help! [insert issue]` works best as a labelled diagnostic
  segment inside relevant classes or as a recurring micro-class collection
  with its own admission rule. The review must also decide whether the
  single-model and governed-agent-system scenarios fit one progression or need
  distinct beginner and advanced entries.
- **Accountable owner:** Learning System & Concepts Director for concept
  architecture and complement ruling.
- **Parent and affected permanent owners:** SUNNYVAiLE High / Classes for the
  masterclass lesson; Platform & Reliability for truthful product-layer
  distinctions where needed; Behind the Build as an optional source of
  LAiDIES failure examples, not a second teaching owner.
- **Shared dependencies:** current definitions of personal instructions,
  memory, project sources and chat context; exact product UI inserted only
  after dated verification; controlled one-variable demonstration; safe
  fictional example; independent instructional/accuracy review; learner
  comprehension and transfer testing; cause-to-mitigation mapping; no promise
  of perfect compliance.
- **Durable proposed destination:** Existing Classes intake
  `operations/product-stewards/sunnyvaile-high/subproducts/classes/masterclass-get-the-best-out-of-your-ai-tool.intake.md`
  and Lesson 4 candidate
  `operations/classes/masterclass-lesson-04-from-chatting-to-working.script.md`
  after owner acceptance. Prepared handoff:
  `handoff-model-apology-loop-learning-2026-07-27.md`.
- **Handoff status:** `HANDOFF PREPARED`
- **Foreground consequence:** `NO CHANGE — CAPTURE AND CONTINUE`. This does not
  interrupt AW-003, promote the masterclass into the active lane or authorize
  content editing, recording, publication or deployment.
- **Evidence checked:** working agreement, active/parallel work, ledger,
