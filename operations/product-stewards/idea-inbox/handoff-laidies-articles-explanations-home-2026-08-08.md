## Strongest objection to NewsStand as the permanent home

NewsStand's four publications are dated jobs: Breaking handles urgent change,
Daily handles the current edition, Weekly synthesizes developments, and
Tribune makes a sourced argument. An evergreen mechanism explanation placed
there as its sole home will age out of the newspaper chronology and can be
mistaken for a current event. Tribune is especially wrong unless LAiDIES is
arguing a position and presenting the strongest counterargument.

   The working title candidate is **_Dear Miss Jeeves_**: a prominent feature

The collective corpus therefore remains one LIBRAiRY book. NewsStand and KSVL
are presentations and discovery channels for individual answers, not places

## Content boundary for the example

An eventual explanation should distinguish at least these causes without
claiming one universal reason:

- a language instruction can influence output but cannot guarantee factual
  truth;
- the model may not possess or retrieve the needed evidence;
- instructions can be ambiguous, conflicting, outside the active scope or
  overwhelmed by other context;
- the surrounding product may add memory, tools, files, policies or system
  instructions the visitor cannot see directly;
- generation remains probabilistic and residual failure is possible even when
  the instruction was available; and
- important claims need source-grounding, constrained workflows, checks and
  human judgment—not a magic prohibition.

Those are proposed teaching obligations, not approved public claims. Current
technical and product-specific wording requires primary-source verification,
the Tier 1 content-producer contract and independent learner review before
publication.
