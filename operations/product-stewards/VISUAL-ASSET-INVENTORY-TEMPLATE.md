## Asset disposition register

| Asset ID | Exact file/source | Route · state · placement | Family | Governing references | Current full-resolution/render finding | Disposition | Reason | Replacement/adaptation owner and output | Exact-use verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Allowed dispositions:

- `KEEP` — passes current full-resolution, canon, style and exact-placement
  review; evidence is named.
- `ADAPT` — the underlying asset is eligible, but requires substantive
  re-authoring or an authored responsive/state variant. A filter, tint or
  saturation change is not sufficient when composition/craft/canon is wrong.
- `REPLACE` — wrong subject, room, character, period, style, palette,
  hierarchy, quality or purpose; or stale/superseded/duplicated/generated with
  unresolved defects.
- `REMOVE` — the placement is redundant, misleading or no longer part of the
  intended experience.
