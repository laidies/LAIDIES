1. Ali selects the system from the controlled Homepage championship.
2. Brand seals the chosen Homepage reference renders, tokens/rules and
   Homepage image treatment with hashes.
3. One representative building translates that sealed system into its own
   building-as-experience design. This is a translation validation, not a new
   style competition.
4. Brand and the building owner verify that the system works beyond the
   Homepage and make only explicit rule refinements.

6. Building and content owners then produce against that exact version.

A later building, image or component cannot silently redefine the system; it
must pass Brand conformance and update the register through an explicit
successor version.
