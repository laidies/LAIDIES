1. represents the current canonical building, character, product or episode;
2. meets the selected adult, polished and legitimate visual standard;

5. has an appropriate mobile crop and useful accessible alternative text; and
6. is recorded with every consumer before the sitewide register is sealed.

## Duplication rulings

The prior vibrancy/duplication audit identified repeated product jobs, not just
repeated files. The next candidate must consolidate these before Ali review:

- one first-visit orientation sequence, not competing hero/method/weekly/town
  explanations;
- one obvious current learning entry, with secondary episode actions beneath
  it rather than multiple starts;
- one reference-lookup handoff;
- one coherent town-exploration sequence, without a map, pseudo-district
  cards and directory all repeating the same destinations;
- one membership/Resident Card explanation;
- one KSVL entry;
- one mission/“why LAiDIES” explanation; and
- no image reused merely to decorate two or three of those repeated jobs.

Consolidation must preserve the locked current Homepage wording and product
meaning. It is information architecture and image-job correction, not
permission for another unsolicited copy rewrite.
