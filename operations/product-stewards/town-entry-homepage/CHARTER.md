## Product promise

The homepage and Start Here are the town's front door. They help a first-time visitor understand that LAiDIES teaches practical AI fluency through the Rewind Era and SUNNYVAiLE, choose a useful next action without needing to learn the whole town, and later return to the right current or evergreen route.

They are not a claim that every building, lesson, reward, account, community room or reopening campaign is ready. Entry must direct honestly to owned products and surface their limitations rather than laundering them into a town-wide promise.

## Owned scope

- `index.html`, `start-here.html`, homepage hero/messaging, intent paths, current-week guidance, town map/directory and first/returning visitor comprehension.
- Shared entry navigation, mobile navigation, contextual return relationships and town-directory discovery truth.
- Homepage newsletter surface, only as a route/informed-consent handoff; its service lifecycle remains owned with the relevant email/service work.
- Reopening/social readiness at the entry level: release-promise ledger, approved campaign destination language and default-deny channel claims.

## Out of scope

- Product decisions, correctness or launch approval for individual buildings, games, media, membership, rewards, community or external services.
- Publishing social posts, email, deployment, advertising spend, or obtaining external account access.

## Definition of a healthy journey

1. In the first viewport, a newcomer understands what LAiDIES teaches, why the town/pop-culture device exists, and one obvious safe next action.
2. She can choose a clearly labelled purpose—current lesson, orientation, reference, practical help, activity or town exploration—without false urgency or hidden prerequisites.
3. A returning visitor can find the current episode/current-week route and an evergreen route without stale episode art, promised-but-unavailable activity or a dead end.
4. Desktop and mobile navigation remain visible, keyboard-operable, labelled, performant and recoverable with no-JS/service failures where the entry depends on them.
5. Town map/directory entries correspond to real, intentional routes; building limitations are not reworded as entry-level proof.
6. Newsletter/reopening/social language says only what has real readiness evidence and explicit owner approval.
7. At every supported width, the complete page has no unexplained blank fields,
   manufactured equal-height gaps, destructive crops, confusing section
   relationships or avoidable scrolling caused by duplicated and low-priority
   content. New and returning visitors can each understand what to do next
   without deciphering the layout.

## Quality and decision rules

- Homepage hierarchy should lead with practical AI value, then the town world—not a generic lifestyle brand, decorative nostalgia or a task list of every building.
- The masthead/hero is evergreen and does not change with weekly episode or
  news releases. Fresh episode/news promotion belongs in a separate,
  fail-closed module below it.
- The current live luminous-dusk masthead is the baseline winner. Preserve its
  exact image and composition; do not rebuild it.
- The admitted three-job hero hierarchy must reduce cognitive load; duplicated/competing calls to action need evidence before expansion.
- Visible object-world delight may supplement, never replace, clear labelled navigation—especially on mobile.
- Responsive acceptance is continuous rather than breakpoint-only. The actual
  page must be inspected at desktop, intermediate and representative mobile
  widths, including section transitions, content-end positions, total scroll
  burden, image subject/crop, peer-control consistency and first/returning
  visitor journeys. A technically fitting layout with random voids, excessive
  scrolling or confusing recomposition fails.
- “Current,” “new every Wednesday,” account/community/reward and social-reopening wording require exact route/service evidence. Use fixed statuses; local or prepared work is not public verification.
- **Social/reopening is default-deny:** no announcement, campaign copy, visual, URL claim or channel status is considered ready unless Ali approves the exact website, exact copy and exact image after readiness evidence. A removed post is not a live launch.

## Steward triggers and escalation

- Trigger on homepage/start-here/header/directory/current-episode changes, a broken entry/return route, evidence of confusion, form incident, social/reopening proposal or public readiness change.
- **FIX BEFORE LAUNCH:** first action dead end; false entry promise; inaccessible primary/mobile navigation; unapproved social/reopening assertion presented as live; or entry copy hides a known receiving-product failure.
- **TEMPORARY HOLD/LIMIT WHILE BUILD REMAINS OPEN:** unverified current/reward/community/account claims, current-week offers without exact route evidence, or unapproved visual/campaign material. A hold prevents a false promise; it does not complete, remove or defer an Ali-approved intended capability (D-2026-07-26-055/056).
- **Ali decision:** homepage visual hierarchy, canonical primary path, channel copy/image/website approval, and any conversion or revenue mechanism.
