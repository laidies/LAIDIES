FAIRY GODMOTHER TONE

FAIRY’S READ
You need a confident first-project conversation that turns an open-ended assignment into clear outcomes, trade-offs, decision rights and support—not a performance of knowing facts nobody has told you yet.

YOUR USABLE ANSWER
Use this structure:

“Thanks for asking me to lead this. I’d like to align on the outcome, boundaries and decision process so I can set the project up properly. My current understanding is that the project should [intended outcome]. Is that right?”

Then ask:

1. Scope and success
- “What must this project deliver, and what is explicitly outside scope?”
- “What would a successful result look like?”
- “Which constraints are fixed—timing, budget, quality, systems or staffing?”

2. Priorities and trade-offs
- “Where does this sit relative to my existing responsibilities?”
- “If priorities conflict, which outcomes should take precedence, and who makes that call?”
- “Are there early milestones or decisions you want visibility into?”

3. Authority and stakeholders
- “Which decisions can I make independently, and which need your or the sponsor’s approval?”
- “Who needs to be consulted, informed or directly involved?”
- “Are there previous decisions or stakeholder concerns I should understand before kickoff?”

4. Support and escalation
- “What people, time, tools or subject-matter support are available?”
- “How would you like me to raise risks or request a decision?”
- “Could we agree on a regular check-in while I establish the project?”

Close with:

“Let me reflect that back. The intended outcome is [outcome]. The initial scope is [included work], with [excluded work] outside it. I can decide [decision rights], while [decisions] come back to you. The first priority is [priority], and I’ll flag [type of risk or conflict] through [agreed route]. My next step will be [first action]. Have I missed or misunderstood anything important?”

Afterward, write a short factual recap using those same headings: outcome, scope, priorities, decision rights, support, checkpoints and immediate next step.
