Create your town ID at MAiKEOVER on MAiN, open your Closet and start using your Puffy bookmarks. You can do this before signing in. Your Card and activity begin in this browser; Puffy bookmarks stay on this device.
Connect your private account
Sign in to keep your Card, episode position, completed tours, charms and trading cards available across devices. You also get more time with FAiRY: up to three cases a day in the current beta. Each completed case includes up to three follow-up fittings to work through her advice.
