## Girl Talk

Card: Truth: what have you copied from AI without checking? Dare: give AI a clearer brief using a made-up task. Your turn.

Full description:

Remember Girl Talk? Truth or dare is back at Delta LAi Nu—with your AI habits on the table. Own up to an AI answer you didn’t check, or practise a clearer brief using a made-up task. Choose a card, decide how far you want to take it, and skip anything you don’t fancy. Your answers stay yours; sharing is up to you.
