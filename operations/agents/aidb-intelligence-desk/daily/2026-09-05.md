## AIDB's argument map

AIDB and guest Nufar Gaspar argue that advanced AI work is moving from one instruction toward controlled cycles and then coordinated groups of agents. Their central practical claim is that knowledge work lacks the automatic pass/fail checks available in software, so a person must design a concrete finish line before letting an agent keep iterating. They repeatedly qualify the pitch: use one pass when it works; do not loop work whose value is human judgment; cap time/turns/spend; use safe sandboxes; separate roles only when one agent has a demonstrable limitation.

That is a useful analytical and teaching frame, not a factual authority or a universal product guide. The plain-language reader translation is: **a loop is an AI worker retrying against a check; a graph is several workers with defined handoffs. The hard part is deciding what “done” means and what still needs a person.**
