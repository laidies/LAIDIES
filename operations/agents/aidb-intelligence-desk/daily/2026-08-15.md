## 1. LAiDIES operations

**Verdict:** TEST / NO CHANGE

**Affected surfaces:** workflow selection, review burden, privacy and authority

- Do not adopt the 0–10 score or “deputize” terminology as operating policy.
  The edition's score has no validation evidence and can hide an important
  failure: a frequent, teachable, easy-to-check task may still contain data the
  tool is not authorized to receive.
- The useful operating question is narrower: before applying AI, identify the
  recurring job, expected benefit, reversibility, check cost, stakes,
  privacy/permission boundary and the human or relationship judgment that must
  remain owned.
- If an operations owner wants to test the heuristic, use one fictional or
  non-sensitive recurring workflow and compare the score with actual setup,
  correction and review time. No shared config, account, product or workflow
  changed in this cycle.
