## Why the earlier decision was incomplete

The edition contains eleven headline items and eleven main-episode items. The earlier intake retained the Fable 5.1 story but did not leave an item-level decision for the rest of this edition. That made absence look like an editorial decision when no auditable decision existed.

This ledger treats AIDB as a scout and attributed analytical voice. It separates what primary sources establish, what AIDB argues, and what LAiDIES should do. `KEEP` means the item earns continued candidate work; `CHANGE` means its useful core survives only after correction, attribution or merger; `DISCARD` means it does not earn a distinct public item now. A `HOLD` reason is stated where evidence is incomplete.

## Edition-level conclusion

The complete edition produces four plausible ordinary-news packages and two watches, not twenty-two articles:

1. **OpenClaw shared sessions — KEEP as a distinct story candidate.** The useful story is not “OpenClaw won.” It is that an agent session can become shared work: another authorized person can inspect the same context, add information, steer it or take over. Official OpenClaw material substantiates the product mechanism. AIDB's prediction that this is where all agents go next must remain attributed analysis.
2. **Anthropic agentic incidents and reward-hacking audit — CHANGE and merge into one safety continuation.** Anthropic and UK AISI substantiate important incidents and mitigations, but AIDB compresses distinct pauses and overstates both air-gapping and the 10% finding. This needs one careful continuation to the tracked agent-safety theme, not two sensational briefs.
3. **ChatGPT advertising economics — CHANGE into a new follow-up to the August 31 story.** OpenAI now confirms a $1 billion annualized advertising run rate and availability to advertisers in more than 40 countries. LAiDIES must publish a successor explaining what changed; it must not rewrite the earlier story. “Run rate” is a recent pace projected over a year, not $1 billion already collected, profit or guaranteed annual revenue.
4. **Data centres become explicit election politics — KEEP as one Politics/Data Centres candidate, currently HOLD for original-post recovery.** Contemporaneous reporting substantiates the shift and quotes Trump and Vance. The exact Truth Social post and all politician/social originals have not yet been recovered. The story is distinct from the current Big Picture: it reports the political development and links to the admitted Big Picture for the underlying community bargain; it does not alter Big Picture without Ali's exact-version review.
5. **Coordinated frontier pacing — CHANGE to an attributed policy watch.** Anthropic's statement is real but is support for a desired lawful, verifiable and effective mechanism, not an implemented agreement or concrete design.
6. **Guardrail-stripped cyber model and China/CCTV claim — HOLD.** Neither has a sufficient primary evidence chain for publication. The cyber item also misspells the provider's domain and attributes benchmark and open-weight properties that were not recoverable from an exact public model artifact. The China item rests on a state-media-linked social account whose original post was not recovered; it cannot be represented as the Chinese government's settled position.
