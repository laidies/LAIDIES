### Public freshness direction

Ali directed that books and other living entries visibly show when they were
checked or updated and why the content changed.

The accepted direction distinguishes:

- `CHECKED [date]` — sources were reviewed and the answer still holds;
- `UPDATED [date]` — reader-facing meaning changed, with a one-line reason;
- `CORRECTED [date]` — a prior claim was wrong or misleading;
- `REVIEW DUE` — a date or event trigger fired and currentness is not yet
  re-established;
- `RETIRED` — the treatment no longer represents the current product or
  evidence.

Public dates cannot be bumped by an automated read, formatting-only edit or
owner handoff. Pattern handoff:
`handoffs/site-refresh/2026-07-27-public-freshness-pattern.md`
