# Practitioner signal pilot contract

## Visitor and operating problem

LAiDIES can miss useful current practice that appears first in official
engineering channels or in first-hand work by practitioners building the
products. The answer is a small candidate roster inside the existing AIDB
reference-tracing system, not another agent, news desk, publication or source
of truth.

## Acceptance boundary

The pilot may:

1. propose a deduplicated roster of 10–20 official or first-hand practitioner
   sources across OpenAI/Codex, Anthropic/Claude Code and one broader tooling
   ecosystem;
2. keep private signal states `NEW`, `VERIFY`, `TEST`, `ROUTE`, `PARK` and
   `RETIRE`; and
3. process only three to five signals through the existing chain:

   `exact original item → current official/primary verification → bounded
   current-version LAiDIES test → duplication check → one best owner route`.

The candidate roster is not a promoted recurring-source registry. A source
becomes recurring only after the existing promotion rule passes: repeated
relevance, reliable provenance, distinct non-duplicative signal and a stable
lawful official feed or endpoint. Personal employee views remain practitioner
evidence, never company authority.

## Roster record

Every candidate record must contain:

- identity, current company and role;
- direct relevance and hands-on expertise evidence;
- tier: `OFFICIAL_AUTHORITY`, `ORIGINAL_PRACTITIONER_EVIDENCE` or
  `SECONDARY_SCOUT`;
- lawful primary channel and exact URL;
- distinct contribution and duplication note;
- last verification date, expiry date and affiliation/source retirement
  trigger;
- monitoring method and terms/privacy boundary;
- cost (`NONE` is required for this pilot); and
- promotion status: `CANDIDATE`, `PILOT`, `PROMOTED`, `PARKED` or `RETIRED`.

Popularity, employer name or follower count is not admission evidence.

## Pilot pass/fail

The pilot passed when at least one of its three to five signals earned a
genuinely useful, evidence-backed and non-duplicative treatment and the named
receiving owner returns an explicit ruling (`ACCEPT`, `MERGE`, `PARK` or
`DECLINE`). The preserved result is thirteen roster entries, four processed
signals and three useful owner rulings.

Control Room reviewed the pilot evidence, source expiry behavior, zero-cost
boundary and collision controls on 2026-08-08. Recurrence is admitted only
inside the existing Intelligence Desk automation. `PROMOTED` and `PILOT`
sources follow their recorded cadence; `CANDIDATE` sources remain on-trigger or
roster-review only.

## Prohibited by this acceptance

- no new agent, scheduler, publication, daily quota or shared canonical store;
- no auto-follow, subscription, X activation, paid access or account action;
- no scraping contrary to site terms or bypassing access controls;
- no publication, deployment, external message, spend or private-data use;
- no silent promotion of roster candidates into standing monitored sources;
- no edit to another owner's canon, state or public surface; and
- no interruption to independent AIDB and Mollick edition cursors.

## Earliest safe trigger

The next scheduled recurrence consumes only sources that are due under the
recorded cadence and promotion boundary. AIDB and Mollick retain first priority;
roster work never delays their cursor processing.
