# New Model, New Manners

**Status:** RESEARCH — ONE FORMAT OPTION, NOT THE DECIDED PRODUCT  
**Authority:** Private AIDB Intelligence Desk concept; no publication or
canonical curriculum status.

## Promise

When an important AI model changes, LAiDIES helps learners stop carrying old
habits into a new system:

> What changed, which old habit now hurts, and how do I work with this model
> well?

This is not a launch recap or a list of clever prompts. It is one possible
deeper learning layer for the open format lab in
`new-model-new-manners-format-lab.md`. Do not presume every release needs it.

## One repeatable course, one versioned edition

1. **Meet the model:** what it is for, where it lives, and who can access it.
2. **What changed:** the consequential differences from the prior model.
3. **Retire the old manners:** inherited prompts, settings, or interaction
   habits that now underperform or create risk.
4. **Five moves to practise:** model-specific ways to brief, steer, use tools,
   set effort, provide context, or define success.
5. **Dress for the job:** compare a flagship and faster/cheaper route on one
   realistic task.
6. **Try, judge, adjust:** a before/after exercise with a visible acceptance
   check.
7. **Safety and boundaries:** what the learner should not delegate or assume.
8. **Keep it current:** evidence date, model/surface scope, and retest trigger.

Target experience: compact enough to finish in one sitting; practical enough
to change the learner's next real interaction.

## Admission gate

A course edition is earned only when the material cannot be taught responsibly
through the smaller broadcast/card treatment and all are true:

- a meaningful release or behavior change affects how people should work;
- current first-party provider guidance is available;
- AIDB's material practitioner references are traced to exact original items,
  or unresolved gaps are explicitly held;
- Chat, API, coding-agent, plan, region, and version boundaries are clear;
- LAiDIES runs at least one representative comparison with frozen success
  criteria;
- the lesson is distinct from the living Model Wardrobe and existing classes;
- a receiving curriculum/product owner accepts the edition.

No course is admitted from benchmarks, vibes, a launch post, or a scout's
summary alone.

## Evidence packet per edition

- release/model card and current provider guide;
- migration and prompting guidance;
- original practitioner tips discovered through AIDB;
- one counterexample, limitation, or failure mode;
- LAiDIES test brief, variants, result, and limitations;
- dated “works here / does not establish” summary;
- freshness trigger and retirement rule.

## Relationship to other LAiDIES formats

- **NewsStand:** why the release matters now.
- **New Model, New Manners:** how to work differently in practice.
- **Model Wardrobe:** durable cross-provider selection guide.
- **Study Pack:** exercises and comparison cards.
- **Field Notes / Behind the Build:** what LAiDIES learned when its own setup
  succeeded or failed.
- **Latest Tip:** one compact move pointing into the evidence-backed lesson.

These are connected jobs, not duplicate copies.
