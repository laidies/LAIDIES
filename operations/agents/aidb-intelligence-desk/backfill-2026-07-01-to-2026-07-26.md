## Executive finding

The most valuable July material is not a pile of isolated news stories. It is a
coherent LAiDIES teaching spine about **fit**:

> A more expensive model and a higher reasoning setting are not universally
> better, just as couture is not better beachwear. The right outfit depends on
> the job, the setting, the freedom of movement required, and what failure
> would cost.

That spine can support one living cross-provider guide, one practical class,
one memorable weekly episode, model-release NewsStand updates, and smaller
social/Study Pack derivatives without making five copies of the same lesson.

## Highest-priority content package

### P0 — The Model Wardrobe

**Working promise:** Choose the model, reasoning effort, and interaction style
that fit the job across ChatGPT/OpenAI, Claude/Anthropic, and Gemini/Google.

**LAiDIES analogy:** “You don’t wear couture to the beach.” Maximum capability
can be slower, costlier, overdressed, and less functional. Flip-flops are not a
lesser shoe when the assignment is sand.

**Distinct format jobs:**

- **Living LIBRAiRY guide/book:** current model/tier/effort matrix, task-fit
  decision tree, version dates, and provider-source links.
- **Class:** learners compare one real task across a smaller/faster model and a
  flagship model at two effort levels, then judge usefulness, time, cost, and
  overwork.
- **Weekly episode:** the couture-at-the-beach mental model, frustration caused
  by carrying old prompting habits into new models, and the confidence to turn
  a dial down.
- **NewsStand:** dated release notes explaining what actually changed for a
  reader when OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google ships a new model/version.
- **Study Pack:** “What am I dressing for?” task card and before/after routing
  exercise.

**Primary July inputs:** Jul 1, 8, 9, 10, 13, 17, 20, 21, 22, and 26.

### P0 — Ramble First, Tailor Second

**Working promise:** Voice is not sloppy prompting. A spoken context dump can
surface assumptions, stakes, examples, and uncertainty that a polished written
prompt edits away. The useful workflow is:

1. talk naturally;
2. let the AI identify the goal, constraints, unknowns, and contradictions;
3. confirm what matters;
4. turn that into the reviewable work brief.

This needs an explicit privacy boundary: do not voice-dump confidential names,
customer data, credentials, health details, or other information the selected
product/account should not receive.

**Best route:** practical class plus episode segment; shorter social
demonstration only after the teaching source is accepted.

**Primary July inputs:** Jul 9 and Jul 20.

### P1 — New Model, New Manners recurring public-service series

Every meaningful model generation can trigger a compact, versioned edition—but
only after the release changes how a learner should work and the lesson
survives provider-source review plus a small LAiDIES test. Each edition
answers: **What changed, which old habit now hurts, and how do I work with this
model well?**

The recurring regression-and-discovery ritual is:

- rerun representative tasks;
- remove one group of inherited instructions at a time;
- test the current effort and one level lower;
- check whether blanket brevity now removes necessary detail;
- describe tone as observable writing behavior, not adjectives;
- re-check boundaries, approval rules, and success criteria;
- test one higher-ambition task, not only old work made faster;
- record what should switch, wait, or stay unchanged.

**Format remains open:** the strongest current hypothesis is a short
public-service broadcast—part radio segment, part public television
demonstration—supported by one practical card. A deeper workshop or mini-course
is optional when the model change genuinely needs practice. Every edition
points back to the living Model Wardrobe rather than copying the full
cross-provider guide.

**Treatment gate:** current provider documentation; exact original practitioner
sources behind AIDB's advice; explicit Chat/API/coding-agent boundaries; one
representative LAiDIES test; a freshness trigger; and a receiving-owner
decision. No treatment from benchmarks, a launch post, or AIDB commentary
alone.

**Best route to test:** recurring Model Fitting Room public-service segment
connected to a NewsStand release note and the living Model Wardrobe; compare
audio-first, video demonstration, interactive clinic, and short-course forms
before canonizing the container.

**Primary July inputs:** Jul 1, 9, 20, and 26.

### P1 — From Chatting to Managing

Teach the difference between a chat, a delegated task, a goal/evaluator loop, a
scheduled agent, and a self-driving-company claim. The lesson should emphasize
verifiable endpoints, source-of-truth state, failure handling, and human
authority—not autonomous-agent theatre.

**Best route:** class plus Behind the Build, connected to existing LAiDIES
lessons about recipes versus running systems.

**Primary July inputs:** Jul 5, 10, 15, 19, 20, and 26.

## Operations recommendations

### Keep now

- Keep the existing Sol/Medium foreground, High planning, Terra/Medium
  background, and Fast-off baseline.
- Keep AIDB as comparative intelligence, never operating authority.
- Keep model changes tied to representative evaluation rather than launch
  excitement or benchmark rank.

### Test next

1. **Prompt diet audit:** select three long recurring LAiDIES agent prompts.
   Remove one repeated-instruction cluster at a time and compare completion,
   evidence, unnecessary work, token/credit use, and correction rate. Do not
   mass-rewrite every prompt.
2. **One-level-lower test:** for a representative bounded foreground task,
   compare the current model/effort with one lower effort. For a mechanical
   lane, compare Terra/Medium with Terra/Low.
3. **Voice-to-brief pilot:** use a non-sensitive real idea. Capture the spoken
   dump, have the model return goal/constraints/unknowns, confirm them, then
   compare the result with a manually polished prompt.
4. **New-model admission card:** create one reusable evaluation record covering
   task fit, quality, overwork, time, cost/credits, tool use, boundary behavior,
   and rollback. A release never changes the default by itself.
5. **Context hygiene trigger:** when a materially new model arrives, review
   current instructions for stale workarounds, aspirational user assumptions,
   duplicates, and product-specific facts that moved.

### Watch, do not change yet

- model routers and automatic model selection;
- persisted reasoning and explicit caching;
- proactive/multi-agent expansion;
- private-model fine-tuning or open-weight hosting.

Each may become useful, but July's AIDB coverage is not evidence that LAiDIES
needs the added operational surface today.

## Evidence reconciliation for the July 20 idea

Current official OpenAI guidance supports the central operating lesson:

- GPT-5.6 has Sol, Terra, and Luna tiers for different workload/cost roles.
- Preserve the current reasoning baseline, then compare the same setting and
  one lower on representative tasks.
- Reserve maximum effort for the hardest quality-first workloads.
- Leaner prompts can outperform repeated instructions; remove one group at a
  time and rerun evals.
- Keep domain context, hard constraints, approval boundaries, and success
  criteria even when simplifying prompts.

The official guidance does **not** establish equivalent best practices for
Claude or Gemini. Their version-specific guidance must be verified separately
before the Model Wardrobe presents a cross-provider recommendation.
