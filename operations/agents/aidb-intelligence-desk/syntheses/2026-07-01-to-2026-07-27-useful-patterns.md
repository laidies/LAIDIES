## The strongest additional finding

The July episodes repeatedly imply that **a good agent setup is not chiefly a
prompt**. It is a fitted working arrangement:

`right model + usable context + bounded permissions + clear finish line +
human outer loop + stopping rule`

That is the practical spine connecting the most useful ideas below.

## 1. The Agent Job Card

**Move:** Before delegating substantial work, provide six fields:

1. job and desired artifact;
2. relevant context and source of truth;
3. hard boundaries;
4. permissions and approval points;
5. observable acceptance criteria;
6. stop/escalation condition.

**Why useful:** It turns “manage the agent” into something a beginner can
actually do and prevents a powerful model from amplifying an unclear finish
line.

**Evidence:** Supported by current OpenAI model guidance on domain context,
hard constraints, approval boundaries and success criteria; current Anthropic
prompt-engineering guidance starts with success criteria and empirical tests;
Google's current agentic prompting guidance distinguishes risk, permissions,
persistence and completeness. AIDB July 15, 19, 20 and 26 converge on the same
structure.

**Route:** practical card/tool + core Model Fitting Room demonstration.  
**Status:** STRONG CANDIDATE — exact consumer/API wording must remain
surface-specific.

## 2. The Permission Fitting

**Move:** Grant capability in stages:

`read → prepare → draft → ask → act`

Start at the lowest level that can complete the useful job. Sending, deleting,
purchasing, publishing, changing shared state and unrestricted computer use
remain explicit gates until the workflow has earned more trust.

**Why useful:** Mollick's July guide gives the memorable real example: one
agent drafted an email while another sent it because send permission had
already been granted. The difference was the harness, not the prose prompt.

**Evidence:** Mollick's canonical July 23 post is resolved; applicable product
permission behavior still needs a current surface-by-surface provider check
before public teaching.

**Route:** safety segment in every agent/model treatment; possible interactive
“What would you let it do?” fitting game.  
**Status:** TEST / PROVIDER-SURFACE VERIFICATION REQUIRED.

## 3. The Outer Loop Is the Human Job

**Move:** Let the agent handle an inner execution loop while the person owns
direction, taste, trade-offs, evidence and acceptance. A loop is not finished
because the model stopped; it is finished when the work passes a check the
person chose.

**Why useful:** This provides a more honest and approachable explanation than
“autonomous agent.” It also matches LAiDIES' existing receipts, evaluators,
handoffs and independent-review gates.

**Evidence:** AIDB July 15 and 19 attribute the inner/outer-loop framing to
engineering practitioners and Replit's self-driving-company account. The
general control pattern is consistent with current provider guidance, but the
named original practitioner items and Replit performance claims are not yet
resolved in the desk's source trail.

**Route:** public-TV-style visible demonstration; class exercise; Behind the
Build using a real accepted/rejected artifact pair.  
**Status:** STRONG CONCEPT / ORIGINAL SOURCES PARTIALLY UNRESOLVED.

## 4. Ramble First, Tailor Second

**Move:** For a complex, non-sensitive idea, speak the context naturally; have
AI return the goal, constraints, unknowns and contradictions; correct that
brief; then delegate the work. Voice supplies context, while text remains the
reviewable result.

**Why useful:** It helps people who lose important context while trying to
write a “perfect prompt,” without teaching that raw rambling itself is a
finished brief.

**Evidence:** Repeated AIDB recommendation on July 9 and 20. It is an
immediately testable workflow hypothesis, not a universal provider fact.

**Route:** 90-second radio demonstration + before/after Study Pack.  
**Status:** TEST — use non-sensitive material and compare against a typed brief.

## 5. The Infinite Backlog Needs a Closing Bell

**Move:** Every agentic work block needs a budget and stopping rule: maximum
time/turns/credits, one reviewable result, what may be deferred, and what
requires a fresh decision.

**Why useful:** Faster agents can create more work, more review and more
simultaneous projects rather than leisure. The constraint shifts from
production to planning, oversight and recovery.

**Evidence:** AIDB July 12 synthesizes productivity/exhaustion research and
practitioner reactions. The original ActiveTrack, Berkeley and survey sources
must be resolved and methodologically reviewed before numerical claims are
used publicly. The operational value of explicit budgets and stop conditions
is independently consistent with current agent guidance.

**Route:** Field Note / Tribune on “AI brain fry”; operational closing-bell
template.  
**Status:** OPERATING TEST; PUBLIC NUMBERS ON HOLD.

## 6. Skills and Instructions Have Expiry Dates

**Move:** A meaningful model release triggers review of skills, global context,
templates and agent instructions—not just selection of the new model. Remove
one stale workaround or repeated cluster at a time and rerun representative
tasks.

**Why useful:** A reusable skill can become an autonomy trap if it quietly
preserves the behavior of an older model.

**Evidence:** Current OpenAI GPT-5.6 migration guidance directly supports
representative comparisons and one-change-at-a-time prompt simplification.
AIDB July 15 and 20 provide the scout/practitioner framing.

**Route:** New Model, New Manners recurring check; LAiDIES skill-expiry field
and retest date.  
**Status:** VERIFIED PRINCIPLE / LOCAL CONTROLLED REPLAY PENDING.

## 7. Pack a Work-Safe AI Passport

**Move:** Create a short, portable context card describing the person's role,
working preferences, goals, useful constraints and how the AI should challenge
them—but exclude secrets, private people, customer data, health information,
credentials and anything inappropriate for the selected account.

**Why useful:** AIDB's Summer Adventure calls this “Pack Your ID.” The useful
LAiDIES adaptation is not one universal biography pasted everywhere; it is a
small set of purpose-specific, privacy-graded packing cards.

**Evidence:** AIDB July 26 describes its own learning activity. Benefit,
privacy behavior and portability have not yet been tested by LAiDIES.

**Route:** interactive packing-list tool or Study Pack.  
**Status:** FORMAT CANDIDATE / PRIVACY REVIEW + DUPLICATION CHECK REQUIRED.

## 8. Automate the Workflow, Not Every Tiny Task

**Move:** Observe a real workflow, identify the bottleneck and source of truth,
then redesign one end-to-end path. Do not scatter agents across isolated tasks
without understanding the handoffs and review burden they create.

**Why useful:** It shifts LAiDIES from “where can we add AI?” to “what useful
outcome keeps getting stuck, and what whole path would make it easier?”

**Evidence:** AIDB July 12 attributes a two-week pod method and workflow-level
results to Uber; July 19 uses Replit as a systems-integration example. Exact
original posts and performance claims need resolution before public use.

**Route:** internal workflow clinic; later Behind the Build if a before/after
case is measured.  
**Status:** TESTABLE METHOD / ATTRIBUTED NUMBERS ON HOLD.

## 9. Expertise Becomes the Recovery System

**Finding:** When AI produces more implementation, human value shifts toward
framing, judgment, recognizing errors, recovering from them and deciding what
is worth doing.

**Why useful:** This is a constructive answer to “will AI make my knowledge
irrelevant?” Domain knowledge is what lets someone delegate well and detect
when fluent work has gone wrong.

**Evidence:** AIDB July 24 summarizes Anthropic economic research and a personal
essay by its head of economics. The original research packet must be reviewed
before teaching employment or productivity conclusions.

**Existing treatment:** This is already substantially covered in the
LIBRAiRY's live-indexed **Straight Answers About AI**, under Jobs & Work:

- “Is AI replacing jobs right now?”
- “Which jobs are most affected?”
- “Will AI replace ME specifically?”

The third answer already teaches that expert judgment, briefing, checking and
decision ownership are what make expertise more—not less—valuable.

**Route:** `LINK / UPDATE EXISTING`. Reconcile Anthropic's July evidence against
the existing receipt map and update the answer only if it materially changes
the conclusion, confidence label, limits or recheck trigger. NewsStand acts
only if the underlying evidence changes the dated employment picture enough to
qualify independently. Do not create a separate durable lesson.

**Canonical files:** `content/library-books/straight-answers.md`,
`content/library-books/rendered/straight-answers.html`, and live index record
`ref-straight-answers` in `content/site/site-index.json`.  
**Status:** DUPLICATE TREATMENT FOUND / CONSEQUENTIAL NEW CLAIMS HELD.

## No-change findings

- Do not adopt automatic model routing merely because routing is fashionable.
- Do not widen agent permissions because another expert's setup worked.
- Do not use “self-driving company” as an infrastructure objective.
- Do not publish numerical productivity, neuroscience, employment or
  enterprise-performance claims from AIDB's summary alone.
- Do not create a separate artifact for every idea; several belong inside one
  Model Fitting Room pilot and one practical toolkit.
