## Reader question

**If ChatGPT can remember activity from other apps and websites, what can I
control, and how is that different from chat history, ordinary memory and
current context?**

This is not a missing-topic case. Setup 101 already teaches the durable
distinctions among current chat, optional memory, standing instructions,
project context and information that should be stored nowhere. The new product
feature is a dated implementation example that may make the existing guidance
incomplete if it is not named and bounded.

## Trigger and primary evidence

OpenAI's official ChatGPT & Codex changelog added [Computer
History](https://developers.openai.com/codex/changelog#codex-2026-08-13-app)
on 2026-08-13; accessed 2026-08-14.

The official entry says Computer History is an opt-in feature in the ChatGPT
desktop app on macOS. It can turn activity across selected apps and websites
into memories and a timeline usable by ChatGPT and Codex. A user can choose
contributing apps and websites, pause collection, and review or delete the
history. The entry lists ChatGPT Pro, Business and Enterprise availability;
Business and Enterprise administrators must enable access. Initial
availability excludes the EEA, Switzerland and the United Kingdom.

## Limits that must survive any update

- This evidence applies to the ChatGPT desktop app on macOS and the plans and
  regions named above as of 2026-08-13. Do not generalize it to mobile, web,
  Windows, Linux or consumer Free accounts.
- `Opt-in`, app/site selection, pause, review and delete are established. The
  changelog does not establish a universal retention duration, training-use
  policy, enterprise administrator visibility, security guarantee or legal
  compliance result.
- Computer History is not the same thing as the current chat, ordinary saved
  memory, referenced chat history, standing instructions, project files or
  model training. The owner must preserve those distinctions rather than add
  another vague use of “memory.”
- The desk did not enable, connect or test the feature. Availability and
  control behavior are documented but not locally observed.
- No reader should infer that allowing collection is safe for confidential,
  client, regulated or workplace material. Applicable authority and policy
  still govern whether the source material may be exposed at all.
