## Reader-choice hypothesis

When a local proposal appears, the practical question is not whether a reader
is generally pro- or anti-AI. It is whether the actual permit, rate design,
disclosure, public-review process and community-benefit terms protect her town.
The dated news job is to show why politicians are moving toward pauses and
rules now; the durable learning job already belongs to Straight Answers.
