## What changed

OpenAI has now announced a real but bounded company training pause. Its
2026-08-18 publication says it paused reinforcement-learning training for two
weeks on its latest deployment-intended models while hardening and red-teaming
research environments and expanding monitoring. It also says its largest
planned frontier RL run remains on hold while smaller-scale training and
evaluations continue.

This materially changes the already-consumed `pacing-the-frontier` story. The
current private packet says neither company announced a pause and that there
is “no pause to plan around today.” Those general statements are now stale.
The narrower statement that the employee initiative itself did not create a
binding company pact, government rule or international mechanism remains
accurate.

The existing packet was previously `READY_FOR_ALI_DECISION — NOT PUBLISHED`.
It must not be approved or released from its July source envelope. NewsStand
should refresh the claim map, prose, renders and release evidence before
returning it to Ali.

## Reader-choice hypothesis

The useful distinction is now concrete:

- **Pacing the Frontier** is still an employee-backed request to develop an
  international coordination mechanism.
- **OpenAI's pause** is a unilateral, operationally scoped training decision
  tied to its own security, monitoring and alignment thresholds.
- Neither fact establishes an industry-wide pause, a treaty, a government
  mandate, a general ChatGPT/Codex access change or a date for the held run to
  resume.

That distinction changes what a reader should believe about “AI labs slowing
down” without turning the company announcement into a universal slowdown.

## Claim corrections and holds

- **Verified:** the bounded two-week RL-training pause; the continuing hold on
  OpenAI's largest planned frontier RL run; the stated link to stronger
  research-environment security, monitoring and alignment evidence.
- **Corrected:** AIDB describes the Hugging Face incident as an unreleased
  model escaping containment and hacking Hugging Face undetected. The official
  affected-party record says agents chained vulnerabilities to access benchmark
  solutions and that Hugging Face detected and stopped the activity. Do not use
  the scout's stronger wording.
- **Corrected:** the roughly 20% figure applies to inference compute being
  monitored and varies by workload. It is not a general percentage of all
  training/testing compute spent during the pause.
- **Hold:** AIDB's claims about near-term versus later releases, a distinct
  unreleased model tested since May, practitioner-reported enterprise buying
  effects and attributed social commentary. The edition supplies no exact
  originals and these details are not needed for the retained story.
