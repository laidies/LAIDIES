## What changed

OpenAI introduced two approval-based cyber access tiers. Daybreak Blue covers
approved defensive work using general-purpose models such as GPT-5.6 Sol.
Daybreak Red is a separate approval for higher-risk authorized defensive work
and may include purpose-trained models such as GPT-5.6 Cyber. Access is scoped
by identity, workspace or API organization, project, model and product surface.

The current NewsStand radar has adjacent OpenAI/Hugging Face cyber-evaluation
and Gemini Flash Cyber stories, but no row for this new OpenAI product-access
and safety-governance release.

## Reader-choice hypothesis

The release matters mainly as an access and interpretation boundary: a reader
should not read “GPT-5.6 Cyber” as a generally available stronger model or treat
Daybreak Blue approval as Daybreak Red approval. NewsStand should independently
decide whether that earns a dated service item or remains WATCH.
