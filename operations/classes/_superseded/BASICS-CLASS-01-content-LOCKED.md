## What it is
A tool you talk to in plain English. You type what you want; it writes back. No code, no special
words — if you can text a friend, you can use it. The big ones are ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini;
they are the same kind of thing and mostly do the same jobs, so once you can use one, the others
feel familiar.

## What it can actually do for you — real jobs off a real week, four kinds

**① Make sense of something confusing.**
- "Explain what my accountant means by 'accrual basis' like I'm smart but new to it."
- "Here's a letter from my insurance company — what is it actually telling me, and do I need to
  do anything?" (you paste it in)
- "What are the actual differences between these two health plans for a family of four?"

**② Write the first draft you're dreading.**
- "Turn these messy bullet points into a clear email to my director."
- "Draft a warm but firm reply to this client" — you paste their email, it answers in context.
- "Help me write a wedding toast for my best friend — heartfelt, not cheesy."

**③ Digest something long, fast.**
- "Summarise this 40-page policy into the five things my team actually does differently."
- "Read this contract and flag anything unusual I should ask about before I sign." (a second
  pair of eyes, not a lawyer)
- "Here's a 60-message email thread — what was decided and what's still open?"

You hand it a long document and it does the reading. ⚠ **Exactly how you get the file in — the
upload button, what types it takes, how big — is different for each tool, so we cover that
properly in your tool's own course.** (Ali 2026-07-22 — point file-upload specifics forward.)

**④ Think something through with you.**
- "Plan three days in Portland for me and my sister — we love food, hate hiking."
- "I've got chicken, spinach, and twenty minutes. What do I make?"
- "Help me figure out what to actually say in a hard conversation with my mother-in-law."

## How you get your stuff in (the thing nobody explains)
Three ways, and all three appear above:
- **Type or paste** it into the box.
- **Upload a file** — Word, PDF, spreadsheet (the paperclip / ＋ button).
- Later, **connect** your email or drive so it reaches things without you pasting (its own class).

## The parts they all have
A box to type in · your past chats saved down the side · a "new chat" for a clean slate · a
settings area for the deeper stuff. (New chat only when you want a fresh topic — same chat
remembers the thread, new chat starts over.) Each tool names its extras differently — that is
what your tool's own course decodes.

## How to start
Open your tool, pick one thing from up there that's real for you today, and type it. (What to
type to get a GOOD answer is its own lesson — the prompting episode / class.)
