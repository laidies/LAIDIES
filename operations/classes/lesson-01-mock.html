Two things here, and they are not the same. Everything you can click is the tool. The part that produces the words is the model. The tool is what you set up. The model is what writes.
Write a note to my team about the delay.
Dear Team, I wanted to take a moment to reach out regarding the recent delay. We appreciate your patience as we work to resolve the situation…
Ask a hundred people to describe a chair and you get four legs, wooden, nothing you'd remember. Give it almost nothing, and the most likely answer is the most average one. It is not being lazy — that flat answer is what "most likely" looks like.
