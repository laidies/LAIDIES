## Directions

### A — One adult comic/graphic-novel world

Website environments, operated objects and characters use the episodes'
dimensional ink, faceted lighting, depth and controlled print texture. Reading
surfaces remain calm and editorial. This offers maximum continuity but risks
making every quiet task feel like an episode panel or increasing replacement
cost dramatically.

### B — Deliberate dual system

Town/building environments retain a painterly, dimensional storybook quality;
episodes remain adult comic/graphic novel. Shared palette, typography, canon,
object language, transitions and selected comic accents make the difference
feel intentional. This preserves the established town atmosphere but risks
feeling like two brands if the bridge is too weak.

### C — Controlled hybrid

Rooms retain painterly depth and atmosphere while operated objects, characters,
content illustrations, results and high-energy states use decisive comic ink
and faceted planes. The transition is functional: what the visitor can operate
or learn from becomes graphically explicit. This may best balance atmosphere
and clarity but has the highest risk of looking pasted together without strict
edge, lighting, texture and palette rules.
