# LAiDIES sitewide visual-style championship

**Status:** BUILDING — THREE CONTROLLED DIRECTIONS REQUIRED
**Owner decision:** Ali selects the final public style after comparable visual
evidence exists.

This competition tests rendering language only. Its candidates must preserve
the same functional composition and geometry. The separate Library experience
competition under `operations/design-explorations/library-redesign-20260726/`
may change Library structure while preserving Library capabilities; it cannot
select the sitewide style or become a structural template for other buildings.

## Product-owner trigger

The public website is substantially painterly while the episodes use an adult
comic/graphic-novel language. A building champion cannot consistently design
rooms, characters, operated objects, subpages and generated artwork until the
portfolio decides whether this contrast is a defect, a deliberate format
distinction or the basis of a controlled hybrid.

## Controlled test case

All directions use the same complete LIBRAiRY experience:

- arrival inside a recognizable Library room;
- shelves integrated into the environment;
- book covers/spines as operable controls;
- Ask Miss Jeeves as the reference-desk path;
- visible availability and held states;
- Puffy stickers as device-local saves on books/sections;
- clear explanation of everything available in the building;
- visible relationships and return paths to related LAiDIES experiences; and
- desktop plus compact-mobile composition.

Typography, statuses, accessibility labels and functional controls remain live
HTML/CSS. Generated images do not carry fake signage, logos or UI text.

## Directions

### A — One adult comic/graphic-novel world

Website environments, operated objects and characters use the episodes'
dimensional ink, faceted lighting, depth and controlled print texture. Reading
surfaces remain calm and editorial. This offers maximum continuity but risks
making every quiet task feel like an episode panel or increasing replacement
cost dramatically.

### B — Deliberate dual system

Town/building environments retain a painterly, dimensional storybook quality;
episodes remain adult comic/graphic novel. Shared palette, typography, canon,
object language, transitions and selected comic accents make the difference
feel intentional. This preserves the established town atmosphere but risks
feeling like two brands if the bridge is too weak.

### C — Controlled hybrid

Rooms retain painterly depth and atmosphere while operated objects, characters,
content illustrations, results and high-energy states use decisive comic ink
and faceted planes. The transition is functional: what the visitor can operate
or learn from becomes graphically explicit. This may best balance atmosphere
and clarity but has the highest risk of looking pasted together without strict
edge, lighting, texture and palette rules.

## Required evidence

Exactly three independent 1440×1024 Library mockups, one per direction, using
the same capability hierarchy and functional composition. Each direction also
records:

- reference roles and prompt;
- treatment of room, shelf, books, Miss Jeeves, Puffy state and cross-links;
- mobile translation;
- content/readability strategy;
- character and canon behavior;
- production cost and migration implications;
- rules for episodes, buildings, subpages, games/tools and editorial reading;
- red-team failure modes; and
- independent scores for product quality, trust/accuracy, LAiDIES brand,
  visual coherence, UX/accessibility and maintainability.

No direction becomes sitewide authority merely because one generated image is
attractive. Ali's bounded decision occurs only after the three comparable
systems and trade-offs are visible.
