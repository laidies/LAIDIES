while the episodes use the adult comic/graphic-novel system. The shared-world
direction below remains a credible candidate but is not a settled instruction

older site-palette notes where they conflict. It does not override the episode
people-style authority in `operations/episode-visual-system-lock.md`.

## The whole-page rule

Design the complete composition as one authored system before implementing or
showing isolated sections. Do not improve the site by accumulating unrelated
cards, images, effects, or one-off reactions.

The website and episodes must feel like the same world:

- adult, dimensional comic / graphic-novel rendering
- bright daytime SUNNYVAiLE and clear 1990s/Y2K cultural specificity
- polished enough for a senior professional to share
- playful without becoming childish
- expressive without becoming chaotic

## Colour

- Keep the existing light background gradients.
- Use a near-black blue for dark mastheads, gutters, hard shadows, and ink.
- Use a full vibrant 1990s accent spectrum. Pink, purple, cyan and teal remain
  available, but must not become the only repeated quartet.
- Add cobalt, sky blue, periwinkle, coral, orange, lime, mint, and small
  sunflower-yellow or red accents where the composition needs them.
- Do not balance the page toward yellow and red.
- Do not default every building to teal plus magenta.
- Do not use electric hot-pink on black as the site-wide identity.
- Preserve quiet, low-noise areas around body copy and controls.

## Typography and word art

- Keep Jost for body copy, navigation, forms, and functional UI.
- Comic display lettering must be constructed from the saved references in
  `operations/reference/comic-text-emphasis/` and
  `operations/reference/font-and-text-emphasis/`.
- Plain Bungee is rejected as the site comic-display substitute.
- Use one deliberate lettering treatment at a time: dimensional face,
  substantial dark keyline, coloured secondary edge, and hard offset shadow or
  a purposeful burst when warranted.
- Do not scatter unrelated comic fonts across the page.
- The current approved LAiDIES wordmark remains authoritative.
- Brand words keep the accented lower-case `i`, with `Ai` coloured separately:
  LAiDIES, SUNNYVAiLE, MAiVENS, LUMINAiRY, MAiKEOVER.
- The technology term is `AI`, both letters capitalized.

## Images

- People must use the exact locked episode style anchored by
  `assets/episodes/ep-04/pixel/ep04-heroine-face-lock-approved-ali.png`.
- Do not use ambiguous ensemble images as known residents, MAiVENS, or other
  canon groups.
- Do not use an unapproved face merely because an image is already in the repo.
- Avoid painterly, flat-vector, juvenile cartoon, or visually unrelated people.
- If an approved person is unavailable, use a verified environment or object
  until the correct character art exists.
- Site environments and buildings should inherit the episode world's ink,
  faceted lighting, depth, and controlled printed texture.
- Painterly or stained-glass work may appear only as an intentional in-world
  artifact, not as the default site-rendering language.

## Layout

- No generic SaaS card grid.
- No long centre-column template repeated across every building.
- No chaotic gallery of images in boxes with text boxes placed on top.
- No boring equal boxes everywhere.
- Use full-width composition, editorial reading rhythm, confident negative
  space, and a small number of purposeful high-energy moments.
- The homepage stays guided rather than becoming an inventory.
- Building pages follow the LIBRAiRY principle: the building's function is the
  page mechanic, and the pictured object or room is what the visitor operates.
- Page construction must proceed before final replacement art is ready. Build
  stable asset slots and mechanics, then swap approved final art into them.
- Desktop and approximately 390px mobile layouts must both feel intentionally
  edited, with no duplicate controls or horizontal overflow.

## Motion

- Motion language: panel slides, page turns, ink reveals, halftone wipes,
  caption stamps, and lettering that appears, switches on, or lights up.
- Avoid generic floating, slow parallax, and repeated fades as the main motion
  system.
- Background animation should stay subtle and local to the intended object.
- Provide reduced-motion alternatives.
