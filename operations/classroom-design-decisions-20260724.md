# SUNNYVAiLE High classroom v2 construction decisions

Date: 2026-07-24

## Source truth

The inherited class page was already one of the site's stronger destinations:
one real classroom, an operable AV-cart television, an overhead transparency,
dated class-tape evidence, before/after teaching data, related destinations,
and a live period register sourced from `content/site/high-classes.json`.

The class source, television behavior, currency checks, and lesson rendering
were not rewritten.

## Construction

The useful room mechanic was preserved and extended:

- the classroom now fills the arrival rather than sitting inside a rounded
  image card;
- the chalkboard title and television hotspot retain their real alignment;
- the overhead transparency remains the first teaching object below the room;
- lesson notes are continuous ruled sections instead of a stack of generic
  cards;
- the class-tape evidence is a dark physical record beside the lesson;
- related destinations are a ruled route register;
- periods and subjects are a continuous timetable;
- the television still opens the real class film when present and a truthful
  Please stand by test card when it has not been filmed.

## Visual language

- the light site gradient remains below the classroom;
- midnight blue anchors the transparency surround and tape record;
- vivid pink, cyan, purple, cobalt, coral, and mint are used as functional
  accents;
- Anton is limited to the overhead and section display lettering; Jost remains
  the teaching face;
- rounded content cards and pill timetables are replaced by ruled lesson
  sections and squared registers.

## Boundaries

- `content/site/high-classes.json` remains the single class source;
- the current classroom is a structural bridge and not a future rendering lock;
- no unfilmed class was represented as filmed;
- the approved Episode 04 Heroine face remains the character rendering lock;
- the class construction must accept later classroom art without changing
  television geometry, teaching records, or period navigation.
