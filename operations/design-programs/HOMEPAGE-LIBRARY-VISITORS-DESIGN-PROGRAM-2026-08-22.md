# Homepage, LIBRAiRY and Visitor's Centre design program

**Status:** `PREPRODUCTION GUARDS READY / NO VISUAL CANDIDATE BUILT`

## Outcome

Produce reviewable examples for Homepage, LIBRAiRY and Visitor's Centre from
Ali's current walkthrough decisions without recovering retired visual language,
stale character art, rejected page shells or unverified feature claims.

The three pages share one header/type foundation and one prevention system. They
do not share one layout or one visual concept: each page keeps its own job,
environment, hierarchy, content truth and responsive composition.

## Dependency order

1. Preserve the operating guard in a clean pushed commit.
2. Design the shared header, type roles and deterministic controls once.
3. Produce one smallest representative first-screen proof for each page, using
   only the manifest's admitted existing assets and declared new-art roles.
4. Run maker pixel review at 1440px desktop and 390px mobile, then the independent
   design-review admission gate.
5. Show Ali only exact admitted bytes from an exact pushed commit.
6. Commit and push each completed page direction set and each accepted successor
   separately. A completed Homepage unit is preserved even if LIBRAiRY or the
   Visitor's Centre remains in progress.
7. After Ali approves an exact page candidate, deploy that bounded unit without
   waiting for the other pages. Verify its real desktop/mobile journey on
   `laidies.ai` and preserve its rollback identity before moving on.

## Page jobs

- **Homepage:** the guided town-square entrance. Explain the LAiDIES to
  SUNNYVAiLE causal model immediately, reveal what is happening now, group real
  visitor jobs and route directly to named destinations.
- **LIBRAiRY:** a physical, working reference library. Search and browse lead to
  readable covers seated in distinct room sections, then one-click pre-open
  previews with truthful held/open state. Miss Jeeves is a question-led guide,
  not catalogue search.
- **Visitor's Centre:** the complete tourist-information centre. Explain what
  SUNNYVAiLE is before how to use it; orient by buildings and goals, support one
  contextual tour system, FAQs and postcards only at their admitted truth state.

## Non-negotiable prevention

`homepage-library-visitors-20260822.json` is the machine-readable authority for
this program. Existing imagery is default-deny. A candidate must declare every
referenced asset and its hash; undeclared references fail. Candidate-generated
art must live under the candidate's tracked directory and perform a declared
new-art role. The checker is calibrated to reject the old Visitor lobby and the
rejected Homepage Miss Jeeves scene.

The recovered historical rejection fixtures are quarantine evidence only. Their
presence makes the admission test capable of failing; it does not make any of
their designs or assets eligible for reuse.

Incremental release does not collapse status boundaries. `PUSHED`, `DEPLOYED`
and `PUBLICLY VERIFIED` are separate facts, and only the final one establishes
that the visitor-visible improvement is live and working.
