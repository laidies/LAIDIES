## ChatGPT observation

Evidence:
`chatgpt-logged-out-consumer-response.jpg`

Response shape:

- one-sentence introduction;
- email body without a subject line;
- directly states the missed Friday deadline;
- apologizes for the timing change;
- proposes Tuesday and explains that it supports quality;
- offers to rewrite in a more apologetic, direct or formal tone.

Observed style for this one run:

- short-to-medium;
- direct with a light conversational opening;
- closely follows the task, then adds one optional next step;
- mostly plain paragraphs.

Limits:

- no underlying model was disclosed;
- logged-out behavior may differ from signed-in behavior;
- personality, Characteristics, custom instructions and memory may change a
  signed-in answer;
- one answer cannot establish a standing ChatGPT tendency.

## Gemini observation

Evidence:
`gemini-flash-temporary-consumer-response.jpg`

Response shape:

- one-sentence introduction;
- includes a subject-line placeholder;
- directly states the missed Friday deadline;
- proposes next Tuesday as a question;
- uses a warmer, slightly more expansive explanation;
- adds a separate `A Quick Tip` section suggesting how to mention the reason
  for the delay.

Observed style for this one run:

- medium detail;
- conversational and warm;
- follows the task and adds an extra idea;
- more structured than the ChatGPT answer because it includes a subject and
  separate tip.

Limits:

- this was Gemini consumer chat in visible `Flash` mode;
- Temporary chat reduced persistence but does not make the output universal;
- account instructions, selected mode, personalization and future product
  changes may alter the answer;
- one answer cannot establish a standing Gemini tendency.

## Claude observation

Evidence:
`claude-sonnet5-medium-incognito-consumer-response.jpg`

Response shape:

- briefly explains its editorial choice before the draft;
- opens a structured email composer with separate subject and message fields;
- supplies a specific subject line;
- gives a longer email than the ChatGPT and Gemini outputs;
- includes a reason for the delay, a quality rationale and a fallback if
  Tuesday does not work;
- offers shorter, more casual or more explanatory revisions; and
- exposes a `Send via Gmail` action, which was not used.

Observed style for this one run:

- medium-to-detailed;
- direct, warm and more explanatory;
- follows the task while adding context, contingency language and revision
  options;
- strongly structured by the dedicated email-composer treatment.

Limits:

- this was Claude consumer chat on the Free plan in Incognito mode;
- the visible model was `Sonnet 5` with `Medium` effort;
- Claude labelled Sonnet 5 as `Most efficient for everyday tasks`; paid
  `Fable 5` and `Opus 5` options were not used because this was an ordinary
  newcomer task, not a toughest/complex-task test;
- the composer treatment is part of the product surface and must not be
  mistaken for an inherent model personality;
- style settings, effort, selected model and future product changes may alter
  the answer; and
- one answer cannot establish a standing Claude tendency.

## Reconciled design learning

The completed fixture supports the Try-On comparison mechanic:

- the same task can return answers with visibly different length, structure,
  warmth and extra suggestions;
- the products can also present the work differently: plain answer, answer
  plus tip, or a dedicated email composer;
- descriptive style sliders make those differences easier to notice;
- usefulness ratings must belong to the learner and her real task;
- current account, settings, model/mode and observation date must remain
  visible; and
- LAiDIES must not convert a single run into fixed provider personalities.
