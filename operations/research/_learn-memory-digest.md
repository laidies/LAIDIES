### The plain-teaching rule
- 🔴 **PLAIN-TEACHING RULE:** write the teaching exactly as plainly as you'd explain it to a smart friend in chat — plain mechanic, plain words, shortest true sentence. **The Y2K voice and the analogy are GARNISH; they decorate a clear idea, they never carry it** — (plain-teaching-garnish-not-carry.md)
- 🎯 **THE TEST ALI GAVE:** *"the way we laid it out during our chats is much clearer than what's coming out in the article."* → **If the plain chat-style explanation is clearer than the drafted script, the script is wrong.** Flatten until the script matches the chat's clarity — (plain-teaching-garnish-not-carry.md)

- Failure mode: **the core teaching must answer the episode's own opening question**, not a side-metaphor bolted on at the end — (plain-teaching-garnish-not-carry.md)
