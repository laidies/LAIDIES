## The structure — LOCKED

**Each tool is a SUBJECT. Each subject runs EIGHT short single-topic PERIODS.**

Ali's call, and it overrode my first proposal. Mine was concept-first — one class on "containers"
covering Projects and Gems and Spaces together. Tidy, but nobody wakes up wanting to learn about
containers; they want to know what ChatGPT does. **Tool-first is how people actually search and
actually think.**

The one change made to her instinct: a tool's deep dive is **not one long video**. Biology isn't
one lesson, it's a term of them. Eight ~2-minute tapes add up to a complete deep dive while
staying re-shootable.

### Why short beats long here — the research finding that forced it
Vendor feature names rot faster than anything else in this subject:
- ChatGPT: Connectors → Apps → Plugins Directory, three names in under a year. Agent mode →
  "ChatGPT Work" caught mid-flip; one live help page documents a feature it says is retired.
- Gemini: Extensions → apps → Connected Apps. **NotebookLM → Gemini Notebook, renamed
  2026-07-16 — six days before we checked.**
- Perplexity: Spaces → Projects; Pages "temporarily retired"; two same-day pages contradict
  each other on remote MCP.
- Copilot: Notebooks/Pages mid-merge. Claude: Cowork/Plugins/scheduled tasks all beta.

A 16-minute ChatGPT tour dies the day one menu changes. **Eight small tapes mean a rename costs
one re-shoot.** That is the difference between a library you maintain and one that goes stale.

## The eight periods — same order in every subject

Same running order for all three tools, so **one script is filmed three times** and the reader
can compare tools period-for-period.

| P | Class | What she watches happen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Tour** | Every part of the screen, named. What each tier actually shows. |
| 2 | **What It Already Knows About You** | Memory — open it, read it, edit one line, delete one line. |
| 3 | **Set It Once** | The standing instruction written once, then a fresh chat obeying it. |
| 4 | **A Room of Its Own** | The container built end to end, and the trap inside it. |
| 5 | **The Thing Beside the Chat** | The panel opening, being edited, being kept — or lost. |
| 6 | **Same Question, Two Answers** | One question, two modes, answers side by side. |
| 7 | **What You're Actually Granting** | The real consent screen, read line by line. Then revoking it. |
| 8 | **While You're Asleep** | A recurring job set up in plain language. |

**Period 1 is the most important class we make.** Everyone teaches prompting; nobody teaches the
furniture. "What everything means" is the actual gap, and nothing else lands until she can name
what she is looking at.

## Subject order — LOCKED
**ChatGPT ships complete first**, then Claude, then Gemini. One finished subject beats three
half-built ones. Gemini last deliberately — the research flagged it as the most volatile.

## Rules these force
1. **No vendor feature name in a class title.** The titles above contain none — so a rename
   costs one clip, not a re-titled syllabus.
2. **Date stamp on every tape.** Built into `/learn/class.html`: Filmed, Checked, who gets it,
   and whether it ages fast, with a link to the vendor page it was checked against.
3. **Re-check before filming anything rated HIGH volatility.** ChatGPT Period 6 is marked
   `researched-verify-before-filming` for exactly this reason — the Chat/Work transition was
   mid-flip on the day we checked.
4. Do not film anything marked NOT VERIFIED in the research files without checking live first —
   36 such items across the five tools.
