# Canonical instruction dependency map

**Status:** ACTIVE OPERATING CONTROL
**Purpose:** prevent a new brief, prompt, task summary or agent handoff from
silently replacing more detailed LAiDIES work.

## Authority and inheritance rule

A summary routes work; it does not become product authority. Every maker,
owner, reviewer and dispatcher must resolve the exact governing sources before
acting. Use this precedence order:

1. current Ali rulings and supersession records in
   `operations/engine/LEDGER.md`;
2. `operations/CODEX-WORKING-AGREEMENT.md`, `operations/ACTIVE-WORK.md` and
   standing sitewide standards;
3. the owning product's current `CHARTER.md`, `OPERATING-SPEC.md`,
   `EXPERIENCE-BRIEF.md`, `FUNCTIONALITY-MAP.md`, state and accepted evidence;
4. current shared-platform and dependency-owner contracts;
5. current visual/building briefs and exact admitted asset references;
6. implementation and evidence; and
7. task prompts, handoffs and summaries.

Lower-ranked material may add bounded execution detail. It may not weaken,
omit, reinterpret or supersede a higher-ranked requirement. If sources
conflict, stop the affected work, name both sources and route an exact
reconciliation. Do not choose the shorter or newer-looking file by default.

## Product-specific inheritance

Every registered building or function inherits the sitewide dependencies above
and must also read its exact product-steward dossier. Those dossiers contain
requirements that cannot be flattened into a universal page template—for
example:

- Visitor's Centre: truthful 17-destination orientation and map/list fallback;
- LIBRAiRY: fail-closed admitted-book retrieval, Miss Jeeves and saved lookup;
- Blend & Snap: café counter/order/Study Pack creation loop;
- SUNNYVAiLE High: lesson, attempt, feedback and demonstrated transfer;
- NewsStand: claim/date/source/correction publication lifecycle;
- KSVL: canonical programme registry, playback and request moderation;
- FAiRY Godmother: safety classification and atomic Play accounting;
- Town Hall, Post Office and Community: separate provider, consent,
  moderation, privacy and receipt lifecycles;
- Mall: shop-specific jobs and future transaction/fulfilment boundaries;
- Chick Flicks and episode experience: admitted media, accessible playback and
  truthful same-device/account continuation;
- Resident Card, MAiKEOVER and Closet: identity, ownership, migration,
  deletion and cross-device propagation; and
- Dream Phone: current owner-decision hold; existing pages do not select the
  intended product model.

The complete product inventory lives in `operations/product-stewards/` and is
validated by `node scripts/check-product-stewards.mjs --owner-entry <id>`.

## Brief and handoff admission

Before a new brief, prompt, work order or agent handoff becomes executable, it
must record:

1. the governing ledger decision IDs;
2. exact sitewide standards used;
3. exact owner dossier and dependency contracts used;
4. exact admitted references/assets and explicit exclusions;
5. visitor states and transitions affected;
6. which requirements are objective build-time blockers;
7. what requires independent human judgment;
8. evidence reused versus evidence that must be produced;
9. collision boundary and files it may change; and
10. the exact next state, owner and return trigger.

A missing dependency is `SPEC RECOVERY REQUIRED`, not permission to improvise.
A complete checklist without artifact-bound execution evidence is not a PASS.
