## Reconciliation checks

- [ ] Canonical, performance and as-recorded lyric files are all identified.
- [ ] Every pronunciation-sensitive word records its meaning and intended
      sound.
- [ ] Tool/model/version/date is recorded for every performance workaround.
- [ ] Performance-only spellings and structure/delivery cues are absent from
      public lyrics, captions, cards, search and articles.
- [ ] Every omission, repetition, substitution and ad-lib in the approved
      audio is present in the as-recorded transcript.
- [ ] Every generator deviation is accepted, regenerated or rejected
      explicitly.
- [ ] Semantic changes are reconciled into episode canon.
- [ ] Fact corrections are reconciled into the fact ledger and all affected
      surfaces.
- [ ] Chorus/title/quotable changes reach every exact reuse.
- [ ] Public lyrics match the final audio after public-spelling normalization.
- [ ] Timed lyrics/captions use the final approved audio.
- [ ] KSVL title, artist, file and lyric version identify the approved track.
- [ ] Any Before/After learning candidate records permission, evidence and
      publication state.
