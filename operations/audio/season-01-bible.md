# LAiDIES — Season One Bible

The season is ONE story: a woman goes from eight months of "I'll get to
it" to being the person other people come to when the future gets
confusing. Every episode stands alone (one lesson, one try-on), but
listened front to back the twenty-four episodes are her year. The four
promises in the announcer's season promo are the four act milestones:

> she stops feeling behind → she learns to delegate to machines →
> she builds her own little squad of AI helpers → she becomes the woman
> other people come to.

## Format (locked)

- **Length:** SHORT. 8–12 minutes target. One lesson per episode, no
  exceptions. The article carries the depth; the episode carries the story.
- **Native scripts only** — written as a show from the concept, never
  adapted from the article (Ali 2026-07-07). Template:
  `series-intro-standard.txt`. Episode shape: cold open scene →
  announcer promo → brief welcome → the story (stats as discoveries) →
  the cocktail party explanation (recurring branded teaching segment) →
  the try-on → announcer next-time → "Remember, ladies" → **"See you
  next Wednesday... in Sunnyvale."**
- **Episode Zero** ("Welcome to Sunnyvale") carries ALL orientation:
  what the show is, the town tour, the weekly rhythm, the residence
  card. Numbered episodes never re-explain the premise — one "New here?
  Episode Zero is the tour" line maximum.
- **Audio-complete:** nothing requires eyes. Spoken URL, spoken
  directions. The Screening Room version adds visuals; it never carries
  information the audio lacks. (This is what makes drive-time work.)

## Serialized elements (what makes it ONE story)

- **The heroine** (voiced by Jessica) — narrates in retrospect, a few
  steps ahead. Her competence compounds on-mic: by Act II she's visibly
  faster; by Act III she's teaching.
- **Steve** (revenue operations, pickleball, "visionary") — the
  recurring foil. Arc: accidental AI poster boy → exposed as shallow
  (he never checks anything) → genuinely decent guy who, in the finale,
  asks HER for help. Never a villain; the SYSTEM is the villain.
- **The stakeholder who reads tone into line breaks** — recurring
  minor character; her emails are a running measure of the heroine's
  growing speed.
- **Running gags:** footnotes, pickleball, fleece vests,
  "Groundbreaking," the Tetris calendar, "we have receipts."
- **Cold opens are chronological** — each one is a new moment in her
  work life, so the season reads as a diary even though each lesson
  stands alone.
- **"Next time on ladies"** hooks every episode into the next.
- **"Remember, ladies"** — one fresh aphorism per episode; the 24
  lines together are the season's quotable spine (poster potential).
