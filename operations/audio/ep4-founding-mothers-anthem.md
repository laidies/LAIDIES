## FINAL LYRICS (as recorded)

```
[Intro]
(soft strings, low choir hum)
Nobody asked where the machine came from.
So I went looking.
And oh... what I found.

[Verse 1]
Eighteen forty-three, all the gentlemen agree:
it's a fancy adding machine. That's not what Ada sees.
She writes the first instructions, says the numbers are just the start.
They gave the man the glory, and they wrote her out of the part.
Then the most beautiful woman in the world, in between her scenes,
draws a signal that keeps hopping, too quick to ever be seen.
Every call you'll ever make is riding on her wave.
She never made a dime, but darling, look at what she gave.

[Pre-Chorus]
They hung the founding fathers in their frames of gold,
but the margins of the blueprint hold a story no one told.
So turn the lights on in the back wing, listen and you'll hear:
it was her. And her. And her.

[Chorus]
It was women all along!
(all along, all along)
Every spark, every line,
two hundred years of genius that the credits left behind.
Oh, the future has a mother,
she's been here the whole time.
It was women all along,
and the next line is mine.

[Verse 2]
Nineteen forty-five, six women make a computer run.
They're right there in all the photos, their names not under even one.
Then they told Grace, "It's only numbers, it will never learn a word."
She said, "That's a failure of imagination." Have you heard?
Now we talk to it in our words, 'cause she met it in the middle,
and every app you've ever opened owes her more than a little.

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Then the winters came, the funding froze, the dream went out of style,
but the women kept on working in the cold all the while.
And a woman up at Cambridge said it best back then:
(spoken) "Computing is too important to be left to men."

[Chorus]
It was women all along!
(all along, all along)
Every spark, every line,
two hundred years of genius that the credits left behind.
Oh, the future has a mother,
she's been here the whole time.
It was women all along,
and the next line is mine.

[Bridge]
(stripped back, one voice, choir joining line by line)
Ada saw it first (saw it first)
Hedy hid it in the air (in the air)
Six women made it run (made it run)
Grace gave it our words (our words)
Karen taught it what to hear (what to hear)
Fei-Fei made it see (made it see)
Joy held up the mirror (the mirror)
till it saw her face and said her name (said her name)
Timnit told the truth (told the truth)
Emily named the parrot (the parrot)
Kate showed us what it costs (what it costs)
(spoken, over building strings)
So when they ask you who the father of the future is,
tell them: the fathers only named it.
It took a Godmother to make it.
(strings swell)

[Final Chorus]
(key change, full choir)
It was women all along!
(all along, all along)
Every spark, every flame,
we keep carrying their light and we keep saying their names.
(say their names!)
Oh, the future has a mother,
she's been here the whole time.
It was women all along,
and the future... the future is OURS.

[Outro]
(one quiet voice, strings underneath)
You were never behind, honey,
you were just never told it's yours.
(choir builds, drums return)
So carry their light! (carry their light!)
Say their names! (say their names!)
Two hundred years and counting,
and our future... is... bright!
```
