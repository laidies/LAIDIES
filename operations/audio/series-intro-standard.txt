LAiDIES episode template — Episode 02 onward.
(Episode 01 carries the full "what is ladies" premiere welcome; later
episodes assume you've met us.)

THE FORMAT (locked by Ali 2026-07-06/07): episodes are WRITTEN AS A SHOW,
never adapted from the article. "We need to create a script for the
episodes, not just read out what is on paper." The article and the
episode are two outputs from one outline — same lesson, same stats, same
jokes; the audio version is born as the heroine's story.

THE FICTION: Jessica IS the heroine — the season promo's "she." She
narrates in retrospect (Carrie Bradshaw model: protagonist + columnist),
a few steps ahead of the listener, which is what licenses the stats and
the teaching. Facts arrive as things she went and found, inside scenes
("when I finally went looking, I found the number…"), never as lecture.

EPISODE SHAPE:

1. COLD OPEN — a scene, in medias res. A clock, a name, a specific
   detail (Ep 1: "It's 4:52 on a Tuesday afternoon, and a man named
   Steve is being called a visionary."). The episode's question
   crystallizes out of the scene and lands on the locked line:
   "[thoughtful] And I couldn't help but wonder..."
2. [ANNOUNCER — male, classic TV promo voice]
   [bright] Previously, on ladies: [2-3 line third-person recap of last
   episode's lesson]. [playful] And on this episode: our heroine
   [one-line third-person tease — no spoilers]. Episode [NUMBER]:
   [TITLE].
   (One consolidated block — never ping-pong with the heroine.)
3. WELCOME BACK (heroine, brief for Ep 2+):
   [warm] Welcome back to ladies — smart, busy women learning AI one
   Wednesday at a time, from a little internet town called Sunnyvale.
   (Optional beat: [smile, friendly] Get in loser! [chuckles] We're
   learning AI... [smirk] — use when it's earned, not by default.)
4. THE STORY — her week, in scenes. Research/stats woven in as
   discoveries. Cameo voices (one line each) for quotes.
5. THE COCKTAIL PARTY EXPLANATION — recurring branded segment, same
   name every week: "Which brings me to my favourite part of every
   episode. We call it the cocktail party explanation. This week's
   question: [X]." This is where the teaching payload lives.
6. THE TRY-ON — ritual outro. URL + spelling live HERE (ladies dot A I,
   L-A-i-D-I-E-S): study pack at the Blend and Snap, pop quiz at
   Sunnyvale High, anthem on K-S-V-L ninety-nine point nine, extra
   credit, residence card at Makeover on Main (first-timers).
7. [ANNOUNCER] Next time on ladies: [third-person tease — she/our
   heroine, never "you"].
8. SIGN-OFF (heroine):
   [warm] So remember, ladies: [fresh line each week]. Small sips. Big
   moves. — then a brief nod to the Rooms challenge (members-only chat
   at the sorority house; card gets you in the door; Ep 1 carried the
   full explainer).
9. [warm, smiling] See you next Wednesday... in Sunnyvale.
   The final line never changes. It is the series' last words, every week.

Sign-off challenge channel (Ali 2026-07-06): THE ROOMS at the sorority
