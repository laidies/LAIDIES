## The basics

**What is this, actually?**
LAiDIES is a show about learning AI, made for women who are already smart and already busy. It works like the TV we grew up on — a new episode every Wednesday, each one shorter than your commute, each one teaching you exactly one useful thing. It all takes place in SUNNYVAiLE: a little internet town, permanently set around 1999, where every idea has an address.

**Who is it for?**
Women who keep meaning to "figure out AI" and haven't had ten spare hours to do it. If you're smart, busy, and a little tired of AI being explained by men in fleece vests — you're in the right place.

**Do I need to know anything about AI to start?**
No. That's the whole reason we built it. You don't have to know anything yet — you just have to show up. We start at the beginning and never assume you've done homework.

**Is it free?**
Yes. The episodes, the weekly tour, your Residence Card, the quizzes, the community rooms — all free. **[confirm: keep it simple as "it's free," or add a line that a few extras down the road (like burning your KSVL mix to a CD) may be paid?]**

**Is this a course? Do I have to finish it?**
It's a show, not a course — there's no finish line and nothing to fall behind on. Do one episode, do the whole season, or dip in whenever. Nothing expires.

## How it works

**What happens every Wednesday?**
A new episode drops, and the town dresses for it: a new anthem on the radio, a new study pack at the coffee shop, new charms hidden around town. But the town is open every day — Wednesday is just when the new thing arrives.

**What's an "episode"?**
A short audio story (shorter than your commute) where our heroine learns one AI concept the way your sharpest girlfriend would explain it over coffee — with the pop culture you were raised on and none of the jargon you weren't. There's a written version too, if you'd rather read.

**Do I have to do it on Wednesday? What if I miss a week?**
Not at all, and nothing's lost. Every episode stays on the shelf forever — go back to episode one anytime, binge if you're behind, or slow down and sit with one. Wednesday is when the new thing arrives; every other day is yours.

**What's "the tour"?**
It's the recommended weekly loop that makes the learning stick. Two options, both count: the **Express Tour** (this week's episode, the study pack, the quiz, and the song) or the **Full Tour** (all eight stops around town). Do as much or as little as you want.

## Your Residence Card & signing up

**What's a Residence Card?**
Your ID for SUNNYVAiLE. It's how the town remembers you — your quiz scores, charms, stickers, and tour progress all save onto it. Without one you're a tourist (things happen, but nothing sticks); with one, you're a resident. It's free and takes about two minutes to make at MAiKEOVER on MAiN.

**What's the difference between "signing up" and "making a card"?**
Signing up (at the Post Office) gets you the **Wednesday Postcard** — one friendly email a week when the new episode is out. Making a **Residence Card** (at MAiKEOVER) is the free account that saves everything you do in town. You can do either or both.

**Do I need a password?**
No. You sign in with a magic link sent to your email — no password to remember, ever.

**What will you email me?**
One note a week: the new episode, and links to do it. That's it. No spam, no three-emails-a-week, no "are you still interested?", nothing sold to anyone. **[confirm]**

**How do I cancel or unsubscribe?**
Every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and you can stop anytime. Your card and your progress are yours. **[confirm — is there an in-app delete/leave flow, or just unsubscribe for now?]**

## The town

**Why is it a town instead of a normal website?**
Because "learning AI" is a mall directory with sixty locations and no floor plan — so we drew the floor plan. Every idea gets a place: the vocabulary lives at the LIBRAiRY, the prompt practice lives at the coffee shop, the community lives at the Sorority House. Analogies that stick, in a world we already know. It's the teaching method, not just a theme.

**Do I have to learn the whole map to start?**
Nope. Start at the Visitor's Centre, do this week's episode, and let the rest of town reveal itself. You'll have favourite spots by Friday.

**Is the AI stuff actually up to date?**
Yes — keeping it current is a rule we take seriously. AI moves fast, so we date what we teach and update it rather than let it go stale. You won't be learning last year's version of the tools.

## Rewards, community & safety

**What can I earn?**
Butterfly clips for quiz scores, charms hidden in the town's pictures, puffy stickers, trading cards, and badges — all collected on your card in your Closet. Bring a friend to town and you both earn a half of the BEST FRIENDS necklace.

**What are the community rooms?**
Members-only chat at the Sorority House — post a win, ask for advice, gossip in the Burn Book, play a round of Girl Talk. You need a Residence Card to get in the door. It's a group chat, not a lecture.

**Is it a safe/private space?**
Yes — the rooms are residents-only (card required at the door), and the vibe is "your sharpest girlfriends," not the open internet. **[confirm — moderation / reporting details to add?]**
