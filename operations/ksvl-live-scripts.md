# KSVL "Tune In Live" · Script Pack

> ⚠️ 2026-07-04 — street addresses updated to the new canonical layout (Blend & Snap 8→4, Bronze 5→7, CLAi-O 6→5, Mall 4→9, Chick Flicks 7→3). Spots recorded BEFORE this date speak the old numbers — see the re-record list.

**Purpose:** Interstitial audio between songs on the KSVL Radio stream. Two types — **Commercials** (20-30 sec, fake radio spots for SUNNYVAiLE businesses/places) and **DJ Transitions** (10-15 sec, DJ SunnyV patter). These rotate randomly between songs to make KSVL feel like a live small-town station.

**Output:** Ali records via ElevenLabs (voice-clone-per-character) or self-records. Files land in `content/music/ksvl-spots/` with filenames matching the slug in each script's header.

**Voice direction:** every character has a distinct voice register. Never break character. Deadpan reads are almost always funnier than energetic ones. Y2K low-budget local-radio energy across the whole pack — nothing polished.

## COMMERCIALS

### 1 · The Deb PSA (aka "State of the Town, Address One")

**Filename:** `spot-deb-psa-loop-me-out.mp3`
**Length:** ~20 sec
**Voice:** Deb. Flat. Weary. Like she's been forced to do this at gunpoint (metaphorically — HR sent an email that had a deadline).
**Setup:** Someone finally cornered her into a live address. She's using the airtime to campaign AGAINST her re-election. It will not work. It never works.

**SCRIPT:**

> *[dead air · 1 sec · mic click]*
>
> "They told me I couldn't go home until I addressed you all.
>
> So here it is.
>
> For the love of god, someone else run for mayor.
>
> **Loop me out.**
>
> Deb out."
>
> *[mic click · 1 sec dead air]*

**Delivery notes:**
- **"They told me I couldn't go home until I addressed you all."** — read like she's reading it off a Post-it note the intern handed her
- **"So here it is."** — beat before continuing. She was hoping that would be enough.
- **"For the love of god, someone else run for mayor."** — deadpan. No plea in her voice. She's just filing a request.
- **"Loop me out."** — said the way she says it every day. Reflex.
- **"Deb out."** — signoff. Mic click. Done.
- **No inflection lifts. No warmth. No urgency. She wants to leave.**

**Canon tie:** This is the ONLY State of the Town address Deb has ever given verbally. Every year since, the annual written address gets posted on the KSVL Radio bulletin board with her signature at the bottom — nobody has ever seen her write those. This 20 seconds is the sole verbal record. See [[deb-mayor-origin-story]].

## Production Notes

**For ElevenLabs:**
- Voice-clone one master voice for **DJ SunnyV** (Ali? Or a specific voice actor). Use across T1–T5 + the LUMINAiRY spot.
- **Deb** should be its own dedicated voice — flatter, drier, weary.
- **Mme CLAi-O** distinct — the East European accent is important.
- Other spots (Blend & Snap, BRONZE AiGE, Post Office, Delta LAi Nu, Mall, SUNNYVAiLE High, Chick Flicks) can share a small pool of 2-3 "announcer" voices — variation is fine.

**Audio treatment:**
- Slight lo-fi compression on all — sounds like actual local radio, not podcast-clean
- Mall PA spot: extra compression, slight tinniness
- SUNNYVAiLE High: slight PA-echo (school-hallway reverb)
- BRONZE AiGE: background ambient bar sound could be nice (glasses clinking, low chatter)

**File format:**
- MP3, 128kbps, mono is fine
- Drop into `content/music/ksvl-spots/`
- Filename convention: `spot-{building-slug}-{topic}.mp3` for commercials, `dj-jaidy-{topic}.mp3` for transitions
