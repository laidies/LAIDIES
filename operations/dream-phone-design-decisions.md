# Dream Phone design decisions

**Authority:** Ali's direct rulings, 2026-08-10
**Status:** ACTIVE — product direction selected; exact full-game mechanic still
requires design and representative proof

## Locked product direction

1. Dream Phone's neutral entry screen offers two equally prominent,
   first-class choices: **Just Call** and **Play the Full Game**. Neither mode
   auto-opens and neither is presented as the secondary experience.
2. **Play the Full Game is not a claim-verification or receipts game.** Source
   judgment and verification remain important AI skills, but LAiDIES already
   gives that subject a full episode and Dream Phone must not duplicate it or
   reduce AI literacy to checking claims.
3. The full game should explore broader, practical ways of working with AI.
   Its learning territory includes choosing the kind of help needed, asking
   well, noticing what the response enables or misses, improving the exchange,
   and deciding what the human does next. Verification may appear inside a
   suitable situation, but it is not the product premise or dominant loop.
4. The current scripted claim deck is retained as historical experiment and
   technical evidence only. Its public presence, existing code, admitted round
   or prior internal score does not make it the selected full-game model.

## Still open

- The exact full-game fantasy, rules, caller roles, progression, win/payoff and
  replay structure.
- Which broader AI situations are most useful, funny and distinct from existing
  LAiDIES episodes, books, classes, tools and publications.
- Whether any existing claim-deck material earns reuse as a minor situation
  after the new game model is selected.

No art direction, implementation, deployment, reward model or public release
is authorized by these product decisions.
