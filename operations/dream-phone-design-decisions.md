# Dream Phone design decisions

**Authority:** Ali's direct rulings, 2026-08-10
**Status:** ACTIVE — RESTORED CLAIM-GAME DIRECTION REOPENED FOR A BOUNDED
PLAYABLE PROOF; NOT BUILD-, RELEASE- OR EQUAL-DOOR-APPROVED

## Locked product direction

1. Dream Phone is intended to offer two equally prominent first-class choices:
   **Just Call** and **Play the Full Game**. The game door earns active equal
   billing only after the restored loop proves useful and genuinely fun.
2. The eligible full-game direction is the original multi-caller claim game,
   repaired rather than replaced by one of the rejected 2026-08-10 concepts.
   Its distinct learning job is short practice preventing, investigating and
   safely using mixed AI claims; it must not become a prose recap of the
   existing verification episode or imply that checking claims is all of AI.
3. Every round requires multiple bounded callers. No single caller, including
   Miss Jeeves, may reveal the complete answer or resolve every adjudicated
   clause. The player must combine partial information and make a consequential
   final call.
4. End-of-game guidance includes tailored ways to reduce the problem before it
   occurs: a reusable prompt, relevant tool/settings guidance and a small set
   of AI House Rules. Recommendations respond to the player's actual decisions
   rather than presenting the same generic checklist after every score.
5. Preventive instructions reduce risk; they never guarantee truth. The game
   must not teach that “be accurate,” citations, browsing, custom instructions
   or any provider setting removes the need for proportionate human judgment.

## Guidance freshness rule

The final prompt, settings guidance, AI House Rules, warning signs and any
provider-specific instructions are learning content and enter the same
freshness system as the factual rounds.

Every reusable recommendation must bind:

- a stable recommendation ID and exact displayed wording;
- its learning owner and evidence/source owner;
- whether it is provider-agnostic or names a specific product, feature or
  setting;
- the evidence checked date and next review trigger/date;
- the game states, result patterns and surfaces that consume it; and
- a correction/replacement state.

Provider-specific guidance requires current official product documentation and
recheck on a relevant product, feature, interface, naming, availability,
pricing, policy or safety change. Provider-agnostic guidance still receives a
scheduled learning review and immediate review when learner evidence or a
known failure contradicts it.

Missing, stale, corrected or unowned guidance fails closed: hide the affected
recommendation or replace it with bounded provider-neutral guidance that has
its own current admission. Never silently show an old setting name, imply a
feature exists for every user, or present stale instructions as current.

## Smallest representative proof

Before repairing all thirteen historical rounds, prove three distinct cases:

1. inflated number or missing denominator;
2. true event mixed with an unsupported location, scope or causal clause; and
3. an absurd-sounding but well-supported claim that tests plausibility bias.

The proof must include limited calls, distributed clue coverage, a meaningful
commitment, consequence, tailored preventive guidance and an unfamiliar
transfer case. If that slice is not enjoyable without explanatory copy doing
the work, park the full game.

## Rejected directions retained as evidence

- Workplace task with a manufactured hidden need.
- Time-management or project-management caper.
- The Last Call future-phone mystery.
- Don't Lose the Plot transformation premise.
- Dream Team helper matching as a one-off abstract exercise.

These concepts remain in
`product-stewards/dream-phone/CONCEPT-COMPETITION-2026-08-10.md`; none is
selected or approved for build.

## Still open

- Exact call budget, caller roles, surprise-call behaviour and final consequence.
- Exact final verdict language and whether `Hold Up` joins `For Real` and
  `As If`.
- Exact three-round pilot content and which historical rounds earn repair.
- Whether any provider-specific settings guidance is necessary; prefer durable
  provider-neutral guidance when it produces the same learner outcome.
- Visual direction, implementation, rewards, persistence, analytics,
  deployment and public release.

No art direction, implementation, deployment, spending, provider integration,
reward model or public release is authorized by these product decisions.
