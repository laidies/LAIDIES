## Canva capability findings

- Canva AI reports that it cannot currently be used inside this Video design.
- Magic Media exposes text-to-video, not the controlled image-to-video workflow
  needed to preserve the approved LAiDIES characters and source frames.
- Image-to-video options surfaced in Canva are third-party apps.

Two third-party Canva apps were inspected without opening or authorizing them:

1. **Image Animate by Vimmerse**
   - Requests permission to read and change the design and upload media to Canva
     on the user's behalf.
   - Has public pricing and a multi-model workflow.
   - The least expensive published test option is a US$2 trial for 120 credits.
2. **AI Image to Video by AppBanter**
   - Requests permission to read and change the design.
   - Public model, pricing, and production-rights information is much less clear.

No third-party app permissions were granted, no LAiDIES media was transmitted to
either provider, and no purchase was made.

## Production decision

The existing Canva design can be used as a source/sequence reference only. It is
not a finished motion pass and should not be rolled into the episode masters.

Recommended controlled next test:

1. Use one approved Episode 04 source frame.
2. Generate one approximately five-second image-to-video result.
3. Score face/body stability, wardrobe continuity, useful environmental motion,
   editability, commercial-use terms, generation cost, and export quality.
4. Do not scale any provider until this proof passes.

The safest no-purchase first test is Adobe Firefly, subject to account access and
available free credits. If the owner specifically wants the test to remain inside
Canva, Vimmerse is the better-documented option, but it requires explicit approval
for its third-party permissions and trial purchase.
