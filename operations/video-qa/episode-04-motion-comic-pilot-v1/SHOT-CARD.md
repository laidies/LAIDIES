## Maker verdict · 2026-09-04

**HOLD — mechanism retained; visual approach is insufficient on its own.**

The exact frames at 3, 6, 8 and 12 seconds preserve the source composition and
show the CRT/indicator-light event without morphing or style drift. That proves
localized motion can safely protect approved art. However, the shot still reads
primarily as a static illustration because the event occupies too little of the
frame. Environment effects may support a sequence, but they cannot carry a
narration beat whose real job is character action or spatial change.

The production rule exposed by this pilot is: every narration beat receives one
meaningful start/middle/end action. Local light, rain, dust and camera movement
are supporting motion only. A shot with supporting motion but no primary action
remains an animatic and does not enter a full Episode successor.
