# SUNNYVAiLE Town-Keeper Roster (living tracker)

Every building on MAiN gets a **keeper** — a SUNNYVAiLE *native* character who runs it.
Natives are **original** (not imported icons — those live in the LUMINAiRY), which makes
them the honest lever for **diverse representation**: no recognition bar, no box-check,
full freedom. Ali names her natives; ethnicity/look are her call.

Each keeper ideally embodies one AI-fluency idea in the world (characters teach).

## Notes
- **THREE tiers now (refines the old LUMINAiRY-vs-natives line, [[town-characters-live-at-town-hall]]):**
  (1) **LUMINAiRY** = imported *lead* icons you REVERE as patrons (each owns an AI lesson) — Cher, Elle, Miranda…
  (2) **Native keepers** = original SUNNYVAiLE characters who run buildings — JoJo, Paige, Penny, Matron Lumen…
  (3) **Resident cameos** = beloved imported *supporting* characters who live/work in town (not revered patrons) — **Paulette** (Legally Blonde) is the first. Distinct from a saint; distinct from a native.
- **Representation strategy (Ali 2026-07-18):** don't tokenize the LUMINAiRY saints (imported
  icons skew to Ali's 90s-white-Canadian canon); carry real diversity in (a) the MAiVENS +
  TRAiLBLAZERS wings (real WOC in AI) and (b) **these original town natives**, where it's free
  and authentic. See [[saint-roster-rethink-2026-07]].
- **POC keepers so far (Ali 2026-07-18):** FAiRY Godmother, JoJo (barista), Miss Jeeves, Paige —
  plus the still-open BRONZE AiGE bartender / Chick Flicks clerk / Delta LAi Nu head are the natural
  spots to keep building it. Town-native representation is already solid; LUMINAiRY saints stay
  cast-from-love without box-checking.
- **NAMING RULE (Ali 2026-07-18):** if a keeper's name **isn't a title**, it's a **classic 90s
  first name** (the girls in your class: Ashley, Tiffany, Nicole, Paige, Penny, Crystal…), ideally
  with a *subtle* thematic echo — NOT a forced pun. Model: **JoJo** the barista (real 90s name +
  quiet "cup of joe"). Reverent building (the LUMINAiRY) gets a **title that sounds church-like but
  isn't clergy** — e.g. **Matron / Vestal Lumen** (Ali's pick pending). Avoid "Keeper of X" (that's
  the MAiVENS' format).
- Each keeper eventually owes: a name, a portrait (town-character style), and a home on their
  building page. Portrait batch lives in `operations/codex-prompts/town-characters-portrait-batch.md`.
