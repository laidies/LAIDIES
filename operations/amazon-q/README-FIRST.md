## What LAiDIES must become

LAiDIES helps smart, busy women without a technical background understand and
use AI at work and at home. It teaches enough of the real mechanism—from
compute, training and models through context, tools, products and outputs—for a
reader to connect the parts, judge claims and participate in decisions about
work and society.

SUNNYVAiLE is a cohesive Rewind Era learning town with 17 different buildings.
The world is not decorative theming: place, characters and familiar references
make difficult ideas memorable and enjoyable. Practical value still leads. A
first visitor must understand what LAiDIES offers and how to begin without town
lore, an account or a membership promise.

The voice is the reader's smartest, funniest, enthusiastic Rewind Era friend:
warm, specific, lightly irreverent, accurate and useful before clever. Explain
the plain mechanism, connect the system, use an analogy only when it genuinely
helps, show a real work/home example, and leave the reader able to do, explain
or question something. Episode 1 is the voice benchmark, subject to current
corrections routed by `operations/DECISIONS.md`.

Non-compensable floors: usefulness, factual accuracy and freshness, teaching
quality, LAiDIES voice, visual/Brand craft, UX, accessibility, function and
truthful state. Strength in one cannot compensate for failure in another.

5. never let a simulated reader occupy a real-human evidence slot.

artifact-handoff shape, task stop/rescope budgets, unavailable rather than fake
zero metrics, and two measured weeks of parity before retiring legacy control

## Failure patterns that keep recurring

Ranked by damage:

1. **False pass / wrong bytes:** reviews, manifests or claims refer to an older
   candidate while the displayed artifact changed.
2. **Ali as QA:** an artifact reaches Ali before objective, reader, visual or
   interaction defects are removed.
3. **Known failures recur:** rejected candy palettes, white card walls,
   corporate-module prose, generic dashboards and stale names return because
   the producer did not consume the current negative registry.
4. **Integrity impersonates quality:** build success, hashes, route loads,
   schema completeness and screenshots are labelled or interpreted as review.
5. **Planning outruns delivery:** specifications, packets, owners and evidence
   multiply while publicly verified throughput remains near zero.
6. **Parallelism creates reconciliation:** too many agents advance shared work
   and one founder must resolve the collisions. Use at most two lanes and keep
   shared-context building in one foreground lane.
7. **Source-of-truth drift:** old canon, legacy status files, old architecture
   and retained rejected files remain searchable and are mistaken for current
   authority.
8. **Building becomes generic UI:** scenery sits above ordinary cards/boxes
   instead of the place itself providing an intuitive, scalable interface.
9. **Downstream production starts too early:** visuals, audio, quizzes and
   derivatives are built before prose/product foundations are admitted.
10. **Local becomes public in language:** a file, test, commit, preview or
    deploy is described as live or complete without exact public verification.

## How the site should run after opening

### Twice daily — NewsStand radar

Check primary sources, independent reporting, qualified practitioner leads and
the AIDB cross-check. Decide `THE BREAKING`, `THE DAILY`, `THE WEEKLY`,
`THE TRIBUNE`, `WATCH` or `PASS`. There is no quota and no filler. A clear day
is success. The radar prepares; it never auto-publishes.

### Daily — freshness and Daily production

- scan product/model/access/price/policy/security/practical-workflow signals;
- resolve exact current primary evidence;
- compare affected registered LAiDIES claims and consumers;
- assemble only source-ready Daily columns; unavailable columns use an honest
  governed empty state;
- objective check → role-distinct editorial judgment → Review Door;
- publish or hold with the exact reason; and
- interrupt Ali only for a public-voice or consequential-claim decision.

### Weekly — episode engine

- **Monday:** angle and primary-source fact sheet; exact claim mapping.
- **Tuesday:** prose against current canon and Episode 1; producer self-read and
  internal repair.
- **Wednesday:** role-distinct voice/teaching judgment → Review Door → one Ali
  text decision. Produce audio, visuals, quiz and cards only from approved text.
- **Thursday:** propagate concepts, links, indexes, freshness and correction
  effects to every consumer.
- **Friday:** deploy, verify publicly, measure and record one reusable learning
  or explicitly none.

### Weekly and release-triggered — registered claim freshness

Run the claim/source/consumer review before every episode or affected release.
A material due, blocked or contradicted claim holds only the affected package.
A signal may create a correction order; it cannot rewrite or publish canon.

### Weekly — product and audience review

Review broken journeys, feedback, accessibility, performance, publication
results and bounded growth experiments. Analytics are `UNKNOWN`, not zero,
until privacy-safe aggregate sources are connected. Retain only changes that
alter a real decision or visitor outcome.

### Monthly

- product health for identity, community, rewards, privacy and trust;
- real browser/device accessibility, search, metadata and performance review;
- cost/vendor review and backup/restore health; and
- one randomly selected published paragraph read cold for voice quality.

### Quarterly

Review teaching/learning methods and the maintained Hannah Fry communication
benchmark. Update the production standard only when evidence changes it; do
not churn every artifact.
