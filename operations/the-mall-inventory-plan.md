## Filters (carried over from REFERENCE-CLOSET.md — keep as Mall-wide rule)

- **Taste filter** — references should be widely known enough that the audience gets the joke quickly. David Rose is the standing exception because specificity is the point. The canon should not rewrite Ali as the coolest person in the room. Favor mall/suburban/mainstream/workplace-adjacent/lovingly specific over retroactive cool-girl credibility.
- **Geography filter** — Ali's memory bank is Canadian, so some U.S.-centric references may not feel personally vivid. That is fine; LAiDIES has users outside Canada, so broader North American/global Y2K references can belong when widely recognizable and useful.

### Naming pun (triple-layer)
1. **MAiYBE** = Maybelline with brand-Ai natural in MAi (only second store besides CLAiRE'S with brand-Ai)
2. **Question / answer structure** — store name asks ("MAiYBE She's Born With It?"), parenthetical answers ("MAiYBE it's drugstore glitter"). Subverts the original Maybelline tagline ("Maybe she's born with it. Maybe it's Maybelline.") by replacing the brand-promise punchline with the deflated truth. The original sells; the LAiDIES version tells.
