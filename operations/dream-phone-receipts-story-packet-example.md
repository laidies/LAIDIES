# Dream Phone · "Is That Even True?" — LOCKED MODEL + worked round

## THE MODEL (locked 2026-07-14)

**Premise.** The AI states a wild claim about a real 90s/Y2K thing. You call town characters; each gives **2–3 leads** (references), never a verdict. You work several, weigh the sources, and make one call.

**Player's call = clean BINARY:** ✅ **FOR REAL** (trust it) / ❌ **AS IF** (don't buy it). No fuzzy middle.

**Each claim = 1–3 tagged ASSERTIONS.** Each is `true` / `inflated` / `made-up`:
| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **true** | happened, fairly described |
| **inflated** | real kernel, but a number/magnitude is juiced |
| **made-up** | didn't happen at all |

**Verdict logic:** all `true` ⇒ For Real. Any `inflated` or `made-up` ⇒ As If. The player only presses For Real / As If; the **reveal** names the flavor (taught, never refereed at the button).

### THE TWO DETECTION RULES (the whole thing lives or dies here)
The resolving evidence must **never be a source that states the answer** — a handed number is trivial, and you can't catch a lie by *absence* (2 sources saying "it was on 20/20" just reads as *true*). So:

- **Made-up → the confirmers name *different specifics*.** Don't have two sources agree on "20/20." Have them scatter: one says 20/20, one Dateline, one 60 Minutes, one Oprah. **Nobody agrees on which show → nobody actually saw it → made up.** The tell is the *contradiction you can point at,* not a spooky absence. (A real event: everyone names the same show.)
- **Inflated → the denominator is *missing from the packet*.** Everyone shouts the scary number (injuries); **nobody says how many were sold.** The catch is the *instinct to ask "…out of how many?"* and go dig up the missing piece yourself. You catch it by noticing what's **not there**, never by validating a ratio you were handed.

### Design rules (hard-won)
- Assertions are about **different things**, never two flavors of one (don't pair "recalled" w/ "banned" — they blur).
- Inflated = a **believable number, not a cartoonish superlative** ("thousands," not "most dangerous toy ever").
- Reals are **actually real + sourced**; fakes are **myths/distortions** so a wrong call still corrects toward truth.
- **No oracle:** every contact only *points*, and each whiffs on part of it. The winning insight is a judgment *you* make (spot the scatter / find the missing denominator), never a doc handed over.

## THE ROUND — Sky Dancers

**☎️ THE AI:** *"Sky Dancers — those fairy dolls you'd snap onto a launcher and send spinning into the air with a pull-string — injured* ***[thousands of kids]*** *and even got* ***[their own primetime TV exposé]*** *before they were finally pulled off shelves."*

**Assertions (2, cleanly separate — one of each kind):**
- 🔴 **A — "thousands of kids"** → **INFLATED.** Truth: ~150 injuries. But the denominator — **~9 MILLION sold** — is nowhere in the packet; you have to realize you need it and go get it.
- ⚫ **B — "their own primetime TV exposé"** → **MADE UP.** Never aired. The "confirmers" can't agree which show, because none of them saw it.
- *(the recall itself is true, woven in as context — not graded)*

**Overall: AS IF.** Truth source: **CPSC recall notice** — https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2000/cpsc-galoob-toys-inc-announce-recall-of-sky-dancers-flying-dolls
(All quotes below except the CPSC facts are illustrative game noise.)

### Who points to what (2–3 leads each, biased by vantage)

| Contact | Their leads | Serves |
|---|---|---|
| 📚 **Miss Jeeves** | news clipping: *"~150 reported injuries"* + *"we don't keep TV transcripts, dear — can't help on the telly bit."* | real injury count (A) · whiffs on B |
| 🧸 **Toy-Shop Keeper** (mall) | *"Sky Dancers?? Those flew off the shelves — we reordered every week, must've sold millions. Everybody had one."* | **the missing denominator (A)** — but only if you think to ask |
| ☕ **The Barista** | forum post: *"I was there, it clipped my sister's eye, ER trip"* (real, one anecdote) + *"the TV thing? pretty sure that was **Dateline**?"* | injuries-real (A) + B scatter |
| 📧 **Deb** | chain email: *"THOUSANDS BLINDED — it was on **20/20**!! fwd to 12 friends!!"* + Kathy on ICQ: *"i think it was **60 Minutes**??"* | echoes "thousands" + B scatter |
| 🗣️ **Office Gossip** | *"everyone I know got hurt, it was thousands"* + *"no no, I saw it on **Oprah**"* | echoes "thousands" + B scatter |
| 📻 **DJ SunnyV** | *"a caller swore she caught it on **some news show**, couldn't remember which"* | B scatter |
| 🔮 **Mme CLAi-O** | *"the cards sense… studio lights… cameras… $40, please."* | pure vibes |

### Sample playthrough
1. **Deb** → "thousands blinded, on **20/20**" + Kathy "**60 Minutes**?" Two lies — and the shows already don't match.
2. **Office Gossip** → "thousands hurt" + "I saw it on **Oprah**." **DJ SunnyV** → "some news show." That's four different shows now. Nobody who *actually watched* a program gets the channel wrong → the exposé is **made up.**
3. **Jeeves** → the real injury number is *~150* (not thousands) + no TV transcript exists.
4. **150 injuries… out of how many?** Nobody's said. That's the missing piece — so you go get it: **Toy-Shop Keeper** → *"we sold millions."*
5. 150 out of millions = a rounding error → *"thousands"* is **inflated.** Four un-matching shows → the exposé is **made up.**
6. **Call it: AS IF.**

### The reveal (fun + lesson)
> **As If. ❌**
> Recalled? Yes. *"Thousands"?* Try **~150** — out of about **9 MILLION sold.** A rounding error dressed up as a massacre. Nobody in your rolodex *volunteered* how many sold, either — you had to know to ask. **(Inflated: always find the denominator.)**
> The TV exposé? Four people "saw" it — on **four different shows.** Nobody who actually watched a thing gets the channel wrong. It never aired. **(Made up: a pile of confident echoes that can't agree isn't a source.)**
> One juiced, one invented. *As if.* 💅

### What this round teaches
- An AI answer is a **bundle of separate claims** — check each; one bad part poisons the whole.
- **Inflated:** the scary number is meaningless without the denominator — and nobody hands it to you, so you learn to *go ask for it.*
- **Made-up:** confident echoes that **contradict each other on the specifics** = nobody saw it. Scatter is the tell.
- **No oracle:** Jeeves gave you the injury count but whiffed on the show *and* never mentioned sales — you had to work the phones and do the thinking.
