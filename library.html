How to use the LIBRAiRY

You don't sit and read it. You pull what you need. Power Map when you're working a stakeholder problem. Chamber of Receipts when you need to back up the take. Ask Jeeves when you don't even know what to call the thing you're stuck on. Then you close the cover and go.

If you're trying to learn AI in a structured way — sit-through-the-class, take-the-quiz way — that's at SUNNYVAiLE High, not here.
