Welcome to SUNNYVAiLE.

SUNNYVAiLE is the fictional small town where LAiDIES lives. Every Wednesday a new episode airs from it. Every section of the site has an address in it. You don't need to understand the town to be here — but once you know you're in a place, everything starts to make more sense.

Same relationship as Buffy and Sunnydale. You weren't watching a show about a town. You were watching Buffy, and Sunnydale was just the texture that made it feel like somewhere real instead of nowhere in particular. LAiDIES is the show. SUNNYVAiLE is where every episode takes place.

Why a fictional town?

Because the women who built this were raised on 24-episode seasons, iconic female characters, and a lesson threaded through every story arc. We think in analogies. It turns out that's exactly how AI makes sense — when you put it somewhere familiar, it stops being abstract and starts being something you can actually use.

The references aren't decoration. They're the map.

The town.

SUNNYVAiLE has a Town Library where you look things up, a Video Rental Shop where the episodes live, a Sorority House where the community meets, and a SANCTUAiRY where the Patron Saints reside. There's The BRONZE AiGE for happy hour, a Community Radio Station, a NewsStand on MAiN Street, and a Town Hall where Deb is somehow the mayor. (She's been here longer than anyone. Nobody is sure what she does. IT has accommodated her setup without complaint.)

The town is built from the cultural texture of the era that shaped us. Taping songs off the radio. Friday nights at the video rental shop. Sleepovers with Dream Phone and cootie catchers and board games that somehow taught us everything about reading a room. That world didn't just entertain us — it wired us. SUNNYVAiLE rewards remembering it.

You live here now.

Every Wednesday a new episode drops. Every section has an address. The Patron Saints are at The SANCTUAiRY, the Grimoire is at the Library, and the coven has a reservation at The BRONZE AiGE at 4.

Find your bearings. Then get to work.
