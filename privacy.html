Privacy

What the site saves.

LAiDIES keeps the signup flow intentionally light. Resident Card asks for an email and optional profile answers such as work lane, AI experience, generation vibe, and what you want from the site. Those choices help shape future packs and community features.

Before You Sign Up

Before signing up, quiz scores, trading-card pulls, secret stickers, and Resident Card choices are saved in this browser using local storage. They do not follow you across devices unless Resident Card login is connected and you sign in.

Resident Card

When you sign in with your Resident Card, LAiDIES uses Supabase to send the login email and store your Resident Card profile plus town rewards like stickers, badges, trading cards, and quiz progress. Rewards are for fun and progress tracking, not verification or certification.

Newsletter

The newsletter is handled separately through Buttondown. During Resident Card signup, the newsletter checkbox is optional and off by default. You can also subscribe without creating a Resident Card from the standalone newsletter form.

Public Cards

Member cards are meant to help the room feel like a real community: easier introductions, shared interests, and more ways to find people building with AI too. Sharing is encouraged, but it should never feel required. Public sharing uses a separate card-builder approval step. Keep confidential work information, customer data, and anything private out of submissions.

Analytics

To see what's working and make the site better, LAiDIES uses two privacy-respecting tools. Plausible counts visits and page views in aggregate — no cookies, no personal data, nothing that identifies you. Microsoft Clarity provides anonymized heatmaps and session playback (where people click and scroll) so confusing spots can be found and fixed; recordings are automatically masked — text you type and personal details are hidden — and are never used to identify anyone. Neither tool is used for advertising.

Deletion Or Questions

For deletion requests or privacy questions, contact LAiDIES through Instagram @laidies.ai until the dedicated support inbox is added.
