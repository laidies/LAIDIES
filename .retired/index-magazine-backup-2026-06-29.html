From the Editor

I grew up in the era of 24-episode seasons, iconic female characters, and a lesson woven into every story arc. I think in analogies, and it turns out it's a pretty useful (and fun!) way to explain AI.

I'm also passionate about women being the ones who shape AI, not just catch up to it. That's why I started LAiDIES.

So I built us a whole world to learn it in. LAiDIES lives in the fictional small town of SunnyVAiLE — full of the shops, spots, and characters we grew up with. Every section of the site has an address here. Every Wednesday, a new episode drops at the video rental shop.

AI fluency and community for smart women with full calendars, high standards, and no patience for beige tech explanations. The 90s and Y2K shaped us. AI is shaping now. LAiDIES and SunnyVAiLE is where they meet.

I'm glad you're here. Now get in loser — we're learning AI.

— Ali, Founder

This Week · Episode 04

A Slayer Needs a Watcher

On this episode…

The one in which she opens five browser tabs — one per tool, like a cable TV lineup from 2001 — types the same prompt into all of them, gets back five wildly different responses, briefly considers building a comparison spreadsheet (does not build the comparison spreadsheet), and arrives, somewhere between ChatGPT delivering a confident five-paragraph essay, Claude stopping to ask a clarifying question she didn't know she needed, Perplexity producing an answer with actual citations attached, and Gemini casually noting it already has access to her Google Calendar, at the question she should have been asking from the start: not which tool is best, but who is right for this scene. Not a ranking. A casting guide. Nobody gets eliminated. They all get a role. The coven has a reservation at 4.
