# SUNNYVAiLE credits v1 — image-generation prompt set

Mode: built-in image generation. All outputs were generated as 16:9 high-fidelity pixel-art plates,
then copied into `assets/video/sunnyvaile-credits-v1-plates/` before video assembly.

Shared constraints for every plate:

- Visible square pixels, ordered dithering, shading-built edges, semi-realistic adult anatomy.
- Town palette: plum `#4b2148`, rose `#9b3f5f`, pink `#e982ab`, teal `#57b6c0`,
  gold `#c9a227`, cream `#fffdfb`.
- No words, captions, readable signage, watermarks, robots, circuitry, generic AI imagery, chibi,
  anime, or smooth digital painting.
- No flags from real places. Final rerolls additionally prohibited flags, pennants, banners,
  flagpoles, bunting, and political symbols.
- Characters are candid and working, not posed or staring at camera.
