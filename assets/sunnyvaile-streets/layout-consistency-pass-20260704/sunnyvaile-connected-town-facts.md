## Map Signage Language

- The town map should use tourist-map labels integrated into the artwork, aligned with roads or placed in a directory/map legend.
- Avoid giant floating municipal blades on the map. Municipal street blades are for close street scenes.
- The top map title should not be a large roadside sign dominating the composition.
