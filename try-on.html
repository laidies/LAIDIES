The Try-On

Prompt Like Elle.

That is the copy-paste move. Not AI cosplay; an instruction you paste into your own AI tool so the draft comes back with claims, support, missing receipts, fragile details, uncertainty, and a safer version before anything borrows your name.

Mini example

Here were the notes. Here is what AI made too tidy.

Messy notes

July could work if procurement clears by Friday.
Training might move to phase two.
Final approval: account owner to confirm.

AI answer

The client approved a July rollout and asked us to remove the training module from scope.

Receipts pass catches
  • “Could work” is not approved.
  • “Phase two” is not removed.
  • The account owner still has the timeline.
Step 1

Open your approved AI tool in another tab or app. This box will not answer anything; it only helps you copy the prompt.
