How rental works.

The Chick Flicks is a video store — but nothing's actually rented, nothing's ever due back, and no one's going to lecture you about the late fee.

  • New releases drop every Wednesday on the front wall.
  • The back catalog is open whenever — no late fees.
  • The Study Pack for each episode lives next door at The Blend & Snap — grab one before the Pop Quiz at SUNNYVAiLE High.
  • The Wednesday Postcard sends new releases straight to your inbox if you'd rather not walk over — sign up at the LAiDIES Post Office.
  • Be kind. Please. Rewind. Even though it's streaming. It's the principle.
